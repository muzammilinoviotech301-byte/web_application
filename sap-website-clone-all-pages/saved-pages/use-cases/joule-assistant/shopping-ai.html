Overview

The Shopping Assistant actively reshapes site content and layouts while engaging shoppers through chat, voice, and text in real time to turn intent into transactions. By unifying behavioral signals, inventory awareness, and promotional intelligence, it accelerates conversion and drives revenue across the buying journey.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Detecting and scoring shopper intent signals
  • Personalizing site content and layout in real time
  • Selecting and applying promotions dynamically
  • Personalizing and optimizing the checkout experience
  • Monitoring and executing conditional purchases
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Increasing conversion from discovery through checkout
  • Reducing cart and checkout abandonment
  • Capturing demand with personalized promotions
  • Monetizing high-intent shopper sessions
  • Automating purchases for price-sensitive shoppers

Benefits

Convert intent into transactions
Turn shopper signals into timely actions across discovery, promotions, and purchase to transform interest into completed transactions.

Personalize every touchpoint
Continuously adapt site content, layouts, offers, and interactions so every shopper sees what’s most relevant to them in the moment.

Maximize revenue capture
Increase close rates with personalized promotions through checkout and automated purchasing that converts ready‑to‑buy demand.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Shopping Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Shopping Search Intent Agent

Tailors site content and layouts in real time using shopper behavior, context, and intent signals.

Promotions Personalization Agent

Optimizes promotions dynamically based on inventory, demand, and shopper intent.

Conditional Purchasing Agent

Monitors shopper defined price and timing conditions and automatically completes purchases when criteria are met.

Commerce Order Capture Agent

Interprets order emails, resolves product availability and pricing, and creates pre-populated carts.

Shopping Orchestration Agent

Understands shopper intent to enhance search, recommendations, cart, checkout, and agent routing.
