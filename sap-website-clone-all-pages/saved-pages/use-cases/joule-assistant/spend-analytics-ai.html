Overview: Deploy agents today. Unlock even more tomorrow.

The Spend Analytics Assistant in Joule Work automatically surfaces quantified cost savings, compliance improvements, and supplier opportunities, handing procurement teams impactful information to act on. It coordinates specialized agents that continuously provide data quality improvement recommendations, enrich spend and supplier classification data, and benchmark performance against industry peers, turning raw spend data into measurable business impact.

The agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they'll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI Platform and SAP Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Continuously analyzing incoming transactions and detecting potential savings opportunities, compliance concerns, and risks
  • Investigating identified opportunities and gathering supporting evidence to substantiate their validity
  • Generating corrective actions with estimated impact and timeframes for realization
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Comprehensive spend visibility with unified, AI-enriched and classified data
  • Increased savings impact through supplier consolidation and tail spend opportunities
  • Address risk within the supply base, whether single or limited source
  • Resolution of compliance concerns through off-contract spend recommendations
  • Performance benchmarks against industry peers to increase procurement performance
