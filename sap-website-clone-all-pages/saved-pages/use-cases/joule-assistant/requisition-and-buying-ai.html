Overview: Deploy agents today. Unlock even more tomorrow.

The Requisition Assistant in Joule Work simplifies purchasing by creating requisitions conversationally. It coordinates specialized agents that intelligently guide users to the right buying channels, identify procurement information based on context, and propose alternate items and suppliers for non-catalog purchases. It helps employees get what they need faster with less maverick spending and full buying policy compliance.

The agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they'll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI Platform and SAP Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.
