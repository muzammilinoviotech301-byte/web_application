Overview

The AI Governance Assistant helps you govern the AI agents, LLMs, and MCP (model context protocol) servers throughout your organization. It surfaces agents and AI technologies needing attention, drafts regulatory assessments for your legal team's review, compares agents, and flags governance gaps and redundancies. It performs these actions using your AI asset inventory and connected business architecture.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Prioritizing high business impact agents in the inbox
  • Performing regulatory assessment drafts per agent
  • Completing cross-agent comparison on all available data
  • Surfacing context-aware agents
  • Detecting governance gaps, on demand or proactively
  • Identifying redundant or duplicate agents
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Enterprise-wide AI agent oversight
  • Agent portfolio readiness for AI regulation
  • Reduction of agent sprawl and duplication
  • Proactive governance gap closure
  • Risk-based prioritization across the agent inventory

Benefits

Enterprise-wide visibility
