The AI agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they’ll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI platform and Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

The Category Management Assistant helps procurement teams create, refine, and continuously optimize category strategies using AI-enhanced insights. It combines spend data, supplier context, and external market intelligence to deliver actionable recommendations, identify risks and opportunities, and adapt strategies in real time, enabling more informed, proactive execution.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Identifying risks, opportunities, and value leakage
  • Drafting and refining category strategies
  • Recommending strategy adjustments and actions
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Continuous category strategy updates driven by real-time spend and market signals
  • Analysis of emerging supplier, cost, regulatory, and demand shifts for new opportunity alerts
  • Spend visibility and value leakage detection through unified classification and insights across all categories
