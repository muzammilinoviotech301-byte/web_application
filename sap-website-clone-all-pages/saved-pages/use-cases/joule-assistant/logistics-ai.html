Overview

The Logistics Assistant orchestrates autonomous detection, planning, and execution across warehousing and transportation, turning intelligence into coordinated actions. It helps you keep operations running from inbound shipments to outbound deliveries by managing labor, inventory, dispatch, and freight decisions. The assistant continuously senses disruptions and resolves issues—driving efficient, resilient, and scalable logistics operations.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Validating receipts and overseeing put-away
  • Aligning labor and replenishment to demand
  • Optimizing outbound execution and dispatch decisions
  • Detecting and mitigating disruptions
  • Resolving delivery issues autonomously
  • Monitoring inventory levels, managing reservations, and controlling batch expiration
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Reduced dock-to-stock time with automated receiving and put-away
  • Delivery performance protection with proactive disruption detection and replanning
  • Improved inventory accuracy through automated monitoring and corrections
  • Resolution of delivery issues before they impact customer commitments
  • Faster, more reliable outbound fulfillment through optimized task execution and error reduction

Benefits

Faster plan-to-execution time
Align labor with demand, validate receipts, maintain bin replenishment, and optimize dispatch decisions to accelerate dock-to-stock and pick-to-ship times.

Increased disruption resilience
Sense real-time logistics events, detect plan and inventory anomalies early, and autonomously replan to avoid disruption impact and accelerate recovery.

Lower cost to serve
Simulate dispatch, consolidate deliveries, adjust bins, correct inventory, and minimize batch waste to cut operational costs and avoid expedites.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Logistics Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Warehouse Workload Optimization Agent

Predicts shift workloads and balances resource allocation to cut bottlenecks and idle time.

Warehouse Outbound Optimization Agent

Detects problem patterns and resolves outbound delivery issues to prevent delays.

Inventory Supervisory Agent

Enables full inventory transparency to surface stock imbalances and recommend next actions.

Consignment Order Advance Shipping Notice Agent

Validates and reconciles delivery documents and automates postings to shorten dock-to-stock time.

Transportation Dispatching Agent

Recommends, simulates, and executes dispatch decisions based on the transportation planner’s instructions.

Logistics Issue Resolution Agent

Detects and resolves integration issues and blocked processes to keep logistics operations on track.

Fixed Bin Maintenance Agent

Optimizes bin assignments and replenishment based on demand to prevent stockouts and reduce travel.

Outbound Warehouse Task Management Agent

Automates outbound task bundling and queue assignment to accelerate picking and reduce effort.

Internal Stock Replenishment Agent

Keeps fixed bins, pick locations, and line-side positions stocked through automated replenishment.

Material Reservation Processing Agent

Manages material reservations at scale through natural language to reduce effort and prevent delays.

Batch Optimization Agent

Monitors batch expiration and triggers actions to minimize waste.

Logistics Configuration Agent

Guides transportation setup through conversational dialogue to process variants, carrier rules, and schedules.
