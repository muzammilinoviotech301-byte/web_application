Overview: Deploy agents today. Unlock even more tomorrow.

The AI agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they’ll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI platform and Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

The Supplier Management Assistant helps streamline the full supplier lifecycle by using AI agents to unify supplier data, assess performance, deliver risk-aware insights, and automate supplier collaboration. These capabilities support more informed decision-making and build more resilient sourcing and procurement processes.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Finding vetted suppliers by category and region
  • Automating supplier creation, data collection, enrichment, and validation
  • Segmenting suppliers based on policy-driven orchestration of downstream procurement activities
  • Recommending best-fit suppliers based on automated screening and financial risk assessment
  • Using dynamic performance assessments and dashboards for data-driven decisions
  • Prescribing corrective action plans before performance risk materializes
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Supplier relationship management across registration, qualification, onboarding, performance, and offboarding
  • Support for sourcing activities through supplier performance analysis and data
  • Improvement of supplier health and satisfaction to achieve customer-of-choice status

Benefits

Faster supplier profile creation

Smarter supplier decisions

Better supplier collaboration
Automate supplier data collection and related activities to improve supplier relationships.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Supplier Management Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Supplier Management Creation Agent

Automates supplier data collection, validation, and enrichment for compliance and accuracy.

Supplier Management Performance Agent

Performs automated trend analysis to identify suppliers that are on a deteriorating trajectory before they miss a milestone.

Supplier Management Data Quality Agent

Monitors supplier master data to detect completeness gaps, duplicates, and expiring certificates and routes issues to the right owner for resolution.

Supplier Management Identification and Optimization Agent

Automates supplier matching, capability scoring, financial risk assessment, and environmental, social, and governance profiling.
