Overview

Manage the external workforce with clarity and control across the full lifecycle. Keep teams focused on policy-aligned decisions, streamlined approvals, and verified skills data so outcomes stay compliant, efficient, and auditable. As agents iteratively pass structured inputs, evidence, and approvals, work moves faster, enabling better decisions, shorter time to hire, and stronger accountability.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating compliant job postings with the right templates and skills
  • Guiding rate setting, candidate review, onboarding readiness, and worker changes within policy
  • Reviewing worker lifecycle events, validating compliance requirements, and finding missing documents or issues
  • Surfacing KPI trends, risks, and exceptions while routing evidence and approvals across the workflow
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Policy-aligned external workforce hiring and job posting creation
  • Market-informed rate guidance and competitive offer decision support
  • Review-ready candidate assessments and job-relevant qualification validation
  • Worker lifecycle management and compliance oversight including changes

Benefits

Faster hiring and assignment preparedness
Reduces rework and accelerates start times by  supporting job creation, rate guidance, candidate review, and mobilization.

Stronger compliance and audit readiness
Improves consistency and auditability by applying policy checks, workflow controls, exception handling, and evidence management.

Better program oversight and risk management
Helps teams monitor activity, identify issues early, and prioritize next steps through enhanced visibility and risk detection.
