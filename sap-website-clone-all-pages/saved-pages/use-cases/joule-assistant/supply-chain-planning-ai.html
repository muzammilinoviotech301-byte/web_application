Overview

The Supply Chain Planning Assistant introduces AI agents into day-to-day planning workflows: exception management, demand fulfillment, inventory assessment, and capacity analysis. It helps planners detect issues earlier, understand constraints, and respond consistently to change with one-click scenario simulation, root-cause analysis, and actionable recommendations.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Converting supply chain alerts into one-click resolutions
  • Identifying structural capacity issues and persistent shortages, and creating data-driven scenarios for quick decision-making
  • Evaluating new inventory targets, identifying key drivers, and approving workflows
  • Resolving unfulfilled demand by pinpointing gating factors and recommending feasible mitigation paths
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Accelerated exception management including monitoring, context creation, what-if scenarios, and plan change recommendations
  • Proactive identification of structural capacity issues and persistent shortages, including evaluation of strategic mitigation options
  • Automated analysis of supply chain constraints and gating factors to resolve unfulfilled demand situations
  • Orchestrated evaluation and approval guidance for new inventory targets, including identification of inventory drivers

Benefits

Accelerated decision-making
Compress decision time through automated analysis and one-click resolutions to protect service levels and prevent costly expedites.

Proactive risk mitigation
Identify structural capacity issues and persistent shortages in advance to improve adherence to scheduling and inventory targets.

Improved order fulfillment
Resolve unfulfilled demand by analyzing constraints and propose feasible mitigation options to increase order fulfillment rates, improving customer satisfaction.

Intelligent inventory control
Optimize stock targets while maintaining full governance and traceability to increase working capital efficiency.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Supply Chain Planning Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Exception Management Agent

Detects and prioritizes exceptions and proposes mitigations to reduce reactive work.

Inventory Investment Allocation Agent

Analyzes inventory levels, generates data-driven scenarios, and evaluates cost and service-level trade-offs.

Footprint Optimization Agent

Highlights footprint hot spots, simulates reduction strategies, and compares scenarios against the baseline to enhance decision‑making.

Supply Plan Explainability Agent

Automatically surfaces the root cause of gaps in supply plans and provides actionable recommendations in plain language.
