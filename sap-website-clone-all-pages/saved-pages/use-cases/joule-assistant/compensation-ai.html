Overview

The Compensation Assistant enables smarter, more consistent compensation decisions. It brings together people, pay, and performance data, guides managers through planning cycles, and uses specialized agents to surface insights and streamline approvals. By connecting compensation and performance, it helps organizations execute fair, compliant, and market‑aligned rewards programs with confidence.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Aggregating people, pay, and performance data into compensation‑ready views for planners
  • Highlighting employees at risk of compression or inequity
  • Guiding managers through compensation planning steps and policy guardrails
  • Flagging budget exceptions and helping leaders rebalance allocations across teams
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Compensation planning
  • Pay equity checks
  • Budget control and compliance monitoring

Benefits

More consistent, policy‑aligned pay decisions
Apply guidelines and guardrails consistently so pay decisions align with budgets, policies, and governance standards.

Faster, more confident planning cycles
Reduce manual analysis and speed up compensation cycles while improving decision quality.

Improved pay equity and risk management
Highlight potential inequities, compliance risks, and outliers using built‑in checks and insights.
