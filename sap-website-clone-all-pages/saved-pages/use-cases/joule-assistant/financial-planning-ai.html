Overview

The Financial Planning Assistant provides an AI‑driven orchestration layer for enterprise planning. It senses internal and external signals, reasons across forecasts, models, and scenarios, and acts by updating plans—enabling continuous planning beyond static plan‑do‑check‑act cycles.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Translating business intent into planning actions
  • Sensing KPI deviations and emerging risks
  • Orchestrating forecasting, variance, and sentiment analysis
  • Generating and simulating financial scenarios
  • Updating models, assumptions, and plans continuously
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Continuous rolling forecasts driven by real‑time signals
  • Ad‑hoc scenario analysis for emerging risks or opportunities
  • Strategic mid‑ and long‑term planning with live assumptions
  • Alignment of operational responses with financial targets
  • Faster decision cycles across the enterprise

Benefits

Make faster decisions as conditions change
Move finance from periodic planning cycles to continuous decision‑making by sensing change, reasoning on impact, and acting directly in plans.
