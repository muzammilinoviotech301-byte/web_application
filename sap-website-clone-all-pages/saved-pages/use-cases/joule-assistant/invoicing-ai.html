Overview: Deploy agents today. Unlock even more tomorrow.

The AI agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they’ll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI platform and Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

The Invoicing Assistant automates invoice management, turning invoice data into actionable business intelligence. By coordinating validation, matching, exception handling, and payment scheduling autonomously, it frees AP teams from routine processing tasks and enables them to become strategic partners to procurement and treasury, driving working capital optimization, risk mitigation, and supplier value.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Supporting e-invoices from SAP Business Network or government portals
  • Capturing email invoices and extracting invoice data from PDFs and images
  • Identifying fraud and anomalies across invoices, POs, suppliers, and expenses
  • Detecting invoice exceptions and orchestrating resolution
  • Recommending and completing AI-informed actions
  • Routing invoices for approval on the central SAP Ariba launchpad or task center in SAP S/4HANA
  • Posting to SAP S/4HANA for payment
Featured use cases for this assistant:
  • Automation of AP processing
  • Touchless management of invoices to accelerate cycle times
  • Reduction of manual effort and errors
  • Proactive fraud detection and financial risk mitigation
  • Scalable AP operations with minimal human intervention
  • Accurate tracking of AP performance and elimination of bottlenecks

Benefits

Improve invoice capture and interpretation
Capture and interpret invoices from any channel using AI-powered document intelligence.

Reduce invoice errors and exceptions
Validate invoices against procurement and financial data, and resolve matching, tax, and compliance exceptions with actionable insights or autonomous resolution.

Evaluate risks before payment

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Invoicing Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Missing Goods Receipt Agent

Detects missing goods receipts early, prompting receivers before invoices are due for compliance.

Invoice Capture and Extraction Agent

Extracts invoice data from PDFs and images with high accuracy at scale.

Accounts Payable Invoice Duplicate Check Agent

Identifies duplicate invoices based on factors like the supplier, invoice date, or amount data.

Dynamic Invoice Insights Agent

Enables fast invoice creation and analysis using natural language for real-time insights.

Payment Terms Validation Agent

Resolves conflicting payment terms automatically using learned patterns and established guidelines.

Invoice Validation and Exception Handling Agent

Handles invoice exceptions autonomously or by guiding users with resolution options.

Invoice Reminder Agent

Streamlines invoice reminders with clear posting and payment details across systems.

Missing Invoice Reference Agent

Matches line items to purchase orders and goods receipts and suggests remediation steps.

Accounts Payable Invoicing Approval Agent

Detects approval delays, prompts approvers, applies policies, and automates invoice approval decisions.

Invoice Dispute Collaboration Agent

Prioritizes invoice disputes and recommends corrective actions while maintaining audit trails.

Accounts Payable Invoice Fraud Detection Agent

Detects invoice fraud and overpayment risks with prioritized alerts and recommended actions.

Invoice Fraud Prevention Agent

Detects fraudulent invoices by identifying data mismatches, price anomalies, and PO discrepancies.

Invoice Tax Validation Agent

Detects incorrect taxes and suggests the right ones using PO data, invoice history, and tax engines.
