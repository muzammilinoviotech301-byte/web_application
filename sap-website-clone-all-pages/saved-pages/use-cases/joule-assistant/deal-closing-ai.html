Overview

The Deal Closing Assistant accelerates revenue by automating the final mile of sales. Using AI to turn opportunity data into accurate quotes, validated pricing, and confirmed orders, it reduces errors, ensures compliance, and speeds time to close. This enables sales teams to convert agreements into revenue faster while delivering a connected, trusted customer experience at scale, every time.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Automating quote creation using opportunity details, product, and customer data
  • Driving intelligent pricing, inventory validation, and discounting without manual intervention
  • Checking product availability and configuration in real-time
  • Generating ready-to-approve quote drafts from email engagement and opportunity information
  • Initiating order creation and downstream processes automatically from accepted quotes
  • Maintaining real-time synchronization of quote, pricing, and order data across systems
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • AI‑driven quote creation using opportunity, product, and pricing context to generate accurate, customer‑ready quotes
  • Real‑time pricing accuracy and compliance through AI‑based validation of pricing rules, discounts, and policies
  • Faster proposal responses and approvals through rapid creation, review, and approval of quote drafts
  • Automatic conversion of accepted agreements into orders without manual handoffs or delays
  • Seamless deal execution across systems through orchestrated, agentic workflows for quoting, pricing, and order creation
