Overview

The Incentive Compensation Assistant coordinates AI agents across SAP Incentive Management and enterprise policy data to deliver timely answers; provide guidance for inquiries, disputes, and exceptions; and support governed workflows. It reduces manual handling, improves decision consistency, and enables compliant, auditable compensation operations at scale.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Providing real-time contextual guidance on commission payments
  • Accelerating dispute resolution by delivering supporting evidence and analysis
  • Governing exceptions and executing policy-compliant adjustments
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Commission payment inquiry management
  • Compensation dispute resolution
  • Compensation plan governance and auditability

Benefits

Enhanced visibility for sellers
Receive real-time visibility into commissions as agents analyze incentive plan structure and sales performance data.

Faster evidence-based dispute resolution
Reduce the need for manual interventions and process inquires more quickly with managed dispute intake and evidence-based resolution.

Improved incentive plan governance
Analyze payment, transaction, plan, and policy data and document evidence-based recommendations for consistent, policy-compliant incentive plan decisions.
