Overview

The Workforce Management Assistant takes the complexity out of time and absence management. It monitors submissions and approvals, nudges employees and managers about missing entries, and deploys AI agents to help fill, submit, and approve time from anywhere. With native connections to payroll, it keeps time data accurate, complete, and ready for every pay run.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Detecting unsubmitted timesheets and nudging employees
  • Pre-filling timesheets based on work patterns
  • Validating submissions against policies at the point of entry
  • Guiding employees to review, adjust, and submit timesheets
  • Highlighting unapproved timesheets and surfacing approver status
  • Rerouting approvals to prevent stalled workflows
  • Sharing time-related context for payroll questions
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Time tracking and approvals
  • Leave and absence management
  • Time and attendance policy enforcement
  • Overtime monitoring
  • Payroll readiness

Benefits

Faster, easier timesheet completion
Reduce the time to fill and submit timesheets with proactive notifications and guided submission flows.

Fewer time-related HR tickets
Use AI-powered guidance to give employees and managers instant answers to local leave policy questions.

Fewer stalled approvals and delays
Use agents to spot approvers on leave, reroute approvals, and clear bottlenecks so timesheets are approved on time.
