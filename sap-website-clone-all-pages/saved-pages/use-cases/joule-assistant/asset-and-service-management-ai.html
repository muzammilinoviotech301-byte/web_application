Overview

The Asset Operations Assistant orchestrates AI agents to turn asset signals into coordinated maintenance action and improve uptime.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Assessing asset alerts and recommending next-best actions
  • Creating and prioritizing maintenance work based on asset condition, business priority, and operational demand
  • Optimizing maintenance schedules and workforce assignments
  • Briefing technicians and summarizing completed work
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Reducing unplanned downtime by responding sooner to emerging asset issues
  • Improving maintenance productivity by focusing teams on the highest-priority work
  • Increasing execution readiness by ensuring the right people, parts, and tools are available
  • Keeping operations on track when emergencies, resource constraints, or changing conditions disrupt the plan

Benefits

Reduce unplanned downtime and operational risk
Identify critical asset conditions earlier and coordinate the right maintenance response before issues escalate into larger operational disruptions.

Maximize workforce productivity
Automate prioritization, planning, scheduling, and routine coordination so maintenance teams can spend more time on high-value work and exceptions.

Continuously improve asset performance
Use asset health, risk, work outcomes, and financial impact to refine maintenance strategies and prioritize the actions that deliver the greatest business value.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Asset Operations Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Asset Performance Health Analysis Agent

Interprets health signals to deliver clear, easy-to-understand summaries for individual assets, as well as across asset fleets.

Asset Performance Risk Assessment Agent

Proposes risk assessments for newly synced technical objects and gives reliability engineers a way to review, update, and confirm the assessment.

Asset Performance Alert Processing Agent

Enriches alerts with historical incident analyses and resolutions to enable faster decision-making.

Maintenance Request Screening Agent

Screens and ranks open maintenance notifications by risk and criticality for supervisor review.

Maintenance Request Initiation Agent

Creates notifications, prioritizes issues, and initiates work orders with asset and operational context.

Operational Maintenance Planning Agent

Builds weekly maintenance plans that combine preventive, predictive, and corrective tasks.

Maintenance Plan Cost Simulation Agent

Aggregates maintenance cost data and runs cost projections for chosen periods through natural language.

Service and Maintenance Dispatching Agent

Creates job assignments by matching technicians and crews to tasks based on company-specific objectives.

Technician Briefing Agent

Summarizes maintenance jobs using manuals, asset history, and relevant external data.

Maintenance Job Debrief Agent

Completes voice-based post-job debriefs, capturing time, parts, and failure insights from fieldwork.

Equipment Insights Agent

View equipment usage, lifecycle, maintenance history, and current condition in a single summary.

Maintenance Event Planning Agent

Analyze real-time data and reprioritize maintenance tasks to boost asset reliability.
