Overview

The Deal Qualification Assistant deduplicates and enriches lead data, assesses fit and intent in real time, and automates routing and engagement. This reduces manual work, improves pipeline quality, and increases conversion rates—enabling faster response times and more consistent sales execution across the pipeline.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Deduplicating, enriching, and pre-qualifying leads using AI signals
  • Lead routing based on fit, territory, and availability
  • Generating personalized outreach based on customer context and intent
  • Converting leads to opportunities and recording creation workflows
  • Scoring and prioritizing leads in real time for faster follow-up
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Automated inbound lead deduplication, enrichment, and qualification before assignment to sales
  • Prioritization of high‑intent leads based on strong buying signals for faster follow‑up
  • Intelligent lead routing to the most suitable seller based on territory, expertise, and availability
  • Personalized outreach at scale using customer data and engagement signals
  • Seamless lead‑to‑opportunity conversion with pre‑filled data to reduce manual work
  • Improved pipeline quality through prioritization of enriched and qualified leads

Agents used by this assistant

Agent

Description

Lead Enrichment Agent

Checks for duplicates and pulls the latest firmgraphic and contact data from external sources.

Lead Qualification Agent

Applies pre-qualification prediction models to score and qualify based on fit and further qualify based on behavioral signals to identify hot/warm leads.

Lead Routing Agent

Assigns qualified leads to the best-fit seller based on territory, rules, and lead attributes.

Sales Engagement Agent

Analyzes customer signals to personalize outreach and improve engagement effectiveness.

Lead Conversion Agent

Converts qualified leads into opportunities and creates associated customer records.

Deal Qualification Orchestration Agent

Coordinates the specialized agents within the Deal Qualification Assistant.
