Overview

The Revenue Recognition Assistant orchestrates specialized agents across revenue recognition activities, explaining revenue calculations, detecting and resolving issues that might impact the financial close, and guiding contract combination decisions. Accountants can focus on generating strategic insights and reduce manual investigation and reconciliation mechanics.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Reviewing dozens of contracts per customer per period across multiple SAP applications to propose multi-element arrangement (MEA) combination candidates
  • Ensuring your revenue recognition guideline is followed for each MEA, including policy citation for each proposal
  • Answering revenue accounting-contextual questions such as “Why don’t these numbers match?” or “Why was revenue not posted?”
  • Providing step-by-step fix recommendations when errors are detected in the financial close and executing the corrections after human approval
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Resolving revenue recognition exceptions in minutes instead of hours
  • Protecting period-end-closing schedules
  • Increasing focus on strategic insights for revenue accounting teams rather than manual investigation and reconciliation mechanics
  • Reducing dependency on specialized expertise with an assistant that provides plain-language recommendations to support decisions across the full revenue lifecycle under IFRS 15 and ASC 606

Benefits

Accelerate revenue close and issue resolution
Move from reactive troubleshooting to proactive resolution using AI-driven root cause analysis and guided remediation. Improve close cycle efficiency by diagnosing issues in minutes instead of hours, decreasing manual contract review efforts, and reducing reconciliations.

Scale expertise and ensure policy compliance
Reduce dependency on specialized expertise with an assistant that provides plain-language recommendations and policy-based guidance to support consistent and compliant decisions across teams.

Strengthen controls, audit readiness, and operational quality
Improve governance with complete remediation audit trails, automated documentation, and execution of approved actions. The assistant continuously learns from past close activities to help you enhance audit readiness and prevent recurring issues.
