Overview

The Campaign Assistant coordinates AI agents to plan, execute, and optimize end-to-end campaigns in real time by drawing on unified customer data and live business signals. It continuously adapts engagement strategies to changing conditions, reduces manual effort, and maintains enterprise compliance and control.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Building and refreshing dynamic audience segments using customer and business signals
  • Configuring campaign rules, triggers, and decision logic using operational context
  • Developing campaigns across channels and journeys
  • Adapting campaign strategies based on audience and business conditions
  • Partnering with human decision-makers to act quickly on signals with more context
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • High-value, at-risk, and emerging customer segment identification
  • Campaign strategy, journey flows, and channel mix optimization from performance signals
  • Real-time campaign timing and messaging adaptation based on customer behavior
  • Campaign anomaly detection and cross-channel impact monitoring
  • Continuous campaign performance optimization through closed-loop AI learning

Benefits

Activate and adapt campaigns seamlessly across the customer lifecycle as customer and business conditions change.

Continuously improve campaign performance
Analyze results, surface insights, and apply closed-loop learning to improve outcomes.
