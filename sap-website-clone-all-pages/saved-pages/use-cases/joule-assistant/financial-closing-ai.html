Overview

The Financial Closing Assistant orchestrates the agents managing the period close. Users control the agents’ level of engagement for handling journal postings, clearing activities, and analysis. This transforms the financial close by increasing productivity and improving flexibility for finance and accounting teams.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Clearing receivables and payables
  • Calculating and posting accounting accruals
  • Resolving errors
  • Detecting anomalies in asset accounting
  • Analyzing financial consistency
  • Managing fixed assets master data
  • Reviewing general ledger accounts
  • Clearing general ledger balances
  • Reconciling goods receipt and invoice receipt
  • Matching and reconciling intercompany transactions
  • Posting accruals
  • Posting journal entries
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Close preparation
  • Close orchestration
  • Final review and reporting
  • Transaction recording
  • Transaction accrual
  • Account reconciliations
  • Intercompany matching during consolidation
  • Final close review and reporting
  • Error resolution for the financial close
