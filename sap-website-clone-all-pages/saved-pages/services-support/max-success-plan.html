Frequently asked questions

  • What is a success plan?

    Success plans are tiered engagements that provide a rich set of services and features such as personalized partnerships and self-guided resources that help ensure your continuous success.

  • What is the value of the Max Success Plan?

    The Max Success Plan helps you drive value creation as SAP’s most comprehensive guidance and advisory services offering. It helps you maximize the return on your cloud investment​ and accelerate value realization for your transformation journey​.

    The Max Success Plan also provides ongoing strategic, proactive partnership with specialized expertise and enhanced support performance for the lifetime of your cloud subscription.

    As a tailored service plan focused on AI-infused business process improvements, this plan empowers continuous transformation, accelerates innovation adoption, activates AI innovation, and enables your center of excellence.

  • Will there be any change in my SAP MaxAttention, SAP ActiveAttention, or SAP Value Assurance contract?

    In line with SAP’s commitment to customer continuity, your organization will continue to receive full support for currently licensed SAP MaxAttention, SAP ActiveAttention, and SAP Value Assurance offerings throughout the duration of your existing contract terms.

    For customers with cloud solutions: At the upcoming renewal of your SAP MaxAttention, SAP ActiveAttention, or SAP Value Assurance contract, SAP is offering your organization the opportunity to transition to the new success plans.

    For customers with on-premises solutions: At the upcoming renewal of your SAP MaxAttention, SAP ActiveAttention, or SAP Value Assurance contract, SAP is offering the opportunity to transition to an on-premises-specific version of your premium engagement contract.

  • I have SAP MaxAttention and want to move to the Max Success Plan. What’s the difference?

    What stays the same:

    • Strategic engagement driven by assigned experts with support from an enterprise architect​
    • Access to a catalog of premium expert services focused on key areas of transformation​
    • Specific governance including SAP sponsorship, access to SAP centers of excellence, and community peer networking opportunities

    What is enhanced:

    • Accelerated transformation with unlimited access to cloud-based entitlements
    • Activation of the latest cloud innovations
    • Ability to share adoption guidance
    • Improved efficiency with embedded AI-powered tools
    • Faster initial response times with a premium SLA and designated support
