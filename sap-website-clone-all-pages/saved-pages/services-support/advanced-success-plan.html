Frequently asked questions

What is a success plan?

Success plans are tiered engagements that provide a rich set of services and features such as personalized partnerships and self-guided resources that help ensure your continuous success.

What is the value of the Advanced Success Plan?

With the Advanced Success Plan, your SAP Business Suite solution becomes a catalyst for sustained growth and innovation. Teams gain the knowledge and confidence to act decisively, aided by unlimited guidance from experts and intelligent insight, ensuring every feature delivers measurable business value. Intelligent tools and AI-driven recommendations help you optimize processes, anticipate risks, and keep performance at its peak.

The result is not only higher ROI, but also greater trust—in your systems, your strategy, and your ability to stay ahead of change.

Will there be any change in my SAP Preferred Success contract?

In line with SAP’s commitment to customer continuity, your organization will continue to receive full support for currently licensed SAP Preferred Success offerings throughout the duration of your existing contract terms.

At the upcoming renewal of SAP Preferred Success and SAP solutions contracts, SAP is offering your organization the opportunity to transition to the new success plans.
