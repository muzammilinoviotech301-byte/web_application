Frequently asked questions

  • What is a success plan?

    Success plans are tiered engagements that provide a rich set of services and features such as personalized partnerships and self-guided resources that help ensure your continuous success.

  • What is the Foundational Success Plan?

    The Foundational Success Plan is centered around SAP Enterprise Support and is expanded with onboarding and technical cloud operations. It’s included with every SAP cloud solution.

  • How do I get the Foundational Success Plan?

    The Foundational Success Plan is included with every cloud subscription. It’s delivered through SAP Enterprise Support, onboarding services, and technical cloud operations.

  • I purchased my SAP solution through a partner. Do I still get the Foundational Success Plan?

    Yes. The Foundational Success Plan is automatically included with your SAP cloud subscription, whether you purchased it directly from SAP or through a partner.
