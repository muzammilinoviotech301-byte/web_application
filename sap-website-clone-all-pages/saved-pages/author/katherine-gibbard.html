Katherine Gibbard, PhD

Senior Research Scientist

Dr. Katherine Gibbard researches the future of work in service of SAP SuccessFactors’ product development, marketing, and strategy. Her PhD is focused on trust in AI, and she has six years of experience as an AI researcher. Katherine regularly presents at events such as HR Connect, SuccessConnect, and Unleash. Her focus areas include AI and leadership.
