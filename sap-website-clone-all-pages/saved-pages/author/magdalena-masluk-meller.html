Magdalena Masluk-Meller, PhD

Senior research scientist and contributor for the Future of Work Research Lab at SAP

Dr. Magdalena Masluk-Meller examines how digital transformation, evolving work models, and shifting employee expectations are reshaping work and informing the next generation of HR technology. Her focus areas include payroll, time tracking, and talent acquisition. She holds a Ph.D. in Cultural Sciences, specializing in Intercultural Management.
