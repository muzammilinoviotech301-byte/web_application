Overview

The Business Network Assistant enables procurement and supply chain professionals to more effectively collaborate with their suppliers and third-party providers such as contract manufacturers. From capturing market signals to facilitating source-to-pay processes for goods and services, optimising logistics and managing assets, it supports a wide range of core processes.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Sourcing, contracting, procuring and managing payments
  • Co-ordinating transportation flows by monitoring events and recommending actions when disruptions occur
  • Monitoring, scheduling and executing asset maintenance and work orders
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Supplier recommendations aligned with technical, regional and compliance requirements, including rapid identification and validation of alternate supply sources during trigger events
  • Buyer support for RFx creation and supplier enablement for RFx responses
  • Automated document processing for order confirmations, advanced ship notices, goods receipts, service entry sheets and invoicing
  • Detection of logistics disruptions with recommendations for cost‑efficient fulfilment options
  • Automated sharing of work order details

Benefits

Increased visibility into procurement and supply chain processes
Monitor market triggers to identify opportunities or potential risks, analyse options and recommend actions.

Improved efficiency across sourcing and procurement processes
Reduce manual effort for buyers, operators and suppliers with agent-led execution of RFx creation, response and analysis along with agent-assisted document processing.

Enhanced regulatory compliance
Identify suppliers that meet policy and regulatory requirements, transact in accordance with regulations and comply with documentation requirements.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Business Network Assistant to orchestrate specialised AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or customised agents you create.

Onboarding Agent

Reduces supplier onboarding time from days to minutes, enabling teams to onboard at scale without increasing resources.

Order Confirmation Automation Agent

Issues automatic confirmations for orders that meet configured rules and conditions.

Logistics Predictive Insights and Response Agent

Monitors real-time logistics events to detect, predict, avoid or mitigate disruptions via recommended optimised plans.

Advanced Ship Notice Automation Agent

Streamlines and automates ASN creation by aggregating orders and consolidating deliveries.

Supply Chain Impact Analysis Agent

Detects supply chain exceptions and risk, maps them to customer orders and quantifies impact.

Service Entry Sheet Automation Agent

Simplifies and automates service entry sheet creation.

Invoice Automation Agent

Automates invoice generation, shops costs and adheres to customer-specific rules and preferences.

Invoice Dispute Collaboration Agent

Automates dispute resolution by assembling supporting evidence and recommending corrective actions.

Supply Chain Collaboration Exception Management Agent

Detects, notifies and resolves supply chain exceptions in forecast, order and ASN processes.

Maintenance Planning Alignment Agent

Orchestrates cross-system maintenance planning and automates data exchange with contractors.
