Why time tracking is important for productivity and compliance

Time tracking is key to understanding employee productivity and attendance, helping ensure employees are paid correctly and working as efficiently as possible. By knowing how much time is spent on tasks and projects, organizations can identify inefficiencies and implement solutions, such as reallocating tasks or providing additional training or support, and make more informed decisions about future assignments and project timelines. For service-based organizations, time tracking is essential for creating accurate invoices and building customer trust.

As hybrid and remote work continue to shape the modern workplace, reliable and accessible time tracking remains essential for confirming when employees are working and how productively they’re spending their time. Time tracking also plays a foundational role in workforce management, helping organizations align talent with business goals, optimize staffing, and maintain visibility across distributed teams.

Additionally, AI-enabled time tracking tools are helping organizations automate time capture, detect anomalies, and generate predictive insights that support smarter scheduling and improved compliance.

What does time tracking software do?

Time tracking software digitalizes and automates time tracking processes, such as clock-ins and clock-outs, time sheets, GPS tracking, reporting, and analysis. This software helps organizations accurately track and manage employee work hours, attendance, and productivity. It also helps guarantee that employees are paid correctly for the hours they work, including overtime.

Organizations use employee time tracking software for:

Employee time and attendance tracking

A clock-in and clock-out system provides a centralized view of employee arrival times, absences, breaks, overtime, vacation, and personal time, helping organizations schedule resources and projects, identify inefficiencies, and calculate payroll and overtime.

Resource and task allocation

By monitoring productivity and workload across teams, time tracking software helps organizations make informed decisions about resource and task allocation, keeping projects on schedule and making the best use of employees’ time.

Client billing

Automated time tracking eliminates manual steps and errors to help ensure client billing is accurate and timely, helping build trust between organizations and clients and improving on-time payments.

Project management

By tracking time spent on each phase of a project—from planning through review—time tracking software enables organizations to better manage timelines, resources, and costs, keeping projects running on time and on budget.

Performance evaluation

Time tracking software that collects data on task completion times and employee productivity provides valuable insights into skills gaps and employee time management, enabling managers to provide constructive feedback in performance reviews and set attainable goals.

Compliance reporting

Consistent and reliable time tracking methods enable organizations to record and report accurate working hours, helping ensure all employees are treated fairly and in accordance with labor laws and regulations for working hours, breaks, and overtime.

How does employee time tracking software work?

Time tracking software works by allowing employees to easily record their work hours and tasks completed throughout the day, week, or month. The software then compiles and organizes this data, providing reports and insights to managers and HR. Cloud-based time tracking software integrates with other business systems to provide a unified view of employee time and attendance.

Key time tracking terms:

  • Time tracking is the act of recording time spent on a specific task or project.
  • Time sheet is a paper or digital document used for recording employees’ arrival and departure times and the amount of time spent on a task or project.
  • Time tracking software is a digital tool that records and analyzes time spent on tasks or projects, in place of manual time tracking methods, such as punch cards and sign-in sheets.
  • AI-enabled features enhance this process by automatically detecting anomalies, validating entries in real time, and providing predictive insights that help HR teams optimize staffing and reduce compliance risks.

Time tracking methods

The methods of capturing time have evolved over the years—from paper and pen to employee time clock with a punch-card system (credited to Willard Legrand Bundy) to AI-enabled time recording, analysis, and reporting. Some of the most common methods used today include:

  • Excel templates, which use formulas to calculate time, and require manual data entry and analysis.
  • Mechanical punch clocks that stamp times on a piece of paper employees insert into a machine to record time in and time out.
  • Biometric control systems that scan employees’ fingerprints or pupils to verify their identity and record the time they enter and exit the workplace.
  • Swipe cards (like credit cards), which are swiped through a card reader at the front desk or workstation when employees start or end their workday.
  • Paper spreadsheets, which are completed and submitted by hand, stamped, and retained for future reference.
  • Digital spreadsheets that record, calculate, and store time tracking data on a hard drive or USB stick.
  • Mobile apps that use GPS and geofencing features to track employees’ time and location, remind employees to clock in and clock out, and provide detailed reporting.

What are the benefits of time tracking software?

By centralizing and streamlining time tracking processes, time tracking software makes it easier for organizations to collect accurate, real-time information that leads to improvements in:

  • Productivity: Accurate time tracking helps organizations increase employee accountability, provide constructive feedback during reviews, prioritize projects and tasks, and optimize workflows.
  • Cost savings: Insights into labor cost inefficiencies, such as excessive overtime, lengthy processes, and inappropriate staffing levels, can help organizations implement cost-effective solutions.
  • Compliance with local, industry, and company policies: Maintaining a clear, accurate record of employee working hours helps organizations comply with labor laws and union rules for overtime, breaks, and minimum wage, which can be crucial during labor audits.
  • Employee satisfaction: By giving employees visibility into their work hours, overtime, and pay, organizations can boost employee motivation, morale, and trust.
  • Forecasting: The data from time tracking can help predict future labor needs and costs, helping organizations create reliable, cost-effective budgets and financial forecasts.

Challenges of implementing time tracking software

While time tracking software offers many benefits to employers and employees, cost and integration can present challenges for organizations preparing to move to cloud-based time tracking. Some of the key challenges organizations should consider include:

  • Cost: To alleviate the costs associated with time tracking software, organizations can start with just the features they need and choose pricing plans and solutions that best fit their budget.
  • Compliance: Taking compliance into consideration, organizations must ensure the software can scale to meet all relevant labor laws, local and regional regulations, and union rules.
  • Integration: To provide a seamless user experience, time tracking software should smoothly integrate with existing HR, project management, and payroll systems.
  • Technical difficulties: Unpredictable system performance, software bugs, and compatibility issues can prevent users from consistently using time tracking tools, which makes the data less reliable.
  • Training and support: Additional time and resources are needed to provide sufficient training and ongoing support to ensure all employees can use time tracking tools effectively.

By understanding business goals and challenges upfront and taking the time to explore different cloud and on-premise software options, payment plans, and vendors, organizations can find the best time tracking software to fit their business needs and budget.

Easing adoption of time tracking software

To simplify the adoption of employee time tracking software and ensure employees are using it consistently and correctly, organizations should:

  • Provide training sessions tailored to different types of users and organizations
  • Create self-paced demos on recording time and reviewing working hours, payroll, and overtime
  • Hold open forums to discuss concerns about time tracking processes and highlight the benefits, such as eliminating time spent on manual processes and ensuring more accurate, timely paychecks

Key features of effective employee time tracking software

With time tracking software, it’s easier than ever to keep track of employee time, attendance, and productivity without relying on manual, error-prone processes. Time tracking software offers a range of features that can help organizations manage projects, timelines, payroll, and client billing more effectively.

  • Automated clock-in and clock-out records start and end times, breaks, and intervals during employee work hours for both onsite and remote workers, eliminating errors and gaps from manual processes.
  • Mobile accessibility allows users to track time and manage tasks on the go using any mobile device.
  • Customization enables users to customize time tracking settings and features based on individual or team needs, as well as local regulations and union rules.
  • Geolocation tracks where employees are working around the globe, which is especially valuable for remote employee time tracking and analysis.
  • Artificial intelligence (AI) automates time tracking processes, analyzes patterns, and generates real-time insights about productivity trends and potential issues and solutions.
  • Real-time reporting summarizes recorded hours, project progress, and other relevant data to help keep projects and people on schedule.
  • Monitoring and analytics provide insights into time usage and efficiency, supporting better decision-making and performance improvements.
  • Integration connects time tracking software with other tools and software used in the workplace, such as payroll time tracking, project management systems, and accounting software.
  • User permission settings allow managers to set different levels of access and permissions for users or teams within the organization.
  • Security measures protect sensitive employee data both within and outside the organization.

FAQs

  • How do employees track hours?

    Employees track hours by clocking in and clocking out of work and tasks using a variety of paper-based, digitalized, manual, and automated processes. Many companies have moved away from manual, paper-based time tracking, such as punch clocks, swipe cards, and spreadsheets, to cloud-based, AI-enabled time tracking software that automates and streamlines time tracking, analysis, reporting, and invoicing.

  • Why is employee time tracking important?

    Time and attendance tracking provides essential insights that can help organizations manage efficiencies, resource allocation, invoicing, and costs for all types of workers across onsite, remote, and hybrid working models.

  • How does employee time tracking help with payroll and billing?

    A time and attendance system works by recording the amount of time employees work, including time in and time out, breaks, overtime, and time off. Some systems automatically track employee time while other systems require employees to enter their time manually.
