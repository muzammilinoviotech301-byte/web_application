Business AI

SAP Business AI enhances productivity with cutting-edge technology that supports organizations' AI strategies, from developing effective agents to managing AI. At SAP Sapphire, the company introduced new innovations, partnerships, and integrations that enable real-time, proactive assistance. Joule, SAP’s generative AI copilot, is now accessible to all users across SAP and non-SAP systems. Additionally, SAP advanced its agentic AI strategy by launching new agents for various use cases and an AI operating system that will simplify AI development, helping developers build, deploy, and scale solutions more easily.

SAP Makes Joule More Prevalent and Proactive Across SAP Business Suite and Third-Party Applications

SAP is rolling out significant updates to its Joule copilot that will help provide enterprises with always-on AI that breaks down vendor silos, anticipates business users’ needs, and delivers deeper insights by combining SAP and third-party data. This proactive copilot enhances productivity for SAP customers whether they are using SAP or third-party solutions.

New Omnipresent AI Helps Break Down Silos

SAP is introducing features that will make Joule omnipresent and help create a common AI experience across SAP and third-party cloud applications. Significantly widening its reach beyond SAP Business Suite, Joule assists users as they traverse different systems. Leveraging this information, Joule can help users find data, surface in-the-moment insights, and streamline workflows. Its omnipresence helps advance productivity at scale and simplify users’ AI experience, promoting greater adoption.

Further strengthening this enterprise interoperability is the bidirectional integration of Joule and Microsoft 365 Copilot. Users can leverage the capabilities of Joule from within Microsoft 365 Copilot for a unified AI experience across SAP and Microsoft systems. The first step in this bidirectional integration is planned for general availability in Q2 2025. It is expected that the integration of Microsoft 365 Copilot in Joule will be complete in Q3 2025.

New SAP Action Bar for Joule Provides Always-On, Proactive Recommendations

SAP is introducing a new action bar for Joule that will radically change how people work. Currently, Joule reacts to specific prompts and within specific guidance. With the action bar, SAP takes a significant step forward in advancing Joule to become an always-on, omnipresent, and proactive copilot that can anticipate business users’ needs before they arise. General availability is expected by Q3 2025.

Leveraging technology from WalkMe solutions, the action bar studies users’ behavior and the business context within SAP or third-party cloud applications. Joule uses this information to independently—and proactively—provide real-time insights, support, and recommendations to customers wherever and whenever they are working. Whether customers are e-mailing a supplier or investigating a financial transaction, SAP uniquely provides AI that—with its simplicity—democratizes AI for all users.

New Answer Engine Connects External and Internal Data to Solve Complex Business Challenges

SAP and Perplexity AI, an AI-powered answer engine that provides accurate, trusted, and real-time answers to any question, are collaborating to extend SAP’s generative AI and search capabilities by bringing together the best of both worlds in unstructured and structured data. By integrating Perplexity’s ability to address questions accurately and transparently with business answer capabilities, Joule and AI agents can deliver richer, context-aware answers using SAP Business Suite data through Joule with external signals from Perplexity AI. Through this collaboration, enterprises can get instant solutions tailored to their business needs including enhanced analytics and decision-making across SAP Cloud ERP solutions. Learn more.

SAP Expands Agentic AI Footprint Across SAP Business Suite

SAP launches an expanded network of Joule Agents designed to foster autonomous collaboration across systems and lines of business, delivering smarter insights and enhanced efficiency. Partnering with industry leaders, SAP offers an ecosystem of interoperable agents that can execute end-to-end processes.

This interoperability strategy—combined with deep industry expertise and SAP Business Suite—uniquely positions SAP to turn its agentic AI into tangible business outcomes. Additionally, SAP agents go beyond serving suggestions to users. Using advanced reasoning capabilities, agents can “think” through business problems, coordinate cross systems, decide what action should be taken, and act autonomously—with human oversight.

Enabled by SAP Business Data Cloud, Joule Agents can access enterprise data and act across both SAP and third-party systems. Agents can deeply understand data structures, semantics, and processes, and this additional context can drive further business impact for Joule Agents.

The new agents span customer experience, supply chain management, spend management, finance, and human capital management, and promise to help companies streamline operations, accelerate decision-making, and enhance cross-functional processes.

Transforming Customer Experience

SAP introduces two agents to enhance sales and order management, with general availability planned for Q2 2025:

  • Quote Creation and Management Agent
    Transforms e-mail quote requests into ready-to-send quotes and orders, enabling customers to place and modify orders while helping eliminate manual data entry and errors.
  • Catalog Optimization Agent
    Maintains and optimizes product data, improving merchandising accuracy and agility.

Elevating Supply Chain Agility

SAP is integrating agents across its supply chain applications. General availability is planned for Q4 2025:

  • Maintenance Planner Agent
    Performs reasoning using real-time data and suggests adjustments to maintenance schedules. This helps streamline planning, enables more precise resource allocation, and reduces disruptions to critical assets.
  • Shop Floor Supervisor Agent
    Retrieves and reasons through disruption details, assesses impact, recommends production rescheduling, and orchestrates stock, minimizing shop floor disruption effects.
  • Field Service Dispatcher Agent
    Reasons through real-time resource-availability information to autonomously schedule and optimize service orders, saving time and helping ensure the right resources are allocated for each job.

Enhancing Spend Management

SAP introduces new spend management agents, with general availability planned for Q4 2025:

  • Meeting Location Planner Agent
    Automates time-consuming tasks for group travel that will improve efficiency and user experience. Agents can plan and estimate travel costs, identify central locations, source accommodations, and find the most suitable flight times and fares.
  • Expense Report Validation Agent
    Uses alerts and guided experiences to help employees fix issues based on past data, company policy, and patterns before submitting reports, therefore reducing rejections and resubmissions.
  • Sourcing Agent
    Reasons through sourcing event requests, past event data, and supplier information to act autonomously and create a tailored sourcing event. This supports faster, better-informed purchasing decisions, and more agile responses to supply chain disruptions and other changing business conditions.

Transforming Finance in SAP

New agents will bring intelligent automation to core finance operations, planned for general availability in Q3 2025:

  • Dispute Resolution Agent
    Reasons through disputed details and relevant business records to validate cases and propose solutions. It acts on the human-approved resolutions to quickly close disputes, improve cash flow, and enhance customer satisfaction.
  • Accounts Receivable Agent
    Processes data related to overdue receivables and performs appropriate follow-up tasks with customers, saving time by analyzing open items, minimizing bad-debt write-offs, and reduces the number of days sales outstanding.

Elevating People Management

SAP introduces a new human resources agent with general availability planned for Q4 2025:

  • Performance and Goals Agent
    Prepares people managers with data-driven insights ahead of one-on-one conversations with their employees, helping to promote consistent and productive conversations.

New AI Foundation Provides an Operating System for SAP Business AI

SAP introduces AI Foundation, the AI operating system for all SAP Business AI solutions to make AI development and operations easier and more productive. AI Foundation gives developers a single entry point for using a full set of tools that help build, extend, and run custom AI solutions and agents at scale.

Composed of new and well-established SAP AI solutions and components, such as the generative AI hub and Joule Studio capabilities and SAP Knowledge Graph solution, AI Foundation will accelerate AI innovation, boost productivity, simplify AI operations through a unified system, and reduce overall total cost of ownership (TCO).

AI Foundation includes these existing SAP solutions, which feature new enhancements:

  • Joule Studio
    Part of the SAP Build solution, Joule Studio helps developers create custom AI. A new skill builder capability, planned for general availability in Q3 2025, will help developers create, deploy, monitor, and manage custom skills for Joule. Joule Studio will also help developers create custom AI agents using low-code and no-code capabilities, with general availability planned for Q4 2025. Learn more.
  • SAP Knowledge Graph
    This solution helps Joule provide more accurate answers and allows users to explore data directly within Joule, instead of using complex workflows from each line of business. Initial support for the SAP Cloud ERP and SAP Business Data Cloud solutions is expected to be generally available by Q3 2025. Other lines of business will be added in future.
  • AI agent hub in SAP LeanIX solutions
    This capability uses SAP LeanIX technology to provide organizations with a centralized inventory and governance for its AI agents. Companies can map AI agents to their business capabilities, helping IT managers more easily identify other use cases and lines of business where AI agents could be deployed to optimize their organization. General availability is planned for Q4 2025.

 Accelerating Predictive Scenarios Using Tabular Data

AI Foundation will also include a new tabular AI service, based on SAP Foundation Model. Tabular AI will redefine how businesses use structured data for predictive insights. Currently, leveraging structured, tabular data is difficult; it’s often fragmented and siloed with separate AI implementations that lack interoperability or a unified workflow. This  service offering makes it easier for enterprises to convert tabular data into actionable insights smoothly and at scale and is planned for general availability in H2 2025.

By consolidating multiple AI services (like data-attribute or personalized recommendations) and embedded matching capabilities, tabular AI simplifies AI adoption for developers and democratizes access to predictive capabilities for business users.

New Feature Promises to Eliminate Cumbersome Prompt Engineering

A standout feature of the AI operating system is its new prompt optimizer, which can quickly create the most effective AI prompts. Developers use prompt engineering—typically manual, time-consuming and cost-intensive—to draft and refine prompts.

The prompt optimizer ushers in a new era that will drastically reduce this effort by automatically generating more effective prompts, customized for large language model (LLM) or specific use case requirements. For complex use cases, customers can reduce days of manual work to minutes. Prompt optimization is unique in its ability to rapidly utilize some of the best available AI LLMs. It automates the complex process of prompt optimization across multiple models, effectively eliminating the need for prompt engineering and letting customers immediately leverage new models without the usual delay caused by manual conversion and testing. It also enables customers to convert the prompts of their own custom AI solutions.

This service was designed collaboratively with Not Diamond, a frontier AI lab renowned for its model-agnostic approach. Leveraging SAP’s framework and Not Diamond’s state-of-the-art technology, the prompt optimizer will help ensure peak performance across a wide range of AI models. This lays the foundation for a multi-model future, where applications are decoupled from specific models and can utilize the most suitable model at any time. Moving forward, organizations can seamlessly utilize AI capabilities without being locked into single-model dependencies, fostering both flexibility and innovation in how businesses build AI solutions. Learn more.

Already in use internally at SAP, the prompt optimizer enhances ready-to-use scenarios in Joule and embedded AI use cases such as the SAP Joule for Consultants solution. A limited customer release program is now available with general availability expected later in 2025. Customers may register to test the prompt optimizer here.

SAP Accelerates Cloud Transformation with New Transition Guidance

SAP introduces transition guidance, an integrated app that orchestrates transformation-related assets, tools, and services to help customers accelerate their cloud transformation. With Joule as the entry point, personalized guidance and actionable recommendations can be tailored to an individual’s transformation objectives. Transition guidance packages bring together capabilities from the following SAP solutions:

  • SAP Joule for Consultants
    Maps out interfaces, migrates data, and automates testing.
  • SAP Signavio
    Delivers in-depth insights into current business processes, optimizing efficiency and decision-making.
  • SAP LeanIX
    Provides a thorough understanding of the business implications of cloud transformation, including architecture, risks, and dependencies.
  • SAP Cloud ALM
    Provides highly integrated tools and services to support data-driven decision-making processes, and project planning, execution, and testing throughout the entire transformation.
  • Joule for developers
    Helps adapt custom ABAP development tools, optimizing business applications for the cloud.

Transition guidance will release the first key features by the end of 2025, with more functionality coming throughout 2026.
