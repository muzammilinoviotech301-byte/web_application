SAP's AI design principles

SAP's AI Design principles guide the experiences that help you work better.

  • Traditional enterprise software required navigation, training and manual analysis. SAP’s AI-first software enables conversations instead of navigation and automation over repetition. SAP Business AI focuses on enhancing applications by embedding AI capabilities directly into business process decisions, while SAP Design elevates the experience with conversational ease and intelligence. We do that with six principles in mind:

    • Design for Clarity
    • Earn Trust
    • Give Control
    • Design Fairly
    • Boost Flow
    • Fail Safe
