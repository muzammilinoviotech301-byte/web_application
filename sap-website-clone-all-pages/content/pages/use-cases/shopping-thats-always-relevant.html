Use Case

Make shopping experiences more relevant

Show visitors the right products at the right time to drive conversions and discovery.

Published on May 14, 2025

Overview

Product recommendations help you display the most relevant items at each stage of the shopping journey. Using real-time data and business logic, the system automatically promotes trending, related, and complementary products—boosting conversions, product discovery, and user engagement across your storefront.

Benefits

  • Deliver relevant product suggestions using real-time shopper behavior and business logic
  • Keep your site fresh with automated product displays based on performance data
  • Tailor recommendations using visitor preferences, filters, and categories
  • Launch promotions automatically on a schedule—no manual updates needed

Business value

  • Improved product discovery

    Help users find relevant products faster with intelligent suggestions

  • Higher conversion performance

    Show the right products at the right moment to increase sales

  • Less manual merchandising

    Automated updates reduce the need for hands-on campaign management
