Use Case

Associate learning items with relevant skills automatically

Skill Inference for Learning in SAP SuccessFactors Learning uses AI to tag courses with skills that match learner needs. By improving search relevance and reducing time spent browsing unsuitable courses, this feature helps employees find the right training faster—and complete it more often.

Published on December 4, 2025

Overview

Skill Inference for Learning uses AI to associate skills with learning items in bulk through the import process. This helps HR specialists maintain accurate skill tagging across large course libraries and ensures employees can discover training that directly supports their roles and growth goals.

Benefits

  • Help employees quickly find training that matches their skills and job requirements
  • Reduce incomplete course starts by improving training relevance
  • Streamline skill tagging across large learning catalogs

Business Value

  • Faster course discovery

    15% less search time for employees finding relevant training

  • Higher completion rates

    15% increase in course completion for skill-aligned training

  • Automated skill tagging

    AI-driven association of learning items with relevant skills
