Overview

The Manufacturing Assistant orchestrates autonomous, agile, and aligned manufacturing—unifying people, operational goals, and processes. It senses disruptions in real time, executes corrective workflows for quality, scheduling, and workforce, and recommends next-best actions with explainable pros and cons—keeping production on plan while operators focus on the exceptions that matter.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Real-time disruption monitoring
  • Autonomous execution (option for HITL) of agentic corrective processes for quality, scheduling, workforce, and resolution
  • Material staging, quality, and inventory impact orchestration
  • Next-best-action scenario recommendations that include pros and cons of each
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Initial onboarding of production environment
  • Governance of production processes
  • Continuous optimization of operations
  • Workforce guidance
  • Alignment between the enterprise and operations

Benefits

Autonomous
AI agents streamline workflows, reduce downtime, and eliminate manual tasks—improving throughput and reducing response times.

Agile
Organizations can adapt to demand shifts and disruptions with real-time guidance to enable faster decisions and scalable output.

Aligned
AI agents reduce waste and inefficiencies by digitally coordinating across systems and workflows to ensure everyone is working to a common goal.

Adherent
Manufacturers can ensure compliance with built-in traceability, nonconformance processes, and electronic signatures.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Manufacturing Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Production Master Data Readiness Agent

Reviews data, production patterns, and compliance requirements to create and optimize routings and inspection plans.

Production Configuration Agent

Automates connection, configuration, validation, and release steps for new production environments.

Production Planning Order Progress Agent

Analyzes critical orders across multiple levels, detects cross-order issues, and proposes steps to resolve them.

Production Planning and Operations Agent

Validates material, capacity, and scheduling availability and recommends work-arounds for conflicts.

Production Diagnostics Agent

Monitors production conditions, detects hidden trends before issues occur, and triggers corrective measures.

Workplace Safety Agent

Supports safety professionals and mitigates risk by identifying hazards, linking to the right risk assessments, recommending controls, and generating updated safety instructions.

Material Requirements Planning Agent

Prioritizes material shortages automatically and proposes solutions to keep production on track.

Manufacturing Quality Nonconformance Resolution Agent

Monitors quality data, detects emerging issues, and guides corrective and preventive action (CAPA) through effectiveness checks.

Product Inspection Readiness Agent

Identifies inspection items, retrieves quality specs, and records results automatically.

Supplier Nonconformance Management Agent

Automates defect triage, root-cause analysis, and supplier nonconformance resolution from end to end.

Production Order Missing Component Resolution Agent

Detects missing production components and recommends resolutions before delays occur.
