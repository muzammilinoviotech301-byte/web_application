Overview

  • The International Trade Assistant supports trade compliance specialists by automating product classification, processing product profile documents, and analyzing screening hits—delivering accurate, consistent results across complex cross-border operations.

  • Tasks this assistant can help with:

    • Classifying products with tariff/commodity codes (with legal rationale)
    • Building complete, audit-ready product profiles from multiple sources
    • Analyzing sanctions screening hits—resolving high-confidence cases automatically and escalating others with a proposal to specialists
    • Orchestrating end‑to‑end trade workflows and autonomously prioritizing classification workloads

  • Example use cases for this assistant:

    • Accelerated harmonized system (HS) code classification through AI-supported recommendations and one-click expert validation
    • Streamlined classification of new product catalogs with guided execution planning
    • Automated resolution and audit logging for high-confidence sanction hits
    • Escalation of uncertain cases to specialists with detailed explanations

Frequently asked questions

  • What is SAP’s International Trade Assistant?

    The International Trade Assistant brings intelligence to trade compliance and customs processes across supply chain operations. It supports trade compliance specialists by automating product profile building and assisting with product classification to deliver accurate, audit-ready results grounded in legally binding customs standards. The assistant covers the most time-intensive trade compliance activities: HS code classification, product profile building, and sanctions screening analysis.

  • How does the International Trade Assistant work?

    The International Trade Assistant coordinates specialized agents that gather product data from multiple sources, semantically interpret it, and produce an audit-ready profile, including GRI pre-analysis. Its agents then apply this profile to recommend tariff numbers and commodity codes with full legal rationale, completing the GRI sequence.

  • Who should use the International Trade Assistant?

    The International Trade Assistant is designed for trade compliance specialists, customs classification teams, and supply chain compliance managers who are responsible for managing import/export compliance across complex product catalogs and jurisdictions. It is particularly relevant for organizations with large volumes of products requiring HS classification, those dealing with frequent new product introductions, and compliance teams that process sanctions screening hits and need to distinguish high-confidence automated resolutions from cases requiring specialist review.

  • How does the International Trade Assistant classify products with tariff and commodity codes?

    Classification begins with the International Trade Product Profile Agent, which builds a structured product profile and performs GRI pre-analysis. The International Trade Classification Agent then applies this to recommend tariff numbers and commodity codes, generating up to five ranked proposals, each with legal rationale covering GRI rules, legal notes, and official taxonomy descriptions. Trade compliance specialists review and validate the final decision, producing an auditable record and making HS code classification AI-assisted rather than fully manual.

  • How does the International Trade Assistant help handle sanctions screening hits?

    The Sanctions Screening Hit Classification Agent analyzes sanctions screening hits and distinguishes between high-confidence cases that can be resolved autonomously and those that require specialist escalation. Auto-resolved cases are documented in an audit trail. Cases below the confidence threshold are escalated to screening specialists with AI-generated reasoning, so compliance teams focus their attention on genuinely ambiguous situations rather than routine processing.
