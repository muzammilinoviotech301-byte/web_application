Overview: Deploy agents today. Unlock even more tomorrow.

The Sourcing Assistant orchestrates the sourcing lifecycle from supplier discovery to award. It understands buyer intent; creates events grounded in contracts and past sourcing data; analyzes bids with supplier risk and market inputs; and generates counteroffers at scale. This results in agility through faster event launches and award decisions, while delivering cost savings through policy-driven negotiations.

The agents you stand up in SAP Ariba will lay the foundation for the future of procurement, and they’ll hit even harder in next-gen SAP Ariba. That's because the SAP Business AI Platform and SAP Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating sourcing events using contractual, historical, and supplier data
  • Recommending qualified suppliers and discovering new suppliers from a network of millions
  • Analyzing bids across cost, quality, and risk dimensions with market inputs
  • Recommending awards with explainable trade-offs and reasoning
  • Automating negotiation and counter-offer generation
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Reduced manual effort in event creation and bid evaluation
  • Enhanced sourcing agility for urgent and high-volume events
  • Improved decision quality for better total cost analysis and transparent trade-off analysis
  • Automated negotiations that ensuring no savings opportunity is left on the table

Benefits

Faster time to award
Compress sourcing cycles through automated event creation, bid analysis, and award recommendations.

Smarter supplier decisions
Analyze bids by supplier risk, market data, and sourcing objectives with transparent reasoning.

Stronger negotiation outcomes
Generate counteroffers focused on price, payment terms, and delivery levers based on competitive bid data.

Reduced manual workload
Eliminate event setup and bid comparison tasks with recommendations grounded in past events, contracts, supplier data, and deep research.
