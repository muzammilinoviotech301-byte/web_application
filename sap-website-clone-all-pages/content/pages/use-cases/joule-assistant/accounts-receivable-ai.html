Overview

The Accounts Receivable Assistant helps teams accelerate cash collection, resolve disputes efficiently, and manage credit risk proactively. It improves operational efficiency and accelerates cash realization by reducing manual activities, minimizing delays, and ensuring consistent, accurate, and compliant financial execution.

  • Tasks this assistant can help with:

    Collection worklist

    • Focusing collections agents on the most critical accounts
    • Providing account background information
    • Surfacing next-best-action recommendations
    • Preparing and sending out customer communication

    Dispute management

    • Creating disputes from customer communications
    • Extracting key information
    • Validating customer claims and recommending next actions
    • Collaborating across departments to resolve disputes faster

    Customer account insight

    • Providing a 360-degree view of customer accounts
    • Displaying overdue items, balances, dispute cases, promises to pay, resubmissions, and contact history in one place
    • Supporting informed customer conversations

  • Example use cases for this assistant:

    • Overdue account monitoring and daily accounts receivable management
    • Customer invoice dispute creation and resolution
    • Customer call preparation with full account context and next‑best actions
    • Follow‑up tracking

Benefits

  • Accelerated cash collection

    AI-driven insights and next-best-action recommendations help collectors focus on the right overdue accounts at the right time, speeding cash recovery and reducing DSO.

  • Complete customer account context

    A consolidated view of overdue items, balances, disputes, and contact history gives collectors the full picture for more effective conversations and faster payment commitments.

  • Faster dispute resolution and cash release

    Automated root‑cause identification and guided resolution steps shorten dispute cycles, freeing blocked cash while improving customer satisfaction.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is SAP’s Accounts Receivable Assistant?

    SAP’s Accounts Receivable Assistant helps finance teams accelerate cash collection, resolve disputes efficiently, and manage credit risk proactively. It supports worklist processing, creates and analyzes disputes, and provides a 360-degree customer account view, giving accounts receivable teams the context and next-best-action guidance needed to improve revenue conversion and cash flow.

  • How does the Accounts Receivable Assistant work?

    The assistant orchestrates several specialized AI agents that perform key accounts receivable functions. They include the Collection Account Preparation Agent, which surfaces account background and next-best actions; the Collections Email Outreach Agent, which prepares and sends customer communications; and the Dispute Creation and Dispute Resolution Agents, which extract key details from customer communications, validate claims, and recommend resolution steps.

  • Who should use the Accounts Receivable Assistant?

    The Accounts Receivable Assistant is designed for collections specialists, dispute managers, and AR team leads who manage overdue accounts, customer disputes, and credit risk. Organizations running high volumes of customer invoices and looking to reduce days sales outstanding, accelerate dispute closure, and cut manual call-prep time will also benefit from using this assistant.

  • How does the Accounts Receivable Assistant support dispute resolution?

    The assistant creates dispute cases directly from customer communications, extracts key information, validates claims against invoice data, identifies root causes, and recommends next actions. It routes cases for resolution and tracks progress. Rather than requiring collectors to switch between multiple screens, all dispute context—disputed items, source documents, and resolution steps—appears in one customer account view.

  • Can the Accounts Receivable Assistant generate written outreach to follow up on overdue items?

    Yes. The Collections Email Outreach Agent prepares and sends customer communications. It derives and summarizes account background, overdue balance details, open disputes or promises to pay, and recommended next actions, reducing the time spent composing and tracking follow-up messages.
