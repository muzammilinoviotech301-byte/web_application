Overview

The Sales Assistant embeds AI into daily workflows to manage opportunities, automate updates, and keep deals moving. By orchestrating AI agents to analyze interactions, surface insights, and recommend next-best actions, it reduces manual effort, improves pipeline accuracy, and drives consistent, high-quality sales execution across each engagement.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Updating opportunities autonomously based on interaction signals
  • Recommending next-best actions
  • Capturing meeting insights and generating structured summaries
  • Creating follow-up tasks and assigning workflows
  • Analyzing email interactions and detecting intent
  • Identifying pipeline risks and monitoring deal health
  • Examining forecast signals and updating opportunity scoring
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Deal progression through recommended next steps that keep stalled opportunities moving forward
  • CRM automation by streamlining data entry and activity tracking
  • Consistent execution across the sales lifecycle through standardized workflows and actions across teams

Benefits

Reduce manual sales effort
Automate opportunity updates, meeting summaries, and follow-up tasks, enabling sellers to focus on high-value interactions and closing deals faster.

Accelerate deal progression
Guide sellers with AI-enabled next-best actions based on real-time signals to help maintain momentum and move opportunities forward with greater precision.

Improve forecast accuracy
Analyze pipeline activity continuously and update opportunity data in real time for more reliable forecasts and better revenue predictability.

Enhance seller productivity
Streamline the sales workflow by reducing context switching and enabling faster, more informed decision-making throughout the deal cycle.

Strengthen pipeline health and visibility
Identify risks and signals early, allowing teams to take proactive action and maintain a clean, high-quality pipeline.
