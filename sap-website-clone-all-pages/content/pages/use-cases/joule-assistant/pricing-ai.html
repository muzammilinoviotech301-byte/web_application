Overview

  • The Pricing Assistant transforms pricing from a reactive, manual process into an intelligent, continuously optimized capability. Specialized AI agents recommend optimal prices, detect risks, help protect margins, run pricing simulations to evaluate the impact of price changes, and guide sales teams to close deals profitably. All decisions are grounded in SAP ERP context.

  • Tasks this assistant can help with:

    • Recommending optimal list prices for products
    • Providing deal price guidance for quotes, orders, and contracts
    • Detecting expiring prices, pricing anomalies, and data inconsistencies
    • Alerting users about at-risk pricing KPIs and potential margin erosion
    • Simulating price change effects on margin, revenue, and sales volume
    • Creating and updating pricing models at speed and scale

  • Example use cases for this assistant:

    • Competitive and optimized pricing for new product launches
    • Product margin protection during supply cost increases or market disruption
    • Higher deal win rates and profitability through AI-guided price recommendations
    • Enterprise-wide pricing accuracy that catches expiring prices, anomalies, and data gaps
    • Faster pricing strategy adaptation to market and competitive shifts through scenario simulation

Frequently asked questions

  • What is SAP’s Pricing Assistant?

    The Pricing Assistant transforms pricing from a reactive, manual process into an intelligent, continuously optimized capability. Specialized AI agents recommend optimal prices, detect anomalies, and run pricing simulations to evaluate the impact of price changes—with all decisions grounded in SAP ERP context.

  • How does the Pricing Assistant work?

    The Pricing Assistant coordinates four specialized agents. The Pricing Orchestration Agent acts as the intelligence hub of the Pricing Assistant, interpreting each incoming request and coordinating the right specialist agents in the right sequence. This means the end-to-end process of detecting a pricing risk, simulating the financial impact, and surfacing a recommendation happens in a connected workflow, rather than across days of manual monitoring, modeling, and cross-team coordination. The result is faster reaction time: pricing issues are identified and resolved before they reach a customer quote or invoice, protecting margin where it is most at risk. Every recommended change requires user approval before it takes effect in SAP ERP.

  • Who should use the Pricing Assistant?

    The Pricing Assistant is designed for pricing managers, commercial managers, and sales teams responsible for setting, maintaining, and applying prices in SAP environments. It benefits pricing teams that manage large product portfolios where manual pricing model maintenance is slow, as well as finance stakeholders monitoring margin health, by providing AI recommendations grounded in live SAP ERP pricing data.

  • How does the Pricing Assistant help protect margins during cost increases or market disruption?

    The Pricing Health Check Agent monitors pricing data for anomalies, expiring prices, and inconsistencies, alerting users to at-risk pricing before issues compound. When cost increases or market conditions require a pricing response, the Pricing Simulation Agent evaluates the impact of proposed price changes on margin before they are applied, replacing the manual analysis cycle that typically delays pricing decisions during volatile periods.

  • Can the Pricing Assistant simulate the impact of price changes before they’re applied?

    Yes. The Pricing Simulation Agent runs pricing simulations to evaluate the effect of proposed price changes on margin. Teams can test scenarios—such as a list price increase across a product line—and see the projected impact before any changes are committed. This replaces offline spreadsheet modeling with a simulation capability grounded directly in SAP ERP pricing context and live data.
