Overview

The Travel Assistant streamlines travel planning and booking, coordinates approvals using AI-generated trip cost estimates that align with company travel policies, and provides personalized itineraries based on traveler preferences. It offers policy guidance, supports cost-effective group travel planning, enables up-front financial controls, and provides a single dashboard showing delegate-managed team travel with insights to improve travel management.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Estimating trip costs
  • Generating individual travel itineraries
  • Recommending tips based on policy
  • Coordinating logistics for group travel
  • Generating personalized, policy aware trip itineraries
  • Providing in-the-moment policy clarity
  • Providing insights into delegate-managed travel
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Streamlined pre-trip approval requests
  • Personalized, policy-compliant travel planning and booking
  • Real-time spend estimate visibility with booking-stage pricing breakdowns and travel class justifications
  • Optimized group travel planning for cost and logistics
  • Centralized visibility into team travel and expenses for delegates with proactive recommendations and insights

Benefits

Personalized, policy-aware trip planning and booking
AI agents create more efficient approval workflows and deliver individualized itineraries.

Optimized group travel and meeting planning
AI agents support efficient team trip planning by balancing costs, logistics, and policy compliance across multiple travelers.

Streamlined delegate managed travel and expense
AI agents provide insights and recommendations in a single dashboard to help delegates prioritize their work and save time managing team travel and expenses.
