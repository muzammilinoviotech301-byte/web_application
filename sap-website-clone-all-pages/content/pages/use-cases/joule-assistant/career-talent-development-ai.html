Overview

The Career and Talent Development Assistant creates strategically aligned development plans and cultivates healthy talent pipelines. AI agents continuously monitor individual development needs and provide tailored career guidance, helping ensure talent readiness and engagement.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Generating development plans aligned to individual and business objectives
  • Creating personalized career paths
  • Matching internal talent to opportunities
  • Identifying and addressing succession gaps
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Career path discovery and guidance
  • Succession planning and pipeline analysis
  • Internal mobility and talent matching
  • Mentorship alignment and recommendations

Benefits

Lower attrition risk
Provide employees with personalized development plans aligned to their individual needs and interests.

Faster time to fill
Use agents to instantly match internal talent to roles, projects, and strategic tasks.

Increased succession coverage rate
Identify and resolve succession gaps proactively, helping sustain a healthy talent pipeline.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Career and Talent Development Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.
