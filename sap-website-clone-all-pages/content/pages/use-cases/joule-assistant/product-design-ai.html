Overview

The Product Design Assistant is an AI-driven system of agents that connects data across product development and downstream processes. It analyzes product information, supports change decisions, ensures compliance, and coordinates collaboration—helping teams move faster, reduce rework, and deliver higher-quality products across industries.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Autonomously analyzing product designs, recipes, and specifications
  • Preparing product changes, autonomously scoping, and assessing impacts
  • Validating compliance and requirements across product and recipe data
  • Extracting and classifying product data and automating quality improvements
  • Setting up collaborations, tracking tasks, and autonomously creating notifications
  • Preparing product data for handover to production and supply chain execution
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Product and recipe change execution including analyzing designs, validating requirements, assessing impacts, coordinating tasks, and preparing compliant changes for release
  • Production‑ready product data handover including extracting, cleansing, validating, and packaging product data to ensure accurate, compliant transfer to manufacturing and downstream processes

Benefits

Accelerated innovation
Analyze designs, recipes, and product data to surface insights instantly and release products faster.

Autonomous lifecycle guidance
Proactively flag issues, assess change impacts, validate requirements, and recommend next steps for reduced rework.

Connected development process
Link design, engineering, compliance, and downstream processes and help teams stay aligned.

Built-in compliance and quality
Check designs, materials, and recipes against internal rules and regulations for risk detection and quality control.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Product Design Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Product Knowledge Retrieval Agent

Finds similar products, parts, or recipes and explains design history in natural language.

Product Change Management Agent

Drafts and routes change requests, identifies affected items, and guides end‑to‑end change execution.

Product Data Maintenance Agent

Extracts, structures, and maintains high‑quality product data across materials and specifications.

Product Structure Optimization Agent

Optimizes product structures by aligning bills of materials (BOMs) and recommending cost‑ and risk‑reducing alternatives.

Recipe Formulation Agent

Enables recipe search, explains recipes, and performs input item exchange.

Change Record Management Agent

Finds similar change records, proposes new drafts, and enables faster, more confident decisions.

Product Compliance Assessment Agent

Evaluates compliance of products with regulation-backed reasoning and simulation of change impact.

Product Change Impact Analysis Agent

Analyzes product change impacts, BOM dependencies, and risk, cost, and mitigation priorities.

Product Development Collaboration Agent

Turns a brief into a live collaboration, tracking risks, blockers, and stakeholder actions.

Project Completion Agent

Reduces open project backlogs by identifying closure candidates and resolving blockers.

Packaging Compliance Agent

Automates PPWR compliance by filling data gaps, validating requirements, and generating declarations.

Product Handover Management Agent

Optimizes product handovers by automating manufacturing BOM creation, routing proposals, and change updates.
