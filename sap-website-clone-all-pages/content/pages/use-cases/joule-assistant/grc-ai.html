Frequently asked questions

  • What is SAP’s Governance Assistant?

    SAP’s Governance Assistant simplifies governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) operations by helping teams monitor business risks, streamline compliance workflows, surface control exceptions, and turn complex governance data into clear, actionable insights. It covers risk detection and identification, compliance gap remediation, continuous control monitoring, audit acceleration, and business partner integrity screening with a human-in-the-loop approach that keeps GRC teams in control.

  • How does the Governance Assistant work?

    The Governance Assistant orchestrates specialized GRC agents, including the Integrity Screening Insights Agent for business partner screening against sanctions, politically exposed persons (PEPs), and adverse media lists, and the GRC Audit Agent for evidence collection, analysis, and report drafting. It also coordinates AI agents for risk identification, continuous control monitoring, issue remediation, and more. Every AI-generated proposal is held for human review before it is committed, keeping GRC teams in control while agents handle the time-intensive work of evidence gathering, risk classification, and compliance execution across processes.

  • Who should use the Governance Assistant?

    The Governance Assistant is designed for GRC managers, risk officers and risk champions, compliance teams, internal auditors, and business integrity screening specialists. It suits organizations that need continuous control monitoring at scale, faster audit preparation, proactive risk identification, and automated partner screening. It’s also useful for businesses that want GRC activities embedded in operational processes, rather than managed through separate manual workflows.

  • How does the Governance Assistant maintain human oversight of GRC decisions?

    The Governance Assistant surfaces risk information, control exceptions, and compliance gaps with context and recommended responses, rather than acting unilaterally. It analyzes audit evidence and presents it for reviewer judgment, aggregates and surfaces screening results for analyst review, and proposes actions with rationale. This keeps GRC professionals as decision-makers while the assistant handles detection, evidence gathering, and recommendation, creating a traceable record of every governance action.

  • How does the Governance Assistant screen third parties for sanctions, PEPs, and adverse media?

    The Governance Assistant’s Integrity Screening Insights Agent screens business parties for ownership structure, sanctions list exposure, PEP status, and adverse media coverage in a single workflow. Rather than triggering separate point checks across systems, the agent collects evidence across multiple sources, aggregates it into a confidence score, and surfaces the result with supporting context, enabling faster risk decisions and reducing the delays typical of manual third-party review. Results are presented to compliance reviewers with enough supporting detail to act or escalate confidently, supporting onboarding due diligence, ongoing monitoring, and exception-based reviews.
