Overview

The Learning Assistant creates tailored learning journeys for employees based on individual and business objectives. It helps close skills gaps by surfacing targeted training opportunities and strengthens compliance by intelligently monitoring required course deadlines.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating personalized learning journeys
  • Assigning structured learning assignments
  • Monitoring compliance and course completion
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Intelligent content curation
  • Learning assignment automation
  • Compliance risk mitigation

Benefits

Higher course completion rate
Use agents to proactively notify employees and managers of upcoming compliance training deadlines.

Reduced learning costs
Align learning investments with business strategy to lower the risk of overspending.

Increased internal mobility
Close skills gaps and foster a culture of continuous growth with personalized learning journeys.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Learning Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.
