Overview

The HR Service Assistant provides always-on, scalable support. It answers employees’ HR questions, guides them to the right self-services, and orchestrates specialized agents for time, payroll, and core HR when needed. By resolving routine requests conversationally and only creating cases for complex scenarios, the assistant reduces ticket volume while improving speed and consistency.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Providing guided, policy-aware answers
  • Guiding employees to the right self-service actions to complete common HR tasks
  • Answering time and payroll questions via specialized AI agents
  • Helping employees maintain HR data for personal and work events
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Personalized employee support
  • HR ticket creation
  • Policy guidance and compliance
  • Time and payroll explanations

Benefits

Faster answers for employees
Provide instant guidance that reduces the time to resolution for HR service inquiries.

Lower HR service center workload
Reduce HR tickets and free up resources by automatically redirecting and resolving routine questions.

More consistent, policy-grounded responses
Anchor answers in current policies with document grounding and knowledge graph capabilities.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the HR Service Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.
