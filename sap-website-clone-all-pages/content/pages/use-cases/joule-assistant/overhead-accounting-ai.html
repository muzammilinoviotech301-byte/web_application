Overview

The Overhead Accounting Assistant orchestrates the agents managing overhead cost accounting. The process becomes self-executing, self-explaining, and change resilient, freeing overhead accountants and controllers to focus on cost optimization rather than transaction execution.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating and managing cost center, internal order, and profit center master data
  • Resolving master data issues before they impact business activities
  • Validating settlement rules and improving accuracy
  • Explaining allocation results and drivers
  • Providing actionable guidance to resolve runtime errors before they become blockers
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Accelerated financial close with errors resolved before they impact timelines
  • Improved reporting accuracy for better business decisions
  • Stronger governance and compliance with enhanced audit readiness

Benefits

Accelerate period-end close with intelligent automation
Reduce manual effort in settlements and activity allocations by automating repetitive overhead accounting tasks. The assistant helps finance teams achieve faster financial closes, minimize human errors, improve compliance, and focus more on analysis and decision-making and less on operational troubleshooting.

Improve financial accuracy, transparency, and audit readiness
Increase confidence in financial data through guided allocations, automated validation checks, and enhanced visibility into posting logic. By reducing inconsistencies and making allocation decisions easier to trace and justify, the assistant strengthens reporting quality and simplifies audit processes.

Reduce dependency on experts and ensure high-quality master data
Support finance users with key master data maintenance and embedded guidance for activity allocations. The assistant helps ensure complete, compliant, and accurate data while lowering operational risk, decreasing support overhead, and reducing reliance on specialized experts for day-to-day activities.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Overhead Accounting Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Allocation Results Analysis Agent

Uncovers insights from allocation results, explains drivers, highlights monthly trends, and provides actionable guidance to resolve runtime errors.

Allocation Traceability Agent

Traces any cost center allocation back to its origin in the universal journal.

Controlling Settlement Management Agent

Runs settlement processes and applies guided or automated resolutions to any issues.

Cost Center Management Agent

Improves cost center master data quality and resolves issues before they impact processes.

Internal Order Management Agent

Simplifies internal order management tasks, from creation to monitoring, closure, and reporting.

Internal Order Variance Analysis Agent

Explains internal order variances beyond budget vs. actual, identifying root causes and drivers.

Overhead Activity Allocation Agent

Translates business scenarios into allocation logic that’s executed after approval.

Profit Center Management Agent

Manages the profit center master data lifecycle, including health checks and error resolution.

Settlement Rule Generation Agent

Proposes and creates settlement rules for business scenarios based on expert input.

Statistical Key Figures Management Agent

Simplifies use of key-figure-based allocation cycles, reducing error potential.

Universal Allocation Management Agent

Manages and executes allocation processes through natural language interactions.
