That's because the SAP Business AI Platform and Business Data Cloud significantly sharpen agentic decision-making, amplifying the value of everything you've already launched.

The Procurement Contract Assistant tracks commitments, monitors obligations, alerts users to risks or expirations, and supports renewal, renegotiation, or new sourcing decisions.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating legally compliant first drafts of contracts
  • Monitoring contract terms continuously
  • Analyzing expiring contracts to recommend a renewal strategy
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Instant first-draft contracts with missing terms flagged for contract owners
  • Protecting contracts from potential obligation leakage and impacts before period-end
  • Deciding whether to extend, renegotiate, consolidate, or exit a contract

Benefits

Prevent value erosion
Monitor contract commitments, pricing, usage, and SLAs to identify risks before they impact value. Automatic alerts notify users when obligations are breached or key deadlines are approaching.

Improve renewal outcomes
Analyze usage and supplier performance to recommend actions and drive timely renewal workflows.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Procurement Contract Assistant to coordinate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Procurement Contract Authoring Agent

Generates compliant first-draft contracts, fills standard clauses, and flags missing terms.

Procurement Contract Compliance Monitoring Agent

Tracks contract value against commitments, detects leakage risks, and alerts owners before terms end.

Procurement Contract Renewal Optimization Agent

Analyzes expiring contracts to recommend optimal renewal actions and triggers 30-, 60-, and 90-day workflows.

Purchase Contract Renewal Advisory Agent

Analyzes expiring contracts and recommends renewal actions based on supplier performance, pricing, and market conditions.

Purchase Information Records Mass Change Agent

Finds purchase records by user criteria, updates eligible records upon confirmation, and tracks job progress and status.

Purchase Contracts Mass Change Agent

Identifies change attributes, presents them for user review and confirmation, then updates all eligible purchase contracts and summarizes the results.

Material Spend Insights Agent

Identifies purchase orders without contract assignments, recommends fixes, and alerts purchasers to reduce spend leakage.

Material Spend Advisory Agent

Detects purchase orders without contract assignment and delivers prioritized fixes to reduce leakage and improve coverage.

Purchase Contract Insights Agent

Enables purchasers to generate on-demand contract summaries that include alerts, risks, and recommended actions directly from the contract page.
