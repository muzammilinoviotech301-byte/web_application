Overview

The Sales Operations Assistant enables sales teams to quickly identify fulfillment issues, receive recommended resolutions, and create or update sales documents directly in their workflow, which minimizes manual rework and delays, reduces order-to-cash cycle time, and improves customer satisfaction, enabling teams to operate more efficiently.

Tasks this assistant can help with:

Order monitoring and issue resolution

  • Monitoring supply, credit, billing, and delivery issues
  • Recommending actions to resolve issues
  • Categorizing agent actions and recommended actions
  • Removing blocks
  • Executing actions, such as removing blocks and creating and updating sales documents after human approval
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Monitoring and resolution of credit, delivery, and supply issues in sales orders
  • Recommendations for proposed actions and removal of blocks to ensure sales order fulfillment
  • Minimized delivery and payment delays through faster error resolution
  • Improved accuracy in sales order data
  • Automated sales document creation and update

Benefits

Accelerated problem resolution
Identify fulfillment issues and speed resolution with actionable solutions.

Time savings and error reduction
Automate sales document creation to shorten order cycle times and minimize mistakes.

Improved deal quality and pricing decisions
Maintain accurate, up-to-date sales data that supports more informed pricing choices.

Agents used by this assistant

Use the Sales Operations Assistant to orchestrate specialized AI agents, including Joule Agents that apply SAP’s process expertise or custom agents you create.

Agent

Description

Billing Block Resolution Agent

Displays billing block details and recommends corrective actions, such as updating payment terms.

Credit Block Issues Resolution Agent

Recommends corrective actions—such as reviewing credit limits or checking open deliveries—to resolve credit block issues.

Delivery Issue Resolution Agent

Proposes outbound and partial deliveries, alternative products, or adjusts delivery dates to resolve delivery blocks.

Supply Issue Resolution Agent

Evaluates supply issues across purchasing and manufacturing, verifies production dates, and links related documents.
