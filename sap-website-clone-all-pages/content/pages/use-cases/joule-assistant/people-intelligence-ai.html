Overview

The People Intelligence Assistant turns workforce data into proactive, actionable insight. It continuously monitors KPIs across headcount, attrition, hiring, skills, and cost—detecting meaningful shifts and explaining what drives them. By pairing root-cause analysis with prescriptive, persona-aware recommendations, it helps leaders move from dashboard watching to confident, insight-led decisions.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Monitoring workforce KPIs across segments and surfacing deviations from defined thresholds
  • Detecting skills signals and keeping skills profiles current and relevant for employees and administrators
  • Recommending policy-aware, workflow-linked actions tailored to personas
  • Generating executive- and board-ready summaries, including financial and ROI views of talent decisions
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Workforce insights and reporting
  • Headcount planning
  • Skills gap analysis
  • Attrition and retention forecasting

Benefits

Faster insight-to-decision cycles
Automate detection, explanation, and action to shorten the time from KPI deviation to decision.

Proactive, not reactive, workforce management
Drive continuous monitoring and threshold-based alerts to help leaders act when trends emerge, not months later.

Deeper, explainable workforce insights
Use analytical agents to decompose drivers and correlations across multiple dimensions, giving HR and business leaders clear, natural-language explanations.
