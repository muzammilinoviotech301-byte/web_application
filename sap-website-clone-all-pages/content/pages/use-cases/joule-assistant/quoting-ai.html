Overview

The Quoting Assistant transforms manual quoting into an intelligent, guided selling experience. It automates quote creation, product configuration, upsell recommendations, and document assembly. Every action is grounded in your CPQ rules, pricing policies, and historical outcomes, enabling sales teams to close deals faster and with greater accuracy.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Converting unstructured emails, RFQs, and PDFs into structured CPQ draft quotes
  • Proposing compliant product configurations from natural-language customer intent
  • Recommending upgrades, add-ons, and cross-sell options in real time
  • Assembling proposal documents and drafting customer emails
  • Routing multi-step requests to the appropriate specialized agent automatically
  • Enforcing pricing compliance without manual manager review
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • End-to-end RFQ response automation from data extraction to proposal delivery
  • Faster quote generation for complex configured products
  • Increased deal opportunities through guided upsell and cross-sell during quoting creation
  • Improved sales team productivity with AI-guided configuration and pricing
