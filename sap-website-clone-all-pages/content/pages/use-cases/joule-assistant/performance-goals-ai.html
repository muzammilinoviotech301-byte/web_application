Overview

The Performance & Goals Assistant creates strategically aligned goals and continuously monitors progress and business impact. It supports managers in preparing for performance check-ins and reviews, helping drive fair, data-driven decisions.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Creating and aligning goals to business objectives
  • Monitoring goal progress
  • Preparing for performance reviews
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Goal setting and alignment
  • Continuous performance monitoring
  • Performance review automation

Benefits

Higher quality goals
Generate SMART performance goals aligned to strategy and role context.

Reduced risk
Track goal progress in real time and surface risks before deadlines are missed.

Faster performance review preparation
Drive meaningful conversations with performance reviews that are automatically prepared using evidence-based insights.
