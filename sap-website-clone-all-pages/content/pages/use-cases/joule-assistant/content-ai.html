Overview

The Content Assistant coordinates AI agents to generate, assemble, and activate personalized content in real time using unified customer data and live business signals. Unlike static content tools, it continuously adapts content and experiences across channels based on customer behavior and business context, with built-in guardrails for brand consistency, security, and compliance.

Tasks this assistant can help with:
  • Generating personalized copy, visuals, and content variants from customer and business contexts
  • Assembling channel ready assets from modular content blocks
  • Adapting content across regions, languages, and cultural contexts
  • Inserting products, offers, and pricing based on customer behavior and intent
  • Enforcing brand and campaign consistency across content, channels, and touchpoints
Example use cases for this assistant:
  • Real-time messaging, imagery, and offer personalization based on behavioral signals
  • Microsegment variant generation
  • Channel-specific formats, layout, and tone recommendations
  • Performance patterns and engagement signal detection
  • Automated brand, legal, and compliance standard enforcement across generated content
  • Dynamic product and offer recommendations to increase conversions
