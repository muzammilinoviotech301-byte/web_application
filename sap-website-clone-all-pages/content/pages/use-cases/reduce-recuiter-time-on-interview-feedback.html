Use Case

Reduce recruiter time on interview feedback by 70%

Use AI in SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting to summarize interviewer notes, highlight skills and concerns, and support faster, better hiring decisions.

Published on October 13, 2025

Overview

AI-assisted interview feedback insights in SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting generates summaries of candidate evaluations, including skills, competencies, and recommendations. Recruiters can regenerate insights as new feedback arrives, ensuring hiring teams have up-to-date, comprehensive views for faster and more informed decisions.

Benefits

  • Improve recruiter and hiring manager efficiency in reviewing feedback
  • Avoid manual effort required to summarize interviewer notes
  • Identify consensus or concerns across the interview team
  • Regenerate summaries as additional feedback becomes available

Business value

  • Faster processing

    70% reduction in recruiter time spent summarizing interviewer feedback

  • Improved decision-making

    Hiring managers gain clearer insights into candidate fit

  • Higher efficiency

    Automated summaries reduce manual effort and speed the process
