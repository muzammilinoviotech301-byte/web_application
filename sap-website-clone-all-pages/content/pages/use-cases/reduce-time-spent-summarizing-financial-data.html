Published on May 14, 2025

Overview

Timely financial decisions rely on fast, actionable insights. The generative AI side panel in the Cost Center Review Booklet app helps analysts explore reports with quick actions that summarize data, surface KPIs, and recommend next steps—turning raw cost center data into guided insights for faster, more confident decision-making.

Benefits

  • Empower analysts with complete visibility, including AI-generated summaries and root cause analysis
  • Improve efficiency by prioritizing tasks, automating repetitive work, and suggesting next steps
  • Lower finance costs with instant access to KPIs, shareable reports, and actionable recommendations
  • Accelerate decision making by turning complex data into clear, concise insights

Business Value

  • Faster data analysis

    Reduce the time to analyze cost center summaries by 50%

  • Streamlined reporting

    Cut time to document and summarize reports by up to 65%

  • Lower operational costs

    Gain faster visibility into financial metrics and suggested actions
