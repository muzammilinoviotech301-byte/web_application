Frequently asked questions

Sustainability in finance is the practice of incorporating environmental, social and governance (ESG) factors into financial strategy, planning and reporting with traditional financial metrics. Rather than treating sustainability as a separate initiative, organisations integrate carbon, climate risk and ESG data into core financial decisions, such as capital allocation, budgeting and risk management, to better understand both long-term value and exposure to regulatory and market pressures.

ESG reporting software helps organisations collect, manage and report on environmental, social and governance data in compliance with regulatory frameworks such as CSRD and IFRS. It centralises data from across the business, enables audit-ready reporting on metrics like emissions, energy use and workforce diversity and supports target setting and performance tracking. It replaces manual, spreadsheet-based processes with automated, verifiable data flows.

Carbon management and carbon accounting software enables organisations to measure, track and reduce their greenhouse gas emissions across Scope 1, 2 and 3. It calculates corporate and product-level carbon footprints using actual operational data, rather than estimates and supports compliance with emission reporting standards such as the GHG Protocol. Leading solutions embed carbon data directly into financial and procurement systems so that emissions become a factor in everyday business decisions.

 

Integrating sustainability into finance and business planning gives organisations a more complete picture of risk and performance. It connects emissions and ESG data to financial outcomes, enabling more informed decisions on capital allocation, supplier selection and cost management. It also streamlines regulatory compliance by generating audit-ready sustainability disclosures from the same data used for financial reporting, reducing duplication and the risk of inconsistency across reports.

SAP offers an integrated sustainability portfolio—including SAP Sustainability Control Tower, SAP Sustainability Footprint Management, SAP Sustainability Data Exchange and SAP Green Ledger—that embeds carbon and ESG data directly into ERP and financial processes. Because sustainability is integrated into SAP's core business systems rather than added as a separate tool, organisations can use actual transactional data to comply with regulations, produce auditable Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions reports and factor carbon costs into financial planning.
