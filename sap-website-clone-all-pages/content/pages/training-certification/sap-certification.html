What is SAP Certification?

SAP Certification is a globally recognized credential that validates job-ready skills across key SAP solution areas and roles through practical exams.

Choose the path to certification that fits your needs:

  • Start preparing with free self-paced learning on the SAP Learning site.

  • Build deeper skills with SAP Learning Hub, which includes premium learning, hands-on practice, expert-led live sessions, four SAP Certification exam attempts per year, and annual assessments to stay certified.

  • If you're already prepared and simply need to take the test, you can also purchase standalone SAP Certification exam attempts.

Frequently asked questions

To get SAP-certified, choose a certification that aligns with your role or career goals and prepare using SAP Learning Hub, SAP's recommended learning experience. SAP Learning Hub provides learning content, live sessions, hands-on practice systems, and four exam attempts to help you build the skills and confidence needed to succeed. You can also purchase a standalone certification exam attempt.

 

For more information, see our step-by-step guide to SAP Certification.

An SAP Certification exam is a comprehensive evaluation of the skills required for a specific SAP solution or role. 

 

Assessments are short, non-proctored quizzes with unlimited attempts that focus only on the knowledge introduced between software releases.

 

These assessments help learners stay up to date with the latest solution updates and maintain their certification. Assessments to stay certified are available exclusively through an SAP Learning Hub subscription.

SAP Certification pricing varies based on your certification pathway. You can purchase standalone SAP Certification exam attempts or an SAP Learning Hub subscription that includes certification attempts, guided preparation, hands-on practice, and certification maintenance support. Partners can log in to SAP PartnerEdge for partner-specific pricing information.

SAP Certification uses a practical, open-book exam format designed to assess how candidates apply SAP skills in realistic scenarios. Depending on the certification, exams may include system-based or scenario-based tasks supported by learning journeys, hands-on practice, and other preparation resources.
