What is a clean core?

Clean core is a set of guiding principles that support continuous business transformation by designing agile, innovative and efficient ERP systems.

Published on May 12, 2025

Why is a clean core important?

Think of a clean core as ERP without the baggage. Traditional ERP systems are often deployed on-premises and heavily customized to align with unique business processes and industry-specific needs. While these customizations can provide short-term flexibility, they can also bring long-term challenges.

Over time, layers of custom code create complex systems that are difficult to maintain, expensive to upgrade, and prone to error. Each system update or new feature rollout becomes a risky, time-consuming project, requiring extensive testing and rework. This technical debt—the accumulated cost and complexity of maintaining outdated custom code—slows down innovation and makes it harder for businesses to adapt to changing market demands.

That’s where a clean core comes in. At its heart, a clean core means keeping your ERP system as close as possible to its standard, vendor-provided state while handling custom modifications through cloud extensions and side-by-side innovations. It’s like decluttering your ERP system—getting rid of unnecessary customizations that slow down progress while still allowing your business to be flexible and innovative.

The business case for a clean core strategy

Adopting a clean core isn't just about IT efficiency—it’s about staying agile in the face of change. A clean core strategy enables your organization to:

  • Upgrade faster and innovate continuously
    When businesses keep their core ERP systems clean, they can quickly adopt new software updates without worrying about breaking customizations. This means they can access the latest features, security patches, and regulatory compliance updates as soon as they become available.
  • Reduce technical debt and keep costs low
    A clean core minimizes the need for custom code, leading to lower maintenance costs and a more manageable, efficient IT environment that’s easier to maintain and scale.
  • Increase system performance and reliability
    Excessive modifications can introduce instability. By sticking to standard processes, you can help ensure your ERP system remains stable, scalable, and high-performing.
  • Embrace cloud flexibility
    Instead of modifying your core system, you can use cloud-based tools like SAP Business Technology Platform (SAP BTP) to add functionality while keeping your ERP foundation intact. That allows for flexibility without the downsides of traditional customizations.

How to transition to a clean core ERP system

Moving to a clean core doesn’t happen overnight—it requires a clear strategy and a shift in how you think about ERP customization. Here’s how you can begin your transition:

  1. Assess your current customizations
    Start by auditing your ERP system to identify which customizations are truly necessary and which can be replaced with standard functionality or cloud extensions.
  2. Prioritize standardization
    Whenever possible, adopt industry best practices and use out-of-the-box ERP features instead of custom-built solutions.
  3. Leverage side-by-side extensibility
    Instead of modifying the ERP core, use SAP BTP to build custom applications and integrations that sit alongside the core system.
  4. Adopt an agile mindset
    A clean core isn’t a one-time project. It’s an ongoing commitment with enormous benefits in the long run. To get the best results, consider taking an agile approach that focuses on flexibility, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
