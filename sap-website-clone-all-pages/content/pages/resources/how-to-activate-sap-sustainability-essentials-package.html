Step 1: Get to know your sustainability essentials package capabilities

SAP's sustainability essentials package integrates ERP-centric, AI-enabled solutions within your existing SAP Cloud ERP processes to help maximize audit-ready ESG reporting, financial impact management in sustainability, and sustainability footprint optimization.

The following capabilities are already available to SAP S/4HANA Cloud customers.

SAP Sustainability Control Tower

SAP Sustainability Control Tower is an AI-enabled ESG reporting and sustainability performance management solution. By activating it, organizations establish a governed data foundation for automated ESG data collection, audit-ready disclosure, and AI-assisted report generation across multiple frameworks. Organizations can reduce reporting costs, manage compliance risk, and build stakeholder confidence from a single governed system.

  • Establish your governed ESG data foundation: Set up your organizational structure, configure regulatory frameworks, and collect ESG metrics from SAP and connected systems, with full traceability from source to published disclosure.
  • Generate audit-ready sustainability disclosures: Produce governed disclosures across IFRS S1/S2, ESRS, GRI, and other frameworks using AI-assisted report generation, automated change logs, and governed workflows that meet finance-grade assurance standards.
  • Manage and improve ESG performance: Track performance against targets, monitor ESG metrics across reporting periods, and use analytics to support planning decisions and produce verified disclosures that meet investor and stakeholder expectations.
  • Gain AI-enabled regulatory readiness: Tackle new and changing ESG regulations with the Sustainability Regulatory Readiness Agent.

SAP Sustainability Footprint Management

SAP Sustainability Footprint Management provides ERP-centric carbon management capabilities that support decarbonization at scale. This solution enables organizations to calculate emissions, connect sustainability data across systems, and incorporate carbon insights into day-to-day business decisions.

  • Calculate carbon footprints enterprise-wide: Automate corporate and product carbon footprint calculations with standardized methodologies and scale across thousands of products and suppliers.
  • Embed sustainability into existing ERP: Connect SAP S/4HANA Cloud data with supplier information, facility data, and external sustainability sources through automated integration and data acquisition capabilities.
  • Enable decarbonization decisions: Bring carbon insights directly into business processes to support sustainable procurement and carbon management strategies.
  • Turn environmental footprint data into reduction opportunities: Reduce carbon footprint more efficiently with an AI agent that can help decide where to focus sustainability efforts.

SAP Sustainability Data Exchange

SAP Sustainability Data Exchange helps organizations standardize how they share product carbon footprint data across value chains in a secure and scalable way. The solution supports greater Scope 3 emissions transparency by enabling collaboration with suppliers and customers while helping organizations improve the quality and consistency of sustainability data.

  • Share carbon footprint data across your value chain: Exchange actual carbon and packaging data with suppliers and customers while maintaining interoperability and data sovereignty across industries and business networks.
  • Support carbon reduction initiatives: Identify opportunities to reduce emissions, improve product footprint visibility, and use supplier-specific data to support more informed sustainability decisions.
  • Standardize sustainability data exchange: Simplify audits and improve data accuracy through standardized data models, workflows, and industry-aligned exchange frameworks.

FAQ

  • What is the sustainability essentials package from SAP?

    The sustainability essentials package from SAP is a set of ERP-centric, AI-enabled cloud solutions embedded into SAP S/4HANA Cloud that weave sustainability into your cloud ERP system. It helps organizations:

    • Streamline regulatory compliance and ESG reporting.
    • Access real-time sustainability insights that can improve decision-making.
    • Enable scope 3 product data sharing across their value chain.

    SAP's sustainability essentials package includes SAP Sustainability Control Tower, SAP Sustainability Footprint Management, and SAP Sustainability Data Exchange.

  • How do I get started with SAP's sustainability essentials package?

    Organizations can get started with SAP's sustainability essentials package in four simple steps:

    1. Understand the available sustainability solutions included with their existing license.
    2. Review product demonstrations.
    3. Schedule a solution walkthrough led by SAP.
    4. Contact SAP to begin activation.

    This approach helps organizations quickly realize value from their sustainability investment.

  • What is SAP Sustainability Control Tower?

    SAP Sustainability Control Tower is an AI-enabled ESG reporting and sustainability performance management solution. It delivers finance-grade ESG metrics and end-to-end traceability from a single governed data foundation, supporting audit-ready disclosure across multiple regulatory frameworks through AI-assisted report generation and governed workflows.

    Included in SAP's sustainability essentials package, it also enables organizations to track performance against targets, monitor ESG metrics across reporting periods, and connect sustainability data to business decisions on cost, risk, and capital.

  • How does SAP Sustainability Control Tower support ESG reporting and management?

    SAP Sustainability Control Tower supports ESG reporting by helping organizations:

    • Automate ESG data collection through governed, workflow-driven processes.
    • Manage finance-grade ESG metrics aligned with regulations and sustainability standards.
    • Generate audit-ready ESG report drafts using AI-assisted, workflow-driven reporting.
    • Produce disclosures that strengthen investor confidence and support access to sustainable finance markets.

  • What is SAP Sustainability Footprint Management?

    SAP Sustainability Footprint Management is a corporate and product carbon footprint calculation solution that calculates corporate and product carbon footprints at scale. It uses ERP and operational data to provide visibility into emissions across the enterprise, including products, facilities, materials, transportation activities, and supply chains.

  • How can SAP Sustainability Footprint Management help with carbon accounting?

    SAP Sustainability Footprint Management helps with carbon accounting by calculating, analyzing, and managing carbon emissions using both enterprise and supply chain data. Integrated with SAP Sustainability Data Exchange, it enables companies to:

    • Calculate product and corporate carbon footprints with greater accuracy.
    • Move from emissions estimates and averages to actual supplier-provided carbon data.
    • Improve Scope 3 emissions transparency across the value chain through standardized carbon data exchange.
    • Identify emissions hotspots and opportunities for decarbonization.
    • Support auditability and reporting with traceable carbon data, workflows, and sustainability analytics.

  • What is SAP Sustainability Data Exchange?

    SAP Sustainability Data Exchange is a solution that enables organizations to securely exchange product carbon footprint data across value chains using standardized data models and processes. Included in SAP's sustainability essentials package, it helps organizations improve Scope 3 transparency, strengthen supplier collaboration, and support more accurate sustainability reporting.

  • How does SAP Sustainability Data Exchange support Scope 3 emissions transparency?

    SAP Sustainability Data Exchange supports Scope 3 emissions transparency by enabling organizations to receive and share actual product carbon footprint data with suppliers and customers. By exchanging supplier-specific emissions information instead of relying solely on estimates, organizations can improve data quality and increase transparency across the value chain.

  • How does AI support SAP's sustainability essentials package?

    AI supports SAP's sustainability essentials package by helping reduce manual sustainability management tasks and improving data processing efficiency. AI also enables organizations to increase reporting automation and accelerate access to sustainability insights that can inform decarbonization strategies.

  • Can SAP's sustainability essentials package integrate with SAP ERP systems?

    Yes. SAP's sustainability essentials package is designed to work with SAP ERP environments, including SAP S/4HANA Cloud, enabling organizations to leverage existing business data and integrate sustainability information directly into operational processes and decision-making workflows.

  • What business benefits can organizations gain from SAP's sustainability essentials package?

    Organizations can gain the following business benefits by activating SAP’s sustainability essentials package:

    • Increase operational efficiency: Optimize resources and reduce waste, driving cost savings.
    • Centralize data and reporting: Streamline data collection and enhance sustainability tracking.
    • Improve sustainable sourcing and supplier management: Increase supplier transparency and reduce procurement-related emissions.
