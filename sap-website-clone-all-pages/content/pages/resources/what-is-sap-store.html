What types of solutions are available on SAP Store?

You can find the following types of offerings from SAP and its partners on SAP Store:

  1. Business essentials: Essential solutions for your business, published by SAP or SAP partners.
  2. Extensions and add-ons: Products, microservices, content, and add-on solutions that require functional prerequisites.
  3. API and technical components: Solutions that connect and enable data transmission between systems, such as application programming interfaces, technical components, and web services.
  4. Technology components: Offerings with a hardware component published by SAP or SAP partners.
  5. Services: Delivered via SAP global services and support, service partners, and business services.
  6. Training: Offerings related to SAP Education solutions and education offerings from other providers.

How SAP Store works

SAP Store helps customers find the right business solutions faster, and partners sell their solutions to SAP customers to expand their reach.

How does SAP Store work for customers?

SAP Store makes it easier for customers to find, try, and buy trusted solutions for their specific needs.

If you’re a customer looking for solutions, visit SAP Store and follow these steps.

  1. Make sure that the country selector at the top right of the page displays your current country.
  2. Type what you’re looking for into the search bar at the top of the page or scroll down the page to browse by category, browse by industry, or check out trending partner solutions.
  3. On the search results page, you can refine the results with filters like categories, industry, works with, solution type, publisher, certification, and trial availability.
  4. Once you’ve found a solution you want to purchase, select Buy now if it includes that option andlog in with your business account.
  5. For solutions with a Request a quote button, enter your information to connect with SAP or the partner directly to continue your purchase.

Many SAP Store solutions also offer free trials, so you can try before you buy.

How does SAP Store work for partners?

SAP Store allows partners to market and sell their solutions to SAP customers across the globe. The first step is to apply to become an SAP partner. SAP’s award-winning partner program provides you with the global brand authority, development technology, and direct customer access you need to build and deploy integrations within the SAP ecosystem. Once you’ve joined the partner program, you can log in to SAP Partner Portal and start listing your solutions on SAP Store.

SAP Store is for partners that  that design, develop, and commercialize extensions, integrations, and applications for SAP solutions. It’s the place where they can list their offerings and reach SAP customers.

Listings highlight relevant details such as industry, deployment model, and SAP product alignment, helping buyers discover solutions that fit their needs.

Offerings span six solution types:

  • Business essentials, which are solutionspublished by SAP or SAP partners.
  • Extensions and add-ons, including products, microservices, content, and add-on solutions that require functional prerequisites.
  • API, technical components, and web services that connect systems and enable data transmission.
  • Technology components, which include offerings with a hardware component published by SAP or SAP partners.
  • Services delivered through SAP Global Services and Support, service partners, or business services.

If you want to set up your listing for success, it’s important to focus on what your solution delivers, how it integrates with SAP, and why it matters to enterprise buyers. SAP can help you shape your message, guided by standards that ensure quality, consistency, and discoverability across the SAP Store.

Partners also get editorial support from SAP for every listing. A professional content team reviews your submissions to ensure clarity, consistency, and alignment with SAP Store standards. This ensures that your listing is well-positioned to resonate with buyers.

Your listing includes:

  • Detailed product description and documentation.
  • SAP validation for compatibility and quality.
  • Options for pricing models, trials, and sales path.

You control how customers engage, whether it’s by offering a free trial, enabling direct online purchases, or guiding them through a quote-based process. You can also integrate your listing into SAP Field Sales motions, helping you reach buyers through both digital and direct channels.
