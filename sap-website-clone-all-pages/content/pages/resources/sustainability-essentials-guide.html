What sustainability challenges are finance leaders facing?

In recent years, sustainability and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) priorities have evolved from a corporate initiative into a business necessity. Finance leaders are increasingly responsible for tracking, validating, and showing measurable sustainability progress while ensuring compliance with evolving regulations and disclosures.

Organizations across the globe are facing a growing list of regulations and reporting obligations, including the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), and IFRS® Sustainability Disclosure Standards (IFRS S1/S2).

Under IFRS S1 and IFRS S2, developed by the International Sustainability Standards Board (ISSB), organizations must disclose sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could affect enterprise value, including climate-related metrics such as Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions, energy consumption, and the financial impact of climate risks and opportunities. These standards apply the same financial materiality lens that investors and boards use for financial data. That means organizations must provide sustainability information that meets the same standards of accuracy, traceability, and governance as financial reporting.

Against this backdrop, organizations are asking CFOs to deliver much more than financial results. Today’s finance leaders are also expected to:

  • Provide integrated sustainability reporting for finance.
  • Support regulatory compliance.
  • Mitigate risks and manage rising compliance costs.
  • Improve investor confidence.
  • Quantify the financial impact and provide sustainability performance management.
  • Help guide business decisions that balance profitability and sustainability goals.

Successful CFOs are going beyond responding to external sustainability pressures. They’re proactively transforming their processes to offset costs, manage risks, and advance sustainability progress.

In this landscape, to execute their strategy, it’s critical for finance leaders to embed sustainability into their core business processes. That means tapping into the right sustainability solutions, whether it’s carbon accounting software or ESG reporting software.

The most effective sustainability management software allows leaders across the organization to bring their objectives together under one framework that addresses sustainability challenges holistically, accurately measures KPIs, and drives sustainable growth. That’s where an ERP-centric solution elevates sustainability from compliance to competitive advantage.
