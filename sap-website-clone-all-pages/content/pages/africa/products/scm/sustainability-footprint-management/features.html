ERP foundation and integrations

Seamlessly connect sustainability to your core business systems to scale data collection and influence decision-making.

Reuse SAP Cloud ERP data

Use existing business data on material flows, production activity, energy consumption or transport data, and connect any ERP system via public APIs.

 

Embed results into core business processes

Feed footprint insights directly into planning, procurement, and finance to enable sustainable business decisions.

 

Leverage the SAP Sustainability portfolio

Make use of direct integrations with SAP Sustainability Control Tower, SAP Sustainability Data Exchange, and SAP S/4HANA Cloud for EHS environment management to cover extended sustainability use cases.

Emission factor management

Improve footprint results and reduce manual effort by using AI-supported mapping, centralised emission factor management, and supplier-specific footprints.

Map emission factors with AI

Automate mapping of emission factors to ERP data, minimising manual effort and improving accuracy.

 

Increase the share of primary data

Collect footprints from your suppliers through integration with SAP Sustainability Data Exchange or SAP Ariba Sourcing or using push API.

 

Use pre-delivered lifecycle assessment data

Manage third-party content, such as 45,000+ pre-delivered emission factors from EMBER, GLEC, EPA and others, create custom emission factors, or upload your own datasets to one unified app.

Carbon footprint calculation at scale

Calculate product and corporate footprints for carbon and other environmental impact categories in one calculation designed for enterprise scale.

Product and corporate levels

Use calculation standards such as GHG-Protocol or PACT V3 to use for CSRD, IFRS, SBTi FLAG, Catena-X requirements, and more.

 

Advanced calculation engine

Use our advanced calculation engine covering energy flow modelling or complex production processes, set up allocation schemes and choose your calculation method.

 

Future-ready, beyond carbon

Build on a scalable model that can support broader environmental indicators, including emissions from land use, fossil fuels, and biogenic sources in a single calculation.

Watch the demo video

Carbon footprint analytics

Use intuitive visualisations and analytics tools to understand key emission drivers and enable better business decisions.

Tailored and flexible breakdowns

Drill into results on multiple levels and with individual focus like scope 1, 2, and 3 breakdowns and sold products or energy consumption.

 

Sankey diagrams for intuitive flow analysis

Visualise the breakdown of inflows and outflows to quickly understand how impacts accumulate across your value chain and organisation.

 

Actionable product fact sheets

Present product carbon footprints and their contributors across lifecycle stages in a detailed and intuitive product fact sheet.
