What is SAP Sustainability Control Tower?

SAP Sustainability Control Tower centralises sustainability and ESG data and performance, using embedded AI to support auditable compliance and embed sustainability into core business operations.

Build trust with your stakeholders and maximise the value of data through a centralised sustainability management solution that allows you to:

  • Capture ESG metrics with enterprise-wide data from SAP and third-party systems.

  • Generate ESG reports with metrics and content from SAP and our partners.

  • Comply with reporting standards such as ESRS and EU Taxonomy. 

  • Drive progress with sustainability embedded into core business processes.

Key features

Sustainability reporting foundation 

Configure, calculate, and manage ESG metrics using SAP-delivered or custom definitions. Incorporate the results of your materiality assessments into a reporting scope to translate regulatory requirements into sustainability metric selection.

Data sourcing and collection

Streamline your data sourcing from both SAP and third- party systems and efficiently gather and consolidate data across your organization.

Performance insights and business steering

Analyse ESG metrics with financial and operational data. Use interactive dashboards and granular filters to uncover trends, track progress and steer sustainability performance across business units and locations.

ESG report generation with AI

Generate ESG reports using prebuilt templates and AI-assisted drafting. Integrate with SAP or partner disclosure tools to tag, validate, and publish reports aligned with ESRS and other regulatory frameworks.
