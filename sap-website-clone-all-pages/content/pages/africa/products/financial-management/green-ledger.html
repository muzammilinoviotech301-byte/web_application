What is SAP Green Ledger?

SAP Green Ledger is a carbon accounting solution that integrates greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions data directly into your SAP ERP financials—giving every business transaction both a carbon and a financial dimension.

Manage your emissions profile with double-entry precision, transaction-level allocation and Joule’s immediate analysis so you can:

  • Post Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions data directly to your SAP Cloud ERP.

  • Allocate emissions to cost centres, profit centres and business segments.

  • Budget and forecast carbon alongside revenue, margin and cost.

  • Account for CBAM certificates and liabilities within your financial ledger.

Key benefits

Connect your business’s financial and carbon data

Collect, account for, and allocate financial and carbon data across profit centres, business segments and other financial dimensions with finance-grade controls.

Equip decision-makers with carbon insights

Go beyond compliance with an integrated cost-and-carbon view. Ask Joule to query data, generate reports and create charts in natural language.

Turn CBAM into auditable financial positions

Manage CBAM liabilities and certificates in the general ledger with fair-value measurement and audit-ready postings in SAP Cloud ERP.

Drive carbon strategy from your financial core

Identify where to reduce your carbon footprint. Reconcile business and environmental goals based on actual transactions in your ERP system.

Key features

Parallel carbon accounting ledger

Enable consistent carbon bookkeeping across the enterprise with a dedicated parallel ledger for GHG emissions that mirrors the financial ledger structure in SAP Cloud ERP. Emissions data is posted at the transaction level using the same general ledger framework.

Carbon cost settlement

Automatically settle carbon emission costs across cost centres, profit centres and legal entities. Configurable distribution keys determine how costs are allocated, allowing precise GHG accountability through standard SAP settlement processes.

 

Real-time emissions analysis

Gain real-time visibility into Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions with built-in analytics. Drill down from aggregated portfolio totals to individual emissions transactions using the same reporting infrastructure as financial analytics in SAP Cloud ERP.

Financial control of CBAM exposure

Recognise obligations as financial liabilities, manage certificate assets, revaluate costs as EU Emissions Trading System prices change and post financial impacts to the right accounts—from exposure through settlement.
