Frequently asked questions

Human Capital Management (HCM) involves practices and software to recruit, manage and develop a workforce. While HCM and HRMS are often used interchangeably, HCM goes beyond traditional HRMS by integrating talent management, workforce analytics and strategic decision-making. SAP HCM offers advanced tools for core HR, payroll, compliance and talent development for companies of all sizes.

Autonomous Human Capital Management (HCM) uses AI, machine learning and automation to streamline HR processes, reduce manual effort and improve workforce decisions. It automates routine tasks, surfaces predictive insights, powers self-service, personalises learning and careers and strengthens compliance. Explore the Joule agents and assistants for HCM that bring this to life.

A vendor management system is a cloud-based platform used to procure and manage temporary, contract or contingent workers. Most VMS applications integrate with other on-premises or cloud-based enterprise HR systems. VMS software helps enterprises gain clear insight into who is working for them, as well as where, why, when and at which rates.

Smaller companies can absolutely start small and the “enterprise-only” idea is a misconception. SAP GROW for HCM is built for midsize and growing companies with lean HR teams, letting you begin with core HR, time and payroll essentials and expand as you grow. The SAP HCM Base package is sold per user in blocks of 1 with a minimum order of just 1 user while the premium package has a higher 500-user minimum.

It’s a public cloud SaaS solution hosted, secured and updated by SAP, with no dependency on an existing SAP system, yet fully capable of seamless integration with SAP S/4HANA and other enterprise solutions. See deployment details.

SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors is SAP’s comprehensive, AI-powered recruiting and applicant tracking system. As SAP’s end-to-end recruiting platform, it seamlessly integrates with the SAP SuccessFactors HCM to enhance candidate sourcing, engagement and high-volume hiring processes. Designed to meet the needs of organisations of all sizes, SmartRecruiters combines advanced analytics, configurable workflows and AI-driven capabilities, such as the optional Winston conversational assistant, to deliver tailored hiring experiences and drive recruiter productivity.
