Frequently asked questions

An autonomous supply chain leverages AI, automation and real-time data to predict disruptions, optimise operations and automate decision-making across planning, sourcing, production and fulfilment. The goal is to improve resilience, efficiency and responsiveness while reducing manual effort and helping organisations adapt more quickly to changing demand and supply conditions.

A sustainable supply chain integrates ethical, environmentally responsible and profitable practices across the entire value chain, from sourcing and manufacturing to logistics, returns and recycling. It relies on end-to-end transparency and digital technologies to reduce waste, manage risk and support sustainability goals while improving resilience and performance.

Designing a sustainable supply chain starts with building resilient, transparent and data-driven networks that align business performance with sustainability goals. SAP recommends improving end-to-end visibility across suppliers and logistics; embedding sustainable design and circular economy principles; leveraging AI-driven insights; and using responsible sourcing practices to reduce risk, emissions and waste while creating long-term value.

Supply chain sustainability is important because it helps organisations improve resilience, meet growing regulatory requirements, strengthen supplier accountability and make better business decisions with trusted data. SAP emphasises embedding sustainability into core processes and using cloud ERP to connect environmental, operational and financial data, enabling organisations to reduce risk, increase transparency, track emissions and turn sustainability into a competitive advantage.
