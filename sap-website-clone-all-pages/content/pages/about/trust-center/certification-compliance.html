Customers remain responsible for determining their own CMMC scope, implementing required controls, and obtaining applicable certification or assessment.

EU

EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)

The Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) aims to increase the level of cybersecurity of software or hardware products with digital elements for consumers and businesses, including their remote data processing solutions. SAP will need to implement mandatory cybersecurity requirements covering the planning, design, development, and maintenance of such products with the intent of removing or mitigating vulnerabilities. Products need to be (self-) assessed for conformity and if successful will bear a CE marking. The CRA entered into force on December 10, 2024. The main obligations introduced by the act will apply from December 11, 2027, with reporting obligations to apply as of September 11, 2026.

The SOC 1 report covers all live customer systems during the audit cycle. It provides information about controls at a service-organization level that is relevant to the customer's internal control over financial reporting, known as IT general controls.

 

IT general controls cover:

  • IT strategy

  • Environment and organization

  • Logical and physical systems

  • Access controls

  • Program development

  • Change management

  • Computer operations such as incident management, backup, and monitoring

The SOC 2 report provides the management of a service organization, customers, and others with a report about the controls of a service organization that is relevant to the security, availability, and processing integrity of its system and the confidentiality and privacy of the data processed by that system.

 

While security is always assessed in each SOC 2 report, management may decide to scope in other trust services criteria: confidentiality, integrity, availability, and privacy.
