Glossary: Filter terms

  • Compliance Offering – Look for the ISO, SOC, or one of the various other compliance offering typed needed.

  • Compliance Entity – Scroll through the list of cloud solutions from SAP and related SAP services or functions.

  • Assessment Period/Issue Date – Select the period of the assessment or the issue date of the document.

  • Region/Country – Pick the country where the SAP data center is located.
