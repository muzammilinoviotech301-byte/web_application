Data center capabilities

Service capabilities

SAP data centers provide a comprehensive range of capabilities to support a wide range of business applications and workloads.

Cloud hosting and management

SAP data centers host cloud solutions such as SAP S/4HANA and SAP Business Technology Platform. and provide robust infrastructure management services to help ensure optimal performance and uptime.

Data residency and compliance

Globally dispersed data centers allow businesses to store and process data within specific geographic locations, meeting local regulatory requirements.

Scalability and flexibility

Businesses can adapt data center resources—extra storage, more computing power, or tailored configurations—to meet increasing demands.

Disaster recovery and backup

Advanced disaster recovery solutions help ensure data protection and recoverability in the event of an unforeseen incident.

Security and compliance measures

Security is at the core of SAP’s data center operations. We adhere to the highest industry standards to protect data from unauthorized access, breaches, and other security threats.

Monitoring and threat detection 24/7

Security operations teams at data centers utilize cutting-edge tools to detect and respond to potential threats in real time.

Compliance with global standards

Data centers comply with international standards and certifications, including GDPR, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 reporting.

Encryption and data protection

Robust encryption protocols for at-rest and in-transit data safeguard information from unauthorized access.

Access controls and audits

Only authorized personnel have access to critical systems and data while regular audits help ensure compliance with security policies and procedures.

Disaster recovery strategies

Minimized downtime and data loss help ensure business continuity in the face of catastrophic events.

Redundant systems and backups

Redundant power supplies, networking, and storage help prevent single points of failure. Additional regular backups are conducted so data can be restored quickly.

Geographic distribution

Data is replicated and distributed across multiple locations. In the event of a disaster at one site, data and services can be restored from another.

Automated failover

Automated failover mechanisms allow for rapid recovery of services with minimal manual intervention.

Comprehensive testing

Regular testing through simulated scenarios helps ensure readiness and effectiveness, providing peace of mind that data is protected and recoverable.

Sustainability

We operate our data centers to reduce environmental impact.

Energy-efficient infrastructure

Data centers are designed with technologies, such as advanced cooling systems and power management, to lower energy consumption and reduce carbon emissions.

Renewable electricity usage

Wherever feasible, data centers are powered by renewable energy sources, reducing overall carbon footprints.

Sustainable practices

Data center operations follow practices to recycle electronic waste, minimize water usage, and reduce reliance on nonrenewable resources.

Environmental certifications

We adhere to environmental standards, such as ISO 14001 and ISO 50001, to show a strong commitment to operating environmentally responsible data centers.
