About SAP Labs Network

The network comprises all of SAP’s major global research and development (R&D) hubs across more than 20 countries and represents more than 80% of SAP’s engineering workforce.

The art of being globally connected

SAP Labs exemplify our culture of diversity and innovation. Each lab partners with local institutions, universities, and startups to develop top-tier enterprise solutions and establish thought leadership in its focus area.

SAP Labs United States

Founded in 1996, SAP Labs US has championed design thinking, created award-winning user experiences, and brought deep cloud expertise to help customers become intelligent, sustainable enterprises. SAP Labs US advances SAP Business AI and delivers trusted, industry-grade capabilities embedded across SAP solutions. SAP Labs Canada

Operating since 1984 with locations in Montreal, Waterloo, and Vancouver, SAP Labs Canada is a global R&D hub with more than 35 years of expertise in analytics, database technologies, and customer experiences. SAP Labs Latin America

Since 2006, SAP Labs Latin America has been a key innovation hub, operating in Brazil and Mexico. Its culture of excellence is rooted in teamwork, accountability, and trust, earning regular recognition from Great Place to Work for a commitment to employee well-being and growth. Deeply committed to social responsibility, SAP Labs Latin America sheltered more than 50 employee families and led donation campaigns after Brazil’s devastating floods in 2024, reflecting the passion for making a difference where it matters most.
