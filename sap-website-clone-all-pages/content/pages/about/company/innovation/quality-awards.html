SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success

Celebrating customer success and excellence in software implementations

The prestigious SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success celebrate and recognize customers who have excelled in the implementation of their SAP software solutions by using SAP's ten quality principles to:

  • Effectively plan, execute and manage their implementations.

  • Achieve fast, low-cost implementations – and meet today’s demand for rapid time to value.

  • Simplify business processes and deliver significant business benefits to their organization.

Award Categories

Find out which of the award categories best fits your implementation profile.

All SAP customers with successful implementations of any SAP product can submit a nomination for the SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success as long as they have gone live and been in productive use between two and eighteen months prior to submission.  The project size, duration and effort as well as the solution(s) in scope should be considered in order to determine the most appropriate category.

Rapid Time to Value

Two of the three criteria should be met in order to apply in this category:

  • <  600 users.

  • <  7 months project duration.

  • <  1.000 person days estimated implementation effort *.

Business Transformation

Two of the three criteria should be met in order to apply in this category:

  • >  500 users.

  • >  6 months project duration.

  • >  900 person days estimated implementation effort *.

Artificial Intelligence Category

These two criteria should be met to apply in this category:

The project must have successfully implemented and gone live with at least one of SAP's strategic Business AI solutions.

Achieve quantifiable business outcomes by demonstrating measurable improvements in key performance indicators related to strategic alignment, decision making, efficiency, revenue, or risk management.

Eligibility and Criteria

If your organization is based in or has been served by one of the following SAP market units / geographies, you can apply for the SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success:

  • Austria

  • Central and Eastern Europe (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Georgia, Hungary, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Macedonia, Moldova, Montenegro, Poland, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Ukraine, Uzbekistan)

  • Germany

  • Italy

  • Switzerland

Entry and Evaluation Criteria

  • The SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success are open to all organizations with successful implementations of any SAP product.

  • The SAP implementation must have gone live and be actively used by the business or organization between 2 and 18 months before the award submission.

  • The implementation must include products from SAP’s solution portfolio where business users are impacted, whether deployed in the cloud or on premises.

  • Only projects that have not been nominated for the SAP Quality Awards for Customer Success in previous years are eligible. Customers must be served by an SAP market unit.

  • Only customers can apply. Implementation partners cannot apply on behalf of customers.

  • Customers can apply in several categories, provided the entries are for different SAP projects.

  • Each nomination may only be submitted in the SAP market unit that serves the customer or the main location for the implementation.

  • Applicants must be available for follow-up phone calls and potentially for a visit, if required during the evaluation process.

  • Winners must be available for interviews and press activities supporting the award events.

  • All answers on the questionnaire must be completed in English or German.

  • Changes to the applications will not be accepted after the submission deadline set by the market unit in which the nomination is submitted.

  • Adherence to quality principles: How does your nominated project address SAP's 10 quality principles? Applicants should demonstrate how these principles of quality have been realized, including the key performance indicators used. Guidelines on what to include have been provided for all 10 quality principles in the questionnaire.

  • Time and cost of implementation: What project initiatives were employed to reduce total cost of implementation (TCI), total cost of ownership (TCO), and implementation time?

  • Business benefits: What business benefits have been achieved as a result of the implementation?

  • Completeness of answers: All questions must be answered (in English or German.) to achieve the highest possible score.

  • Conciseness of answers: Answers should be as precise as possible with focus on how quality excellence was achieved in each area.

How to apply

  1. Click here to access the nomination portal (S-User required for logon).
  2. Download and preview the questionnaire (A German version will be available within the nomination tool). Instructions on how to fill the questionnaire is available in the nomination portal.
  3. Submit your nomination online.
  4. Access your draft or submitted nomination.
