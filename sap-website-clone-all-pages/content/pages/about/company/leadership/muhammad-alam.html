Muhammad Alam biography

Muhammad Alam is a member of the Executive Board of SAP SE. He leads the Product Experience & Advisory Board area and is responsible for driving AI-enabled product adoption and shaping a data-driven product strategy across SAP’s portfolio. The Product Experience & Advisory Board area accelerates the adoption of SAP innovations by embedding AI across products, delivering actionable telemetry, and generating customer insights across SAP’s Autonomous Suite and SAP Business AI Platform. By using data-driven insights into product usage and adoption, the organization informs product strategy, enhances customer value, and helps drive a more intelligent and connected SAP portfolio.

 

Before being appointed to the Executive Board, Alam served as president and chief product officer of the Intelligent Spend Management program. He was responsible for product strategy, product management, development, design, and cloud operations for direct and indirect procurement applications, external workforce, travel and expense solutions, and SAP Business Network.

 

Alam started his career with Ernst & Young LLP, where he led implementation projects for many ERP and business applications for some of the largest companies in the world. Before joining SAP, Alam spent 17 years at Microsoft, leading various teams in the Business Applications division, ranging from consulting and customer experience and product success to his most recent role as the corporate vice president of Dynamics 365.

 

In that capacity, he led product strategy, product management, development, and operations for the entire Dynamics 365 application portfolio, growing it into a massive multibillion-dollar cloud business.

 

Alam is a certified public accountant in the United States and has a bachelor’s degree from Morehouse College.
