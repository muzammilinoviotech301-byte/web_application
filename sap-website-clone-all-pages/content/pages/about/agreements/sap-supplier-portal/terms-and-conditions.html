Find the applicable terms and conditions when procuring services, deliverables, or goods to SAP SE and its SAP affiliates.

The general terms and conditions, in particular, contain relevant terms on warranties, liability, confidential information, and intellectual property, but also on the SAP billing process. Supplemental terms include, for example, the SAP Supplier Code of Conduct, SAP Supplier Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy, and SAP Standard Insurance Terms.
