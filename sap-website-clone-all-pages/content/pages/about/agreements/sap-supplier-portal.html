Speak Out at SAP

We rely on our employees and our ecosystem to assess every decision and action made on behalf of the organization to be legal and compliant with our Codes of Conduct and policies - as well as respectful towards people and the environment. SAP encourages you to speak out and report any breaches of the Codes, including concerns over potential violations relating to compliance, the law and/or regulations.
You can report your concern anonymously or by name. There are multiple ways of reporting a concern, so you can choose the method you are most comfortable with. All submissions and identities will be maintained confidentially to the greatest extent practical (in light of the need to perform an investigation and, if necessary, take action).
