How e-signature changed SAP 

Electronic Signing

Sign your SAP contract electronically

SAP is using SAP Signature Management by DocuSign® as a provider to support electronic signatures.

It is flexible, easy-to-use, secure and helps speeding contract execution.

Collecting signatures electronically helps us move away from paper-based processes and helps to contribute to a greener world.

How it works

As a signer, you receive an email notification from DocuSign® with a secure link to the document to be signed. When you open the link you can sign or choose one of the other actions. You can sign the document from anywhere, on any device.

Avoid spam filter issues

Please check that the email notification from DocuSign® (dse@eumail.docusign.net) does not get routed to your spam folder.

Legal Validity

DocuSign provides information about the validity of e-signatures around the globe:

eSignature Legality Guide 

In most countries, electronic signatures are allowed for commercial and many other types of contracts.

The EU has a new Legislation eIDAS since July 2016 which makes it legal in all its member states. If you want to know what the legal validity in your country is, please take a look at the DocuSign presentation.

Security

SAP Signature Management is compliant with leading standards for information security and service organizations, including the Digital Transaction Management standard (xDTM), ISO 27001, and Statements on Standards for Attestation Engagements (SSAE) No. 16.
