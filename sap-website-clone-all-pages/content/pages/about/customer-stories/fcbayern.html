The Challenge

A complex data landscape with siloed systems

FC Bayern’s global success is driven not only by its world-class players but also by more than 1,000 dedicated employees in Munich, New York, and Shanghai. As the club expanded internationally and deepened its connection with a growing global fan base, its business operations became increasingly complex.

 

Employees spent too much time on manual, spreadsheet-driven processes that were inefficient and prevented them from focusing on strategic priorities like recruiting, performance management, and employee development. Limited access to personnel data and the lack of self-service capabilities for employees, managers, and HR teams further slowed operations.

 

Many of these employees travel frequently, yet the expense process was anything but smooth. FC Bayern's outdated, non-integrated system made reimbursements cumbersome and offered little transparency into overall travel spend.

 

At the same time, expectations were rising—both in the stadium and around the world. With 75,000 fans at the Allianz Arena each home match and millions more engaging through digital channels, FC Bayern generated vast volumes of data from ticketing, merchandising, HR, and fan engagement systems. But this data was scattered across disconnected platforms, creating a fragmented and increasingly difficult IT landscape to manage.

 

FC Bayern needed an integrated solution to connect these operations, streamline processes, and power a unified digital experience.

At FC Bayern München, transparent HR IT supported people development processes create clarity, trust, and speed — so every teammate knows the why, the how, and the next step, turning shared ambition into consistent excellence on and off the pitch.
Christoph SteinbergDirector HR at FC Bayern München AG

The Solution

Driving business innovation with SAP Business Suite

At the heart of FC Bayern’s operations is SAP Cloud ERP, the digital core of SAP Business Suite. With SAP S/4HANA, the club gains real-time financial insights, continuous merchandising fulfillment, and optimized partner management to help ensure smooth logistics for kits, facilities, and the entire supply chain.

 

SAP Datasphere simplifies FC Bayern’s data landscape by integrating both SAP and non-SAP data in a unified, self-service environment. This helps the club gain deeper insights into areas such as finance and controlling, fan engagement, and scouting performance through SAP Analytics Cloud. By connecting payroll, financial accounting, stadium operations, and other systems, SAP Integration Suite improves both internal workflows and the fan experience on matchdays.

 

Together, these technologies form a unified, scalable enterprise powered by SAP Business Technology Platform—connecting finance, HR, supply chain, and customer data into a single, intelligent ecosystem.

Taking care of the team behind the team

 

FC Bayern utilizes SAP SuccessFactors HCM to manage its employee experience from hire to retire. By moving HR processes to the cloud, the club has modernized and continues to optimize critical functions such as employee self-service, recruiting, performance and goal management, onboarding, compensation, and learning. Embedded AI-driven capabilities will help them attract the right talent at the right time, on or off the pitch.

 

Smooth integration of SAP Concur into FC Bayern’s ecosystem reduces errors and speeds expense claims.

Delivering unique fan experiences

At the Allianz Arena, SAP Customer Checkout centralizes sales transactions, inventory, and customer information on one platform, allowing fans to focus on what matters most—the game.

 

FC Bayern’s passion for providing the ultimate digital fan experience extends beyond matchday. By consolidating data from 52 systems and more than 9.5 million records, the club created a single “golden fan record.” With SAP Engagement Cloud, this complete view of fan preferences and behaviors helps FC Bayern run highly personalized, omnichannel marketing campaigns to connect with fans on their preferred digital channels.

With employees supported and fans engaged off the pitch, FC Bayern relies on SAP Sports One to sharpen performance on the pitch. By utilizing SAP Sports One, the club coordinates administrative, training, and team performance management, and medical processes on one platform.
