The Challenge

Unlocking the power of data

Hockey is a fast-moving sport that generates a lot of data. Collecting, analyzing, and acting on that data enables the NHL to better serve its teams, engage with fans, and advance its business and sustainability goals.

 

Real-time game data helps teams, coaches, and players improve performance on the ice, while centralized player, league and club data sources support NHL Club front offices off the ice. Historical and current game stats also provide passionate hockey fans with a fun and educational digital experience. Beyond the game, operations and carbon impact data collected from its clubs and arenas help the NHL define new environmental standards and share key learnings across the League.

David LehanskiEVP, Business Development and Innovation, NHL
SAP technology helps us advance hockey in ways that are good for our teams, our Club's front offices, our fans, and the planet.
The Solution

Teaming up to support innovation on and off the ice

Enhancing the Game

In-game tracking technology captures the movements of the puck and players, every second of every game. Massive amounts of high-speed game data are processed in the SAP HANA Cloud database in milliseconds – and is pushed to other apps used by coaches, players, and fans to enhance their experiences.

 

During games, coaches and players use the SAP-NHL Coaching Insights mobile app to quickly see face-off wins per zone, player speeds, player time on ice, and more. This type of real-time game data was unattainable before and helps teams make tactical in-game adjustments on the fly.

 

Building on this powerful foundation, SAP also helps empower the NHL's strategic operations off the ice with the SAP-NHL Front Office App for iPad. This innovative app gives NHL general managers and hockey operations departments a unified view of critical data. The app centralizes information from multiple NHL sources, making it easier to manage roster planning, salary cap, and player contracts. Organized into League, Team, and Player views, the app provides comprehensive insights, enabling faster, more informed decisions that drive team performance.

 

Empowering Front Offices

SAP also helps empower the NHL's strategic operations off the ice with the SAP-NHL Front Office App for iPad and iPhone. Built on the SAP Business Technology Platform (SAP BTP), this innovative app gives NHL general managers and hockey operations departments a unified view of critical data at their fingertips – whether on the road or at the office.

 

The app centralizes information from multiple NHL sources, including official Central Registry data, making it easier to manage roster planning, salary cap, player contracts, draft analysis, and more. Organized into League, Team, and Player views, the app provides comprehensive insights, enabling faster, more informed decisions that drive team performance.

 

For the 2025-26 season, the app now includes playoff roster planning capabilities, allowing NHL hockey operation departments  to optimize rosters ahead of and during the Stanley Cup Playoffs. With new playoff salary cap rules taking effect for the first time in NHL history, the tool is also critical during key moments of the season, such as the NHL Trade Deadline and NHL Draft, when the future success of teams is defined, helping Clubs quickly capitalize on opportunities to compete at the highest level.

As you approach the Stanley Cup Playoffs, one move can not only change the result of a particular game, but the result of a season. These are high-stakes decisions that Clubs need to make, often in very little time. The Front Office App gives them the tools to make those decisions and maximize a Club’s chances for competitive success.

Bill Daly
Deputy Commissioner, NHL

Satisfying Fans

Game data also flows to the NHL.com/stats page, which is the go-to source for hockey analytics. The site runs on SAP HANA Cloud and combines real-time data with more than 100 years of historical stats. Last season, over 100 million data-hungry fans engaged with the site. Intuitive dashboards and filters make it easier for users to analyze the performance of their favorite teams and players, as well as legends from the past.

David LehanskiEVP, Business Development and Innovation, NHL
 At a high level, from an innovation standpoint, the NHL is focused on expanding and enhancing the game of hockey. For a big portion of that, we need to capture a tremendous amount of data. We need to process that data, distribute that data, and turn it into meaningful experiences.

Going Green

As a leader in the sports world, the NHL aspires to reduce its environmental impact on the planet. In 2010, the League launched its NHL Green initiative and became the first sports league in North America to voluntarily release two sustainability reports on its carbon footprint.

 

With the help of SAP, the NHL recently used SAP Business Technology Platform to create NHL Venue Metrics, an end-to-end, cloud-based platform designed to revolutionize how the League collects, analyzes, and reports on operations data across its clubs and arenas. The data will be processed through SAP HANA Cloud and visualized with the SAP Analytics Cloud solution. The insights gained will enable the NHL to define and embed sustainability best practices into its business operations and reduce its ecological impact wherever possible.

 

 In order for us to impact any change, we need to measure. NHL Venue Metrics is a perfect example of how we were able to expand SAP HANA Cloud to create a new sustainability solution for the League.
Omar MitchellVP, Sustainable Infrastructure and Growth Initiatives, NHL

Staying Ready for What’s Next

These strategic adoptions will help the League enhance its workforce management and streamline financial operations, further solidifying SAP's role as a comprehensive technology partner for the NHL's entire enterprise.

About the NHL

The National Hockey League (NHL), founded in 1917, is the second oldest of the four major professional sports leagues in North America. Today, the NHL consists of 32 member clubs, each reflecting the League’s international makeup, with players from more than 20 countries represented on team rosters.

About the NHLPA

The National Hockey League Players’ Association, established in 1967, is a labor organization whose members are the players in the National Hockey League. The NHLPA works on behalf of the players in varied disciplines such as labor relations, product licensing, marketing, international hockey, and community relations, all in furtherance of its efforts to promote its members and the game of hockey.
