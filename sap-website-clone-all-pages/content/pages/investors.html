Q3 2026 Financial Results

On October 21, 2026, SAP SE published its financial results for the third quarter 2026.

  • Current cloud backlog of €24.1 billion, up 28% and up 26% at constant currencies

  • Cloud revenue up 26% and up 25% at constant currencies

  • Cloud ERP Suite revenue up 29% and up 28% at constant currencies

  • Total revenue up 13% and up 12% at constant currencies

  • IFRS operating profit up 18%, non-IFRS operating profit up 18%, both at nominal and constant currencies

  • SAP updates its 2026 cloud revenue and cloud and software revenue outlook

SAP Completes Acquisition of Dremio

SAP today announced it has completed the acquisition of Dremio, an open, high-performance data lakehouse platform. The acquisition accelerates agentic AI and expands customers’ ability to combine SAP and non-SAP data to run analytical and AI workloads in real time, with no data movement or conversion necessary, and with vastly improved economics for enterprise analytics.

SAP Completes Acquisition of Prior Labs

SAP today announced it has completed the acquisition of Prior Labs, the pioneer of Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs). The acquisition will accelerate SAP’s success in TFMs that started with SAP-RPT-1 and bring one of the world’s leading TFM research teams into the SAP family.

May 28, 2026

SAP Successfully Places €3.5 Billion Eurobond

SAP SE, rated A1 (stable) by Moody’s and A+ (stable) by S&P Global, successfully completed a Eurobond transaction with a total volume of € 3.5 billion across four tranches with tenors of two, three, five and seven years. The net proceeds from this transaction are used for general corporate purposes, including (re)financing of recently announced acquisitions.
