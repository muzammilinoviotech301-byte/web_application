Frequently asked questions

  • What are SAP Cloud Application Services?

    SAP Cloud Application Services are SAP’s application management offerings, delivered as service subscriptions designed for SAP private, public, and hybrid cloud contracts. These services run your entire solution landscape with practical, proactive support to help facilitate smooth daily operations.

  • What kind of support and expertise can customers expect from SAP Cloud Application Services?

    Customers can expect application management support that includes:

    • An application service manager as the primary point of contact, working closely with customers.
    • A service desk that processes all requests and ensures they are handled within the agreed service times.
    • A service delivery team that handles incident resolution, problems, change requests, service requests, and activity monitoring.
    • 24/7 support from the service desk and the delivery team, depending on contract terms.

  • What are the benefits of SAP Cloud Application Services?

    SAP Cloud Application Services help ensure smooth operation of your SAP private, public, and hybrid cloud solutions. Customers benefit from SAP’s expertise and experience in running digital business in real time and can adopt cloud solutions more quickly and efficiently, increasing customer satisfaction.

  • How can SAP Cloud Application Services increase profitability?

    Using SAP Cloud Application Services can increase profitability by improving user experience, delivering clearly defined services at fixed annual prices, customizing SAP solution management to your specific requirements, and handling technical and functional services end to end. The services are available globally and can be adapted to meet regional, language, sovereignty, and industry requirements.

  • Who should use SAP Cloud Application Services?

    SAP Cloud Application Services are designed for businesses running SAP solutions in private, public, or hybrid clouds that want to strengthen their application management capabilities, improve operational efficiency, and redirect existing personnel to more strategic priorities—including analyzing and adopting SAP innovations.

  • How does SAP ensure data security and compliance in SAP Cloud Application Services?

    SAP Cloud Application Services are delivered by certified experts who follow global security frameworks. The offerings are designed to meet regional and industry-specific compliance requirements, including GDPR, GxP, and EU access, and support audit readiness and data protection best practices.

  • Which SAP cloud solutions are eligible for SAP Cloud Application Services?

    SAP Cloud Application Services support most application areas within SAP Business Suite, along with SAP Business Technology Platform, SAP Business Data Cloud, SAP SuccessFactors, and SAP Customer Experience. These services also support SAP Business AI, including Joule. Solutions deployed on hyperscaler-hosted and SAP-managed infrastructures are supported globally.

  • How do SAP Cloud Application Services work with success plans?

    Success plans and SAP Cloud Application Services are designed to complement each other across your cloud journey. Success plans provide advisory and activation services, including strategic guidance, defined objectives, and enablement. SAP Cloud Application Services focus on operations, delivering day-to-day application management and continuous optimization.

    Together, they create a comprehensive framework: Success plans help you plan and activate your SAP strategy, while SAP Cloud Application Services help ensure your solutions run smoothly in production, aligning strategic goals with operational excellence.
