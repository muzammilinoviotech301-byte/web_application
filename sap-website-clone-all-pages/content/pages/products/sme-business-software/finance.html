It unifies order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, and record-to-report in one solution, helping reduce manual work and reconciliation delays. Built-in controls and traceable data help teams stay audit-ready, while AI highlights exceptions early so finance leaders can act before issues escalate. The result is faster closes, better cash visibility, and the confidence to scale.

AI-assisted tools, including a Cash Management Agent, Receivables Agent, and Billing Assistant, are embedded across these processes.

SAP embeds AI directly into finance workflows rather than offering it as a separate tool. For example, the Cash Management Agent reconciles bank statements and forecasts cash positions in real time, while the Receivables Agent automates analysis to prioritize collections. Financial teams can analyze data through a conversational interface, AI-supported forecasting generates scenario-based financial planning, and allocation run results can be navigated and acted on directly. Together, these reduce the time finance teams spend on data gathering and allow for faster, more confident decisions.

SAP Cloud ERP supports automation across the full finance function. In order-to-cash, sales orders can be created automatically from unstructured data. In procure-to-pay, approvals, confirmations, receipts, and invoice processing are automated with assisted exception handling. In record-to-report, financial close tasks and reconciliations are streamlined. Accounts payable teams can resolve payment exceptions automatically, while intelligent audit checks expense reports for compliance.
