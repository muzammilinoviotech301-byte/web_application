Searchable company profile

Elevate your company’s image on SAP Business Network with a comprehensive, searchable company profile.

Show what makes your brand special with tools that help you

  • Demonstrate your corporate social responsibility to buyers looking to purchase from suppliers with certain specialties by uploading certificates to your profile.

  • Import your sustainability ratings from EcoVadis Inc. or another provider directly to your profile.

  • Share your organization’s human rights information with a standardized questionnaire that you can attach to your profile.

  • Increase your chances of generating leads that are specific to your business by completing your company profile to get discovered.

Order management

Get quick confirmations or changes and simple issuance of invoices and ship notices.

Streamline order management through capabilities that help you

  • Simplify collaboration on end-to-end processes to strengthen your relationships with customers.

  • Improve communication with customers through automated, real-time notifications when their orders have been placed, revised, or shipped.

  • Enhance cash flow through optimized order management that allows you to quickly fulfill orders, get paid faster, and reduce days sales outstanding.

  • Improve process visibility through interactive dashboards that provide up-to-date visibility into order status and details.

E-invoicing

Improve invoice visibility, greater processing flexibility, and on-time payment.

Say goodbye to postal delays and lost invoices with a digital process that allows you to

  • Reduce errors with automatic network validation processes that check for errors or missing fields and flag them for attention.

  • Process invoices faster by converting a variety of document formats into e-invoices and rapidly delivering them to your customers.

  • Improve productivity with a self-service portal that indicates invoice and payment status.

  • Get paid on time with faster, more accurate invoicing.

  • Reduce waste by moving invoicing processes online and decreasing energy and paper usage.
