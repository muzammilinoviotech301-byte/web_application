Frequently asked questions

Planning collaboration enables buyers and suppliers to digitally collaborate on forecasts and demand plans to provide trading partners with demand plan visibility and commit to forecasted requirements, helping ensure supply and delivery of critical materials and components.

Planning collaboration enables buyers to gain visibility into trading partner commitments to optimize constrained supply plans. This can help improve assurance of supply upstream in the supply chain, allowing for more complete and reliable delivery to downstream customers; move uncommitted volume to a secondary supply source quickly for better supply chain resilience; and reduce planning cycle and overall product lead time.

The SAP Business Network Planning Collaboration solution provides buyers and suppliers with a common view for real-time orchestration of supply plans. Because planners are alerted immediately for any under- or over-commitment to component demand, mitigating actions can be taken earlier in the planning cycle.

External manufacturing planning visibility enables complete supply chain visibility and equips organizations to work with multiple tiers of contract manufacturers and suppliers across key supply chain planning and execution processes. In addition, suppliers can report stock-on-hand quantities in their inventory so buyers can use this information to better manage strategic components and materials to proactively detect supply disruptions.
