What is SAP Business Network?

SAP Business Network is a B2B collaboration platform that enables companies to discover trading partners, share information and documentation, execute transactions, and build stronger supply chains.

No business does business alone

In the face of expanding product lines, proliferating compliance requirements, and reduced supply predictability, global supply chains have become increasingly intertwined and complex. The result is a sense of chronic uncertainty—exacerbated by regional conflicts, extreme weather events, global health crises, and economic instability.

 

This is why companies increasingly seek to build resilience into their supply chains. Key to achieving this resilience is efficient, effective collaboration that spans business, public sector, and non-profit organizations and extends across entire supply networks.

 

Successfully running a business in such an environment requires true digital collaboration—the kind that moves beyond e-mail, fax, and phone. The objective is timely information that enables companies to see across their supply networks, respond quickly to disruptions, and adjust operations as needed. With this level of collaboration, companies have the agility needed to survive and thrive. Without it, resilience is merely an aspiration.

 

SAP Business Network is designed to help companies make supply chain resilience a reality.

The world’s largest B2B trading platform

Global strategic sourcing extends the principles of strategic sourcing and applies them on a worldwide scale, allowing businesses to look beyond local vendors and tap into a more diverse supplier base.

 

SAP Business Network is the world’s largest B2B trading partner platform and supports over $6 trillion in annual transactions. It modernizes how procurement and supply chain processes are connected across companies to build stronger supply chains that consistently deliver on the customer promise. SAP Business Network closes the break points between buyers and suppliers by enabling transaction exchange, information sharing, and trading partner discovery, leveraging AI and configurable business rules. This helps companies gain greater visibility into their supply chains, increase operational efficiency, and ensure compliance.

Visibility, efficiency, and compliance: The pillars of SAP Business Network

By connecting critical areas of business collaboration—including procurement, asset management, supply chain, and logistics—SAP Business Network serves as a unified digital ecosystem where information flows seamlessly across organizational boundaries. Let's explore how these interconnected solutions drive efficiency, resilience, and sustainable growth.

Procurement collaboration: Digitalize sourcing and improve supplier performance

Procurement has evolved far beyond simply purchasing goods and services. Today's teams must navigate complex supplier relationships, ensure compliance, drive sustainability initiatives, and deliver strategic value to the business.

SAP Business Network Commerce Automation

The days of manual purchase orders, invoice reconciliation, and payment tracking are over. SAP Business Network Commerce Automation digitalizes the source-to-pay process, creating a seamless commerce experience between you and your suppliers. This solution enables you to automate routine transactions and improve visibility into procurement activities.

 

Customers typically see invoice processing times decrease from weeks to days. For suppliers, benefits include visibility into payment status, faster order responses, and more predictable cash flow, which helps you strengthen your supply chain resilience.

SAP Business Network Discovery

Finding the right suppliers—especially when entering new markets or sourcing specialized materials—takes time and effort. SAP Business Network Discovery transforms this process by connecting you with millions of potential suppliers across the globe.

 

This solution helps you identify qualified partners based on products offered as well as supplier capabilities, certifications, sustainability practices, and diversity status—all within a single platform. Procurement teams can diversify their supplier base to minimize risk while advancing corporate sustainability goals. When disruptions occur, you'll have alternative sources already identified.

Supply chain collaboration: Close visibility gaps and prevent disruptions

Global supply networks in many industries have become complex, characterized by external manufacturing and packaging as well as globally disaggregated sources of supply. Because these networks typically operate with disconnected systems and limited visibility, the result is often inventory shortages or excesses—while production schedules slip and customer deliveries are missed. Effective supply chain management requires not just visibility but true collaboration across your multi-tiered trading partner ecosystem.

SAP Business Network Planning Collaboration

SAP Business Network Planning Collaboration creates a secure environment where you and your partners share forecasts, production plans, capacity information, and supply constraints. Through collaborative planning, you can identify potential bottlenecks in advance and develop solutions—whether adjusting production schedules, qualifying alternative suppliers, or reallocating materials.

 

This synchronized planning reduces the "bullwhip effect" that traditionally amplifies demand variability in the upstream supply chain. Manufacturers see fewer production disruptions, suppliers benefit from more visibility into demand across multiple planning horizons, and customers receive more reliable deliveries.

SAP Business Network Supply Chain Collaboration

SAP Business Network Supply Chain Collaboration enables multi-tiered visibility into critical supply chain processes and data, including inventory levels and requirements, production status, material availability and quality, fulfillment status, and customer demand across multiple time horizons (long term, midterm, near term).

 

When potential disruptions such as supply and demand mismatches are detected, the system provides early alerts. This enables affected parties to address these issues promptly. Such a proactive approach allows you to identify and address supply chain issues before they impact production or deliveries.

Logistics collaboration: Reduce delays and improve delivery precision

Logistics operations often suffer from fragmented processes, limited visibility, and reactive decision-making. Many shippers still rely on manual processes to collaborate with their carriers and service providers. This requires users to exit out of core order and transportation management systems to log into disconnected, external portals. As a result, when shipments are delayed, companies struggle to obtain accurate information and coordinate effective responses across multiple parties. Overcoming these challenges requires seamless logistics collaboration throughout your transportation ecosystem.

SAP Business Network Global Track and Trace

SAP Business Network Global Track and Trace provides end-to-end visibility into shipments as they move through your supply chain. This solution connects shippers, carriers, freight forwarders, and logistics services providers on a single platform. There they can monitor shipment status, assess the impact of delays, and send and receive alerts about disruptions. The result is better on-time in-full order performance and enhanced customer satisfaction.

 

Shippers gain the predictive insight needed to mitigate and avoid disruption. When delays hit, you’ll have the real-time insights needed to take corrective action and keep deliveries on track.

SAP Business Network Freight Collaboration

While tracking shipments is crucial, optimizing freight operations begins much earlier with carrier selection, contracting, settlement, dispute management, and even gate and delivery slot scheduling at your warehouse. SAP Business Network Freight Collaboration streamlines carrier collaboration from tender to settlement with a global network of carriers while also streamlining deliveries through carrier self-booking of dock appointments.

 

Now, you can collaborate with your carriers through direct, digitally connected processes, streamlining your logistics and warehouse operations. Through digitalized carrier collaboration, shippers can reduce costs, gain agility, and improve sustainability through greater efficiency.

SAP Business Network Material Traceability

In industries ranging from pharmaceuticals to automotive to food production, knowing exactly where materials and components come from and how they've been handled isn't just good business—it's often a regulatory requirement.

SAP Business Network Material Traceability captures material and component flows across your supply chain, creating a digital thread that connects sourcing, manufacturing, assembly, distribution, and delivery.

 

For regulated industries, this streamlines compliance reporting and simplifies audit readiness. For consumer products companies, it can verify sustainability claims with traceable, partner-sourced data. And in the event of quality issues, it accelerates the identification of affected products across tiers of the supply chain, so you can quickly take remedial action.

Asset collaboration: Extend equipment life and cut service costs

Malfunction or downtime of critical assets can quickly lead to lost productivity, delayed deliveries, and customer dissatisfaction. Successfully managing assets requires a coordinated effort between original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), equipment owners, maintenance teams, and service providers.

SAP Business Network Asset Collaboration

SAP Business Network Asset Collaboration is a cloud-based solution that connects asset manufacturers, operators, and service providers on a unified platform. It enables online data sharing, collaborative maintenance, and streamlined workflows across the asset lifecycle. By facilitating seamless collaboration, it helps businesses reduce operational risks, improve efficiency, and extend the life of critical assets.

 

SAP Business Network Asset Collaboration breaks down barriers, optimizes asset performance, and fosters partnerships. The result is tangible value to everyone in the asset lifecycle, whether your goal is to improve service efficiency, reduce costs, maximize uptime, or explore new business opportunities.
