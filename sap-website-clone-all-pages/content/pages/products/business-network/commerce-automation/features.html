Supplier collaboration 

Collaborate with your suppliers digitally in a single workspace and gain end-to-end visibility.

Connect with a global network of suppliers to:

  • Transact and exchange documents and information such as purchase orders and invoices in near real time.

  • Optimize payment terms and early-payment discounts to improve cash flow.

Regulatory and business process compliance 

Configure business rules based on your organization’s processes and requirements to automate validation and improve compliance. 

Use automation and digital tools to:

  • Improve compliance through automated document validation based on your configured business rules.

  • Reduce manual effort through digital matching of invoices to POs.

  • Comply with regulatory requirements for e-invoicing.  

  • Enhance reporting with a verifiable audit trail of transactions and changes.

Automated procurement workflows 

Enhance efficiency with a fully digital process from order to invoice.

Standardize and automate workflows to:

  • Boost productivity, decrease operating costs, and free up workforce capacity.

  • Digitally exchange documents with suppliers across a business network.  

  • Reduce paper and energy usage and waste to lower your carbon footprint.

Supplier portal 

Enable trading partners to maintain their own supplier information and access invoice and payment status with an intuitive self-service portal.

Simplify collaboration on a digital platform to: 

  • Exchange ERP-generated documents with suppliers across SAP Business Network.

  • Streamline deployment with embedded SAP expertise and advisory services.  

  • Allow your suppliers to flip a PO received on the network to create an invoice, eliminating data entry and errors.  

  • Give suppliers a view into invoice status updates to reduce inquiries.  

  • Provide suppliers with integrated AI support to reduce invoice errors, respond to RFIs, and create product catalog descriptions.  

The capabilities offered by SAP Business Network allow CDW to integrate efficiently with customers, placing the company in a higher supplier tier in the eyes of our customers.
Gregory Palmer 
Senior Manager Digital Integrations, CDW Corporation

Invoice status portal and transaction visibility 

Give suppliers visibility into the status of invoices and payments, reducing the risk of delayed payments.

Enable a holistic spend view and self-service capabilities to:

  • Improve responsiveness and get immediate feedback on invoice status.

  • Allow suppliers to configure notifications to receive e-mails when their invoice status changes.  

  • Drive adoption for long-tail suppliers through self-service updates and invoice visibility.
