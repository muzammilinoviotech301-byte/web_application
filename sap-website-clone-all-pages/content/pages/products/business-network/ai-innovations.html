FAQ

AI can significantly enhance procurement processes by improving efficiency, reducing costs, and aiding better decision-making. It can analyze purchasing data to identify spending patterns, uncover cost-saving opportunities, and help optimize procurement strategies. AI can also automate contract analysis by extracting essential terms and conditions, ensuring compliance, and identifying potential risks and opportunities. By integrating AI into procurement, organizations can make more informed decisions and streamline processes for improved operational efficiency.

The following AI capabilities are available to suppliers that transact on Business Network:

  • Intelligent invoice conversion: Convert PDFs and image invoice files into Business Network-compatible invoice files.

  • Intelligent error detection: Receive AI-generated summary of invoice rejection reasons for rapid invoice correction and submittal.

  • Enhanced eesponses to discovery postings: Generate accurate responses to buyers’ requests for information and proposals

  • Product catalog generation: Generate accurate responses to buyers’ requests for information and proposals
