What are the core platform capabilities?

Core platform capabilities enable organizations to standardize, secure, and scale across application lifecycle management (ALM), security, interoperability, and administration and operations.

As enterprise demands evolve and complexity grows, these capabilities deliver integrated services that enable your business to:

  • Standardize operations across apps, infrastructure, and connectivity.

  • Secure apps with identity, compliance, and centralized control built in.

  • Scale solutions across landscapes without added complexity or rework.

Key features

Application lifecycle management (ALM)

ALM functions within core platform capabilities help organizations gain oversight of an application’s entire journey, from development and testing to deployment and monitoring. Continuous insights into application health and usage patterns, enhanced with AI-driven analysis, enable proactive maintenance.

Security

Centralized identity and access management protects critical business data in every corner of an organization. A consistent approach to authentication, authorization, and role-based access, strengthened through AI-powered threat detection, reduces disconnected security protocols.

Interoperability

Rapid connections enabled by the core platform capabilities for hybrid or multi-cloud environments and across SAP and non-SAP systems empower businesses to adapt and act quickly. AI-assisted integration insights help optimize connections and accelerate time to value.

Administration and operations

Core platform capabilities for admin and ops start with simple platform setup and configuration. Standardized tools and centralized controls across hybrid and cloud-native environments, combined with AI-driven automation and monitoring, reduce complexity and improve operations.

Frequently asked questions

The core platform capabilities are essential services and infrastructure that support SAP Business AI Platform. They’re the underlying elements that help make SAP Business AI Platform capabilities secure, scalable, and manageable across SAP and complex non-SAP enterprise environments. Core platform capabilities are designed to operate in multi-cloud and hybrid environments, so enterprises can deploy and manage applications wherever they make the most strategic and operational sense.

To access SAP Business AI Platform and use its core capabilities, you’ll need to set up an account in the SAP Discovery Center. From there, access the SAP BTP cockpit, enable and configure your services. You can then explore guided missions to walk through real-world use cases using the platform services and foundation capabilities.

The core platform capabilities include infrastructure, tools, and governance services to build, integrate, and manage applications. They’re broken down by how they’re used and where they operate, including instance-based services consumed through APIs for developers building custom applications, or subscription-based services that run independently with their own UI and are ready to use.

No, core platform capabilities are not strictly required to use every SAP Business AI Platform service-but they’re essential for a consistent, secure, and scalable experience across SAP. Some subscription or standalone services can be used independently, but organizations building or extending applications, integrating systems, or managing hybrid environments benefit from the core platform capabilities being involved.

All core platform capabilities are designed to meet the rigorous demands of enterprise-grade cloud operations. SAP approaches security and compliance holistically with protection embedded into every layer of the platform—from the platform infrastructure to application development to operations.
