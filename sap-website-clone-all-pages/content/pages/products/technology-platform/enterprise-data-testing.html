The SAP Enterprise Data Integrity Testing solution by Tricentis provides automated data testing that helps maintain the accuracy of your data – wherever your data travels and whatever happens to it on its journey.

  • Cloud deployment
  • Comprehensive data testing across SAP and third-party technologies
  • Low-code and no-code automation that reduces the effort to create custom tests
  • Prescreening, reconciliation, profiling, vital, logics, and a variety of other tests

Key benefits

Take back your time, money, and resources

Spend less time figuring out whether your data is accurate or fixing the latest unexpected data discrepancy, so you can spend more time creating value for your business.

Cut costs related to process inefficiencies and compliance issues

Leverage automation to reduce manual testing processes and monitor compliance data to improve your anti-money laundering efforts.

Fill the gaps in your data management software landscape

Test data from SAP and third-party solutions, regardless of data type, source, or format, and use powerful data testing capabilities to fill problematic data management gaps.

Key features

Extension of data management software

  • Complement your existing data quality and data testing solutions by providing automated error checking to test greater volumes of data at faster speeds.
  • Compare data between the source and target and automatically notify the right users if data instances don’t match.

Data testing whenever and wherever you want

  • Test data at virtually any step of any process, from the integrity of data fed into the system to the accuracy of the integrations, transformations, and migrations.
  • Facilitate the early detection of data errors, including missing values, duplicates, incorrect data formats, and more, with prescreening functionality.

Improved data validation and business processes

  • Validate data migrations to solutions and platforms such as SAP S/4HANA.
  • Enhance a wide range of processes, such as monitoring compliance data and validating data, through ready integration with other solutions.
