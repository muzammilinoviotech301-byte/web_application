What is SAP Integration Suite?

SAP Integration Suite, part of the SAP Business AI Platform, is a modern, secure integration platform as a service (iPaaS) and enterprise integration platform that connects AI agents, applications, data, and processes across SAP and third-party environments.

SAP Integration Suite empowers businesses with:

  • AI-ready, event-driven integration at scale.

  • Thousands of prebuilt integrations and accelerators.

  • AI-assisted discovery and development tools.
