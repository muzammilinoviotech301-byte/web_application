What is SAP Customer Identity and Access Management?

SAP Customer Identity and Access Management uses AI-enabled identity protection and compliant consent management to turn customer data into actionable insights.

Deliver comprehensive identity management and adaptive access control with a solution that allows you to:

  • Capture first-party data and manage consent in line with global standards.  

  • Unify global access with rule-based controls for regions, brands and groups. 

  • Grow confidently with a scalable solution built for enterprise expansion.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Customer Identity and Access Management is a cloud-native solution for managing digital identities, user access, consent, and data privacy compliance. It helps organizations authenticate users securely, manage consent records, and meet international regulations such as GDPR and ISO. Our solution supports both B2C and B2B use cases and is built to scale across global digital channels.

SAP Customer Identity and Access Management is designed for organizations that manage customer and partner identities across websites, mobile apps, and in-store systems. It supports businesses of all sizes that need to deliver secure, seamless access, manage user consent, and meet evolving privacy requirements across global markets. The solution supports both high-volume consumer traffic and complex B2B access needs.

SAP Customer Identity and Access Management offers centralized tools for capturing and managing user consent across regions and digital channels. It includes audit-ready logging, version-controlled records, indirect consent capture, and configuration support for global regulations like GDPR, ISO, and CCPA. These features support transparent, compliant data practices at scale.

SAP Customer Identity and Access Management’s  enterprise-grade security features include passwordless login methods such as passkeys, one-time passwords, and magic links, as well as risk-based access controls and real-time fraud detection. All features work across web, mobile, and in-store environments to reduce fraud, minimize password use, and strengthen account protection.
