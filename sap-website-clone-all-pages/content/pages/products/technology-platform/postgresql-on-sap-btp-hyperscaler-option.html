PostgreSQL on SAP BTP - hyperscaler option

Create and consume relational databases with PostgreSQL based on hyperscalers

Using the PostgreSQL, hyperscaler option service, you can provision, manage and consume PostgreSQL databases on hyperscalers (AWS RDS PostgreSQL or Azure Database for PostgreSQL) from within SAP Business Technology Platform and bind them to business applications running on the Cloud Foundry environment.

Benefits

  • Fully managed database service

    Consume hyperscaler PostgreSQL databases, fully managed by SAP and integrated into SA Business Technology Platform experience.

  • Secure data access

    Consume PostgreSQL databases provisioned on hyperscalers with a secure networking setup designed for private and low-latency access.

  • Flexible scaling

    Scale your PostgreSQL databases flexibly, based on application compute or storage requirements.

Features

View media gallery

Managed backup and restore

Turned on by default, automated backups ensures timely recovery of the PostgreSQL database instance from any unfortunate system crash, within the retention period of 14 days.

High availability and fault tolerance

With Multi-AZ deployments automatically provision and manage a HA pair with a standby replica in a different Availability Zone and protect your databases against unplanned outages with automatic failover.

Flexible service plans

Benefit from parameterized, configurable service plans which support a ‘land and grow’ approach for independent scaling of database compute and storage.
