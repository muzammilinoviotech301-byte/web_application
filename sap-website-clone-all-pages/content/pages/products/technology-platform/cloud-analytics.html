Frequently asked questions

SAP Analytics Cloud unifies analytics and planning in one analytics and business intelligence (ABI) solution. Business users can build dashboards, run budgets and forecasts, and simulate scenarios on the same governed data, then act on the results without switching tools. It is the analytics and planning layer within SAP Business Data Cloud.

SAP Analytics Cloud is a key component of SAP Business Data Cloud, offering advanced analytics and planning capabilities that contribute to the unified knowledge core and serve as a foundation for its data fabric architecture.

 

Existing customers using SAP Analytics Cloud as a standalone solution can continue without interruption. However, you also have the option to transition your current tenant to a fully managed solution with SAP Business Data Cloud.

Agentic analytics is an AI-driven approach to analytics where AI agents are applied across the data-to-insight workflow, orchestrating analysis tasks semi-autonomously toward a stated goal rather than waiting for a user to build each chart.

 

SAP Analytics Cloud is the foundation for agentic analytics within SAP Business Data Cloud. It grounds  every analysis in its semantic layer, so AI agents and business users work from the same governed definitions and the business can trust the answers. Through Joule, users can access agentic capabilities in SAP Analytics Cloud, including:

 

• Story generation.

• Model creation.

• Planning cycle management.

SAP Analytics Cloud combines analytics and planning in a single cloud solution, allowing you to move from insight to action, simulate scenarios, and generate plans from predictions to drive agile decisions.

 

SAP Analytics Cloud supports smooth integration into SAP applications and data sources to deliver analytics with full business context, embedding analytics at the point of decision.  

 

SAP Analytics Cloud comes with more than 100 prebuilt business content packages across lines of business and machine learning capabilities to accelerate insights and decision-making.

SAP Analytics Cloud can connect to a wide range of on-premises and cloud data sources, including SAP HANA, SAP S/4HANA, SAP BW/4HANA, SAP BusinessObjects solutions, SAP Business Planning and Consolidation, SQL, OData, Google BigQuery, and many more. Users can connect to on-premises sources without moving or replicating data to the cloud.
