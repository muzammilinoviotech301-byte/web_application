People data is needed for both

For both operational and strategic workforce planning, accurate people data is a must. With workforce planning solutions from SAP, you can streamline the flow of people data directly from the source while minimizing errors due to manual transfer processes.

Operational workforce planning

  • Focuses on costs and budgeting for targeted headcount, new hires, and contingent workers

  • Involves detailed planning at the employee level for tracking absences, adjusting employment levels, and more

  • Uses position templates to speed new position planning and analytical tools to aid decision-making

Strategic workforce planning

  • Focuses on long-term planning over multiple years to help the business realize its goals

  • Involves cost and risk assessments for critical job roles based on data from multiple sources

  • Uses data to analyze supply and demand signals, anticipate talent gaps, guide investment, and recommend action
