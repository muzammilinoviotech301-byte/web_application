What is SAP Content Management Core by OpenText?

SAP Content Management Core is a solution that integrates with various software suites to deliver content to users based on their roles and business processes.

Enhance efficiency, compliance, and collaboration through tight integration and robust content management. Take advantage of:

  • Software-as-a-service deployment

  • Accelerated cloud ERP adoption

  • Improved productivity through process-centric collaboration

  • Quick adaption to changing compliance requirements

Learn more 

Benefits

Greater productivity and information flow

Minimize content sprawl and risk with controlled content sharing across SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Office 365, and other software suites.

Improved collaboration and overall efficiency

Shorten cycle times by making content easily accessible to stakeholders inside and outside your organization.

Enhanced corporate record management

Reduce legal risks by managing retention, disposal, and compliance reporting policies.

Features

Document management

  • Manage internal and external content access and link users to business processes.

  • Ensure up-to-date content accuracy with version control and audit trail functions.

  • Find content quickly and identify authors and stakeholders through classifications and metadata.

Records management

  • Equip records administrators to develop, deploy, and track retention and legal hold policies.

  • Facilitate content qualification and disposal with destruction certificates.

  • Provide compliance reporting on policy distribution across applications.

Microsoft Office 365 integration

  • Ensure documents are always up to date with real-time co-authoring.

  • Allow Outlook users to save emails and attachments directly into SAP Content Management Core.
