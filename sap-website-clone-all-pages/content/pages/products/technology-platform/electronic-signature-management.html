What is SAP Signature Management by Docusign?

Signature Management by Docusign is a standards-based application that helps organizations electronically manage documents securely from any device.

Streamline your digital transactions with e-signature management with:

  • An intuitive signing experience on any device

  • Digital signature and approval processes

  • Visibility into document and signature status

  • Enterprise-grade security and compliance controls

Key benefits

Increase efficiency

Automate workflows for everything from simple agreements to the most complex transactions—from purchase orders, to master service agreements, to RFPs.

Enhance mobile accessibility

Send, review, and sign documents on an application that’s fully optimized for mobile devices.

Strengthen integration

Boost productivity with seamless integration with common document authoring, management, and identity management systems.

Key features

Eliminate process friction and manual tasks with a fully digital and mobile solution that improves customer experience.

Human resources

  • Automate the entire HR agreement process, from hire to retire, providing a better employee and candidate experience

  • Gain deeper visibility into agreement statuses for candidates throughout the hiring process

  • Securely manage HR documents that contain critical or sensitive information

Procurement

  • Meet leading information security and service organization standards (e.g., ISO 27001 and SSAE 16)

  • Encrypt confidential documents and make them tamper-evident

  • Enable electronic signature for contracts on SAP Ariba Contracts or SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, enterprise contract management

  • Eliminate friction in your source-to-pay agreement processes

Customer engagement

  • Automate agreement workflows to move business forward

  • Manage and track document distribution and signing in the cloud; e.g., enable sending spend management documents for signature via web app

  • Obtain fully secure, legally compliant, and accessible digital signatures worldwide

Workforce management

  • Send, sign, and return vendor agreements within minutes

  • Enhance security and compliance with trusted, built-in protections

  • Enable electronic signatures for onboarding and offboarding paperwork, NDAs, and SOWs using SAP Fieldglass solutions
