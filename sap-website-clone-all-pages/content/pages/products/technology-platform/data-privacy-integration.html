Streamline Data Processing and Privacy Requests with Our Comprehensive Services

SAP Data Privacy Integration service helps applications realize their data privacy functions to ensure personal data is processed in a compliant manner based on valid business purposes. The service performs data deletion, purpose management, and retrieval of personal data.

Benefits

  • Legitimate processing of personal data

    Establish legitimacy of personal data processed by associating application data with configured business purposes.

  • Timely destruction of personal data

    Ensure data subject data is deleted on end of purpose based on configured residence and retention rules.

  • Transparent processing of personal data

    Trigger retrieval and export of personal data across all subscribed applications.

Features

View media gallery

Manage Business Context

With the Business Context Manager, you can define the business context for the storage and processing of data and tag data with a business purpose. You can manage business purposes for application data by defining legal grounds, data controllers, business processes and sub-processes for processing personal data.

Delete Data Subject Information

Trigger deletion of data subject personal data based on the residence and retention rules created for the data subject in the context of an application.

Retrieval of Data Subject Personal Data

Query personal data across all subscribed applications. Trigger export or correction of personal data.
