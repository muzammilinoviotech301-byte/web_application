Frequently asked questions

Business processes work better when they’re automated. Enterprise automation refers to the use of software to automate and streamline business processes within an organization. The goal is to optimize operational efficiency, reduce costs, minimize error, enhance productivity, and deliver outstanding experiences.

Enterprise automation software refers to business applications and specialized automation technologies that enable organizations to automate, monitor, analyze, and improve business processes. These vary in terms of functionality and features to meet different process automation requirements.

 

Some common types of enterprise automation software include:

  • Business applications, also known as business software or enterprise applications, to support and enhance various business functions and processes within an organization.

  • Robotic Process Automation (RPA) to automate repetitive and manual tasks within a process.

  • Workflow management to create and manage structured digital processes that follow defined flows, variants, and rules.

  • Business AI, trained on industry-specific data and business process expertise, to enhance processes with intelligent automation, insights, and decision-making.

  • Application and data Integration to connect and automate business processes across applications, departments, and stakeholders and to leverage data from various sources.

  • Business process management (BPM) to monitor and analyze processes, to benchmark performance and identify improvement opportunities, and to make data driven decisions on transformation projects.

Spend management solutions, like SAP Ariba, are used to automate and actively manage spending processes. One typical example is the source-to-pay (S2P) process, which encompasses finding, negotiating with, and contracting the supplier of goods, and culminates in final payment for those goods.

 

Robotic Process Automation (RPA) with embedded AI capabilities, like SAP Build Process Automation, can be used to automate the processing of paper-based invoices within a S2P process. The bot extracts PDF invoices from supplier emails, classifies the extracted documents so only invoices are processed, extracts the relevant invoice information, and enters it into SAP Ariba for further processing.

 

Application integration and cross-system workflows can be leveraged to automatically trigger purchasing processes in SAP Ariba. For example, the shared service team creates a profile for a new hire in SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central. Based on defined business rules, this event automatically triggers a workflow to order the relevant equipment for that job profile in SAP Ariba and ship it to the new hire’s office. No further intervention from the shared service center team required.

A comprehensive enterprise automation platform encompasses three tightly integrated components:

  • Business applications to standardize on proven best practice business processes

  • Automation technologies to increase automation rate, support specific requirements, and create differentiating innovations

  • Business process management solutions to monitor, analyze, and benchmark process performance to define process improvements

Standardizing on proven best practices ensures reliable operations and cost control for your business-critical, high-volume processes. Increasing the automation rate drives efficiency and increases first-time right rates. Data-driven process monitoring and analytics, combined with the capabilities to benchmark performance and define improvement steps, enables continuous process improvement.

 

Combining these three components enables the automated enterprise with highly efficient core processes and differentiating processes that enable a competitive edge and deliver outstanding experiences.

These may include:
