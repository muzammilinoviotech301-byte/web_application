What is SAP Build Work Zone?

SAP Build Work Zone is an AI-enhanced low-code tool for creating a central entry point for business users with personalized, role-based access to SAP and third-party applications.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Build Work Zone is a tool that empowers business users to build and personalize digital workspaces. It provides secure, centralized access to SAP and external apps, processes, and data on any device, helping to drive productivity and engagement. Engage with both internal teams and external stakeholders using interactive workspaces, guided experiences, and knowledge sharing to maximize efficiency.

  • Increase efficiency and productivity of individual users and teams working with SAP apps, processes, and self-services.

  • Empower lines of business to smoothly build business sites (create content and publish information without IT).

  • Boost people's engagement and establish interactive communities.

  • Foster active knowledge sharing across the enterprise.

  • Provide a great onboarding and learning experience.

SAP Build Work Zone will unify SAP Launchpad service and SAP Work Zone into a single low-code business site development solution.

The SAP SuccessFactors Work Zone solution remains unchanged.

In digital workspaces, Joule allows users to access relevant apps, tasks, and processes quickly from one central place. Users can also query specific knowledge sources with document grounding capabilities or create new documents and summarize them using generative AI assistance. AI-generated responses based on SAP data from digital workspaces help employees save time and stay productive.
