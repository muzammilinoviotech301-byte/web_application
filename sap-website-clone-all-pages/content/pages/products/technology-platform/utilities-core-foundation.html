Streamline DER and prosumer data management with SAP Utilities Core foundation

Automate and standardize Distributed Energy Resources (DER) data and installations and ensure accuracy by integrating seamlessly with SAP S/4HANA Utilities and SAP Business Technology Platform.

Benefits

  • Standardize and automate processes across divisions

    Streamline installation, modification, and decommissioning of metered assets, reducing manual effort and ensuring consistency across divisions for all DER operations.

  • Improve data quality and accuracy across systems

    Built-in validations and automated workflows prevent manual errors, ensuring reliable and high-quality metering and prosumer data across systems and connected third-party solutions.

  • Integrate seamlessly across systems and applications

    Integrate seamlessly with SAP S/4HANA Utilities and SAP Business Technology Platform, empowering event-driven communication and efficient data exchange with external and third-party applications.

Features

View media gallery

Easily define and visualize measurement concepts

Define and visualize measurement concepts through network diagrams using an intuitive interface, simplifying complex metering structures and enhancing usability for utilities professionals.

Support agile operations with event-based architecture

Support agile, automated, and scalable operations using SAP Event Mesh and open OData APIs to enable real-time, event-driven integration between SAP and third-party systems.

Streamline processes and ensure consistent data

Easily integrate with SAP S/4HANA Utilities using the measurement concept management add-on to streamline processes and ensure consistent master data across both business and technical domains.
