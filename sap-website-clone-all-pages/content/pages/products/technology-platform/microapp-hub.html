Access all available SAP micro-apps to get the most from your SAP solutions

Enjoy a large variety of industry-specific enhancements based on experience from thousands of projects with just a single subscription. Sign up to use SAP Micro-Apps in your production environment. Deployment is fully automated, so you can get started immediately.

Benefits

  • Time and cost savings

    Get jump-started with access to ready-to-use micro-apps without the need for costly, highly skilled resources, up-front costs, minimum usage requirements, or financial commitments, and without time delays.

  • High quality and security

    Benefit from the experience of thousands of projects made available by best-of-breed industry-specific enhancements for your current SAP solutions, offering the same level of quality and built-in security as any standard SAP product.

  • Improved agility

    Accelerate and simplify business tasks by leveraging the latest features and functions of easy-to-use, industry-specific future SAP enhancements immediately as they become available.

Features

View media gallery

Proven integration and security

The SAP Micro-App Hub provides a repository of tried and tested solutions to extend core SAP applications. Easy to deploy, integrate, and manage. It’s ideal for customers wanting to adopt new industry-specific enhancements quickly while still meeting requirements for standard APIs, authorization, and security.

Flexible consumption model

With a flexible consumption model, the fees charged are based only on the usage of individual micro-apps. You only pay for what you are getting value from.

Includes support and maintenance

Any solution requires maintenance and support to ensure all components are up-to-date and bug-free.​ Micro-apps require little or no deployment activities, ​have the same level of quality as any other SAP products, and are supported and maintained by SAP.
