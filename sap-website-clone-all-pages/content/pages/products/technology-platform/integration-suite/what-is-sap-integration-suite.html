Faster barrier-free innovation, collaboration, and results

Support diverse business use cases with ease

Imagine the possibilities when you connect everything with SAP Integration Suite. You can accelerate connectivity to SAP and third-party applications with prebuilt integration flows, connectors to hundreds of systems, thousands of APIs, and AI-assisted capabilities that simplify integration. In addition, you can also accelerate time to value across diverse use cases.

 

Applications: Integrate and automate processes

When different technologies, data formats, and protocols prevent you from easily integrating various applications, it limits the flexibility, scalability, and security of your IT landscape. SAP Integration Suite streamlines how you integrate applications and automate processes using built-in connectors, adapters, and AI-enabled features. As a result, you can access real-time data from applications for continuous information sharing and reduce disconnected processes and redundant, error-prone data entry. You can also create personalized customer experiences across digital touch points, as well as streamline workflows, connections to partner systems, and communications for greater operational agility and efficiency.

 

Business partners: Enable B2B integration and collaboration

Collaborating with business partners can grow your market reach and lead to new revenue streams. However, diverse system requirements and message exchange protocols can complicate B2B information sharing. With SAP Integration Suite, you can create an efficient, more secure, and scalable B2B platform for quickly connecting and communicating with your ecosystem of trading and service partners. By automating processes and communications with partners, you can maximize ecosystem efficiency, reduce costs, and accelerate revenue opportunities.

 

AI agents: Improve efficiencies across your business

AI agents can autonomously run complex tasks—but to do so, they need real-time access to data and IT ecosystems. Integration Suite facilitates the AI adoption process by providing the connectivity AI agents need to excel. Developers can build and manage API connections through the suite or pull from its extensive library of clean, secure APIs purpose-built for agentic AI. A robust event-driven architecture also ensures that real-time signals can trigger agents into action—across SAP and non-SAP landscapes. These features enable AI agents to retrieve and process data instantly and coordinate workflows across different systems.

 

APIs: Manage, protect, and monitor your connections

It can be difficult to manage large numbers of APIs in a secure, centralized environment. The API Management capability within SAP Integration Suite simplifies API management by providing one centralized environment to create, register, publish, and manage API connections between applications and systems, enforce security policies, monitor performance, and more. Efficient API management helps you create simple, connected, and omnichannel digital experiences. You can share and reuse API functionality to save time and money. And you can expose processes and data as APIs to unlock new opportunities, revenue streams, and business potential with an API-centric approach.

 

Governments: Connect quickly and streamline compliance

As growing regulatory complexity increases business risk, many companies seek to save time, reduce errors, and avoid penalties by standardizing and automating processes associated with business-to-government (B2G) scenarios. With powerful, secure digital document management features in Integration Suite, you can standardize the digital registration of compliance documents, automate their transmission, and track and implement regulatory standards globally. And using prebuilt integrations, you can quickly connect to compliance-related systems and data. As a result, you can reduce compliance-related costs and effort and comply in a timely manner.

 

Events: Adopt event-driven architectures

Event-driven architectures enable real-time, asynchronous communications at scale, even across distributed landscapes. Using the scalable Cloud Integration and Event Mesh capabilities within SAP Integration Suite, you can support real-time communications, gain situational awareness, and enable faster responses to business and system changes. Imagine enabling applications to immediately notify interested parties when new business events happen, monitoring business-critical events in real time, and gaining clear insight into customer behavior, business performance, and supply chain issues.
