What is Gigya?

Gigya, a market leader in customer identity and access management (CIAM), was acquired by SAP in 2017. Gigya’s technology offered capabilities to consumers across channels and touchpoints, built rich intelligent profiles, and created a consent-based approach to personalization across sales, service, and marketing.

SAP acquired Gigya in 2017

Gigya’s software capabilities have been integrated into customer data solutions from SAP. This portfolio helps businesses grow revenue by delivering trusted, personalized customer experiences at scale.
