Frequently asked questions

Digital sovereignty is the ability to control your digital operations, including who can access your data, where systems are operated, which laws apply, and how continuity is protected.

No, data residency and sovereignty are not the same. Data residency is about where data is stored. Sovereignty goes further by considering who can access the data, who can compel disclosure, who operates the service, and who could interrupt access.

SAP’s approach covers four dimensions of sovereignty: data, operational, technical, and legal. These dimensions help organizations understand where data resides and how it is accessed, processed, protected, and governed.

When choosing the level of control, organizations can start by identifying the workloads, data classifications, legal obligations, operational dependencies, and access risks that apply. Then match those needs to the appropriate SAP sovereign solution or control model.
