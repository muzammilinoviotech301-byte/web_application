How it works

Every migration path follows a structured three-step approach. SAP guides you through each phase with tools, expertise, and best practices.

STEP 1

Assess

Inventory your on-premises landscape. SAP's automated tools extract scenarios, evaluate complexity, and estimate the effort required for each migration object.

STEP 2

Plan

Design your target architecture and migration plan. Classify objects for migration, retirement, or rebuild. Prepare your team with SAP Learning courses and documentation.

STEP 3

Move

Migrate using SAP's automated tooling. Validate results in your cloud environment. Complete your migration with minimal business disruption.
