FAQ

AI is integrated across SAP Business Data Cloud, from data creation and enrichment to governance and consumption. With Joule, SAP AI Core, and intelligent data products, organizations can move from passive data management to active, AI-driven decision-making across every line of business.

Joule is the conversational entry point to SAP Business Data Cloud. It enables business users to search for and create data products in plain language, generate SAP Analytics Cloud stories from a single prompt, and answer analytical questions across domains, all grounded in governed, trusted data.

SAP Business Data Cloud's business data fabric provides the unified, governed foundation AI agents need to reason and act successfully across the entire enterprise.
