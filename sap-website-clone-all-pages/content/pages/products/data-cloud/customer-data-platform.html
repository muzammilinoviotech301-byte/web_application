What is SAP Customer Data Platform?

From individual consumers to multiple business accounts, customers demand relevant, personalized engagements. With SAP Customer Data Platform, you can surface insights from customer data in real time across the enterprise to deliver winning engagements, boost revenue, and drive growth.

Bridge front-end customer data and back-end customer data to better understand customers and deliver trusted, personalized engagements at any step of the journey.

  • Visualize customer profiles, their connected relationships, and their associated accounts

  • Benefit from a native connection with ERP for supply chain optimization and product innovation 

  • Uncover real-time customer insights with AI learning models supercharged for industry

  • Show respect for customer data privacy by maintaining the purpose of customer data during every engagement

  • Access real-time customer insights that enhance audience segmentation, uncover trends, and fuel personalized experiences

Key features

Gain a deep understanding of your customers

  • Unify customer profiles and associate relationships at the individual or account level

  • Integrate ERP, supply chain management, and other cloud solutions by using a common data model

  • Enrich customer insights and operational intelligence with generative AI

Get the most value from customer data

  • Combine your customers’ identity and purpose data to truly understand and respect how they want to engage

  • Orchestrate customer data with engagement systems based on data privacy permissions

  • Activate permission-based profile data shared directly by customers

Grow your business through focused engagement

  • Define unique profile views for B2B and B2C use cases at enterprise scale

  • Calculate attributes for segmentation, audience management, and real-time decision-making on an ongoing basis

  • Design and deploy integrations that push customer or account data to the right engagement systems based on purpose
