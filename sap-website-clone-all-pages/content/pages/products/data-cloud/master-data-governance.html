What is SAP Master Data Governance?

SAP Master Data Governance is the governance layer of your business data fabric, unifying master data, policy, and metadata—so every data product your applications and agents consume is trusted.

SAP Master Data Governance in SAP Business Data Cloud

  • Create a golden record for every domain with one governed model across every business entity, preserving the semantics and relationships between them.

  • Clean and match data across your business data fabric by applying profiling, matching, merging, and semantic reconciliation across your mission-critical data.

  • Give stewards the workflows to govern data for AI with pre-built change request workflows, matching and consolidation, and continuous data quality monitoring.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Business Data Cloud provides a trusted data foundation by unifying SAP and third-party data, along with the business processes, policies, and logic that make your data meaningful. Customers get best-of-breed compute for every data and AI workload with engines that adapt to business needs. Together with the domain and industry models in SAP Business AI Platform, SAP Business Data Cloud ensures every agent, application, and decision draws from the universal business context and semantics, while continuously learning from both existing and incoming data.

Master data management is the discipline of creating one trusted master reference (a single version of the truth) for all important business data, such as product data, customer data, asset data, finance data, and more. MDM helps ensure businesses don’t use multiple, potentially inconsistent versions of data in different parts of business, including processes, operations, and analytics and reporting. The three key pillars to effective MDM include: data consolidation, data governance, and data quality management.

The key is to first preserve the value and quality of mission-critical master data, leveraging a solution like SAP Master Data Governance, and then to share access to this data in a decentralized way with a flexible business data fabric, leveraging solutions like SAP Datasphere. This allows you to also join your trusted, governed master data with other SAP data and third-party data to accelerate your ability to derive value and gain insights from that data.

The purpose of master data integration is to distribute master data to enable a harmonized view of master data across all applications. Master data integration allows for end-to-end process integration across the enterprise. Master data integration does not change the quality of master data, but always distributes master data in its current state. Instead, integration provides a distribution layer that gives your line-of-business applications a consistent view of data. Ultimately, a master data integration should increase the effectiveness and reduce the cost and effort of data sharing.

After your move to SAP S/4HANA, many business processes will be familiar to you from your existing ERP system; but you will also be implementing new, more automated processes to match the demands of the digital marketplace. You may find yourself implementing processes for automatic replacement of parts inventory, IoT processes, really the sky's the limit. These processes will rely more heavily on clean and correct master data, and if your master data is not up to the task, you will lose much of the value of SAP S/4HANA. To avoid this, we recommended that you curate your master data early and well before the actual implementation begins. The SAP Master Data Governance application offers in-built integration to SAP’s data model and underlying technologies (for example, SAP Business Workflow), and helps you consolidate and actively govern master data for SAP and third-party applications.
