What is SAP Business Warehouse in Business Data Cloud?

SAP Business Warehouse in Business Data Cloud provides a hosted cloud environment that enables you to preserve and consume your on-premises SAP BW data in the cloud without any disruptions.

Preserve your SAP BW investments with Business Data Cloud

  • Effortlessly access SAP BW data with the managed object store

  • Deliver SAP BW objects as data products to all data consumers

  • Integrate historical SAP BW and lakehouse data via Delta Share with SAP Databricks

Key benefits

Safeguard existing SAP BW investments

Maximize the value of SAP BW instances with new capabilities that seamlessly connect to existing BW data, eliminating the need to rebuild models and objects with third-party solutions.

Connect all your data

Harmonize SAP BW data with all your semi-structured and unstructured data across your existing data landscape—from a data lakehouse to a business data fabric.

Implement AI grounded in trusted business data

Integrate historical SAP BW data into advanced analytics, machine learning, and data engineering use cases to provide AI with rich business context.

Key features

Cloud-ready data products

Use the data product generator to transform BW InfoProvider data into cloud-ready data products that are immediately accessible in Business Data Cloud.

Zero-copy data sharing

Seamlessly connect and deliver SAP BW data products bidirectionally with SAP Datasphere and SAP Databricks in Business Data Cloud without data copies or duplication.

Dedicated BW object store

Decouple SAP BW data storage from compute with the managed BW object store in Business Data Cloud for greater efficiency, cost savings, and seamless sharing of data with Delta Sharing.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Business Data Cloud is a fully managed SaaS solution that unifies and governs all SAP data and seamlessly connects with third-party data—giving line-of-business leaders context to make even more impactful decisions. It combines all the powerful functionality of SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud, and SAP Business Warehouse, plus managed SAP Databricks, Intelligent content, and data products.

Business Warehouse in SAP Business Data Cloud provides a hosted cloud environment that enables you to preserve and consume your on-premises SAP BW data in the cloud without any disruptions.

SAP Business Data Cloud is the path forward for current on-premises SAP Business Warehouse application customers. With SAP Business Data Cloud, customers optimize their existing SAP BW data by delivering them as data products and take advantage of the latest data and AI capabilities, such as bidirectional data sharing and machine learning, while modernizing to the cloud.
