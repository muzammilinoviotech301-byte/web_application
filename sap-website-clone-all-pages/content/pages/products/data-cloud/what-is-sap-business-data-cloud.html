What is SAP Business Data Cloud?

What is SAP Business Data Cloud?

SAP Business Data Cloud is a connected, end-to-end solution that bridges SAP and third-party data sources through a business data fabric. It helps organizations get more value from their mission-critical data across their enterprise landscape, delivering governed insights that drive better decisions and trusted AI.

Visit the product page

SAP Business Data Cloud overview

SAP Business Data Cloud uses a business data fabric architecture to bring together mission-critical business process information across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. This creates a unified, governed data foundation that helps organizations generate deeper insights and enable autonomous operations across analytics and AI use cases. It also provides context-rich access to SAP data across key business processes, reducing the cost and complexity of data extraction and replication while ensuring agents act within the true context of the business.

 

SAP Business Data Cloud helps address a common challenge that many organizations face: A global survey of 1,200 business and technology leaders found that 55% cite poor data quality as their biggest obstacle. Data is often fragmented across systems, inconsistent, and difficult to govern, leaving valuable information underutilized and increasing operational costs.

 

This challenge becomes even more significant as organizations invest in AI, since the effectiveness of AI systems is directly dependent on the quality of the data that grounds them. When AI has access to high-quality and well-governed business information, it can generate more accurate insights, support predictive decision-making, and enable more advanced, agentic workloads. Without that foundation, organizations struggle to move beyond isolated AI use cases and achieve meaningful business outcomes.

 

How SAP Business Data Cloud works

Business data fabric

As a complete solution for data and AI, SAP Business Data Cloud delivers fully managed data fabric capabilities across data management and integration, data engineering, planning and agentic analytics, and SAP Business Warehouse (SAP BW) modernization. Additionally, industry-leading AI and machine learning capabilities in SAP Databricks accelerate workloads on your SAP application data.

 

Trusted data products

Get a comprehensive view of key business processes with prebuilt data products that incorporate deep SAP application knowledge and industry best practices. These curated, governed data products deliver ready-to-use data by packaging it with context, business semantics, and documentation to accelerate various domain-specific use cases.

 

Intelligent content that knows your business

These AI-driven, adaptive applications support the shift toward a more autonomous enterprise by learning from your information, understanding business context, and taking action to drive better outcomes. Built on fully managed SAP data products, they reduce the need for extraction and replication while saving time and resources.

 

AI-ready operational intelligence

Build a trusted foundation for AI with tools that are responsible, context-aware, and enterprise-ready. A connected knowledge graph helps agents and intelligent content understand your data’s relationships. Joule automates workflows and extracts key insights, while AI capabilities support complex planning, forecasting, and simulations—including Monte Carlo methods for risk analysis and time-series predictions.

SAP Business Data Cloud architecture

SAP Business Data Cloud’s architecture is designed to unify data across distributed systems while preserving business context and governance. At its core is a business data fabric that brings together SAP and third-party data from across hybrid and multi-cloud environments without requiring large-scale replication.

 

A central knowledge core organizes data into governed data products enriched with business semantics. The SAP Knowledge Graph provides a semantic layer that maps relationships across data, enabling a deeper understanding of how business processes and entities are connected.

 

This architecture supports:

  • Unified access to data across SAP and external systems.

  • Governed data products that are business-ready for analytics and AI.

  • Real-time and historical data processing.

  • Reduced data movement through zero-copy data sharing.

Together, these elements create a scalable architecture that delivers trusted data across the enterprise, supporting both analytics and AI use cases.

 

How SAP Business Data Cloud powers Joule Agents and agentic AI

By combining data products with a connected knowledge graph, SAP Business Data Cloud helps AI systems understand relationships and business context—not just raw data. This enables more accurate, explainable, and reliable AI outcomes.

 

With this foundation, Joule Agents can:

  • Understand business processes and context across systems.

  • Automate routine and complex workflows.

  • Generate insights and recommendations in real time.

  • Take action based on trusted, governed data.

Because the data is unified and governed, organizations can scale AI safely—ensuring the way agents and AI systems respond accurately reflects how the business operates.

 

SAP Business Data Cloud open data ecosystem

With SAP Business Data Cloud Connect, organizations can securely share data between Business Data Cloud and leading platforms such as SAP Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, Google BigQuery, and Amazon Athena. This enables a unified view of data without requiring duplication.

 

Bi-directional zero-copy data sharing capabilities allow data to remain in its existing environments while still being accessible for advanced analytics and AI use cases in SAP Business Data Cloud. This approach reduces data movement, helps maintain data consistency, and maximizes organizational investments.

 

By supporting an open ecosystem, SAP Business Data Cloud enables organizations to:

  • Access and harmonize data across diverse platforms.

  • Reduce data replication and duplication.

  • Maintain governance and trust across environments.

  • Accelerate insights and AI innovation.
