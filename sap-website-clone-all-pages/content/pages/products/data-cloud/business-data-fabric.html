FAQ

A business data fabric is an architecture that connects data and context in one governed foundation, serving as the trusted knowledge core for every enterprise application and AI agent. Unlike traditional data management approaches, a business data fabric preserves the meaning behind your data by design, making it foundational for scalable, trustworthy AI. It brings together critical capabilities such as data integration, lakehouse, data products, data marketplace, semantics, knowledge graphs, and modeling into a unified platform without compromising architectural flexibility.

A data fabric connects and manages distributed data across complex landscapes. A business data fabric goes further by preserving the business context behind that data, including its semantics, policies, and business processes. This gives applications and AI agents consistent access to data with the meaning needed to generate trusted insights and actions.

A knowledge core connects semantics, trusted entities, relationships, metadata, ontologies, and business rules to provide a shared business context for analytics and AI. Unlike a static semantic layer, it is continuously enriched by how data is used across domains and use cases, incorporating new relationships, decisions, and process logic as the business evolves. The knowledge core gives AI agents a consistent and adaptive understanding of how the business operates, helping them reason and act with greater context and alignment.

A business data fabric gives agents governed access to organizational data with the business context needed to understand relationships, reason across domains, and take informed action. By grounding agents in shared semantics, enterprise knowledge, and real-time data, this architectural approach enables more trusted and coordinated AI-driven decisions and workflows.

A data lake or data warehouse stores and organizes data, but it does not preserve the business context around it. A business data fabric goes further by connecting that context across your entire landscape, making data not just accessible but interpretable. This distinction becomes critical when AI systems need to make autonomous decisions that align with real business outcomes.

A semantic layer gives data consistent business meaning by defining shared metrics, relationships, and business logic across domains. Within a business data fabric, it connects these semantics with data models, ontologies, and knowledge graphs so analytics, applications, and AI can interpret and use data consistently across various use cases.

SAP Business Data Cloud, a key component of SAP Business AI Platform, is anchored in a business data fabric that delivers universal business context and serves as the trusted knowledge core for every enterprise application and agent. This knowledge core brings together SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Master Data Governance, and SAP Reltio to unify SAP and third-party data, including SAP Business Warehouse, under enterprise-wide governance so that every AI initiative operates from the same trusted foundation.

 

The SAP business data fabric runs on intelligent compute, giving customers flexibility to run best-of-breed compute for every data and AI workload based on business demands with SAP HANA Cloud, SAP Databricks, and the SAP Snowflake solution extension.

 

Together, this foundation delivers a complete understanding of your business built on knowledge only SAP can provide: business process logic, rich operational data products, intelligent content, and global industry deployments. With AI and Joule Agents, that trusted business context becomes action, helping customers automate processes and make faster, better-informed decisions.
