Frequently asked questions

Master data management is the practice of creating and maintaining a consistent, governed view of all business entities across the systems that use them. It is no longer just an ERP topic—master data is the foundation for automation and agentic experiences across your enterprise.

 

Within a business data fabric, master data management is where that context is built. Entity resolution identifies records that describe the same entity and brings them together into a golden record. Governance enforces policy and quality and relationships show how entities connect, producing unified data products in the knowledge core.

AI agents act on your data with no human to catch errors first. An agent may resolve a customer dispute, reprice a product, or evaluate a supplier. When the underlying records are duplicated, conflicting, or stale, every application, analytic, and AI agent built on it inherits the problem and acts on it. AI agents need the same accurate context your best people rely on.

 

Master data management resolves data from every source into one view per entity, governs how that view is created and changed, and carries the relationships between entities—so every agent works from the same current view.

Data unification turns duplicate, inconsistent business entities from many sources into one consistent, and comprehensive view of each entity—matching and merging duplicates, cleansing and enriching values, and capturing relationships and hierarchies. The result goes beyond a golden record. Each view carries the relationships and interactions that give the entity meaning.

 

A data warehouse or lakehouse collects data—it does not resolve it. Nothing decides which records describe the same customer, product, or supplier, so the duplicates, conflicting values, and gaps come with the data.

SAP Reltio and SAP Master Data Governance are complementary. SAP Master Data Governance keeps master data correct at the point of creation in SAP ERP, enforcing policy and leaving a full audit trail. SAP Reltio unifies master data from other SAP applications, non-SAP systems, and third-party data providers—resolving duplicates and maintaining quality across all of them.

 

SAP has long provided trusted business data and context within SAP ERP. Together, these solutions extend that trusted foundation across all your SAP and non-SAP systems.

Mastering data in SAP ERP alone isn’t enough because the entities your business runs on exist in multiple places. For example, a customer exists in ERP, but also in CRM, marketing, commerce, service systems, and often in third-party data. Governing master data at creation keeps ERP correct, but it can’t resolve the duplicates, conflicting values, and missing relationships that appear once the same entity is represented across a dozen other systems.

 

AI agents act on the whole entity—not just the ERP portion of it. Unifying data across sources gives them a comprehensive, current view rather than an authoritative fragment.
