Frequently asked questions

SAP enterprise resource planning (ERP) is a software system that helps organizations streamline their core business processes, including finance, HR, manufacturing, supply chain, sales, and procurement—with a unified view and a single source of truth. SAP ERP software includes generative AI and AI agents. It helps businesses transform operations and stay competitive by automating tasks, anticipating outcomes, and improving decision-making.

Industries such as manufacturers, retailers, services, utilities, and customer goods and wholesalers use SAP ERP software, but the software’s accurate, real-time information and effective business processes can benefit businesses in every industry.

The difference between SAP GROW and RISE with SAP is that SAP GROW is generally for customers  who are new to SAP Cloud ERP, offering standardized processes with built-in AI across finance, supply chain, and HR. RISE with SAP, on the other hand, supports existing SAP ERP customers in transitioning from on-premises systems, enabling a more, modern tailored transformation with Business Suite.

SAP S/4HANA is a type of SAP ERP software. It differs from typical ERP software in that it processes large amounts of data to process vast amounts of data to support advanced technologies, such as Al and machine learning.

Business One is an affordable ERP solution designed for small businesses, covering accounting, purchasing, inventory, sales, and reporting. SAP GROW, on the other hand, provides access to SAP Cloud ERP with standardized, AI-enabled processes across finance, supply chain, and HR to support sustainable growth. Available at a predictable cost through simplified packages with fixed scope and without surprise upgrades or hidden costs. Learn more about SAP GROW.

Yes, ERP systems can easily integrate with other business applications using SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) and SAP Integration Suite. BTP brings together application development, data and analytics, integration, and AI into one platform. Integration Suite allows you to integrate your business across SAP ERP and third-party landscapes with a modern, scalable, and secure integration platform as a service (iPaaS). It supports integration using one comprehensive, market-leading iPaaS with open connectivity, pre-built accelerators, and embedded AI.

You can implement SAP S/4HANA and get to go-live in weeks with SAP Activate—a cloud adoption framework that offers solution-specific best practices, expert guidance, and a structured approach to streamline transformation.

SAP Cloud ERP applications are the most suitable SAP ERP solutions for midsize companies because they integrate AI into your core business processes so you can easily increase speed, reduce risk, and scale.
