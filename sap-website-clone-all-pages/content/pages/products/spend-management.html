Frequently asked questions

Autonomous Spend Management is the use of agentic AI, data, and applications in SAP Autonomous Suite to optimize processes such as sourcing, contracts, supplier management, buying, and invoicing. It helps organizations achieve measurable outcomes across procurement, accounts payable, travel and expense, and external workforce management by moving from manual decision-making to AI‑enabled, policy‑guarded execution.

AI streamlines procurement, accounts payable, travel and expense, and external workforce management by automating routine work and delivering real-time insights. AI agents execute tasks that deliver tangible results, such as:

  • Faster cycle times and higher touchless rates through auto‑creating POs, matching invoices, and auditing expenses.

  • Better savings and compliance by steering users to contracted items, analyzing bids, and capturing early‑payment discounts.

  • Lower risk through continuous checks that identify duplicates, anomalies, and supplier signals.

  • Consistent 24/7 productivity that scales without added headcount.

  • Continuous learning that improves recommendations and efficiency over time.
