Key features

Multichannel sales repository

  • Collect data from sales channels spread across multiple siloed applications in diverse formats
  • Audit and manage sales transactions coming from point-of-sale systems
  • Gain real-time insight into sales performance, revenue, and gross margin across channels

Replenishment planning

  • Reduce waste with cost-optimal ordering, including prediction of stock spoilage
  • Respond to changes in demand with intraday forecasting and replenishment
  • Simulate and review your order plans and obtain insights on associated KPIs

Assortment planning

  • Support flexible process- and information-driven planning across retail formats – tightly integrated with merchandise planning budgets
  • Rank products by weighting their ranking against chosen KPIs and business roles to consider real-world requirements and limitations 
  • Integrate space for assortment and planogram optimization

Allocation management

  • Trigger initial allocation of seasonal products based on actual and planned availability, latest demand forecast, target settings, and business KPIs
  • Enable automated in-season replenishment
  • Allocate promotional merchandise based on promotional forecast, store inventory, and capacity

Promotion management and omnichannel pricing

  • Design promotions and offers in line with customer preferences and financial objectives
  • Create what-if simulations to predict financial performance and leverage vendor funding to maximize margins from trade funds
  • Enable consistent calculation of consumer prices and promotional offers for each basket at any touch point
