Utilize AI across the order lifecycle to support monitoring, analysis, and decision‑making in complex order environments.

The Order Management Assistant brings together multiple AI‑powered agents that analyze orders, inventory, fulfillment, and execution signals to surface insights, identify issues, and recommend next-best actions.

By combining automated monitoring with contextual analysis and guided resolution, the assistant helps teams reduce manual effort, respond faster to disruptions, and maintain consistent order management process while keeping team members in the loop across diverse order management scenarios.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Order Management Services helps organizations orchestrate and manage orders across channels, inventory, and fulfillment systems. It addresses common challenges such as fragmented order data, limited visibility, manual exception handling, and inconsistent execution by providing a centralized, intelligent foundation for end‑to‑end order lifecycle management.

SAP Order Management Services connects orders from digital, physical, and partner channels into a unified orchestration layer. It coordinates order flow across back-end systems, tracks execution status in real time, and ensures consistent handling across channels, supporting scalable and reliable omnichannel order operations.

SAP applies AI across the order lifecycle to transform siloed data into connected insights. AI capabilities help anticipate risks, surface issues earlier, and guide teams toward priority actions, reducing manual effort while enabling faster, more informed responses as conditions change.
