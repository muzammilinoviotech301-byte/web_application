What is the cloud edition of SAP Customer Checkout?

The cloud edition of SAP Customer Checkout provides point-of-sale (POS) management, real-time sales data, end-to-end ERP integration, and personalized offers like loyalty programs, gift cards, and coupons.

Give your staff the POS functionality to excel in retail, merchandising, catering, and sports and entertainment with a solution that delivers:

  • Two components—a cloud-based back-office and a powerful on-site POS system.

  • A standalone solution that can integrate with SAP S/4HANA or other ERPs.

  • One solution for retail, quick service, and table service.

  • Flexibility to extend and scale the solution.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Customer Checkout, cloud edition, is a cloud-based POS system for retail, sports and entertainment, and catering businesses. It supports common POS needs, various payment methods, gift card, loyalty, and coupon management, and integrates with SAP S/4HANA Cloud. The solution comprises SAP Customer Checkout manager, cloud edition, which is a centralized platform for seamless POS operations, and SAP Customer Checkout POS, cloud edition, which enhances in-store sales and transaction efficiency. These components ensure integrated POS functionalities and smooth system management.

The cloud edition of SAP Customer Checkout supports the legal requirements in Germany, Switzerland, Austria, United Kingdom, Ireland, and France. The cloud edition can also run in the United States, supporting local tax calculation and offering Avalara integration. However, business cases are unique. It is the responsibility of SAP partners and customers to provide necessary adaptations on a project basis. The cloud edition can be sold in non-localized countries, but SAP partners are responsible for localizing the solution. Also, please note we operate in one data center located in Europe.

The UI of the cloud edition of SAP Customer Checkout (POS system and manager component) is available in the following languages: Arabic (limited support), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Greek, Hebrew (limited support), Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Portuguese (Portugal), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Slovakian, Spanish, and Swedish.
