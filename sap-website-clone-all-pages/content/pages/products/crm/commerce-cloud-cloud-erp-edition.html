    Increase conversion, order value, and repeat purchases with guided buying, contract‑driven commerce, and accurate pricing and availability.

  • Run a lean, efficient operation

    Shift routine interactions to self‑service and automate key steps to reduce inquiry volume and order friction—freeing time for sales, service, and operations.

Features

View media gallery

End‑to‑end B2B transactions

Support account-based buying with customer-specific pricing, negotiated terms, approvals, and contract purchasing. Streamline quote-to-order workflows and enable fast reorders, bulk uploads, and repeat purchases across accounts, locations, and geographies.

Self‑service account center

Provide a unified, ERP-connected account center for orders, invoices, and contracts across e-commerce, direct sales, and electronic data interchange. Empower customers with self-service order management while delivering consistent, real-time visibility through SAP Cloud ERP data.

Native ERP connectivity

Connect commerce directly to SAP Cloud ERP with SAP-managed integration to deliver real-time pricing, availability, orders, and invoices using ERP data as the single source of truth. Simplify your landscape, reduce operational overhead, and lower upgrade risk with a clean, ERP-aligned architecture.

Clean‑core scalability

Launch quickly with preconfigured, standards-based commerce capabilities, then scale from core self-service to advanced pricing, AI, and multisite experiences as needed. Extend with in-app and side-by-side extensibility while keeping the commerce and ERP core clean and upgrade-safe.

Plans and pricing

SAP Commerce Cloud, cloud ERP edition

SAP Commerce Cloud, cloud ERP edition is a purpose‑built commerce solution designed for companies running SAP Cloud ERP that want to launch and scale commerce without added complexity. With preconfigured, standards‑based capabilities and SAP‑managed connectivity, it’s simple to deploy, easy to run, and built to grow with your business—allowing teams to focus on delivering great customer experiences rather than managing integrations and updates.

Benefits include:

Native, SAP‑managed connectivity to SAP Cloud ERP for real‑time pricing, orders, and inventory

Faster time to value with preconfigured, standards‑based commerce processes

Lower total cost of ownership through a simplified, ERP‑aligned landscape

Unified customer self‑service across orders from all channels—not just digital transactions

Enterprise‑grade scalability, security, and governance delivered by SAP

Features include:

ERP‑connected account center for orders, invoices, shipments, and account details

Real‑time pricing, availability, and order status sourced directly from SAP Cloud ERP

Self‑service ordering, reordering, and order tracking across all sales channels

Account‑based B2B commerce with customer‑specific catalogs and contract pricing

Quote‑ and contract‑driven buying flows

Embedded AI tools for product content optimization, storefront personalization, and guided buying

Secure identity and access management aligned with SAP standards

SAP‑managed updates and integration lifecycle

Prerequisites:

SAP Finance Base - 8019322 OR SAP Finance Premium - 8019461 OR SAP Finance Base and Supply Chain Base - 8019781
