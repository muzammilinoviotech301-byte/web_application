Key features

Ticket management

  • Maximize ticketing revenue by taking advantage of flexibility to sell across multiple channels
  • Create single tickets, season tickets and passes, vouchers, coupons, bundles, flex plans, parking permits, and accreditations
  • Configure and manage special campaigns, promotion codes, price reductions, and memberships

Ticket sales

  • Generate revenue by cross- and upselling events, parking, merchandise, or food and beverages
  • Incorporate social selling with social media channels and functions
  • Manage sales processes through integration with your existing IT structure

Fan and customer insights

  • Use advanced marketing, CRM, membership, and loyalty features to boost ticket sales and generate customer insights
  • Utilize real-time ticketing analytics information to maximize sales for each seat and event
  • Integrate with SAP Analytics Cloud and SAP HANA for insights for confident decision-making

Technological flexibility

  • Take advantage of maximum flexibility built into a scalable cloud solution based on the latest technologies
  • Integrate SAP Event Ticketing into your technology landscape to meet your IT and process requirements
  • Connect with various admission control systems and gather ticketing data
