What is SAP Agent Performance Management?

By elevating agent effectiveness through strategic incentive compensation management, you can foster profitable behaviors and drive revenue growth.

The SAP Agent Performance Management solution streamlines onboarding, compliance, and incentive compensation management to support the needs of your insurance company, helping optimize outcomes and deliver superior performance.

  • Cloud deployment

  • Integrated compliance and background checks

  • Streamlined dispute resolution

  • Reduction in agent inquiry and payment errors

Key benefits

Optimize agent compensation

Streamline agent incentive management and optimize outcomes by enabling users to create, refine, and distribute effective incentive compensation plans.

Maximize agent performance

Increase agent productivity and help boost agent retention by providing a one-stop shop for agent management with a consistent user experience.

Enhance operational efficiency

Onboard new agents faster, gain visibility into your book of business, manage effective relationships, and streamline communications with enhanced workflows.

Comprehensive onboarding

Advanced incentive management processes

  • Design, distribute, and model agent compensation plans and programs for an unlimited number of payees
