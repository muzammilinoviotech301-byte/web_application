What is SAP Omnichannel Sales Transfer and Audit?

SAP Omnichannel Sales Transfer and Audit is a solution that captures and validates POS transactions in real time to deliver clean, financial-ready data for your ERP system while providing insights on retail store operations.

Take control of high-integrity, real-time processing of complex sales data across diverse POS landscapes to:

  • Capture and manage sales transactions across channels.

  • Conduct audit checks, error detection, and exception handling.

  • Prepare transaction data for your ERP system

Key benefits

Prepare for data audits

Achieve full traceability with detailed change logs, end-to-end transaction lifecycle tracking, and easy access to archived records for transparency and compliance.

Strengthen operational resilience

Maintain clean, accurate sales data and resolve transaction issues to help prevent disruptions in financial close and reporting.

Reduce manual workload

Minimize manual intervention, error tracking, and reconciliation efforts so teams can focus on higher-value operational and analytical work.

Key features

Optimized sales data validation

  • Capture every POS transaction from in-store sales channels.
  • Run automated, configurable validation checks using rule-based logic.
  • Trigger error identifications for timely manual intervention as needed.

Automated audit processing

  • Identify and resolve data gaps, duplicates, inconsistent totals, and configuration mismatches.
  • Access archived sales data for transparency and auditing.
  • Monitor real-time store sales data.

Scalable retail operations foundation

  • Format validated sales, financial, and totals transactions for downstream ERP systems.
  • Integrate new sales channels, ERPs, and other solutions with a cloud-native, API-first architecture.
  • Support data security and compliance across the sales data transfer process.
