Frequently asked questions

A unified commerce solution is a platform that connects every commerce touchpoint—online storefronts, in-store, mobile, and marketplaces—with the back-office systems that run the business—inventory, pricing, order management, and customer data. Rather than managing separate systems for each channel, a unified commerce platform gives businesses a single, real-time foundation that delivers consistent experiences wherever customers engage. It also creates the connected data foundation that AI needs to act reliably across the commerce lifecycle.

These terms describe distinct but related concepts:

  • Unified commerce is the infrastructure layer, connecting channels, inventory, pricing, orders, and customer data into a single, connected platform.

  • Agentic commerce refers to AI agents acting on behalf of buyers to discover products, compare options, and assist in completing transactions.

  • Autonomous unified commerce goes beyond helping customers find and buy products. It enables AI to continuously optimize how commerce operates, from product discovery and demand generation to inventory, fulfillment, pricing, and customer engagement, driving better outcomes with every interaction.

SAP brings all three together on a single data foundation.

 

Unified commerce platforms eliminate the silos that cause inconsistent experiences and missed revenue:

  • Consistent experiences across every channel—same pricing, availability, and service.

  • Real-time inventory visibility for accurate availability and faster fulfillment.

  • Centralized product and order data that reduces manual effort and errors.

  • A reliable AI foundation—agents need connected, real-time data to act reliably.

  • B2B, B2C, and B2B2C support on a single platform.
