What is SAP Omnichannel Promotion Pricing?

SAP Omnichannel Promotion Pricing is a cloud-based solution that ensures consistent promotional pricing across sales channels to foster brand loyalty and drive revenue growth.

Achieve consistent promotional pricing across your sales and engagement channels with a cloud-based solution that supports omnichannel customer experiences.

  • Support for uploading price and promotion information from any source

  • Centralized calculation of promotion pricing for all sales channels

  • Centralized promotions management with a versatile user interface

  • Coupon management during promotion planning and sales processes, with user interfaces to facilitate the maintenance and handling of coupons.

Key features

Uploading of price and promotion data

Use the data upload service to upload regular sales prices and promotional rules and use this data as a basis for the calculation of promotional prices.

Omnichannel promotion rules

Maintain promotion rules directly in a cloud repository using a promotion maintenance UI.

Sales price calculation

Apply promotional rules to calculate effective sales prices in the customer context across web, point of sale, and sales order channels.

Management of uploaded data

Read sales prices and promotional rules and check uploaded data with the data access service, and delete obsolete sales prices and promotional rules through the administration service.

Configuration of behaviour parameters

Establish and configure parameters to influence the behaviour of the calculation service.

Coupon management service

Manage the creation, maintenance, reservation, and redemption of coupons in the promotion planning and sales processes, using two UIs to facilitate the maintenance and handling of coupons.
