What is SAP Agent Lifecycle Management?

With real-time insights into agent effectiveness, hierarchies and relationships, training, and compliance information, including certifications, you can better engage with your agents and empower them to support your company’s mission and goals.

The SAP Agent Lifecycle Management solution can help you navigate the agent lifecycle with an integrated, user-friendly platform that enables you to streamline processes, enhance collaboration, and drive agent productivity.

  • Cloud deployment
  • Comprehensive agent onboarding
  • Integration with compliance agencies
  • Ongoing relationship management

Key benefits

Gain real-time visibility

Empower your users with a unified view of agent information that helps them gain insights, save time, and improve the lifecycle value of individual agents.

Unify onboarding data

Help new agents start selling as soon as possible by onboarding them quickly with customized, automated self-services connected to your agent management system.

Simplify compliance

Maintain proactive regulatory compliance based on a single source of truth while reducing manual data entry and using connected agent management functionality.

Key features

Streamlined agent lifecycle management

  • Manage and credit agents intuitively through complex hierarchies and crediting requirements

  • Reduce agent inquiry and drive greater productivity

  • Manage multiple lines of business on a single platform

Comprehensive onboarding

  • Automate credential management by integrating it with the National Insurance Producer Registry (NIPR), the Depository Trust and Clearing Corporation (DTCC), and the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA)

  • Capture all relevant data in one place

  • Support compliance through embedded onboarding with built-in checks

Strong compliance

  • Gain visibility into every agent, vendor, customer, product, and policy

  • Track and audit any change to help ensure compliance and protect sensitive data

  • Monitor and stay ahead of compliance concerns with preconfigured integrations and synchronization with key regulators
