What is SAP Enterprise Service Management?

SAP Enterprise Service Management is an omnichannel, unified approach to service delivery. It provides AI-powered customer inquiry and case management for efficient user engagements and resolutions.

AI enabled SAP Enterprise Service Management removes complexity from resolution processes—delivering efficient, unified service across the enterprise by:

  • Breaking down organizational silos with connected, AI-assisted inquiry flows.

  • Resolving inquiries faster on any channel with AI-powered instant answers.

  • Responding quickly to changing business needs and service trends with autonomous monitoring and improvement.

  • Unifying service delivery across the enterprise.

Key features

AI-powered inquiry management

  • Deliver 24/7 AI-guided self-service to maximize case deflection.

  • Equip service representatives with an AI-driven, omnichannel workspace that unifies experiences and drives contextual automation.

  • Enable quick resolutions with AI-assisted process automation at every step.

AI-assisted case management

  • Engage AI from case creation through resolution to autonomously support service representatives with guided processes and recommendations.

  • Connect enterprise systems with ease using preconfigured content packages that drive rapid ROI.

  • Accelerate resolutions with AI to provide an intuitive, process-led experience.

Continuous insights and improvement

  • Equip service leaders and managers with AI-powered predictive insights, trends, and automation to drive high-value operational excellence.  

  • Break down siloes with continuous process monitoring and AI-enabled recommendations that optimize business flows and processes.

  • Reduce friction throughout the inquiry-to-resolution journey with instant KPI-focused guidance and recommendations.

HR service delivery

  • Centralize 24/7 HR service management with AI-powered self-service that provides instant guidance and deflects cases automatically.

  • Accelerate case resolution with AI-driven workflows, intelligent recommendations, and automated case handling.

  • Speed time to value with preconfigured HR scenarios, native SAP Success Factors Employee Central integration, and IT-free low-code tools.

Financial shared services

  • Automate the processing of financial service inquiries and ensure accuracy with AI assistants and intelligent business document extraction.

  • Empower revenue operations teams to prioritize, prevent, and resolve receivables issues using AI automation in a centralized workspace, helping ensure predictable cash flow.

  • Deploy rapidly with preconfigured integrations with SAP S/4HANA Finance.

 

Flexible configuration across global business service functions

  • Deploy consistent, expert-quality service enterprise-wide, without custom development or IT-heavy integration overhead.

  • Extend service to any function or line of business with configurable case types, workflows, and forms.

  • Standardize inquiry and resolution processes to deliver structured, trackable service that meets service-level agreements and improves operational margins.
