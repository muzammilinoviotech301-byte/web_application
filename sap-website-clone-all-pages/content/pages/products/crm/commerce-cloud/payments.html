Deliver consistent pricing across channels, drive loyalty with promotions and coupons, and integrate payment service providers into your processes

SAP Commerce Cloud offers robust capabilities for pricing, promotions, and payments—including features to help you manage promotions and prices across sales channels, optimize sales prices, and integrate payment vendors using no code/low code tools.

Single source of truth

Achieve consistent pricing across sales channels with a single source of truth for promotional prices.

Streamlined price management

Centralize price management and make it easier to update and maintain accurate pricing data for products and promotions.

Personalized promotions

Use AI and machine learning to personalize promotions based on customer behavior and preferences.

Open payment framework

Scale business faster with a plug-and-play approach, reducing the complexity of traditional integrations with third-party payment service providers (PSPs).

Agile connectivity

Expand payment processing as your business grows using our API and no-code/low-code tools to quickly integrate new PSPs.

Comprehensive coverage

Define payment processes across authorization, capture, refunds, and re-authorizations while maintaining security and compliance.

Broad extensibility

Integrate with almost any payment vendor using extensible sample integrations and prebuilt use cases in our composable storefront​.

Promotional bundling

Realize more revenue with a single platform for cross-selling goods, services, and digital content.

Flexible bundle types

Create bundles to sell products as promotion kits or to provide guided selling experiences across multiple interrelated goods and services.

Bundling tools

Use simplified business tools and templates to configure, manage, and sell complex bundled offerings.

Promotions

Sales and conversions

Intuitive promotion creation

Pricing

Consistent pricing experience with SAP Cloud ERP

Multi-business-model support

Composable pricing and promotion engine
