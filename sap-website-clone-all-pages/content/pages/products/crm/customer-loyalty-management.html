What is SAP Customer Loyalty Management?

SAP Customer Loyalty Management is an application that delivers personal, experiential, and relevant loyalty programs to customers.

Create experiences that extend your brand presence and culminate in customer advocacy by:

  • Harnessing first-party data more effectively.

  • Running global loyalty programs from one place.

  • Joining or launching loyalty alliances.

  • Scaling programs and proving their value.
