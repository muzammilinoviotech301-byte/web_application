What is SAP Order Management foundation?

SAP Order Management foundation centralizes order, fulfillment, and return processes across all channels.

Establishing a foundation for omnichannel order management at scale enables you to:

  • Unify order orchestration for consistent, ERP-aligned execution.

  • Centralize order processes across all channels.

  • Route orders efficiently to different fulfillment systems, channels, and nodes.

  • Easily integrate with sales channels through cloud-native, modular architecture.

Key features

Unified, consistent order orchestration

  • Capture orders from various channels for consistent, omnichannel order orchestration.
  • Manage and direct orders across a distributed fulfillment network.
  • Access a centralized view of fulfillment updates and events.

Centralized, agentic order monitoring and analytics

  • Monitor and manage order processes with Order Reliability Agent, which proactively detects and mitigates potential issues.
  • Keep employees in the loop with next-best recommendations on complex issues.
  • Gain insights on orders and order performance through Joule.

Scalable omnichannel order foundation

  • Implement a cloud-native, API-first modular architecture to add sales channels.
  • Scale the business to support entry into new markets and territories.
  • Integrate with the SAP ecosystem and third-party solutions to fit a business’s unique needs.
