Frequently asked questions

CX marketing represents the marketing function of the customer experience, which is how customers feel when interacting with your brand or product. Whether the customer is a business (B2B) or a person (B2C), the spirit of honoring their journey in marketing is the same.

CRM (customer relationship management) is an essential part of marketing within the customer experience. CRM software analyzes and manages customer interactions and data throughout the customer lifecycle, thereby building better customer experiences, customer service, and business relationships while increasing revenue.
