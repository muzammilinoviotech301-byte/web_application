Frequently asked questions

Revenue Intelligence delivers both intelligent content for automated insights and data products that provide direct access to governed customer data across your SAP landscape. It connects individual customer touchpoints with operational data from ERP, inventory, pricing, and fulfillment systems through SAP Business Data Cloud, eliminating the complexity and cost of traditional data integration while keeping original semantics and governance intact. This provides full business context for every revenue-impacting customer decision and enables faster action.

Data products provide the foundation—prebuilt data products that unify customer touchpoints with operational data from your SAP applications. This includes customer journey data, sales activity data, digital channel data, and service interaction data.

 

The intelligent content layer sits on top of this foundation, combining prebuilt reporting and analytics, AI and machine learning, MyMetrics (KPIs), semantic models and planning models. Data products provide the governed data foundation while intelligent content delivers the AI-enabled insights and actions layer that revenue teams use daily.
