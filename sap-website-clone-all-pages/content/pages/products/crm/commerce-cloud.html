What is SAP Commerce Cloud?

SAP Commerce Cloud natively connects business data with market-leading commerce capabilities to drive profitability for growing companies and the world’s largest enterprises.

From discovery to delivery, unite every step for customer experiences to:

  • Accelerate time-to-value and drive AI use cases with SAP data connectivity.

  • Use AI assistants and agents to optimize the commerce journey.

  • Run B2B, B2C, and B2B2C commerce end to end on one platform.

  • Choose out-of-box features or fully customize your implementation.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Commerce Cloud is SAP's enterprise e-commerce solution designed to help companies of any size adapt to changing business needs. Supporting B2B, B2C, and B2B2C commerce, it enables organizations to deliver personalized omnichannel experiences; manage complex catalogs, products, and configurations; and scale commerce operations as customer expectations and business requirements evolve.

No, SAP Commerce Cloud is not a standalone CRM. It is a digital commerce solution within the SAP Customer Experience portfolio that enables online selling, product discovery, pricing, promotions, and order management. SAP Commerce Cloud can integrate with CRM and customer data solutions to help businesses deliver personalized customer journeys.

SAP Commerce Cloud is the evolution of SAP Hybris. While "SAP Hybris" was the historic product name, SAP Commerce Cloud is the current cloud-based commerce solution that includes AI-powered capabilities, cloud delivery, and integration with SAP's broader business applications. Organizations researching SAP Hybris today should evaluate SAP Commerce Cloud.

SAP Commerce Cloud helps simplify e-commerce operations by delivering a seamless customer experience from product discovery to purchase. A combination of self-service capabilities, AI-enhanced merchandising, guided selling, assisted service, and chatbots helps improve customer engagement and increase efficiency. The platform also streamlines complex commerce and order processes, helping businesses connect demand with inventory, fulfillment, and supply chain operations.

Prebuilt integration is based on SAP Integration Suite, which helps connect SAP Commerce Cloud with other SAP and third-party applications. Since SAP Commerce Cloud is based on open APIs, you can quickly integrate it with third-party solutions as well. We offer many prebuilt integrations through SAP Store. You can also use serverless microservices to extend your e-commerce architecture through the open-source project “Kyma.”

Yes, SAP Commerce Cloud supports headless commerce through an API-first approach and the decoupled JavaScript-based storefront layer. This approach gives businesses the flexibility to create and manage commerce experiences across websites, mobile apps, devices, and digital channels without changing the core commerce platform.

Yes, SAP Commerce Cloud provides an embedded solution for PIM through SAP Product Content Management, part of the SAP Commerce Cloud portfolio.

 

Product Content Management enables you to consolidate product data and catalogs centrally and publish them through various channels. You can make edits through an intuitive user interface, enhance product content quality with built-in data validation and workflows, bulk edit and mass upload product data, and support multilingual catalogs for a global e-commerce site.
