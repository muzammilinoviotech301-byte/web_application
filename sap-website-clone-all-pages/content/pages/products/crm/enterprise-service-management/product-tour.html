B2B customer support
When B2B customers request support via email, a support case is automatically created and assigned to an agent who offers expert guidance on dispute management, credit terms, and other issues.
Support agents receive real-time notifications for newly assigned cases. They can navigate directly from the notification to the case, review the AI-generated case summary, and proceed with a resolution.
AI-assisted backend entity extraction and document extraction interpret issues, organize information, and consolidate key documents and links into the workspace.
Agents can post payment advice and access real-time financial updates from S/4HANA Finance, providing faster and more accurate support.
Quickly finalize communications using an AI-powered email drafter, which automatically prepares professional customer-facing messages aligned with sentiment.
The AI-generated resolution summary includes recommendations and supports process optimization tools, such as SAP Signavio, to improve operational efficiency and productivity.
