What is SAP Sales Cloud?

SAP Sales Cloud is an agentic AI–enhanced customer relationship management (CRM) solution for sales automation, customer acquisition, guided selling, forecasting management, and dynamic analytics.

Sales Cloud helps organizations utilize agentic AI to accelerate growth and performance through:

  • Cloud deployment. 

  • AI-enhanced sales processes. 

  • Optimized mobile sales experience. 

  • Actionable insights, highlights, recommendations, and analytics. 

  • Insight-led pipeline management and predictive forecasting.

Key features

Sales AI

Equip each sales rep with AI-assisted sales processes and the Joule copilot. Sales Cloud accelerates, improves, and simplifies sales engagement through AI-generated insights and AI content and by automating complex tasks through AI agents.

Intelligent guided selling

Guided selling uses real-time data to provide recommendations for next-best actions as well as highlights, insights, and engagement trends to accelerate deal cycles. Clear direction enables account team members to engage with confidence.

Digital sales performance dashboard

Increase lead generation and conversion rates of high-volume selling activities by improving outbound engagement. The digital selling workspace is the AI-enabled command center of digital engagement. Users can generate emails and scripts, launch calls, schedule meetings, and execute sales campaigns.

Pipeline and forecast intelligence

Track and understand pipeline and forecast strength using business intelligence to improve quota attainment and reduce deal slippage. Analyze forecasted deals for target, best case, estimated, and more using embedded AI and real-time opportunity data.

AI-powered mobile selling

Sales Cloud’s dedicated mobile applications provide sellers with an optimized user experience to suit fast-paced sales engagement from anywhere, any time. Embedded AI improves performance while voice navigation capabilities increase ease of use.

Field sales and dynamic visit planning

Dynamic visit planning provides insights and recommendations that help sales reps plan field visits, optimize routes, and maximize coverage. Field sales reps can improve their efficiency in progressing deals through each pipeline stage—while organizations increase revenue and reduce operating costs.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Sales Cloud gives sales teams a real-time view of their pipeline by bringing opportunities, forecasts, customer interactions, and AI-enhanced insights together in one place. Sales managers can identify at-risk deals, monitor pipeline health, track performance, and make more informed decisions to improve forecast accuracy and increase win rates.

SAP Sales Cloud has autonomous AI agents that help automate lead qualification, opportunity management, meeting preparation, quote creation, email insights, pipeline risk analysis, and other routine sales tasks. These capabilities help sales teams work more efficiently, make faster decisions, and focus on selling.

SAP Sales Cloud can help you improve forecast accuracy, identify at-risk opportunities, and increase win rates with embedded AI that provides recommendations on what tasks or interactions should be performed to increase forecast accuracy. The embedded AI performs continuous correlation analysis to identify links between seller behavior, opportunity win rate, and other complex variables. This allows sales managers to assess forecast accuracy and model various potential outcomes with data-driven analysis of propensity to close.

SAP Sales Cloud includes specialized "perfect store” functionality to help you create retail execution action plans, optimize visit logistics, and improve the efficiency of on-site activities. Dynamic visit surveys and planograms guide retail execution activities while unifying store audit information to enable continuous improvement of retail execution at each location. Unified KPIs provide insight into year-over-year improvement and can be used to improve resource allocation, execution strategy, and store performance across extended distribution networks.

SAP Sales Cloud can help you identify actionable customer insights by centralizing critical data for a full view of each account. Embedded sales intelligence surfaces critical data automatically and enables real-time exploration of the customer journey through each stage of the sales pipeline. Meanwhile, intuitive visualizations can provide valuable buying journey insights and help business users identify and proactively solve sales process breakdowns.

Yes, SAP Sales Cloud connects with SAP ERP and SAP S/4HANA to bring sales, customer, and operational data together in a single workflow. This gives sales teams direct access to customer accounts, product availability, pricing, quotes, orders, and order status without jumping between systems. This product integration helps drive greater productivity, lessen manual effort, and deliver more connected customer experiences.

B2B selling has become increasingly complex, and leading organizations are helping sales teams improve outcomes by providing prescriptive guidance for opportunity progression. Guided selling functionality within SAP Sales Cloud provides a framework to help you understand and respond to customer needs with an interactive, value-driven approach that's shaped by real-time data.

Yes, organizations can leverage dynamic visit planning functionality within SAP Sales Cloud to help reduce overhead and improve efficiency of store and site visit logistics, scheduling, and routing. Embedded intelligence utilizes real-time and historical data to dynamically optimize how and when field sales and retail execution staff visit customer locations.

SAP Sales Cloud supports organizations across a wide range of industries, including manufacturing, consumer products, retail, wholesale distribution, automotive, high tech, life sciences, and professional services. It helps businesses manage complex sales processes, strengthen customer relationships, and improve sales performance regardless of industry.

Yes, SAP Sales Cloud is available on mobile devices, enabling sales teams to access customer information, manage opportunities, update activities, capture meeting notes, and stay productive from anywhere. With online and offline capabilities, sales representatives can continue working even when connectivity is limited and sync their updates once they're back online.
