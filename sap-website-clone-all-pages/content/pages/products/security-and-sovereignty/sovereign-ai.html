What is sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI lets organizations use AI while choosing the region where sensitive data is processed, who can access it, and how AI is operated in alignment with local and industry regulations.

Access advanced AI technologies

Access AI from SAP and trusted partners through a single platform on controlled, regional infrastructure.

Build, deploy, and manage AI on a governed foundation

Sovereign AI is available on SAP Cloud Infrastructure and trusted, certified partner infrastructures.

Keep data, prompts, and execution in your region

All infrastructure, administration, and support for your AI workflows are managed by local SAP teams or certified partners.

Key benefits

Maintain control over AI data and execution

All AI data, prompts, models, and execution stay within your region—no cross-border exposure or dependency on uncontrolled third-party providers. SAP does not share your data or prompts with third parties for model training.

Comply without compromise

Compliance is built into the AI infrastructure from the start, not retrofitted. SAP's offerings are designed to support local regulations and follow the EU AI Act, GDPR, export controls, and cloud-in-country mandates, with auditability and governance controls included.

Innovate with confidence

Adopt AI across your SAP and non-SAP processes without rearchitecting your landscape. Access multiple AI models under a single SAP contract. Maintain consistent governance and operational simplicity as your needs evolve.

SAP AI Core in regional data centers

SAP AI Core, SAP's foundational layer for running AI workloads and accessing large language models, is deployed on SAP Cloud Infrastructure in SAP-operated regional data centers in Europe, North America, and other geographies.

This keeps AI data, prompts, and execution within a defined geographic boundary, supporting data residency requirements and regional compliance needs. Customers building custom AI solutions on the SAP Business AI Platform can use SAP AI Core to run workloads locally without cross-border data transfer. Lifecycle management, metering, and maintenance are handled by SAP.

Who is this for?

SAP AI Core is for organizations that need in-region AI execution and data residency for custom AI solutions, without requiring full legal or operational sovereignty.

EU AI Cloud: Partner AI on SAP Business AI Platform

EU AI Cloud is SAP's full-stack framework for European customers. It combines SAP Cloud Infrastructure, SAP Business AI Platform, and AI partner technology and models under a single commercial model. Both platforms are licensed through standard AI units from SAP (no separate partner license), integrated with SAP identity and governance services, and available under a single SAP contract.

Mistral on SAP Business AI Platform

Mistral Vibe, an enterprise AI chat and agent platform, and Mistral AI Studio, a no-code environment for building and deploying AI workflows, are hosted and operated by SAP in its German data centers. SAP Business AI Platform also provides access to locally deployed Mistral models.

Who is this for?

EU AI Cloud is for enterprises and organizations requiring AI on European infrastructure, particularly public sector, defense, aeronautics, financial services, healthcare, energy, and manufacturing.

Industrial AI Cloud: SAP, NVIDIA, and Deutsche Telekom

Industrial AI Cloud is a joint initiative between Deutsche Telekom, NVIDIA, and SAP.

Deutsche Telekom and NVIDIA provide the physical infrastructure, and SAP provides SAP Business AI Platform as the technology and integration layer that enables customers to connect industrial AI workloads directly to their SAP business processes. The Made for Germany initiative established a sovereign, compliant AI infrastructure designed for public sector and industrial customers in Germany and Europe.

Who is this for?

Industrial AI Cloud is for organizations in Germany and Europe that need sovereign, high-performance AI compute at industrial scale, especially in the public sector, manufacturing, robotics, supply chain, energy, logistics, and defense.

SAP Sovereign Cloud

SAP provides a sovereign deployment for its solutions. SAP Sovereign Cloud delivers sovereignty across four dimensions: data, operational, technical, and legal. Deployment options include SAP Cloud Infrastructure, premium hyperscalers, and SAP Sovereign Cloud On-Site (SAP-operated infrastructure within a data center selected or owned by the customer).

Who is this for?
SAP Sovereign Cloud is designed for organizations with requirements that go beyond data residency. It’s for public sector, defense, critical national infrastructure, and highly regulated industries that require all four dimensions of sovereignty: data, operational, technical, and legal.

Explore more

Data

All data, including telemetry, is stored within the same country, with no unauthorized cross-border transfers.

Operational

Only approved local personnel handle administration and maintenance, with mandatory security clearances where required.

Technical

Control planes are locally deployed and managed, with logical or physical tenant separation.

Legal

Local legal entities mitigate the risk of influence by foreign authorities.

Frequently asked questions

Sovereign AI enables organizations to use AI while maintaining control over where their data, models, prompts, and workloads are stored, processed, and managed and who can access them. It is particularly useful for enterprises and public sector organizations with data residency, regulatory, security, or operational control requirements, especially those in government, defense, critical infrastructure, financial services, healthcare, energy, and manufacturing.

SAP provides control of data, prompts, models, and AI execution by keeping them in a single region and under SAP control. Customers can run custom AI workloads on SAP-operated or -certified partner infrastructure, access SAP and partner models through a governed foundation, and manage them with consistent identity, lifecycle, and metering. SAP does not share customer data or prompts with third parties for model training. SAP Sovereign Cloud offers data, operational, technical, and legal sovereignty.

SAP provides sovereignty across data, operational, technical, and legal dimensions. Depending on the offering, capabilities can support local data storage, approved operational personnel, locally managed control planes, tenant separation, and mitigation of foreign-authority influence. SAP also provides governance and security controls designed to support requirements associated with the EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, CSA STAR, export controls, and cloud-in-country mandates.

Organizations can start with sovereign AI by identifying the level of sovereignty required for their region, industry, workloads, and regulatory profile.

 

Options range from secure public cloud and locally hosted AI on SAP Cloud Infrastructure, to partner-powered sovereign infrastructure and SAP Sovereign Cloud.

 

Full-sovereignty deployment choices include SAP’s infrastructure as a Service platform SAP Cloud Infrastructure, selected premium hyperscalers, and SAP Sovereign Cloud On-Site in a customer-selected or customer-owned data center. Contact SAP to assess requirements and select the appropriate deployment model.
