Frequently asked questions

The Autonomous Enterprise drives business transformation by fundamentally reshaping how work gets done through AI agents that execute complex, end-to-end workflows autonomously across core business functions like Finance, Supply Chain, Procurement, HCM, and Customer Experience. This transformation moves organizations beyond traditional point-solution AI to enterprise-wide autonomous operations that ground decisions in real-time intelligence, automate entire workflows, and proactively improve every function, enabling companies to outperform expectations while reducing operational complexity.

Joule Assistants understand a user’s role, intent, and business context, and decide what needs to happen to achieve a goal. Joule Assistants coordinate Joule Agents, third-party agents, data, and applications to achieve the desired business outcomes. Once directed by the assistant, agents perform specific tasks such as analyzing data, triggering actions, interacting with systems, and executing steps in a workflow. Assistants are embedded in Autonomous Domains, activated via Joule Work, and are the primary access to Joule Agents.​

SAP Autonomous Suite is our integrated, autonomous portfolio of cloud ERP applications that provide the accurate, structured business data, proven workflows, and established governance models AI agents need to execute on complex enterprise operations.

The Autonomous Enterprise runs end-to-end business processes through autonomous domains organized around core business functions (Finance, Supply Chain, Procurement, HCM, and Customer Experience), where specialized AI agents execute complex, multi-step workflows autonomously while maintaining enterprise controls and governance. These agents move beyond simple insights to supervised execution, coordinating across business systems and domains to handle complete processes, from procurement through manufacturing to finance and sales, without requiring manual handoffs between teams or systems,
