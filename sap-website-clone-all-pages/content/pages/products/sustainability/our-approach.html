Our journey to net zero

SAP is committed to achieve net zero along its value chain in line with achieving a 1.5°C future in 2030.

It's our responsibility to lead by example

Sustainability is an integral part of SAP’s purpose: to help the world run better and improve people’s lives. Our corporate sustainability approach is to create positive economic, social, and environmental impact while respecting planetary boundaries and human rights—with a special focus on holistic steering and ESG reporting, climate action, circular economy, and social responsibility. As a global technology company supporting most of the world’s business, we lead by example as a sustainability practitioner in our own operations and across our value chain.
