FAQ

SAP Autonomous Suite refers to Joule Work, AI agents and assistants, data, and applications that work together to execute end-to-end business processes within and across core business areas.

Autonomous domains refer to the organization of AI assistants and agents that transform how five business areas—finance, supply chain management, spend management, human capital management, and customer experience—run and coordinate to manage end-to-end business processes.

SAP’s Industry AI applications extend the autonomous domain model with vertical depth, embedding sector-specific process knowledge, data models, and regulatory logic directly into AI capabilities from day one.
