Key features

Accelerate supplier lifecycle activities

  • Support both internal initiated requests and optional external requests from potential suppliers.
  • Collect detailed information on certifications, financial stability, and regulatory compliance through modular questionnaires.
  • Enable supplier self-service data entry for faster cycle times and reduced errors.

  • Ensure evaluation relevance by tailoring scope to specific business units or geographic needs.
  • Generate objective overall scores powered by detailed scorecards with transparent, weighted scoring of individual questions.
  • Provide a powerful comparative view to benchmark multiple suppliers.

Surface insights with AI-assisted data

  • Generate AI-driven summaries of complex supplier profiles and activities across the entire lifecycle.
  • Automatically extract and highlight key attributes needed for critical procurement decisions.
  • Proactively monitor documents to track and surface certificate and contract expiration dates.

Unify procurement with Supplier 360 profile

  • Provide sourcing professionals with all critical supplier intelligence in a single view.
  • Eliminate the need to search silos for supplier information, speeding up decision cycles.
  • Ensure every purchasing and contract award decision is made with proactive visibility into supplier risk and compliance.

Frequently asked questions

Supplier lifecycle management is the strategy of managing the processes, interactions, and moments that span the lifecycle of your relationship with suppliers. The right supplier lifecycle management strategy can improve your working relationships with suppliers at virtually every touch point and improve effectiveness across your source-to-pay processes.

A supplier management system is most often a software solution that manages data and processes related to suppliers – all in one place. A supplier management system should simplify and streamline every step of the vendor management lifecycle and help your business create more successful relationships with suppliers. Increasingly, supplier management systems must be able to provide a single source of truth where all supplier data is stored and accessible. Using a supplier management system, your business can perform in-depth analysis on supplier data and explore important insights about supplier risk informing all stages of the source-to-pay process.

The number of phases in the vendor lifecycle varies by industry and depends on your company’s needs as well as the needs of your vendors. No matter how many stages there are in your vendor lifecycle, your strategy for vendor lifecycle management should put vendors at the center of your procurement processes.
