What is SAP Ariba Spend Analysis and Insights?

SAP Ariba Spend Analysis and Insights is spend analytics software that unifies, classifies, and enriches data and connects it with powerful analytics and AI agents.

Turn spend insights into a strategic competitive advantage.

  • All your spending data is unified, classified, and enriched.

  • Targeted visualizations help you find savings, prevent contract leakage, and minimize risk.

  • Smarter, faster decisions are made possible by AI-ready data that surfaces opportunities for spend optimization.

Key features

Automated data ingestion

Automated data ingestion from SAP-managed source systems makes data accessible within minutes. Flexible integration channels support customer-managed source systems, including SAP on-premises and non-SAP datasets.

Intelligent spend classification

The solution maps your unified, classified, and enriched spend data to UNSPSC v25 or your custom taxonomy at 95% accuracy. AI and machine learning (ML) models are trained to learn your business, and human experts audit classifications for accuracy by drawing upon SAP’s 20-plus years of experience in scaling enterprise-wide spend analysis.

Powerful analytics

Powerful analytics help answer not just “Where did we spend?” but also “Where should we have spent?” and “Where should we spend now and in the future?”, providing insights across cost savings, risk exposure, compliance, ESG, and productivity.

AI-generated recommendations

AI agents analyze your data to generate insights and recommendations across various opportunity types such as cost savings, compliance, supplier consolidation, risk mitigation, and value from procurement, so you get decisions rather than dashboards.

Efficient execution to drive outcomes

SAP Spend Analysis and Insights is linked to SAP Ariba Category Management and the entire source-to-pay platform to enable efficient execution, whether it's strategically coordinated, AI-driven, or delegated to AI agents ready to act on your behalf.
