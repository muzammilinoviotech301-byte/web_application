Frequently asked questions

Supplier management is the end-to-end strategic process unifying all procurement activities with their suppliers. Encompassing information management, performance management, risk management, and supplier collaboration, it provides a 360-degree view of a supplier’s value throughout the supplier lifecycle.

Supplier management and supplier relationship management refer to two distinct yet overlapping scopes of activity within procurement. Supplier relationship management is about the strategy and collaboration used to manage the most critical suppliers in order to achieve greater business value. Supplier management is more holistic and involves the system and processes needed to manage all your suppliers.

SAP Ariba Supplier Management includes two solutions: SAP Ariba Supplier Lifecycle and Performance supports all the processes and information required in every step of the supplier lifecycle, from onboarding to qualification to ongoing monitoring and assessment. SAP Ariba Supplier Risk is focused on delivering risk due diligence across the source-to-pay process.
