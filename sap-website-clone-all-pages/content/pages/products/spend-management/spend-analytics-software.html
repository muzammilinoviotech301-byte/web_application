What is SAP Ariba Spend Analysis and Insights?

SAP Ariba Spend Analysis and Insights is new spend management software that unifies, classifies, and enriches data and connects it with powerful analytics, AI-generated recommendations, and AI agents.

Turn spend insights into a strategic competitive advantage

Spend Intelligence flips the old Pareto principle (80% data preparation, 20% insights)—all your spending is unified, classified, enriched—AI-ready and infused with benchmarks to support faster, smarter decisions.

Key benefits

Increase operational and cost efficiency

SAP’s data product architecture will eliminate data extraction, transformation, and loading, reducing operational costs and time to value.

Classified spend today, new possibilities tomorrow

Connecting classified spending with AI-ready business insights—going beyond just analytics—will help identify opportunities across cost, risk, productivity, and more.

Optimize spending with AI

Trusted, guided, and actionable insights create future outcomes for procurement, when they can be easily acted upon, whether part of the process or by agents on your behalf.

Key features

Automated data ingestion

Automated data ingestion from SAP-managed source systems – SAP Cloud ERP, SAP Ariba, SAP Fieldglass, and SAP Concur – with data accessible within minutes. Customer-managed source systems, whether SAP on-premise, non-SAP, or third-party datasets, are supported through flexible integration channels.

Powerful analytics

Spend analytics that tell you not only where money was spent but also where it should be spent help you achieve your cost savings, risk exposure, compliance, ESG, and productivity objectives.

AI-generated recommendations

Agents analyze your data to generate insights and recommendations across various opportunity types like cost savings, compliance, supplier consolidation, risk mitigation, or value from procurement. This can provide you with decisions, not dashboards.s.
