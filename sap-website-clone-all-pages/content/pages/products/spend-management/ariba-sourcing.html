What is SAP Ariba Sourcing software?

SAP Ariba Sourcing software helps navigate rising economic pressures and supply chain challenges by streamlining sourcing with AI, automation, and integration for faster, smarter, and sustainable outcomes.

Simplify sourcing events, reduce costs, and scale with confidence. SAP Ariba Sourcing software offers:

  • AI-driven automation from sourcing request to award through Joule.

  • Seamless sourcing integration with broader procurement processes.

  • A vast global supplier network for choice, competition, and innovation.

Key features

Streamlined sourcing processes

  • Consolidate sourcing across categories to launch AI-assisted events that increase volume leverage and lower costs.

  • Increase competitiveness with smart scoring.

  • Optimize awards using advanced simulations.

  • Compare bids instantly with AI summaries, historical data, custom ranges, and advanced filters.

Accelerated sourcing with Joule

  • Use AI to streamline workflows, qualify suppliers, manage event creation, and determine next steps.

  • Quickly navigate key sourcing workflows, such as requests, project creation, and task management.

  • Automate bid reminders, approvals, and process steps with intelligent in-system automation.

Supplier discovery and onboarding

  • Search for suppliers by industry, item, or service needs.

  • Connect and collaborate directly with suppliers through integration with SAP Business Network Discovery.

  • Access trusted profiles with certifications, ESG data, and assessments.
