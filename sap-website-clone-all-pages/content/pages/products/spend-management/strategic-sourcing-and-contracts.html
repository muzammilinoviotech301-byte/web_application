Frequently asked questions

SAP Ariba supports the full source-to-contract process by integrating sourcing, supplier management, and  contract creation in one cloud-based platform,  packaged as SAP Strategic Procurement. This package enables strategic sourcing events, automates supplier onboarding, streamlines contract authoring with templates and clause libraries, and ensures visibility and control across procurement workflows—all while integrating with ERP systems for seamless data flow.

Using a single platform for source-to-contract processes improves visibility, reduces manual work, and ensures data consistency across sourcing, supplier management, and contracts. It streamlines workflows and enables smarter decisions by connecting all procurement stages in one integrated solution, leading to faster sourcing cycle times and supplier onboarding, reduced costs, and greater compliance.
