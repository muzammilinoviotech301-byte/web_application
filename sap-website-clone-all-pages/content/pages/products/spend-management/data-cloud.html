FAQ

Spend Intelligence seamlessly connects to your spend data, providing real-time insights that help reduce procurement costs, manage supplier risk, and improve operational efficiency.

Data products provide the foundation—pre-built data products that unify customer touchpoints with operational data from your SAP applications. This includes customer journey data, sales activity data, digital channel data, and service interaction data.

 

The intelligent content layer sits on top of this foundation, combining prebuilt reporting and analytics; AI and machine learning; MyMetrics (KPIs); semantic models; and planning models. Data products provide the governed data foundation while intelligent content delivers the AI-enabled insights and actions layer that revenue teams use daily.
