What is SAP Ariba Direct Materials Sourcing for Automotive and Industrial Manufacturing in SAP S/4HANA?

It is direct procurement software that helps solve industry-specific source-to-contract problems by streamlining the process and integrating it with engineering, supply chain, and payables workflows.

Place procurement at the center of the product lifecycle and integrate it with design and supply chain flows through:

  • NPDI planning and design collaboration to accelerate time to market

  • Comprehensive contract management, including index-based pricing, periodic renegotiation, and AI-powered contract intelligence

  • A center-led procurement operating model

  • Structured, workflow-driven, cross-functional collaboration

Key benefits

Accelerate time to market

Bring profitable, innovative, and sustainable products to market with speed and precision through effective procurement planning and design collaboration.

Prevent value leakage from negotiated contracts

Operationalize the complex terms and pricing conditions of procurement contracts and long-term agreements without the cost of system and data integration.

Reduce cost and better manage price volatility

Simplify demand aggregation, price renegotiation, and commodity-pricing management to reduce cost and risk in product launches and ongoing procurement.

Key features

Procurement project planning for product launches

  • BOM part lists input from ERP/PLM systems

  • Demand consolidation across the portfolio and product lifecycle

  • Multi-level project planning and stakeholder collaboration for tooling investments

  • Intelligent parts bundling with seamless transition from planning to execution

Seamless digital thread unifies process end-to-end

  • One digital thread runs through the design-to-contract process, seamlessly integrating with extended supply chain processes

  • Address a full range of direct procurement scenarios, including forward sourcing, demand-driven sourcing, strategic sourcing, and contract renewal/renegotiation

Advanced collaboration

  • Collaborate with suppliers through SAP Integrated Product Development (SAP IPD) integration

  • Seamless integration with ERP/MRP for demand and forecast collaboration, supported by SAP Business Network

  • Flexible RFQ collaboration through SAP Business Network or company-specific supplier portals

End-to-end sourcing and contracting support

  • Create and execute sourcing projects for materials that are part of material masters

  • Engage in multiple rounds of negotiation, evaluate and compare supplier bids, and award business

  • Benefit from company-wide procurement policies through configurable workflows and approved supplier lists

Accelerated price renegotiation

  • Select multiple items across multiple contracts to renegotiate price

  • Simulate the impact of price changes on overall saving

  • Engage in multiple rounds of negotiation with suppliers, award the final negotiated price, and update the central contract(s) automatically based on the approval process

Commodity index-based pricing

  • Obtain commodity content inputs from suppliers in the sourcing process

  • Operationalize index-based commodity pricing, without complex custom builds

  • Simulate and calculate pricing efficiently by accessing market data through integration and system-triggered lookups to commodity indexes
