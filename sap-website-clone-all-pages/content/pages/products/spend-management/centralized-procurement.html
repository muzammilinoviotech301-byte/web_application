What is SAP Ariba Central Procurement?

It's a procurement command center that provides control over requisitioning, purchasing, sourcing, and contracts through a single user interface.

Data is unified and procurement processes are orchestrated across multiple ERP systems—without the cost or complexity of changing them.

  • Native integration with SAP ERP, SAP S/4HANA, and SAP Ariba solutions

  • Standard APIs for third-party ERP integration

  • A clear view of requisitions, orders, spending, and payments

  • Global control and visibility while transacting in local ERP systems

Key features

All-in-one command center

  • Centralize compliance, process control, approval workflows, and document output using a single procurement command center

  • Deliver global control and visibility while transacting in local back-end systems​

  • Manage procurement responsibilities centrally

Unified requisitioning experience

  • Free up purchasers’ time by reducing repetitive tasks through a powerful automation framework

  • Create requisitions centrally for all connected back ends using the guided buying capability in SAP Ariba solutions

  • Harmonize workflows for approvals, confirmations, and returns

Central demand aggregation

  • Aggregate and bundle requisitions to take advantage of better volume pricing

  • Create and publish central RFQs from external PRs

  • Generate follow-on back-end POs or central contracts from quotations

Enterprise contract orchestration

  • Negotiate and monitor global contracts for legal entities across your enterprise

  • Orchestrate operational contracts for direct and indirect materials company-wide and distribute details to back-end ERPs for local execution

  • Enable contract distribution at a header or line-item level

Central analytics

  • Monitor the health of your procurement process for continuous process improvement

  • Track central purchase contract consumption and global spend

  • Drill down to get a quick and accurate look at operational details and monitor requisition metrics, such as cycle times and touchless rates
