What is SAP Ariba Intake Management?

SAP Ariba Intake Management software provides a single, AI-driven front door to non-procurement business users for initiating, orchestrating, and tracking non-catalog purchases.

Eliminate the need for users to navigate multiple systems or track down approvals and ensure a seamless, efficient experience with:

  • Context-aware AI to assist requesters in real time

  • AI grounded in policy to guide compliant intake

  • Flexibility to adapt workflows quickly as business needs evolve

  • Custom intake-to-procure workflows with drag-and-drop simplicity

Key features

AI-assisted single front door for demand intake

A context-aware AI assistant engages users by asking the right questions, gathering details, and guiding them step by step. It grounds responses in your policy documents, acting as a real-time compliance advisor to reduce errors and ensure the right process from the start.

Intake dashboard

Designed for end users, the intake dashboard offers a simple way to submit and track procurement requests in real time. It shows request status at a glance and removes the need for e-mail follow-ups. Its flexible design adapts to evolving needs for a fast, efficient experience.

No-Code Platform For Custom Workflows and AI Skills

SAP Build’s no-code, drag-and-drop interface simplifies building custom intake workflows. And with Joule Studio, a capability of SAP Build, you can create automations, business rules, and AI skills to guide users, integrate systems, and manage complex scenarios.
