What is SAP Fieldglass Services Procurement?

Procurement teams face a complex set of challenges in managing external services. SAP’s services procurement solution helps track the who, what, where, when, why, and how of each project to streamline processes, reduce risk, and meet business goals.

Manage external services—including consulting, marketing, maintenance, plant shutdown, repair, and security—with one holistic solution.

  • Cloud deployment

  • Project management office dashboard to govern statements of work (SOWs)

  • Resource management and allocation, service entry sheets, and time processing

  • Intuitive interface with AI-generated SOWs, chatbots, and decision wizard

Key benefits

Secure better outcomes and cost optimization

Track and manage services effectively using a multi-user collaborative digital workspace to get a clear view of service spending with diverse metrics and workstreams.

Mitigate exposure to hidden risks

Improve digital and physical security by rapidly establishing, monitoring, and maintaining a clear chain of custody for every worker.

Boost productivity and results

Complete simple or complex projects quickly and efficiently with built-in flexibility, AI tools, an intuitive interface, and streamlined processes.

Data-driven decision making

Optimize service quality, cost, and speed with accurate and detailed reporting and advanced analytics to augment decision making.

Track resource time and costs automatically

Automate time processing for multiple SOWs, workers, and bill vendors based on agreed contractual rates.

Manage compliance and mitigate risk with confidence

Ensure compliance with local tax and labor regulations, including those in highly regulated industries, to meet government mandates.
