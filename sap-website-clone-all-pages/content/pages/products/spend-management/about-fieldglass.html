What are Fieldglass solutions?

Fieldglass solutions are cloud-based, external workforce management software products designed to help companies manage external workers; namely contingent workers and service providers.

External workers are essential for building a strong, scalable workforce that can adapt to shifting business demands. Using Fieldglass solutions, you can transform your external workforce into a versatile, value-driving catalyst for growth. You'll have the tools you need to source, engage, and manage external talent efficiently and securely while ensuring compliance across global markets.

Key components include:

  • Contingent workforce management: Source, onboard, and manage temporary workers with ease.

  • Services procurement: Optimize spend and performance for project-based services.

  • Worker profile management: Maintain a complete view of your external workforce.

What’s more, when you manage external labor as part of a total workforce management (TWM) strategy—one that unifies both employees and external talent into a single, holistic view—you can gain the agility, visibility, and control needed to strengthen your competitive edge.

FAQ

Fieldglass is a cloud-based external workforce management system that helps organizations manage their contingent workforce and services procurement. It enables businesses to source, engage, and manage workers and service providers efficiently while maintaining compliance and controlling costs.

Fieldglass is used by HR, procurement, and talent management teams in organizations of all sizes and industries.

Fieldglass pricing varies based on an organization's needs. Models include annual subscriptions and consumption-based pricing. As your program scales and spend increases, unit costs typically decrease.

Key benefits of using Fieldglass include faster sourcing and onboarding of contingent and service workers, reduced risk through automated compliance, and cost savings and efficiency with real-time analytics and process automation.

Fieldglass provides real-time, configurable reporting with cross-module data integration, enabling comprehensive analytics with minimal technical effort.
