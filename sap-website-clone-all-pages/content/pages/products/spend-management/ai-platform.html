Frequently asked questions

SAP Business AI Platform helps you get more from your existing spend systems by unifying data, integrations, and innovation in one intelligent foundation. By connecting SAP and non-SAP solutions, you can eliminate silos, streamline operations, and gain real-time insights that drive smarter, faster decisions and better business outcomes.

With intuitive low-code tools and process automation, SAP Business AI Platform makes it easy to tailor your spend management workflows to your organization’s needs. You can quickly build new procurement apps, automate or enhance existing processes, and embed AI and analytics to improve visibility and control. The result is greater agility, more personalized user experiences, and continuous innovation at scale.

SAP Integration Suite, which runs on SAP Business AI Platform, connects data and processes across SAP and third-party systems to create a seamless, end-to-end view of your operations. Prebuilt connectors and APIs simplify integration, while automation and machine learning help optimize spend management. By turning data into actionable insight, you can improve governance, increase efficiency, and accelerate strategic growth.
