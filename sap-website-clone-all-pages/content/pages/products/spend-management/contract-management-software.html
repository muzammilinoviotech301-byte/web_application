What is SAP Ariba Contracts?

SAP Ariba Contracts combines AI and automation to modernize and elevate your contract management lifecycle—helping you reduce costs, strengthen supplier relationships, and improve business outcomes.

Integrate contract data across your source-to-pay processes to boost savings, find opportunities, and stop value leakage. SAP Ariba Contracts offers:

  • Efficient task management through a single contract workspace.

  • Powerful search and reporting features.

  • Real-time monitoring of spend history and compliance.

  • An AI-based Contracts Agent with Joule and contextual guidance with WalkMe.

Key features

Centralized contract management

  • Instantly view contract status, owners, suppliers, and dates using detailed lists and powerful search tools.

  • View and manage contracts in one workspace, keeping track of status, documents, approvals, and collaboration.

  • Create and manage contract items using organized pricing, terms, and details.

AI-enabled workflows

  • Use natural language to create and summarize contracts, and capture important information.

  • Get role-based WalkMe guidance with walkthroughs and tooltips.

  • Facilitate contract authoring, clause assembly, and redlining with embedded SAP Ariba Contract Intelligence by Icertis capabilities.

Built-in analytics and collaboration

  • Gain visibility through embedded analytics and notifications, including contract metadata like expiration dates and notification periods.

  • Monitor contract spending and consumption to drive compliance. Expedite negotiations through direct collaboration with suppliers and internal teams.
