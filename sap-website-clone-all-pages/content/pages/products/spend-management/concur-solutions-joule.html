Concur solutions, Joule

Get smart support to minimize errors and simplify expense submission.

Overview

Leverage Joule with Concur solutions to significantly reduce the complexities of spend management and ensure that expense reports are ready for submission with minimal effort. This means fewer policy alerts, fewer returned expense reports, and less time spent on reviews and approvals. Travel and expense processes become seamless, efficient, and fully supported by SAP’s commitment to integrate relevant, reliable, and responsible cutting-edge AI technology.

Benefits

  • Increases employee satisfaction with simplified expense management
  • Decreases time-on-task with AI-assisted expense creation
  • Improves compliance using Joule to complete transactions and help enforce company policies

Business Value

  • 95% greater efficiency in retrieving information and help resources
  • 5% faster expense report completion through quicker access to navigational and transactional tasks
  • Greater usability with user-friendly, natural language guided interfaces
