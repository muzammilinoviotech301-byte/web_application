Key features

Dynamic clause assembly

This feature automatically inserts clauses based on deal attributes such as region, contract type, and governance law. It seamlessly integrates clause insertion with preconfigured workflows for faster review and approvals while also speeding up authoring cycles and document generation, significantly reducing turnaround time.

Real-time collaboration using Microsoft Word

Collaboration in Microsoft Word enables multiple internal stakeholders and suppliers to use Word online editing to coauthor and redline contracts simultaneously, eliminating version control issues and accelerating negotiations.

Deviation analysis on redlined clauses

This feature automatically identifies and highlights deviations from standard or preferred clause language, enabling legal and business teams to assess risk and approve exceptions more efficiently.
