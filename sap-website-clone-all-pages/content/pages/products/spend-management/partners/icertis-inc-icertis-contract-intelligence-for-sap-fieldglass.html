Transform your SOWs using Icertis Contract Intelligence (ICI) for SAP Fieldglass

Icertis' advanced contract lifecycle management platform integrates with SAP Fieldglass, optimizing downstream execution of labor-based indirect spend. ICI for SAP Fieldglass combines these best-of-breed solutions to help organizations increase efficiency, enforce compliance, and gain visibility.

By Icertis Inc.

This solution is premium-certified for SAP integration and best practices.

Overview

Accelerate contract turnaround times

Seamless integration with SAP Fieldglass enables organizations to quickly author and negotiate contractual terms closer to their clause positions and operationalize them.

Effectively manage legal and business risk

Predefined legal templates, corporate policies, and constant monitoring of contractual obligations and SLAs (Service Level Agreements) enable organizations to effectively manage legal and business risks.

Realize the full value of every contract

Seamless data integration ensures that negotiated commercial terms are enforced during the procurement and payable processes, helping maximize the value of every contract.

Orchestrate end-to-end business processes leveraging best-in-class capabilities

Streamlined management of the SOW process ensures a smooth spend cycle through authoring, operationalization, management, and termination of a wide range of SOW and payment types.

Standardize, streamline and automate every contract - everywhere

Bring together buy-side, sell-side, and corporate contracts and processes, providing unparalleled contract visibility, velocity, and standardization across your organization. Alerts provide the certainty that mission-critical commitments and milestones aren’t missed.
