Simplified sourcing intake

Streamline sourcing projects with efficient capture and conversion of project information for faster execution.

Execute sourcing projects from initial request to RFx delivery with a solution that helps you:

  • Easily capture sourcing project information and requirements with an intuitive intake form.

  • Receive sourcing requests from integrated systems and convert them into sourcing projects, propagating associated information to the new RFx document.

  • Collect structured data from casual users, ensuring all necessary details are captured.

AI‑assisted sourcing from request to award

Unify sourcing intake, event creation, and award with AI-‑generated recommendations that increase productivity, accelerate decisions, and streamline execution.

Enhance productivity with advanced AI and machine learning capabilities that help you:

  • Drive better sourcing outcomes by creating events faster with AI-generated content recommendations, comparing best-matched suppliers and bids, and tracking progress and pricing in real time.

  • Use Joule to navigate quickly to key workflows such as sourcing requests, project creation, and task management.

  • Reduce manual effort and accelerate processes by automating routine tasks such as bid reminders, event status checks, and approvals.

Supplier discovery

SAP Business Network Discovery is integrated with SAP Ariba Sourcing, making it easier to find and select the best suppliers.

Easily connect with reliable, diverse suppliers worldwide with a network that allows you to:

  • Find alternative sources during disruptions, uncover new supplier opportunities with speed, and drive competitive pricing.

  • Search for suppliers based on specific industry, item, or service needs.

  • Find robust and trusted information in supplier profiles including certifications; environmental, social, and governance (ESG) data; and assessments.

  • Select top suppliers and add them directly to your sourcing event—without leaving the event screen.

Supplier network collaboration

Collaborate securely on SAP Business Network to boost efficiency and streamline workflows.

Explore capabilities that help you find, evaluate, and engage the right suppliers—all in one connected experience—to:

  • Invite your strategic suppliers to participate in sourcing events by joining the network and to share data and proprietary information in a secure environment.

  • View detailed supplier profiles and use collaboration tools to request pricing or availability.

  • Use filters to refine supplier searches by category, keywords, certifications, and sustainability criteria.

WalkMe for SAP Ariba Sourcing

Stay focused, confident, and prepared for what’s next though integration of WalkMe with guided sourcing for intelligent, in-workflow guidance.

Get step-by-step guidance for faster, smarter sourcing that helps you:

  • Enhance task completion, reduce errors, and accelerate processes with detailed walkthroughs, contextual guidance, and automated data entry.

  • Identify workflow bottlenecks, pain points, and inefficiencies through advanced analytics to streamline tasks and eliminate unnecessary steps.

  • Guide users with personalized messages and a conversational interface, delivering task-specific support for a more intuitive and engaging experience.

Bid analysis and optimization

Use AI to compare scenarios, optimize awards, and select strategies that maximize value.

Accelerate sourcing decisions with AI and natural language capabilities that let you:

  • Compare bids with advanced filters and confidently trigger single or split awards.

  • Extract key insights from supplier responses with AI-assisted bid summaries that surface risks, opportunities, and anomalies in real time.

  • Optimize awards using a natural language–based scenario setup.

  • Align stakeholders by sharing constraints, using preconfigured optimization templates, and evaluating multiple scenarios with clear, side-by-side comparisons.
