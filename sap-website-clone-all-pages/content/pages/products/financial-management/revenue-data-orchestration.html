SAP Revenue Data Orchestration by DigitalRoute

A solution purpose-built to track and orchestrate data that will be used for billing and by other business systems
Request a demo

Adding business value by enabling and controlling new revenue streams

With the SAP Revenue Data Orchestration solution by DigitalRoute (formerly named SAP Convergent Mediation by DigitalRoute), you can collect data from multiple streams and sources and provide unified control and visibility of revenue stream processes.

Optimize data monetization and impact your bottom line

SAP Revenue Data Orchestration helps convert usage and transactional data into revenue, so you can support new and existing business models.

  • Unified real-time data processing for revenue-critical applications

  • Automated error detection, data deduplication, and filtering

  • Data enrichment by combining information of any type or source

Key benefits

Faster introduction of new business models

Convert usage and transactional data into revenue and support new business models alongside existing ones in your financial systems.

Visibility into revenue stream processing

Enable data collection from multiple streams and sources and provide unified control and visibility of revenue stream processes.

Less revenue loss and revenue leakage

Prepare data properly to prevent revenue leakage, expedite customer billing processes, and provide accurate data in a timely manner to drive profits and growth.

Key features

Business models for the connected economy

  • Connect siloed systems and data and translate their native data feeds for your business systems
  • Identify errors and inconsistencies and eliminate the need for multiple integration applications
  • Limit slowdowns caused by inflexible partners, vendors, and systems and eliminate revenue leakage

Monetization of digital offerings

  • Streamline data collection processes and provide consistent, clean data for consolidated billing processes
  • Use purpose-built functionality that helps address the issues that typically generate revenue leakage
  • Scale to support the massive data sets generated daily by a digitally enabled business

Capitalization on use cases enterprise-wide

  • Apply transaction-based data from billing systems to drive new sources of revenue
  • Use consumer data to allow fraud management software to reduce costs and risk and improve operational processes
  • Leverage financial data to support accounting, bookkeeping, governance, and regulatory compliance
