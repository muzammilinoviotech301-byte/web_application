What is SAP Accounting Automation by BlackLine?

SAP Accounting Automation by Blackline is a solution extension that helps finance professionals with record-to-report processing by turning fragmented close activities into a single connected process.

The real-time integration of SAP Accounting Automation with SAP Advanced Financial Closing solutions helps you:

 

  • Connect process templates that cover various tasks and structured accounting work.

  • Manage closing tasks from a single source.

  • Gain a connected, comprehensive view of accounting activities across systems.

Key benefits

Streamline fragmented close activities

Coordinate and manage sub-tasks, standardize and automate accounting activities, and enable real-time comprehensive status across the enterprise close.

Standardize and automate close activities

Define standardized, activity-level subtasks with process templates based on best practices from real-world experiences of SAP and BlackLine customers.

Coordinate activities without losing context

Gain unified closing oversight in SAP Advanced Financial Closing by importing process templates and their activity status directly from SAP Accounting Automation.

Extend automation across the enterprise close

Combine activity types into comprehensive processes to automate recurring work, reduce spreadsheet dependence, and connect upstream and downstream steps.

Key features

Template-based process modeling

Embed execution in the close process by importing templates from Accounting Automation directly into Advanced Financial Closing. Process templates capture best practices and define various activity-level sub-tasks including imports, standard matching, and variance checks.

Single connected enterprise close plan in one view

Reduce manual handoffs, consolidate fragmented closing tasks, and avoid delayed updates by embedding execution into a single connected close plan that displays activities across systems in Advanced Financial Closing.

Real-time status synchronization across systems

Give teams a real-time view of progress, completions, and errors within the close plan. Status and outcome updates flow instantly from Accounting Automation directly into Advanced Financial Closing.

Automated accounting task execution

Automate tasks and structured accounting work such as advanced transaction matching, imports, automatic journals, and reconciliations. Advanced Financial Closing schedules activities and tracks dependencies from a single source.

Management of file-based import routines

Use the connectivity center to organize and manage frequently used file-based data import routines.
