Frequently asked questions

Treasury and working capital management software from SAP is a software solution that is designed to help organizations manage their treasury and working capital more effectively and efficiently. Here are some of the software’s key features and functionalities:

  • Cash management

  • Financial risk, debt, and investment management

  • Payments and bank communication

  • Receivables and payables management

  • SAP Taulia Dynamic Discounting, Taulia Supply Chain Finance, Taulia Receivables, and Taulia Virtual Cards

By using treasury and working capital management software from SAP, organizations can improve their financial performance, reduce risks, enhance operational efficiency, and gain real-time insights into their financial health.

Treasury and working capital management software from SAP helps improve cash visibility and liquidity through several key features and functionalities, such as:

  1. Real-time cash positioning and instance cash balances
  2. Short-term, medium-term, and long-term cash forecasting
  3. Liquidity planning
  4. Bank connectivity and statement reconciliation
  5. Cash AI agent as a digital assistant 

By leveraging these features, the solution enables organizations to enhance cash visibility, optimize liquidity, reduce borrowing costs, and make informed decisions on cash management and investment strategies.

SAP Working Capital Management (WCM) improves visibility into receivables and payables through a range of features and functionalities designed to provide organizations with real-time insights into their financial position, such as:

  • Accounts receivables and payables aging analysis

  • Real-time receivables and payables monitoring

  • Customer credit risk and supplier risk assessment

  • Supplier invoice matching and approval

  • Payment planning, scheduling, and forecasting

  • Dispute and collection management, DSO and DPO KPIs

SAP WCM helps organizations gain real-time visibility into their receivables and payables, optimize cash flow, reduce financial risks, and improve overall financial performance.
