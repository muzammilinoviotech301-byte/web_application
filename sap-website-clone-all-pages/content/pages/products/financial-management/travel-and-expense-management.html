Frequently asked questions

SAP Concur is a portfolio of solutions that automate and integrate travel, expense, and invoice management. As part of the larger SAP family of companies, SAP Concur solutions simplify spend management and help organizations operate in more efficient and cost-effective ways.

When organizations don’t know how much cash is coming in and going out, it can lead to big problems like budget shortfalls. By automating the expense management process, organizations can control spend, reduce errors and fraud, and base decisions on complete and accurate data.

As part of SAP Finance Premium, Concur Expense includes 100 monthly expense reports, native integration with SAP Cloud ERP, and Concur Analysis Reporting.

An SAP Concur account executive can provide details and pricing for any additional solution needs you may have to address your business requirements.
