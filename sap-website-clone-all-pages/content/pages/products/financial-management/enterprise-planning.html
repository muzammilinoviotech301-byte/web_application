What is SAP Enterprise Planning?

SAP Enterprise Planning is a unified planning solution combining tactical and strategic planning with an agentic layer that continuously senses, reasons, and acts on enterprise data in real time.

Deliver connected enterprise planning with AI agents incorporated from strategy to execution, including:

  • Connected budgeting, forecasting, and performance steering.

  • SAP-managed domain content, KPIs, and data products.

  • Always-on sensing and steering with autonomous agents.

  • Flexible data fabric for compute, storage, and modeling.

Key features

Unified planning, budgeting, and forecasting

Create and manage plans, budgets, and forecasts in one environment using integrated models, versions, and allocations. Operate across strategic, tactical, and operational horizons with consistent governance, enterprise reporting, and AI agents for modeling, planning, and analytics.

Performance sensing and steering

Use always-on agents to monitor internal and external signals against KPIs and plan assumptions. Sensing agents detect deviations and emerging risks, while reasoning agents assess impact and recommend actions, enabling more continuous business steering beyond periodic planning cycles.

SAP-managed domain content and data products

Plan on SAP-managed content with prebuilt KPIs, business logic, and planning structures. Built on curated data products with enterprise-grade accuracy, availability, and governance as a service, content is applied consistently across all angles while remaining fully adaptable to your business needs.

Unified analytics and business insights

Analyze trusted business data with built-in analytics, natural language queries, and AI-assisted insights. Access prebuilt KPIs, models, and data flows while delivering self-service reporting and analytics across SAP and third-party data sources.

Trusted data fabric across SAP and non-SAP data

Connect SAP and third-party data through a governed business data fabric that preserves business context and shared semantics. Use trusted data products, metadata, and a unified foundation for planning, analytics, AI agents, and enterprise decision-making.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Enterprise Planning combines strategic, tactical, and operational decision-making in a single solution. Unlike traditional planning software that relies on periodic planning cycles, it adds an agentic layer that continuously senses change, reasons through business impact, and recommends actions using trusted enterprise data and SAP business content.

SAP Enterprise Planning includes SAP Analytics Cloud for planning as its core engine. It extends SAP Analytics Cloud with SAP-managed domain content, curated data products, SAP Datasphere, and performance sensing and steering capabilities that support continuous, AI-assisted business planning and decision-making.

Agentic workflows use AI agents to monitor business signals, evaluate performance against goals, identify risks and opportunities, and recommend or initiate next actions. These workflows help organizations move from static planning processes to continuous planning and steering.

SAP Enterprise Planning uses trusted enterprise data from SAP Business Data Cloud, including SAP-managed data products, business context, KPIs, and operational data. It can also use non-SAP data from various data estates using the zero-copy concept of SAP Business Data Cloud Connect omitting the need for repeated replication.

SAP Enterprise Planning is built on governed data products, standardized business definitions, and an enterprise-grade platform. SAP-managed content, curated KPIs, and controlled planning models help organizations maintain consistency, transparency, and auditability across planning processes.

SAP Enterprise Planning continuously monitors business conditions, detects deviations from plans and KPIs, and provides recommendations through AI agents. This enables teams to evaluate impact, run scenarios, and take corrective action quickly instead of waiting for periodic planning cycles or manual analysis.

AI agents are embedded across the planning process to support modeling, planning, analysis, sensing, and business steering. They help identify emerging trends, reason through business impacts, generate recommendations, and assist users in making faster, more informed decisions while maintaining human oversight.
