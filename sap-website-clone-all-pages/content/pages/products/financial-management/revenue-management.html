Frequently asked questions

Revenue management solutions from SAP compose a modular portfolio for lead-to-cash processes, connecting sales, billing, cash collection, and revenue recognition. They support products, services, projects, subscriptions, and usage-based offerings with integrated processes, automation, and visibility across the revenue lifecycle.

Revenue management solutions from SAP support audit-ready, traceable lead-to-cash processes and help align revenue handling with ASC 606 and IFRS 15. They support complex scenarios such as subscriptions, usage-based pricing, and bundled offerings by improving data consistency and coordination across sales and finance, helping reduce manual effort and compliance risk.

Revenue management solutions from SAP support subscription- and usage-based models with flexible packaging and pricing, recurring and usage billing, and scalable processing of high-volume events and charges. They help improve billing accuracy, reduce revenue leakage, and keep revenue recognition aligned through contract changes, invoicing, and cash collection.

Lead-to-cash covers the revenue lifecycle from early customer engagement through price optimization, quoting, contracting, billing, cash collection, and revenue recognition. It spans multiple teams and systems, so consistent data and handoffs are critical. Revenue management solutions from SAP help connect these steps to keep revenue processes accurate, compliant, and scalable as business models evolve.

Margin optimization is the practice of maximizing net margin by taking control of every factor that affects it, from pricing and trade promotions to channel incentives, vendor programs, and royalty management. SAP offers integrated cloud-based solutions that provide comprehensive gross-to-net visibility, enabling price optimization. By governing margin decisions in a unified portfolio, organizations gain the insights and control needed to align pricing and incentive strategies with profitability goals.
