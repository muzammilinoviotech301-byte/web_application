Frequently asked questions

SAP tax and trade management solutions are a comprehensive suite of cohesive and modular tools that enable companies to automate tax and trade operations worldwide and strengthen their resilience to stay ready for what’s ahead.

SAP Global Trade Services (SAP GTS), an application within SAP solutions for tax and trade management, connects with core logistics processes and government customs systems to help ensure compliant, efficient import and export management. It helps fulfill compliance duties by checking for embargoes, managing licenses, and using inline blocking and release.

 

It also blocks for immediate review any transaction that fails a sanctioned party list screening. Worklists enable efficient processing of blocked documents and user-friendly workflows escalate questionable entries.

SAP GTS streamlines an organization’s customs clearance processes and supports regulatory compliance by ensuring accurate product categorization for trade documentation and determining the relevant tariff codes for import and export procedures.

Yes, with SAP’s integrated tax management solutions, companies can digitize and standardize operations with a real-time, audit-ready solution to fulfill e-invoicing and tax reporting regulations worldwide. SAP offers solutions to automate control execution, monitor exceptions, and manage risk in real time—reducing manual effort, improving audit outcomes, and enhancing compliance agility.

 

Customers can stay up to date with the regulatory change manager tool, which monitors regulatory changes to help customers stay compliant and run their day-to-day business without disruption. 

With an extended suite of solutions, SAP capabilities for tax and trade management cover all deployment models: On premises, cloud, and hybrid.

SAP GTS streamlines compliance with new trade dynamics and accelerates cross-border supply chain operations with an integrated platform. It has four main functional areas:

  • Compliance management

  • Customs management 

  • Special customs procedures 

  • Trade preference management

 

 

SAP Trade Management is a separate suite that consists of the following offerings:

  • SAP Trade Promotion Planning and Management

  • SAP Customer Business Planning

  • SAP Advanced Trade Management Analytics

 

Tax and trade operations management software from SAP improves global compliance by unifying and automating critical processes across jurisdictions, reducing manual errors, and ensuring that companies always operate according to the latest international regulations. 

SAP tax and trade operations management software is specifically designed to support multi-country tax regulations by offering solutions that automate global operations and ensure compliance with local laws and mandates. The software helps automate tax reporting and e-document creation and submission across jurisdictions worldwide.
