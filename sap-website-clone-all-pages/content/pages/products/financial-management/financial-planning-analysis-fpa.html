Frequently asked questions

FP&A software models outcomes, monitors results, and guides decisions using financial and operational data. It helps organizations support budgeting, planning, forecasting, and performance analysis.

Data fabric lets planning teams direct access to company data and on-site processing. It enables contextual data into planning models, immediately analysis of plans, and model improvement through machine learning and external data without requiring data to be moved or duplicated.

The planning capabilities in SAP Analytics Cloud support tactical and strategic planning through structured, model‑driven Plan–Do–Check–Act cycles—such as for budgeting, forecasting, and long‑term scenario analysis.

 

SAP Enterprise Planning extends this foundation with a rapid, agentic planning layer and shared domain content designed for short‑term, ad‑hoc decision‑making. It enables organizations to sense change, reason through impact, and act immediately, so they can make in‑the‑moment operational decisions that are not feasible with traditional planning cycles.

 

Together, the solutions cover the full planning spectrum: structured planning for medium‑ and long‑term alignment, and flexible, real‑time planning for rapid operational steering.

FP&A teams can use assistants that deploy agents to sense change, analyze impacts across scenarios, and support plan updates—helping teams move from periodic planning to continuous, insight‑driven decisions.

The most directly relevant AI agents for planning are the Conversational Planning Agent, Financial Forecasting Agent, Model Creation Agent, Variance Analysis Agent, and Market Sentiment Agent. In addition, the Financial Planning Assistant can orchestrate specialized AI agents to support continuous planning, forecasting, scenario analysis, and plan updates.
