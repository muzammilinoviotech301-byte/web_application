Key benefits

A single source for contract management

Manage all contracts related to your real estate portfolio in one location, including lease-in, lease-out, customer, and vendor contracts.

Improved sustainability of real estate holdings

Facilitate sustainable property portfolio management that drives greater efficiencies with the help of strategic analytics capabilities.

Businesswide integration of real estate data

Streamline your real estate business processes through native integration with SAP S/4HANA and other connected SAP and third-party solutions.

Key features

Real estate objects management

  • Access a dual view of master data, with an architectural view and a usage view

  • Create and manage all types of real estate objects, including business entity, land, building, rental unit, rental space, and rental room objects

Contract management

  • Assign any number of rental objects from different business entities, buildings, or company codes to any one contract

  • Compliant with government regulations around lease accounting IFRS 16, ASC 842 and GASB 87

Space optimization

  • Account for unusual architectural structures, usage considerations, and technical amenities and features

  • Define spaces flexibly and rent them, extracting them from a larger available space

  • Represent your real estate objects visually via data exchange with external graphic systems

Centralized business processes

  • Integrate asset management postings and reports, real estate controlling, and planning and controlling of new building developments, maintenance, and modernization projects

  • Manage purchase order processing and commitments for real estate maintenance

Intelligent real estate management

  • Integrate SAP S/4HANA for contract, lease, and real estate management with SAP Cloud for Real Estate for SAP Intelligent Real Estate

  • Manage building master data centrally in the cloud to optimize portfolio performance and occupant experience
