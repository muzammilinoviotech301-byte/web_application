The solution also features embedded AI, supporting faster analysis and decision-making while making critical process data more accessible. In addition to uncovering process issues impacting overall performance and guiding performance improvement initiatives, the solution also fosters collaboration across teams and functions. See how SAP Signavio customers drive transformation.

While these solutions are particularly well- uited for business process mining and management across SAP landscapes, they can help any company drive better performance in the core processes the business depends on. Learn about the SAP Business Transformation Management portfolio.
