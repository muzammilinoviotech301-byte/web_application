Frequently asked questions

SAP LeanIX is a cloud-based enterprise architecture management platform that helps organizations visualize, analyze, and manage their IT landscapes. It provides a centralized source of truth for applications, technology components, risks, and roadmaps, enabling faster decisions, improved transparency, and more effective digital transformation.

Enterprise architecture is a strategic framework that aligns an organization’s business strategy, processes, information, and technology to achieve its goals. It consists of all the technology a company relies on along with relevant dependencies and interconnections. Enterprise architecture transformation starts with creating a business capability model and mapping applications to it. This allows companies to ensure that technology supports the business while also enabling the rationalization and modernization of the application and broader IT landscape.

SAP LeanIX features base AI capabilities including AI-powered inventory prompts, AI-assisted text creation, AI-generated context, and AI-supported translation. SAP LeanIX also offers an inventory builder driven by AI that automatically extracts architecture elements from diagrams and images.
