Frequently asked questions

Business transformation management refers to the discipline of guiding and coordinating the changes an organization needs to improve performance, adapt to new conditions, and realize strategic goals. It brings together insights across people, processes, applications, data so companies can understand their current state, plan their future state, and execute transformation in a structured, repeatable way.

SAP offers a Business Transformation Management portfolio of solutions that includes SAP LeanIX, SAP Signavio, and WalkMe. Together, these offerings focus on helping companies develop a scalable, always-on business transformation capability rather than treating transformation as a one-time project.

 

Research shows most organizations undergo several transformations a year, each affecting processes, applications, data, and people. Business Transformation Management solutions from SAP not only reveal how processes, applications, data, and people work today—they also help companies act on those insights by rationalizing their IT landscape, redesigning and implementing processes, and guiding employees through change.

Any industry can benefit from business transformation management tools because every organization relies on technology and processes that people must use effectively. When changes occur, employees need support, and interdependencies across systems and workflows must be understood to avoid disruptions. Organizations with more complex processes or broader technology landscapes often see the greatest impact, but companies of all types can benefit.

Other business transformation management solutions follow their own licensing approaches. Your SAP representative can help you determine the right combination for your needs.

SAP’s Business Transformation Management portfolio, which includes SAP LeanIX, SAP Signavio, and WalkMe, is not an ERP system. It sits alongside your ERP and helps you see your IT landscape, improve how your processes run, and guide your people through change. The solutions integrate deeply with SAP ERP for customers who run it, and they also work across non-SAP systems. Think of the portfolio as the layer that makes transformation repeatable, whether you are moving to a new ERP, consolidating applications, or rolling out new workflows.
