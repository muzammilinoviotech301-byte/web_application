Frequently asked questions

Most SAP mobile apps available in app stores include demo modes that allow you to try each app on an Apple iPhone or iPad and most Android smartphones and tablets.

The SAP applications and solutions you need depend on your industry, business processes, and SAP landscape. Most companies use the Joule Work mobile app as their central entry point for employees and integrate SAP SuccessFactors for human capital management, SAP Service and Asset Manager for supply chain management, or the SAP Concur mobile app for spend management.

 

Contact your IT department or contact SAP to learn more.

SAP’s mobile experiences offer out-of-the-box capabilities for many high-volume use cases that can be found in processes within any line of business.

 

For more individual business challenges, you can opt to create a personalized suite of native mobile and browser-based apps. The SAP development environment helps you create and implement enterprise-grade apps without writing code.

SAP’s mobile apps run natively on popular consumer and professional devices, supporting multi-modal interactions such as voice, touch, and camera. The goal is to support business workers in the most immediate and natural way, for example by just enabling them to ask questions in chats or via voice assistants, take pictures of documents or assets, get notifications on any device, or even be better protected thanks to safety features such as motion sensors. Native apps take full advantage of the latest features of any platform they deeply embed in, making mobile apps the most natural way to work anywhere and collaborate with AI.

SAP mobile apps can offer value to all types of employees, from managers and office workers to deskless workers. SAP’s mobile experiences help IT organizations run holistic enterprise mobility solutions and empower all employees with a mobile-first approach to work.
