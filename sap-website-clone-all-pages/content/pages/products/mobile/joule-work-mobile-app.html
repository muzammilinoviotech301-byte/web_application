Joule Work combines AI productivity tools with deep business context from Joule Agents and Joule Assistants to surface relevant data and automate workflows across SAP and third-party systems.

Conversations allow people to express intent and explore what they want to accomplish through dialogue with Joule. Joule brings together guidance and outcomes from across SAP and third-party systems. Conversations can also act as an entry point to spaces for more complex workflows.

Spaces are dynamic workspaces for simple and complex problem-solving. Each space is composed in real time to bring the right data, tools, and context together for a specific goal, allowing people to work in an environment that adapts to their present goal, instead of navigating applications and passive UIs.​

Frequently asked questions

Joule Work mobile app requires internet access to function and exchange data. Offline capabilities are supported through separate native or offline-enabled apps.

Joule Work mobile app supports secure single sign-on (SSO) using SAML or OIDC and integrates with common identity providers (IdPs) such as SAP IAS, Microsoft Azure AD, Okta, Ping, and ADFS. It works with mobile device management (MDM) and unified endpoint management (UEM) for conditional access, app protection, and remote wipe. Security is further defined by backend authorizations, content security policy, and SAP BTP or SAP Build Work Zone settings to determine the final security posture. Once configured, users can securely access SAP applications without repeated logins.

Joule Work mobile app is a native mobile experience that provides push notifications, a consolidated task inbox, app shortcuts, deep linking, smoother navigation, and access to device features such as cameras, scanners, and widgets. SAP Fiori launchpad is a web-based portal that is only available on mobile devices through a browser.

SAP Build Work Zone, standard edition is a good choice for core portal capabilities such as content aggregation, personalization, and basic mobile scenarios.

 

The advanced edition is better suited for more complex landscapes, such as microapps, custom widgets, richer connectors, scripting, governance, and enterprise-scale integrations.

Yes, you can control what individual users see in the Joule Work mobile app based on roles and permissions. Content visibility can be managed through backend roles (such as SAP S/4HANA or SAP Gateway), SAP BTP role collections, SAP Build Work Zone group rules, and identity provider (IdP) group or claim mappings. This allows you to target content by user, role, department, or pilot cohort. User provisioning and role changes are managed through your IdP and SAP BTP configuration.

You can integrate a wide range of applications into the Joule Work mobile app, including SAP S/4HANA content, such as standard SAP Fiori and SAPUI5 apps, custom web apps, third‑party web apps, microapps, and native mobile apps (SAP or third‑party) that support URL-based scheme launching.

 

Some integrations may require SAP BTP or SAP Build Work Zone connectors, minor configuration or development, or updates to CSP/SSO settings. Embedding behavior and SSO access depend on your security policies and identity provider configuration.

The Joule Work mobile app provides one central entry point to your apps, tasks, and notifications, reducing app switching and simplifying access. It provides a consistent, mobile-optimized UX with device integrations, simplifying rollout and governance while improving user productivity across mobile devices.
