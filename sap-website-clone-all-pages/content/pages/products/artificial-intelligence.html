FAQ

Joule enables intent-driven work across every business function by transforming transactional tasks into intelligent, connected workflows that sharpen decisions and accelerate processes. Grounded in SAP’s harmonized data and trusted architecture, Joule Assistants and Agents deliver reliable performance without compromising on compliance, security, or scale. With Joule, people act with clarity, teams collaborate without siloes, and business-critical processes run autonomously to drive enterprise-wide impact.

Joule differentiates itself from generic AI solutions by offering deep enterprise integration, business-context awareness, and embedded functionality across SAP's cloud portfolio. Unlike other AI solutions, Joule provides role-specific assistance with enterprise-grade security, GDPR compliance, and automatic updates while maintaining complete data isolation within customer SAP tenancies. Joule delivers unique value through pre-trained SAP business process knowledge and seamless access to enterprise data that enables contextual insights without switching between applications.

SAP implements comprehensive data protection measures including data masking and pseudonymization to protect personally identifiable information (PII). Customer data remains within their own SAP tenancy with tenant-level data isolation, and no customer data is stored within Joule. All data processing follows GDPR requirements and SAP's Global Data Protection and Privacy Policy, with data anonymized or pseudonymized where appropriate.

For customers with existing SAP cloud subscriptions you can activate Joule base for free to get started immediately. For customers looking to learn more about how to get started with Joule, you should complete the official Implementing Joule Across your Organizational System Landscape learning available from SAP, which provides step-by-step guidance on system prerequisites, provisioning, configuration, security, and activation within supported SAP cloud solutions. This training helps administrators properly enable Joule in their SAP environment so business users can begin using the AI solution directly within their workflows.

Joule Agents are ready-to-use AI agents that support business users with decision support and task automation that requires non-deterministic workflows. Joule Agents choose from a large set of tools (including Joule skills, other agents, and third-party applications) to action their plans and then reflect and reason on the results to draw conclusions and make recommendations. Grounded by SAP Knowledge Graph and SAP Business Data Cloud, Joule Agents uniquely understand your data and processes to act reliably through business applications.

AI in business helps automate processes, analyze large volumes of data, and improve decision-making across core functions. In finance, it supports risk management and forecasting; in supply chain and procurement, it optimizes planning, supplier management, and inventory; in HR, it enhances recruiting and employee engagement; and in customer experience, it improves customer insights, lead generation, and campaign performance. By embedding AI into business processes, organizations increase efficiency, reduce risk, and drive better outcomes across the enterprise.
