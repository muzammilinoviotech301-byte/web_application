Standardized framework

We distill thousands of past implementations to develop a tailored project plan for your use cases. Recommendations, guidelines, and checkpoints empower you to build a tailored road map grounded in proven best practices.

STEP 1

Discover

Analyze as-is situation, define project scope, plan project, and request project approval.

STEP 2

Prepare

Initiate project preparation and team onboarding by establishing a customized roadmap, including AI activation and a clear success plan.

STEP 3

Explore

Explore capabilities and AI scenarios, run fit-to-standard analysis, and define required extensions.

STEP 4

Realize

Realize value through solution adoption according to your project scope, while managing change and ensuring user adoption.

STEP 5

Deploy

Deploy the configured productive system and ensure quality by conducting final testing.

STEP 6

Run

Run with operational excellence and detect opportunities for continuous innovation.

Accelerate your journey to SAP Autonomous Suite with an agent-led toolchain

Position your enterprise to continuously innovate and capitalize on future developments with an agent-led toolchain that accelerates your transition to SAP Autonomous Suite, modernizes your business, and drives continuous improvement. AI assistants take over resource-intensive tasks, reducing time and effort during migration and modernization.

Our agent-led toolchain helps you transform across all dimensions:

People

  • Guide your organization through technological change, drive effective adoption, and train employees using innovative e-learning content with WalkMe.

Processes

  • Analyze, manage, and optimize your business processes with SAP Signavio solutions to achieve agility that drives successful change and value generation.

Data

  • Migrate and transform your data while increasing data quality with guidance from SAP Business Transformation Center or SAP Advanced Data Migration and Management by Syniti.
  • Reduce your data footprint by slimming historical data while staying compliant and having your documents on hand with SAP Archiving and Document Access by OpenText.

Applications

  • Orchestrate your transformation project with SAP Cloud ALM using SAP Activate project road maps with quality gates and checkpoints for each phase of your journey.
  • Analyze, align, design, and reduce the complexity of your IT architecture to meet your transformation goals using SAP LeanIX solutions.
  • Extend, adapt, and automate SAP Cloud ERP to meet your unique needs with Joule Studio, following clean core principles for continuous innovation.
  • Seamlessly connect and automate your entire business ecosystem using SAP Integration Suite, a secure, AI-enhanced platform designed to simplify connections, automate processes, and drive digital innovation.
  • Accelerate innovation and minimize risk with SAP enterprise application quality assurance solutions by Tricentis, an end-to-end testing solution that automates and optimizes testing processes, ensuring fast and reliable software delivery.

New migration and modernization accelerators

Get support for your most complex, time-intensive transformation activities with AI assistants, including:

  • System Analysis: Builds a trusted, customer‑specific system baseline for a clear starting point.
  • Custom Code: Modernizes custom code to reduce technical debt and enable clean core.
  • Data Management: Prepares and migrates data into a governed cockpit to improve speed.
  • Configuration: Optimizes system configurations based on best practices and customer needs.
  • Test Management: Automates test scope, data, and execution for testing.
  • Rollout: Manages global rollouts with template comparison and automated localization.
  • Project Management: Delivers end‑to‑end guidance, decision support, and training.

Frequently asked questions

The key components of the RISE with SAP Methodology better address the needs of existing on-premises SAP ERP customers. These enhancements include a richer onboarding experience, including enablement on Business Transformation Management tools, SAP BTP, SAP Build, a clean core-focused success plan, and the transformation preparation service for SAP Autonomous Suite.

The transformation preparation service is available to all customers who purchase the Cloud ERP Private package as one of the entitlements.

Quality gates are checkpoints at the end of each phase of RISE with SAP Methodology where you complete a questionnaire. Based on your input, SAP experts respond with feedback and recommendations for next steps and actions to guide your transformation journey.
