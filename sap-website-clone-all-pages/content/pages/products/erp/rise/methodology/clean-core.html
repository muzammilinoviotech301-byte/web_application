What is a clean core strategy?

An ERP clean core strategy helps your organization ensure that business-critical systems remain agile, cost-effective, and ready to adopt innovation.

Clean core is a set of guiding principles that support continuous business transformation and modernization

  • Reduce historically grown complexity and gain agility​

  • Increase the pace of innovation and leverage business value through reliable data and AI innovations​

  • Ensure a clean core by following RISE with SAP Methodology, which promotes standardization and modern extensibility to drive successful transformation

Frequently asked questions

The five guiding principles of clean core include:

  • Processes: A clean business process keeps competitiveness and reduces complexity by staying as close as possible to the SAP standard.

  • Extensibility: Clean extensions are decoupled from the core by leveraging released APIs. A clear governance process ensures leveraging the best available extensibility option—on-stack and side-by-side with SAP BTP​.

  • Data: Clean data implies the continuous effort to keep data clean and compliant, as well as an established data governance model​.

  • Integrations: A clean integration is a robust, reliable, and future-ready approach to connecting systems built on standardized, secure, and scalable technologies.

  • Operations: Clean operations refers to a cross-dimension approach to embed best practices in governance, people, processes, and tools, enabling agile IT transformation in support of business transformation.

 

The RISE with SAP Methodology dashboard supports businesses in their transformation toward a clean core approach in several ways:

  • It encourages adoption of standard processes while enabling custom extensions for differentiation.​

  • This leads to streamlined operations and increased operational efficiency.​

  • A clean core helps keep business-critical systems agile, cost-effective, and innovation-ready.​

  • The dashboard is a tool designed to guide and support customers throughout their transformation journey to a clean core landscape.​
