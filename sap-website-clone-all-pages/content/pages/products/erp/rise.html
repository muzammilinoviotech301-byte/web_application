Frequently asked questions

SAP ECC was designed as a system of record—and it excels at that role. But AI requires ERP to expand into three additional roles: a system of context (providing semantic data relationships AI can reason across), a system of execution (allowing AI to act within governed business workflows), and a system of engagement (enabling people and AI to collaborate through a shared interface). ECC’s architecture can create structural ceilings that prevent this expansion. ERP modernization via the RISE with SAP journey lifts those ceilings while preserving your existing business logic and data.

S/4HANA on premises partially closes the data architecture gap through the universal journal and HANA-native data model, giving you a stronger foundation than ECC. However, the operational model gaps remain:  S/4HANA on premises does not provide the cloud-native services that deliver AI context at scale (SAP Business Data Cloud, SAP Knowledge Graph, and SAP Company Memory preview), embedded AI agent execution via governed cloud APIs, or Joule as a unified engagement layer. Moving to Cloud ERP with RISE with SAP activates these capabilities on top of the S/4HANA foundation you have already invested in with SAP Cloud ERP Private.

RISE with SAP is a guided, agent-led path from on-premises SAP ERP (ECC or S/4HANA) to SAP Cloud ERP Private. It delivers AI value during the migration itself—not only after go-live—through seven AI-enabled migration assistants (System Analysis Assistant , Custom Code Assistant, Data Management Assistant, Configuration Assistant, Test Management Assistant, Rollout Assistant, and Project Management Assistant) that reduce migration effort. These assistants continue operating post-go-live as continuous optimization agents, turning the transformation into an ongoing improvement cycle rather than a one-time project.

Embedded AI in ERP acts within live business processes—creating objects, triggering workflows, and committing transactions using the same governance controls that apply to human users. AI tools built on top of on-premises ERP reads replicated or exported data (not live transactional state), cannot execute back into the system of record with proper controls and compensating transactions, and requires governance to be replicated separately—which is never complete. The difference is architectural, not configurational.

Your business rules—pricing logic, tax compliance, credit policies, approval strategies, and posting controls—represent years of organizational learning and are preserved through modernization. RISE with SAP includes Business Transformation Management tools that help you analyze and standardize your processes. The new  AI-enabled Custom Code Assistant that analyzes your custom ABAP developments (Z-code in ECC and custom enhancements in S/4HANA on premises), and identifies what can be replaced by standard cloud functionality, what needs remediation, and what should be retained. As you achieve a clean-core ERP through the RISE with SAP journey, SAP Business AI Platform enables you to build cloud-compliant, clean-core extensions that support your unique processes, preserving differentiation without compromising architectural integrity.

Joule is SAP’s AI engagement layer—a single interface spanning assistants, specialized agents, and human decision points across all business functions. It is the interface through which AI acts within ERP processes, not a chatbot layered on top. Joule supports three structural oversight postures: human-in-the-loop, human-on-the-loop, and human-in-command, ensuring your teams retain decision authority while AI handles continuous monitoring, exception identification, and routine execution.

SAP Cloud ERP Private provides three capabilities that transform business data into AI-ready context: SAP Business Data Cloud delivers a unified, governed, semantically rich data foundation spanning ERP and adjacent domains. SAP Knowledge Graph maps relationships between business objects across functions. Company Memory preview captures institutional knowledge—policies, decision patterns, and ways of working—so AI acts based on how your specific organization operates, not a generic model. These cloud services work best when the ERP is in the cloud versus on premises.

Governance in SAP Cloud ERP Private is a structural property of the architecture, not a bolt-on configuration. When AI agents execute within ERP, they automatically inherit role-based access controls, posting period restrictions, approval strategies, segregation of duties, compensating transactions, and full audit trails. Every AI-initiated action is logged in the ERP change log with identical structure to a human action. Three oversight postures—human-in-the-loop, human-on-the-loop, and human-in-command—are structural design choices that determine the degree of autonomy granted to AI in each process.

The risk is not that your on-premises ERP stops working, it is that your organization hits a structural ceiling on AI capability while competitors do not. Every quarter on premises is a quarter where AI cannot access your business context at scale, cannot execute within your governed processes, and cannot collaborate with your teams through a unified interface. Meanwhile, AI capabilities in SAP Cloud ERP Private are delivered continuously—new agents, expanded autonomous domains, and deeper Knowledge Graph coverage arrive without upgrade projects. The gap compounds over time.
