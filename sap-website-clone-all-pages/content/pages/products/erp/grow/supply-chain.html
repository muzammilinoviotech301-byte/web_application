SAP GROW helps organizations stay ahead of demand, respond faster to change, and scale operations with real-time, AI-enhanced insights across product design, production planning, logistics, and asset maintenance. Supply chain teams gain visibility into inventory, capacity, and service levels with AI agents that support planning and automate execution. The result is fewer unplanned disruptions, smarter demand and supply balancing, higher product quality with less waste, and on-time delivery, all on a platform that scales as operations grow without requiring reimplementation.

Teams can manage picking, packing, shipping, and transport planning within a single platform connected to sales, procurement, and finance. AI-supported capabilities help align fulfillment with business priorities, supporting on-time delivery within budget.

 

For companies needing deeper capabilities, advanced SCM capabilities, including supply chain planning and enterprise asset management, can be activated on the same system over time.

Yes. SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public edition, the core of SAP GROW, is integrated with SAP Business Technology Platform, which provides over 750 prebuilt integrations and over 700 open APIs, simplifying connection with external logistics providers, carriers, and freight networks.

 

SAP also offers a supplier and business network that supports collaboration across the supply chain. Procurement, logistics, and finance are natively connected within SAP GROW, giving teams comprehensive visibility into commitments, shipments, and cash flow, whether those processes run inside SAP or through connected partners.

SAP GROW includes smart planning applications that provide a unified view of inventory, capacity, and service levels, helping teams balance demand and supply more accurately. AI-supported forecasting generates scenario-based planning, and the Production Planning Agent automates production order release based on material, capacity, and availability. Real-time data across sales, procurement, and operations ensures demand signals translate quickly into action, reducing delays and excess inventory.

 

For companies that need more, advanced supply chain planning capabilities can be activated on the same platform.

SAP cloud ERP supply chain management packages are designed for product-centric businesses that manufacture, assemble, or distribute physical goods. This includes industrial manufacturers, automotive suppliers, high-tech component makers, consumer goods producers, wholesale distributors, agribusinesses, and companies in chemicals, mill products, or mining. Whether the business runs make-to-stock, make-to-order, configure-to-order, or engineer-to-order processes, SAP GROW offers preconfigured scope scenarios for each model, allowing companies to start with what they need and extend as operations evolve.

The finance package serves as a starting point for all industries. This package covers core value stream processes—like lead to cash, procure to pay, and record to report—with AI-enhanced applications and real-time data insights.

 

The supply chain package integrates operational processes and supply chain functions with the rest of your business to help it become sustainable, fast-moving, and future-ready.

 

Many companies start with finance packages and add supply chain packages as they expand into production and logistics. Both types run on the same system, so expansion does not require reimplementation.
