Connecting every aspect of product development

SAP Cloud ERP Private enables your team to define, develop, deliver, and manage innovative and sustainable products.

  • Manage projects company-wide, optimize resources, and keep the portfolio on track – from forecasting and planning to accounting and closure

  • Develop compliant products and understand how changes impact costs, compliance, lead times, and more

  • Realize the benefits of a digital thread by connecting product development to the extended enterprise and eliminate data silos

  • Automate regulatory and sustainability requirements, track registrations, classify products, and manage hazardous materials properly with accurate labeling

Portfolio and project management

Comprehensive portfolio and project management – from defining to closing requests – through tight integration with finance and logistics information

Portfolio management

Submit project proposals, prioritize them with current projects, and monitor and review their progress.

Project management

Use capabilities that help identify critical paths, assign resources, track progress, control costs, and automate reporting.

Resource management

Avoid project bottlenecks by finding the right resources, checking their availability, and optimizing utilization.

Commercial project management

Increase profitability and achieve real-time transparency of customer projects.

Change management

Product recipes
