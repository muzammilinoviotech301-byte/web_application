Frequently asked questions about SAP Business One

SAP Business One is an affordable, all-in-one ERP system for small and midsize businesses. It connects your accounting, inventory management, purchasing, sales, and customer relationships in a single system, so you always have an accurate, real-time view of your business.

SAP Business One is designed for small and midsize businesses (SMB), while SAP S/4HANA Cloud and SAP ERP are designed for organizations of all sizes.

SAP Business One is built for small and midsize businesses, from companies just starting out to those managing rapid growth. There's no minimum size requirement. Over 85 ,000 customers across wholesale distribution, light manufaturing, retail, and professional services run their business on SAP Business One.
