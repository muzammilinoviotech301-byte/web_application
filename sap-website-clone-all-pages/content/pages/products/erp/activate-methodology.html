What is SAP Activate?

SAP Activate is an adoption framework for SAP Autonomous Suite that combines solution-specific best practices, expert guidance, and a structured, phased approach to help organizations streamline and accelerate the transformation journey.

Simplify your transformation with insights and guidance from thousands of implementations that can help you:

  • Embed Joule Agents and Joule Assistants in your processes for AI-first innovation.

  • Enable end-to-end integration and simplified operations between applications and systems.

  • Launch your fit-to-standard processes quickly and efficiently.

  • Reduce complexity with self-service tools, automation, and sustainable processes.

Key benefits

Jump-start deployment with ready-to-run AI-enabled processes

Adopt preconfigured best-practice business processes and employ Joule Agents and Joule Assistants to create a strong foundation for continuous innovation.

Streamline adoption with a clear AI-assisted pathway

Follow solution-specific guidance to simplify configuration, extensions, data preparation, testing, and user enablement.

Address enterprise-wide needs with extensibility

Customize SAP Autonomous Suite capabilities to meet evolving business needs and accelerate innovation across your organization.

Maximize the value of your cloud solutions

Use the SAP Activate framework to give your business the tools to succeed.

STEP 1

Discover

Formulate your business case, understand the value that SAP solution capabilities provide, and determine project scope.

STEP 2

Prepare

Start project planning, onboard the team, and provision the initial environment and application lifecycle management tools.

STEP 3

Explore

Perform a fit-to-standard analysis of preconfigured processes, including Joule Agents and Joule Assistants. Identify gaps and determine necessary extensions.

STEP 4

Realize

Perform a series of agile iterations, enrich your data, and implement and test the solution quickly to realize value.

STEP 5

Deploy

Finalize setup, identify risk factors, complete final testing, and go live with the new solution. Assist business users in adopting the solution.

STEP 6

Run

Maximize the solution value by continuing adoption across your organization and detecting opportunities for continuous innovation.

Frequently asked questions

SAP Activate offers a structured approach to implementing SAP solutions supported by ready-to-run best practice processes, tools, and AI-assisted guidance.

 

The framework’s task content—including setup guidance, enablement resources, templates and accelerators for fit-to-standard workshops, and detailed guidance for test sprints—is embedded in SAP Cloud ALM, offering detailed, role-based guidance for all project team members across every step of the implementation journey. 

The SAP Activate framework provides a structured approach to activation and implementation of SAP solutions with six distinct phases supported by best practices and tools. It builds on SAP’s strong foundation of fit-to-standard and clean core principles to assist customers with implementing robust and agile environments that enable continuous innovation.

The RISE with SAP Methodology encompasses a standardized framework, an integrated toolchain, and expert guidance to support your transition to the cloud. SAP Activate is the framework. It provides best practice and road map recommendations, guidelines, and checkpoints organized into six distinct phases.

The SAP Activate framework includes guidance that’s organized into six phases:

  1. Discover: Understand solution options and define the scope at a high level.
  2. Prepare: Finalize the project plan, set up the environment, and get the team aligned.
  3. Explore: Conduct fit-to-standard workshops and confirm the solution design.
  4. Realize: Configure, build, test, and validate the solution.
  5. Deploy: Cut over to the new system and transition to production.
  6. Run: Operate, support, and continuously optimize the solution.

 

The road map viewer in SAP for Me is the main hub for project teams—as well as customers, partners, and registered users—to access implementation road maps, deliverables, tasks, and accelerators such as templates and guides.

SAP Activate provides a structured framework, comprehensive support, and valuable resources that guide organizations through each phase of their adoption journey. This approach ensures that potential risks are identified and mitigated early, leading to a smoother and more predictable project outcome. Additionally, SAP Activate offers self-enablement resources, preconfigured business processes, AI-assisted guidance, fit-to-standard workshops, agile implementation cycles, and SAP Cloud ALM capabilities to further accelerate the process.

SAP Activate supports continuous adoption of new capabilities with minimal disruption through ready-to-run best practices that are updated regularly. The Run phase is also focused on supporting operations, optimizing processes, and enabling iterative improvements so teams can continuously innovate without needing a major reimplementation.
