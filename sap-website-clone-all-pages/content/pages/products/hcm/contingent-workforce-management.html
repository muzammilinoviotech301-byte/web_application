What is SAP Fieldglass Contingent Workforce Management?

External workers help build a strong, scalable workforce. SAP Fieldglass Contingent Workforce Management is an application that helps you find the right talent fast, engage the best workers, manage their assignment and payments, and complete offboarding and analysis.

Manage the complete lifecycle of your contingent workforce from requisition and engagement to offboarding and evaluation. Our application offers:

  • SAP Business AI and machine learning to source and select candidates faster

  • Centralized processes for holistic visibility, collaboration, and control

  • Automated work orders, time sheets, expenses, approvals, and payments

  • Powerful reporting, benchmarking, and analytics for informed decisions and strategies

SAP Fieldglass Contingent Workforce Management helps you find and onboard the right external talent faster; streamline the processes that support your workforce; and control risk, compliance, and secure onboarding and offboarding across more than 180 countries. It's designed to help reduce labor costs and improve invoice accuracy through real-time market data and automated processing.

Read the guide to contingent workforce management 

SAP Fieldglass integrates natively with SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Ariba, and SAP S/4HANA, plus key partners like DocuSign, giving you a unified view of your total workforce. It supports 21 languages and more than 180 countries with localized employment rules and tax tables, self-service administration tools that reduce your IT team's support burden, and an expert support team with you from program kickoff through ongoing use.

The solution handles complex contingent scenarios, including crew-based hiring, variable pay rates by task, and security credentialing for sensitive work. Every worker gets a persistent security ID that tracks onboarding, offboarding, and rehire eligibility across your entire program.
