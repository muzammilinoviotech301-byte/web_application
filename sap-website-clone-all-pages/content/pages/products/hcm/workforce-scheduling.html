What is SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling?

SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling is a shift-planning solution for manufacturing and production industries that combines demand planning, skills-based scheduling, and compliance into one process.

Transform workforce management by connecting demand planning, shift scheduling, and payroll processes to:

  • Anticipate workforce needs with demand-driven planning.

  • Assign shifts based on skills, certifications, and availability.

  • Adapt to unexpected changes with real-time visibility and insights.

Key features

Demand-based scheduling

  • Support flexible scheduling in one integrated process.

  • Filter workers by skills, qualifications, and certifications to match shifts.

  • Help ensure compliant schedules with built-in validation capabilities.

Real-time monitoring

  • React quickly to absences, demand spikes, and shift changes.

  • Monitor attendance with clock-in, clock-out, and time log visibility.

  • Adjust staffing by adding or removing worker assignments without delay.

Employee self-service

  • Give employees mobile access to manage shifts, absences, and time.

  • Help employees secure the right shifts by keeping skills and certifications current.

  • Send notifications to keep workers informed of schedule changes.

Unified processes

  • Connect workforce scheduling with time tracking and payroll to improve accuracy and efficiency.

  • Integrate workforce scheduling to connect people, machines, and materials across production processes through SAP Digital Manufacturing.

Frequently asked questions

SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling is tailored for production, manufacturing, and operational environments where demand, skills, and compliance must align tightly.

SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling integrates natively with SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking and SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll. This ensures one connected process from scheduling to pay, with accurate time capture, automated validations, and seamless data flow into payroll.

Demand-driven scheduling starts with labor demand—how many workers with which skills are needed at each work center and shift. SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling converts staffing requirements into scheduling proposals by factoring in worker availability, skills, and working-time rules. Schedulers review and publish the final schedule.

Shift scheduling software reduces labor costs by replacing manual planning with demand-driven automation. SAP SuccessFactors Workforce Scheduling helps eliminate over- and under-staffing by matching demand to available workers, reducing unplanned overtime, and connecting scheduling to time tracking and payroll to minimize errors and manual hand-offs.
