Frequently asked questions

The SAP Customer Evolution Program for HCM supports organizations running SAP ERP HCM on-premises that are ready to move to SAP SuccessFactors HCM. It provides proven tools and migration services to reduce risk throughout your project. SAP's migration tools and deep expertise in SAP ERP HCM environments help you get up and running faster.

Tools, assets, and services include:

  • A free ROI calculator and team of experts to help you build a business case and understand the economic benefits of your move to SAP SuccessFactors HCM.

  • SAP Readiness Check to provide insights into your current system’s landscape and prepare for a successful migration.

  • The SAP Customer Evolution kit free workshop to help you assess readiness, identify gaps, and define a customized migration plan.

  • The HCM EvoGuide is a practical guide designed to help IT leaders that work with HR navigate their transition to SAP SuccessFactors HCM.

  • Implementation design principles for structured guidance to help you understand best practices for moving to the cloud and improve project scope accuracy.

  • Architectural leading practices for configuration recommendations to help you finalize early project decisions and jumpstart design sessions.

  • Cost-effective transformation service offerings from SAP and our extensive partner ecosystem.

  • The infoporter tool for helping to reduce risk and increase accuracy of employee master data migration from SAP ERP HCM to SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central.

  • Embedded launch activities including best practice process libraries, guided enablement, and adoption touchpoints, at no additional cost.

  • SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) to maintain integrations and leverage cross-system workflow capabilities to support hybrid deployments.

The SAP Customer Evolution Program for HCM helps existing SAP ERP HCM customers move to the cloud regardless of their wider ERP landscape. The program offers tools to simplify migration steps, best practice content to guide you, and SAP services and a robust partner ecosystem. to provide additional migration support.

 

RISE with SAP combines the power of SAP S/4HANA and the SAP SuccessFactors HCM suite, and is relevant when transforming ERP and HR systems at the same time. Customers can choose from three packages for enterprise transformation, bringing together the solutions and services needed to modernize your people strategy at the pace that works for you.

SAP's Future of Work Research Lab interviewed 12 organizations that made the move. There were several key themes identified as part of their findings:

  • Balance urgency with readiness.

  • Let HR lead the strategy.

  • Redesign processes rather than migrate inefficiency.

  • Invest in change management from day one.

  • Treat migration as a people transformation, not just a system change.
