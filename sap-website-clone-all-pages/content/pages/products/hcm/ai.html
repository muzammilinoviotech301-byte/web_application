Frequently asked questions

Joule is a generative AI assistant integrated into SAP SuccessFactors, designed to streamline HR tasks such as creating job descriptions, managing time-off requests, and updating employee information. By providing quick answers and proactive insights, Joule enhances decision-making and operational efficiency in HR processes. Learn more about Joule with SAP SuccessFactors.

AI is used in HR to enhance efficiency, improve decision-making, and provide a better employee experience. Key applications include:

  • Recruitment and hiring: Screening resumes, matching candidates to job roles, and scheduling interviews

  • Employee engagement: Personalized feedback, surveys, and chatbots for real-time employee support

  • Training and development: Recommending tailored learning paths and monitoring training effectiveness

  • Performance management: Analyzing performance data and offering insights for employee growth

  • Administrative automation: Managing payroll, benefits, and compliance tasks to reduce manual workload

  • Workforce analytics: Predicting turnover rates and identifying workforce trends to inform strategic decisions

Explore AI for HR

Joule Agents are AI agents in SAP SuccessFactors that execute multi-step HR workflows automatically. A Payroll Agent flags issues before payday; a Recruiting Agent matches candidates and schedules interviews; an HR Service Agent answers employee questions instantly. Managers complete complex tasks up to 2x faster. Learn more about Joule Agents for HR.

SAP designs HR AI to support human judgment, not replace it. Joule and Joule Agents surface recommendations and automate routine tasks, but HR leaders retain accountability for every decision. SAP’s responsible AI principles cover human oversight, bias prevention, and transparency. Learn about SAP’s responsible AI approach.

SAP connects data from SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Fieldglass, and SAP Cloud ERP to support scenario-based workforce planning. HR leaders can model org changes, identify skills gaps, and align people strategy with business performance in real time.

SAP use cases show AI in SAP SuccessFactors can save up to 70% of applicant processing time, up to 70% of interview evaluation time, and up to 87% of interview prep costs. Managers complete complex HR tasks up to 2x faster with Joule Agents. Calculate your potential savings.
