Frequently asked questions

Total workforce management gives you visibility and control over your entire workforce, including full-time employees, part-time workers, contingent staff, and service providers. It connects HR and procurement data so you can plan, staff, onboard, and pay all worker types in one place. Learn more about what is workforce management.

SAP SuccessFactors manages your employees, including recruiting, learning, performance, and HR data. SAP Fieldglass manages your external workforce, including requisitions, onboarding, timesheets, and invoicing. Together, they give you a single view of your total workforce in real time. Learn more about SAP SuccessFactors HCM.

An Economist Impact survey found that 50% of organizations plan to increase their use of external workers. Companies rely on contingent staff and service providers to fill skills gaps faster, adapt to market changes, and manage project-based work without the cost of full-time hires. Learn more about managing your external workforce.

SAP SuccessFactors and SAP Fieldglass support compliance in over 100 countries. Automated tools help you stay ahead of local labor regulations and security requirements, while 99% of SAP Fieldglass customer invoices are accurate and compliant. Learn more about SAP Fieldglass contingent workforce management.
