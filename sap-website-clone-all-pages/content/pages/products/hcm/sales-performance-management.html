Frequently asked questions

Business-to-business (B2B) sales applications like SAP Sales Cloud can help businesses improve sales performance, revenue growth, and deal velocity. Sales automation solutions use centralized customer data, giving users improved visibility into opportunities and individual customers. Sales process automation replaces repetitive and manual tasks. AI-driven applications and AI agents for sales improve sales effectiveness. Businesses can become more successful with the advanced analytics and AI-driven forecasting that come from deep integration and data connection.

SAP sales applications enable autonomous sales execution across the entire sales cycle, from lead qualification and opportunity management to quoting and order processing. By combining AI-powered insights with connected business data, they automate routine tasks, help sellers make faster decisions, and improve sales productivity.

SAP sales applications use connected customer data and AI-powered insights to help sales teams understand customer needs, deliver relevant recommendations, and provide personalized interactions throughout the buying journey. This enables more meaningful customer engagement and better sales outcomes.

SAP's agentic AI helps sales teams automate routine tasks, prioritize opportunities, and take action faster with autonomous AI agents. By assisting with lead qualification, opportunity management, meeting preparation, quote creation, and pipeline insights, it helps sellers improve productivity, accelerate deal cycles, and focus on building customer relationships.

SAP’s leading portfolio of sales applications is designed to connect customer engagement with end-to-end business execution. SAP Sales Cloud and SAP CPQ connect seamlessly with SAP Cloud ERP, giving sales teams a 360-degree view of customers, streamlined lead-to-cash processes, and a lower total cost of ownership (TCO). Embedded AI capabilities like email-to-quote, opportunity summaries, email generation, predictive forecasting, and autonomous AI assistants help sellers work more efficiently and improve sales outcomes.

Organizations across most industries, including manufacturing, consumer products, retail, automotive, high tech, life sciences, and professional services, can benefit from SAP sales processes. SAP helps businesses streamline the sales cycle from lead management to quote creation and order execution, improving seller productivity and delivering faster customer experiences.

SAP sales applications help users work effectively wherever they like to work. For example, SAP Sales Cloud includes options for both desktop and mobile. It has a dedicated mobile application with an optimized mobile user experience to. Not all solution providers have desktop and mobile options. Examine use cases, user needs, and available integrations to find the best fit.
