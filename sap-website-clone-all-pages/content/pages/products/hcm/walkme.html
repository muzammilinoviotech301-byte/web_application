What is WalkMe for SAP SuccessFactors HCM?

WalkMe is an AI-powered digital adoption platform embedded in SAP SuccessFactors HCM that helps you accelerate system adoption while decreasing support costs.

Help employees and managers adopt evolving HR processes and AI capabilities while giving HR teams the insight to continuously improve workflows.

  • Personalize guidance by role, region, language, and behavior.

  • Automate repetitive steps across multistep HR workflows.

  • Measure adoption and system engagement across HR processes and AI capabilities.

  • Use AI-generated recommendations to optimize HR workflows.

Key features

Behavioral insights and analytics

  • See how employees engage with workflows and AI-powered capabilities.
  • Identify adoption gaps.
  • Use AI-generated recommendations to improve HR processes.

Personalized guidance and automation

  • Deliver personalized, interactive guidance exactly when and where it’s needed.
  • Automate repetitive actions and multistep HR workflows.
  • Reinforce the right steps for infrequent tasks with in-the-moment prompts and reminders

 

No-code authoring tools and templates

  • Create and update guidance quickly with no-code authoring tools.
  • Accelerate deployments with pre-configured SuccessFactors templates.
  • Publish updates without relying on IT or technical resources.
