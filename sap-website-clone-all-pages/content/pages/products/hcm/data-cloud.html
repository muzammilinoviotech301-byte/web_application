FAQ

People Intelligence is a fully managed SaaS solution within SAP Business Data Cloud that connects to your HR data, harmonizes it, and delivers AI-powered workforce insights across composition, compensation, skills, and more. Joule surfaces AI-driven recommendations so your team can act on the insights, not just report them.

You'll get ready-to-run insights across workforce composition, compensation, skills, talent mobility, recruiting, onboarding, and organizational growth. Pay Transparency Insights, added in June 2025, helps you analyze gender pay gaps and generate Article 9 metrics for EU directive reporting. Joule connects each insight area to a suggested action.

Pay Transparency Insights helps you systematically analyze gender pay gaps across countries, identify compensation outliers, and generate the Article 9 metrics the EU Pay Transparency Directive requires. You can run this analysis across your full workforce or by segment to build reporting supporting the regulation.

People Intelligence uses machine learning to forecast attrition by department, so you can spot when high-performer turnover is trending before it compounds. From there, you can trigger targeted compensation reviews and upskilling pathways directly within SAP SuccessFactors to address the root causes early.

SAP delivers Pay Transparency Insights, a preconfigured analytical capability within People Intelligence in SAP Business Data Cloud. The solution covers overall and median gender pay gaps, breakdowns by pay component, role, and grade, a joint pay assessment for structured comparison across workforce groups, and an outlier analysis. This helps customers address the gender pay gap and comply with new EU regulations.
