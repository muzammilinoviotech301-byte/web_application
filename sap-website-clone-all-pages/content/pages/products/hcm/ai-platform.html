Frequently asked questions

SAP Business AI Platform is the enterprise AI foundation that combines deep process context, unified business data, and purpose-built models with built-in enterprise governance. This allows every team, from developers to business users, to build and deploy agentic AI that delivers trusted results across the enterprise, not just in isolated use cases.

SAP Integration Suite can integrate with any sort of SAP or third-party solution, provided they have published APIs. The typical use cases for integration are third-party (non-SAP), government reporting, legacy on-premises solutions (such as SAP ERP HCM Payroll), and for hybrid landscapes (both on-premises and cloud-based solutions). Additionally, SAP Integration Suite provides event-driven integrations that trigger real-time data and processes that can be used for solutions that utilize an event-engine such as SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central.

You can use Joule Studio to create custom AI agents that reason, plan, and act across SAP and non-SAP systems.  Teams can create agentic workflows using an embedded n8n environment and extend SAP agents based on the needs of their organization. Agents and workflows are deployed to the Joule Studio runtime using low-code, pro-code, and agentic development tools.
