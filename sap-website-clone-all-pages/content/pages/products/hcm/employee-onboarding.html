What is SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding?

SAP SuccessFactors Onboarding is a solution that accelerates global hiring with agentic AI capabilities that orchestrate onboarding and employee transitions, ensuring every hire is productive faster.

Intelligently coordinate employee onboarding, cross-boarding, offboarding, rehire, and alumni programs through a connected, AI-driven solution to:

  • Improve new hire engagement and efficiency, even before the first day.

  • Accelerate onboarding with an intuitive guided process for new hires.

  • Streamline processes, configuration, and administration of all programs.

  • Simplify onboarding with AI, automation, and dashboards.
