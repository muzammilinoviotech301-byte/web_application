What is SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central?

Our flexible, cloud-based human resources information system (HRIS) software helps companies of all sizes standardize HR processes globally and provides visibility to make better people decisions.

Meet the expectations of your people and the needs of your organization with cloud HRIS software.

  • Manage user profiles and organizational charts with confidence

  • Support HR compliance management in over 100 locales across all industries

  • Increase productivity with AI-enabled self-service tools and proactive alerts

  • Leverage embedded capabilities for global benefits administration and absence management

SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central includes capabilities for managing HR compliance. Embedded localization supporting over 100 locales can help you ensure compliance and standardize HR processes across the globe. Additionally, you can stay up to date on legal and regulatory changes within the integrated upgrade center.

Employee Central includes embedded localization supporting over 100 countries and territories. Legal and regulatory updates are delivered through an integrated upgrade center, so compliance teams receive automated notifications when local requirements change. This reduces manual monitoring burden while helping organizations stay current across all operating jurisdictions. Learn more about Employee Central's localization capabilities.
