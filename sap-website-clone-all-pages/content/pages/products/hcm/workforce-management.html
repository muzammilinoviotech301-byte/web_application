Frequently asked questions

Workforce Management from SAP comprises the activities and processes needed to manage and maintain a productive workforce—including budgeting, scheduling, forecasting, tracking employee time, and complying with regulation.

SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking is our time tracking software for employees, managers, and HR teams. It modernizes time and attendance processes with real-time validations and rule-based calculations that help prevent errors before they affect pay. It covers hourly and remote workforces with digital punch clocks, clock terminal integration, and support for complex round-the-clock and cross-midnight schedules. For managing employee absences, SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central provides absence management across 100+ countries.

SAP Fieldglass is our vendor management system (VMS) for procuring and managing external workers, including contractors, consultants, freelancers, and service providers. It gives organizations real-time visibility into who is working for them, at what cost, and with support for local labor and tax regulations across 180 countries and 21 languages. SAP Fieldglass integrates with both SAP and non-SAP ERP systems, reducing reliance on middleware. Learn more about SAP Fieldglass.

A vendor management system is a cloud-based platform used to procure and manage temporary, contract, or contingent workers. Most VMS applications integrate with other on-premises or cloud-based enterprise HR systems. VMS software helps enterprises gain clear insight into who is working for them, as well as where, why, when, and at which rates.

Contingent workers are individuals hired by a company to do role- or project-based work on its behalf, but not as traditional employees. They can be independent contractors, consultants, freelancers, temps, or other outsourced labor, such as gig workers. These workers can be contracted through suppliers or other talent pools, such as staffing agencies, freelance marketplaces, or a company’s HR or procurement department.

Total workforce management gives you a unified view of your entire workforce, covering permanent employees, contingent workers, and service providers. SAP connects SAP SuccessFactors and SAP Fieldglass to unify HR and procurement data, close skills gaps, and manage compliance across more than 100 countries.
