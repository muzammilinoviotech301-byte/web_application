What is SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll?

SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll is an AI-powered autonomous payroll solution, built for global scale, that delivers accurate, on-time, and compliant pay employees trust.

Delight employees and payroll administrators alike with embedded automation that drives efficiency and agility, including:

  • AI-powered Explain Pay that personalizes support to employees 24/7.

  • Compliance with local regulations and tax laws in 60 countries.

  • Continuous payroll with real-time processing, anomaly detection, and guided resolution.

  • Natively integrated solutions for core HR, benefits, time, payroll, and finance.

Frequently asked questions

Payroll software automates employee compensation, tax withholdings, and deductions to ensure accurate, timely payments and legal compliance. It handles gross-to-net calculations, direct deposits, and tax filing, reducing manual errors for HR and finance teams. Learn more.

Continuous payroll lets you monitor and validate payroll data as soon as changes are made, shifting from a batch process at the end of each pay period to a real-time model that detects anomalies early and reduces errors. Learn more.

Autonomous payroll is SAP's vision for AI-native payroll execution where agents sense, orchestrate, and run every pay cycle end to end - proactively resolving issues, ensuring compliance, and delivering accurate, on-time pay. People set the direction; AI executes with governance built in. Learn more.

A single vendor for core HR, payroll, time, and finance removes custom integrations that break down between systems. Data flows directly from HR events to payroll and finance, reducing errors, speeding close cycles, and delivering trusted data for AI-driven insights.

SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll supports 60 countries, 34 languages, and 158 currencies. A team of over 100 in-country product managers monitors regulatory changes and delivers compliance updates directly to your system. Learn more.

The Regulatory Change Manager tracks legal updates across 60 countries, so your payroll stays compliant without manual monitoring. Over 100 in-country product managers deliver updates directly to your system, covering local tax rules, fields, and regulatory reports. Read the solution brief.

SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central Payroll is designed for midsize organizations and large enterprises. It requires SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central as a prerequisite. Learn more.
