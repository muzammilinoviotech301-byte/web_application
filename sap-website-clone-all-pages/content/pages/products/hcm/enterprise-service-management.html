What is SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management?

It’s an AI-powered HR service management that streamlines case handling, routes employee requests to the right service representative, and empowers employees with intuitive self-service tools built on live Employee Central data.

Provide HR service teams with AI capabilities for case management and empower employees with enhanced self-service experience. The solution offers:

  • Intuitive self-service tools for quick resolutions

  • AI driven case management that works smarter and reduces workloads through automation

  • Actionable analytics, built-in compliance, and low-code tools to scale with confidence

Key features

Intuitive self-service

  • Provide personalized, easy-to-use tools to create, update, and track HR service tickets

  • Access quick actions, HR policies, and guided experiences while creating tickets, ensuring fast resolutions

  • Use Joule for conversational guidance, proactive ticket deflection, and automatic case creation so employees get answers without contacting HR

  • Access HR services across web, mobile, chatbot, MS teams and email channels, so employees can submit and track requests from any device.

AI-driven case management

  • Centralize HR service delivery with integrated tools in a unified workspace powered by core HR data from SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central

  • Guide service reps through complex HR scenarios with intelligent case management

  • Use AI to auto-classify cases, summarize content, and draft recommendations, with automated approvals and dynamic forms to support compliance requirements

Insights to action

  • Monitor case status, SLA compliance, and team performance in real time using SAP Analytics Cloud dashboards embedded in the service representative workspace

  • Simplify administration using customizable features and low-code tools to manage and scale systems efficiently

  • Ensure access to up-to-date and accurate HR policies, procedures, and recommendations

  • Integrate Microsoft SharePoint repositories to build a unified HR knowledge base your service teams can search from within their workspace

Frequently asked questions

HR help desk software centralizes employee service requests, routes cases to the right HR agent, and tracks every request from submission to resolution. SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management combines an employee self-service portal, AI-powered case routing, and a unified HR service representative workspace.

What is enterprise service management

SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management is available to midsize and growing companies through GROW with SAP, as well as large enterprises. Midsize organisations use ESM to standardise HR service delivery and support growing HR teams with AI-assisted case management.

Learn about GROW with SAP

SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management connects natively with SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central for live HR data, SAP BTP for workflow automation and integrations, and SAP Fieldglass for extended workforce service requests, all within the SAP SuccessFactors HCM suite.

AI, data, and experience: redefining HR service delivery with SAP SuccessFactors

SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management is designed to work with SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central as its core HR data source, providing live employee records, org structure, and HR policy data for accurate case routing and responses.

SAP Help Portal documentation

Joule deflects common employee queries, guides ticket creation, and answers HR policy questions without HR team involvement. For HR service representatives, Joule auto-classifies cases, drafts responses, suggests next-best actions, and performs real-time translation. HR professionals remain accountable for every decision.
