Frequently asked questions

SAP Business Network is a cloud-based collaboration offering that modernizes how procurement and supply chain processes are connected across organizations. It helps to build stronger supply chains, deliver on the customer promise, gain visibility into your supply chain, increase operational efficiency, and ensure compliance.

Supply chain collaboration is extending your organization’s processes and systems to your trading partners. Gain transparency by collaborating with multiple tiers of trading partners across inventory, quality, and procurement processes along your supply chain, achieving greater customer satisfaction by mitigating supply chain risk and assuring supply.

Procurement collaboration is the practice of partnering with suppliers, vendors, and other stakeholders to streamline and optimize the procurement process. Working closely with key partners helps to enhance transparency, drive cost savings, improve the overall quality of goods and services acquired, strengthen supply chains, minimize risks, and foster mutually beneficial relationships.

Logistics collaboration is working with shippers and carriers to optimize logistics processes, increase on-time deliveries, and mitigate supply risk. A comprehensive logistics collaboration solution helps you gain visibility into the freight order lifecycle to better manage appointments, share insights, digitally manage transactions, optimize logistics processes, notify stakeholders about deviations, and monitor fulfillment.

Asset collaboration is the strategic partnership and coordination between businesses and their suppliers or partners in sharing, managing, and maintaining the assets and equipment critical to their operations. This allows for efficient utilization, timely maintenance, and optimization of asset performance, ultimately leading to improved productivity, reduced downtime, and enhanced cost-effectiveness.
