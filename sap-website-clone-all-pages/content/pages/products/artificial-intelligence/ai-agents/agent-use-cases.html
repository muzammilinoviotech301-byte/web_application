  • Agents and assistants that give you visibility and control because they have proper oversight and governance built in.

Agents only execute within user permissions

Each agent gets a unique identity for reauthentication at every step and all traffic flows through one agent gateway, so agents can only execute what the end user has permission to do directly.

Defense-in-depth with a secure-by-default runtime

At runtime, agents operate in a hardened sandbox with layers of defense, including content filters, resource limits, and connectivity restrictions.

Human oversight is built into the architecture

Oversight options help augment rather than replace human judgment. High-risk processes always require explicit approval and all agent activity is logged and traceable.

Frequently asked questions

Joule Assistants are AI assistants aligned to your business roles and processes, so they understand the context you're working in. You tell an assistant what you want to accomplish in natural language, and it coordinates specialized AI agents to complete it. Assistants turn isolated tasks into connected workflows that help you efficiently complete work because proper oversight and governance are built in.

Joule Agents are ready-to-use AI agents that provide business users with decision support and task automation requiring non-deterministic workflows. Joule Agents choose from a large set of tools (including Joule skills, other agents, and third-party applications) to act on their plans, reflect on the results to draw conclusions, and make recommendations. Grounded in SAP Knowledge Graph and SAP Business Data Cloud, Joule Agents uniquely understand your data and processes to act reliably through business applications.

Joule Assistants coordinate Joule Agents through deep role and business context. Once directed by the assistant, agents connect relevant data, tools, and applications to perform specific tasks such as analyzing data, triggering actions, interacting with systems, and executing steps in a workflow.

Use prebuilt Joule Agents when your needs align with standard business processes in areas like finance, HR, or procurement, and where you want fast time-to-value with enterprise-grade security and SAP-managed updates. Create custom agents in Joule Studio when your workflows are unique or specialized enough that standard agents only partially address your needs, keeping in mind that you take on full lifecycle responsibility for anything you build. Most organizations adopt a hybrid approach, leveraging prebuilt agents for common processes while using Joule Studio to extend or build agents where their unique business context justifies the investment.
