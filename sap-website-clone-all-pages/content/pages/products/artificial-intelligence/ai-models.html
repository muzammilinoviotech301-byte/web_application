What are SAP AI services and models?

SAP AI services and models let you retrieve context-relevant information from SAP solutions, enable developers to build high-quality applications and ABAP code that extends business processes, and support the business to create powerful data predictions.

AI services and models on SAP Business AI Platform enable data, engineering, and IT teams to:

  • Access more than 60 frontier large language models (LLMs) through a single managed API for intelligent automation with less integration complexity.

  • Turn structured data into accurate, explainable predictions with less effort using tabular foundation models (TFMs) from SAP and Prior Labs.

  • Build, deploy, and govern custom ML models for SAP and custom applications.

  • Extract and classify data from documents without building custom pipelines.

  • Apply the latest ABAP knowledge to support faster application migrations.

FAQ

SAP AI models are advanced, fully managed, and large-scale models built and trained on decades of knowledge and best practices of SAP code, data, and processes, plus third-party frontier models from leading providers, that reduce integration complexity and cost while keeping your company up to date with the latest SAP innovations.

SAP provides data, engineering, and IT teams with foundation models for content generation, predictions on structured data, and ABAP development in the model library in SAP AI Core.

SAP AI models are used by the SAP Business AI Platform to reason and contextualize AI onto SAP and customer information. Specialized models such as SAP-ABAP and SAP-RPT are also available through SAP AI Core in the SAP Business Platform.

Tabular AI is an area of artificial intelligence that applies machine learning to structured data to generate business predictions. Organizations are building tabular AI strategies to scale these predictions, matching models to business tasks and bringing LLMs, TFMs, and specialized tabular AI capabilities together on a single platform. With SAP Business AI Platform, organizations can orchestrate and centrally manage models and predictions with comprehensive oversight and governance.
