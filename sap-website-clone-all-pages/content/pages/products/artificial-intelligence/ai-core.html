What is SAP AI Core?

SAP AI Core is a fully managed AI service on the SAP Business AI Platform, designed to operate, govern, and scale AI workloads in a standardized and scalable way. It provides centralized model management, flexible deployment, integrated development, and tabular AI tools to streamline enterprise AI adoption.

  • Access SAP and third-party tabular, large language, and multimodal foundation models through a central model library. 

  • Use built-in governance to ensure compliant, enterprise-ready AI deployment.

  • Achieve faster time-to-market with preconfigured integrations across SAP applications.

  • Reduce operational overhead and risk with automated prompt optimization, orchestration, and inference observability.

  • Accelerate AI deployment and reduce infrastructure burden with a GPU-accelerated model hosting that scales automatically to meet demand.

Key benefits

Operate AI with trusted models and data for confident decisions

Access SAP and third-party large language models, tabular foundation models, and custom models. Connect models to enterprise data and trusted business knowledge to produce contextualized outputs that improve processes and enable confident decisions.

Govern AI workflows and orchestration across the enterprise

Make responsible AI operations part of every workflow with consistent guardrails, observability, and access policies across AI interactions to reduce compliance risk and improve operational oversight.

Scale AI across applications and use cases

Turn AI projects into reusable enterprise capabilities, with a managed path to build, run, integrate, and extend AI across SAP processes and custom business applications with a managed service, reducing IT complexity and costs.

Key features

Ground AI models in enterprise context

Reason over unstructured and structured data, such as enterprise documents, SAP data, and business knowledge, with purpose-built foundation models and a unified context gateway.

Orchestrate every AI workflow

Build multi-step workflows across generative, tabular, and customer-owned models. Apply data masking, content filtering, and monitor quality in production.

Deploy AI on SAP

Accelerate AI deployments in public and private clouds with GPU-accelerated, auto-scaling infrastructure, pre-built SAP application integrations, and the ability to publish AI as reusable services on the SAP Business AI Platform.

FAQ

SAP AI Core implements enterprise-grade security measures, including:

  • End-to-end encryption.

  • Identity and access management integration with SAP Cloud Identity Services.

  • Comprehensive audit logging to protect customer data at every stage of processing.

  • Compliance with major data protection regulations and standards, such as GDPR and ISO 270001.

  • Certification for SOC1 and SOC2.

SAP models are trained with SAP table semantics, relationships, and real SAP use cases, making them ideal to process and analyze SAP customer data accurately and efficiently. Customer data is never used to train SAP models or shared with third-party vendors for training LLMs.

Get started with SAP AI Core with a 30-day basic trial to build custom AI solutions and connect your data.

 

The Solving Your Business Problems Using Prompts and LLMs learning journey provides comprehensive hands-on training to learn how to use models, its interactive playground, and the SDK to solve real business challenges using advanced prompting techniques and AI orchestration capabilities.

SAP-RPT, SAP-ABAP, and third-party tabular foundation models such as TabPFN from PriorLabs are available in the SAP AI Core’s model library SAP’s specialized models include:

  • SAP-RPT foundation tabular model for predictive business insights across finance, sales, and supply chain scenarios.

  • SAP-ABAP in the SAP Business AI Platform for intelligent ABAP development assistance, code understanding, and explanation.

  • PriorLabs TabPFN, a third-party foundation tabular model for zero-shot tabular prediction across delivering instant, in-context predictions on datasets up to 1M rows without training.
