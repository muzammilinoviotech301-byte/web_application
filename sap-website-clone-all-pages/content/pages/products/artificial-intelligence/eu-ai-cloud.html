What is EU AI Cloud?

EU AI Cloud gives European enterprises and German public sector organizations secure, compliant cloud environments for building and running AI. It provides governed access to auditable LLMs from SAP and trusted partners, while meeting EU AI Act and GDPR regulations and supporting European sovereignty needs.

Advanced AI technologies

Accessed from SAP and trusted partners through a single API, running optimally on high-performance European infrastructure.

Build, deploy, and manage AI on a governed foundation

Built on SAP Cloud Infrastructure and SAP Business Technology Platform.

Your data never leaves Europe

All infrastructure, administration, and support is handled by local SAP teams.

Enterprise-grade governance

Gain full visibility, control, and auditability, with your AI resources running on a reliable foundation of SAP Cloud Infrastructure and SAP BTP.

Confident compliance

Operate compliantly with local laws and regulations, protected by strong contractual safeguards and built-in security. Your data stays private, encrypted, and is never used to train third-party models.

Choice and simplicity

Choose the LLMs and data centers that best meet your needs—and seamlessly switch to different options as your technical or regulatory needs change.

Gain the freedom to innovate with confidence

SAP gives you secure, compliant cloud environments with the flexibility to choose the AI models that best support your business goals—all aligned with EU AI Act and GDPR requirements.
