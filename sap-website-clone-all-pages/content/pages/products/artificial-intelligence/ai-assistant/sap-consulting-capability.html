Get started

Understand pricing, estimate your AI Unit needs, and activate SAP Joule for Consultants using SAP AI Units.

Your first step is to confirm whether your organization has SAP AI Units.

 

Visit SAP for Me to:

  • See your available AI Unit balance

  • Assign the total number of users

  • Activate SAP Joule for Consultants

*Not all users have access to SAP for Me or permission to view AI Unit balances or contracts. If you need assistance, please contact your internal SAP administrator.

Visit SAP for Me to check AI units and activate SAP Joule for Consultants*

If you need additional AI Units, you can estimate how many you need and purchase them below.

 

AI Units needed = number of users × number of months x 35 AI Units*

Cost = AI units needed x €7 per AI Unit

*SAP Joule for Consultants consumes 35 SAP AI Units per assigned user per month, including up to 22,900 requests per user each month.

 

Example:

AI units needed = 5 users × 12 months x 35 AI Units = 2,100 AI Units

Cost = 2,100 AI Units x  €7 per AI Unit = €14,700  for 5 users for 12 months

 

Notes:

  • AI Units are purchased annually in blocks of 100.

  • AI Units can be used across SAP premium AI capabilities.

  • AI Unit usage is pooled across assigned users.

  • Unused AI Units expire at the end of the contract term.

  • If you already subscribe to an SAP Cloud application, your organization may already have AI Units available for SAP Joule for Consultants.

FAQ

SAP Joule for Consultants delivers more accurate, SAP-specific guidance because it is built on a knowledge base curated and maintained by SAP experts, including high-value, non-public content unavailable to general AI tools. While tools like Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and others can research public information and generate well-structured answers, those answers may rely on inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated sources. Joule for Consultants provides more accurate answers grounded in authoritative SAP sources, fewer hallucinations and lower risk of incorrect guidance, and up-to-date best practices based on current SAP recommendations and expertise.

AI tools in consulting increase productivity and improve the quality of outcomes by giving consultants faster access to the information they need exactly when they need it. For example, conversational AI solutions can instantly surface system configuration guidance, explain custom code, or provide best practice recommendations. This helps consultants make better-informed decisions earlier in the project lifecycle, reducing costly rework and improving consistency. By automating knowledge discovery and guiding solution design, AI enables faster, more reliable results across transformation projects.

SAP Joule for Consultants supports teams across a wide range of SAP project scenarios—from solution design and configuration to code interpretation and best-practice alignment. It provides real-time, context-aware guidance grounded in SAP’s most authoritative and up-to-date knowledge. Whether assisting junior consultants who need step-by-step support or experienced experts validating design decisions, Joule delivers trusted insights tailored to the task, helping every team member work more efficiently and consistently across projects.

SAP Joule for Consultants accelerates automation and integration workstreams by providing real-time access to SAP best practices, configuration guidance, code interpretation, and system design recommendations. Whether you're connecting SAP S/4HANA to other systems, activating Joule in an SAP workflow, or implementing automation within a transformation program, it surfaces trusted insights tailored to your scenario—helping consultants be more productive, design with confidence, and deliver faster.

SAP Joule for Consultants and SAP Joule for Developers support two of the most critical roles in any SAP cloud migration and implementation projects: functional consultants and developers. Each faces distinct challenges, and Joule provides tailored AI assistance to make their work faster and more effective.

 

SAP Joule for Consultants helps functional experts configure systems, interpret ABAP logic, and troubleshoot issues in natural language. It draws from SAP-verified content and best practices, enabling consultants to resolve issues quickly and keep solutions aligned with SAP standards.

 

SAP Joule for Developers, ABAP AI capabilities assists ABAP professionals directly within their ABAP development tools. It helps generate, test, and remediate code, accelerating custom-code adaptation for SAP S/4HANA and supporting the creation of reliable, upgrade-ready extensions.

By guiding each role where they work, SAP Joule for Consultants and SAP Joule for Developers help teams deliver SAP projects faster, reduce rework, and realize business value sooner.

SAP Joule for Consultants is designed to make every consultant more capable, not to replace them. It significantly reduces time spent searching for answers, allowing consultants to focus on the strategic work that clients value most: design decisions, stakeholder alignment, and delivery leadership.
