SAP AI commercial model and pricing

SAP AI pricing is designed to scale with the value AI delivers—from everyday productivity to automated business processes and outcomes.

SAP AI commercial model at a glance

SAP AI commercial model is organized around three tiers, each suited to a different stage of adoption and a different way of creating value.

AVAILABLE WITH EVERY CLOUD SUBSCRIPTION
Base AI

Base AI provides the foundational AI capabilities that come with every SAP cloud subscription. It's available to every user across your organization from day one, with no AI Units or additional paid license required. Joule Work, base provides an integrated experience to orchestrate everyday tasks while embedded AI capabilities are built directly into SAP applications, helping teams work smarter without leaving the tools they use every day.

Explore Base AI
AVAILABLE WITH AI UNITS
Premium AI

Premium AI moves beyond foundational AI capabilities to orchestrate and execute work end-to-end. Joule Work, Agentic Thinking, provides an intent-based, dynamic workspace for complex orchestration across web, desktop, mobile, and in-app experiences. Joule Assistants use deep role and business process context to coordinate Joule Agents, executing tasks and workflows across your organization. Premium AI also includes high-value AI services such as SAP Joule for Consultants, Document AI, Document Grounding, and more. Pricing scales directly with the value generated.

Explore Premium AI
FREE TO DESIGN, PAID AT RUNTIME
Custom-built AI

Custom-built AI gives you the flexibility to create exactly what you need for your business when you need it. Build your own agents, applications, and n8n workflows with Joule Studio, then deploy and scale them across your system landscape with Joule Studio runtime. It’s for organizations looking to differentiate through AI—not simply adopt it. With consumption-based pricing, you pay for actual usage as your custom-built AI runs and delivers value throughout your business.

Explore custom-built AI

Base AI

Base AI is available with every SAP cloud subscription at no additional cost—no AI Units required.  It combines two essential offerings: Joule Work, base, the interactive natural language available across SAP applications, and Embedded AI, purpose-built intelligence delivered directly within SAP solutions.

  • Joule Work, base is the AI solution that enhances the user’s experience across SAP applications through natural language. Users can ask questions, navigate to the right screen, retrieve information, get contextual guidance, and perform everyday actions—all without leaving their workflow.

  • Embedded AI is built directly into SAP applications, delivering insights, recommendations, and intelligent automation within the business processes where you work—without requiring a separate interaction. Availability varies by SAP solution.

Explore Base AI features

Premium AI

Premium AI is built for teams that need AI to do the heavy lifting— complex end-to-end orchestration, multi-turn queries, running agents, automating processes, and executing multi-step tasks across your organization. It’s priced by AI Units, with consumption-based pricing for agentic thinking, agents and high-value AI services and a per-user plan for SAP Joule for Consultants.

Joule Work, Agentic Thinking

Joule Work is the new UI for AI. A dynamic workspace that, based on user intent, surfaces the right insights, automates routine and complex work, and orchestrates AI agents across every business domain. Accessible through web, desktop, mobile, and in app, Joule Work enables Conversations for quick and accurate answers, custom Skills for workflow automation, and Spaces for visualization.

  • Priced on AI Units consumed per action

  • All usage tracked at the action level

  • Two thinking modes

Explore Joule Work 

Joule Agents

Joule Agents are purpose-built AI agents that automate well-defined, multi-step enterprise workflows across your organization. They are accessed through Joule Assistants which use deep role and process context to coordinate agents and execute complex work autonomously, like smart teammates. There is no separate charge for Joule Assistants; pricing is based entirely on the underlying agents they run.

  • Two agent types: Standard and Advanced, priced by AI Units consumed per action

  • All usage tracked at the action level

Learn more about Joule Agents and Joule Assistants  

AI Features

AI Features are individual high-value AI capabilities you can access on demand—such as document processing and content generation. Unlike agents which are priced per action, AI Features consume AI Units per request or per record processed.

View pricing for all AI Features 

SAP Joule for Consultants

SAP Joule for Consultants is priced in AI units per user per month—designed for consultants working in complex, high-value professional services scenarios where AI value is directly tied to individual productivity.

  • Assigned users, adjustable monthly

  • Fixed per-user monthly pricing

  • Automatic access to new features

Explore SAP Joule for Consultants

FAQ

AI Units are SAP's single virtual currency for accessing and running Premium AI capabilities across SAP solutions. Purchased annually via SKU 8019164, they sit in one shared pool and can be used flexibly across all Premium AI capabilities.

 

AI Units are consumed only when Premium AI actively performs work—such as multi-agent orchestration, agents executing workflows or documents being indexed. Unused AI Units expire after 12 months. Base AI does not consume AI Units.

SAP for Me is your central hub for activating AI capabilities and tracking AI Unit consumption, balances, and billing in near real time.

Joule Assistants use deep role and business process context to deliver insights and execute complex workflows across an organization. They translate intent into concrete actions by coordinating Joule Agents. Joule Assistants aren't sold or priced separately—all commercial measurement happens at the Joule Agent level.

All agents are categorized as “standard” or “advanced.”  Standard agents are priced at 0.02 AI Units per agent action. Advanced agents are priced at .05 AI Units per agent action.  An agent action is a single step an agent takes to complete a task—such as calling a service, triggering a workflow, or generating a response. A task may require multiple actions depending on complexity. When an agent is not running, no AI Units are consumed.

Yes, Joule Agents can be extended with textual instructions without incremental charges. Agent execution remains monetized on the same per-action basis via AI units: extended standard agents continue to consume 0.02 AI units per action, and extended advanced agents continue to consume 0.05 AI units per action. As additional extensibility scenarios become available, pricing details will be shared accordingly.

Each assigned user is charged 35 AI Units per month, which includes a pooled request allowance of 22,900 requests per user per month. Requests are pooled across all users, so unused capacity offsets higher usage by others. If the total pooled allowance is exceeded, additional requests are billed at 2 AI Units per 1,000 requests. The number of assigned users can be adjusted monthly.

Joule Studio is available to all customers who deployed Joule (via AI Units entitlement or zero-cost Joule Base SKU) and have BTPEA, CPEA, or PAYG.

SAP for Me provides near-real-time visibility into your AI Unit consumption and remaining balance. If you exceed your purchased AI Units, you may continue using Premium AI services, with additional usage charged at the applicable AI Unit rate per the excess use terms of the contract. For details about applicable rates and terms, consult the SAP AI Features Guide and your SAP account executive.
