What is Joule Studio?

Joule Studio, part of the SAP Business AI Platform, is an AI-first development solution for building and running enterprise-ready AI agents and workflows.

Joule Studio helps you turn business ideas into enterprise-ready solutions with AI that plans and builds for you.

With Joule Studio, you can:

  • Create agents and n8n workflows from a single prompt.

  • Use your data, processes, and system landscape to shape agentic solutions.

  • Give developers and business users the flexibility to build with their preferred tools, whether low-code or pro-code.

  • Enable teams to use your agents through conversations with Joule Work.

Frequently asked questions

Joule Studio is SAP’s unified managed environment for building, deploying, and managing custom AI agents, and n8n workflows. The Joule Studio environment:

  • Supports both low-code and pro-code development.

  • Combines agent building, n8n workflows, and managed runtime.

  • Connects SAP context, integrations, and open development tools.

Joule Studio is for business users, developers, and architects building enterprise AI solutions and extensions across SAP and third-party systems.

 

The solution can be used by:

  • Business users applying their process and domain expertise to create AI agents and workflows through a guided, low-code experience. 

  • Pro-code developers working in integrated development environments, Git, and continuous integration and continuous delivery pipelines. 

  • IT teams and architects that need enterprise security, governance, runtime management, and scale for agents, apps, workflows, and extensions. 

In Joule Studio, you can build:

  • AI agents and skills for task execution and decision support.

  • Workflows and integrations for both SAP and non-SAP systems. 

Joule Studio uses business and technical context to help generate solutions that are compatible with your processes, applications, and architecture.

 

Landscape insights include:

  • Existing architecture.

  • Central agent repository.

  • Compliance policies.

  • Identity controls.

Application contexts include:

  • Data models.

  • Object relationships.

  • Application content.

  • Application functionality.

  • Application KPIs.

Process insights include:

  • Operational gap identification.

  • Process bottlenecks.

  • Improvement recommendations.

Best practices include:

  • Process templates.

  • Development guidelines.

  • Process and industry benchmarks.

Joule Studio’s supports governance with capabilities that help teams secure, test, monitor, and improve AI solutions in production.

 

Governance capabilities include:

  • Identity and access controls, including SAP Cloud Identity Services.

  • Pre-launch testing and validation.

  • Continuous evaluation and improvement of agent performance.

  • Visibility into agent behavior, decisions, and failures.

  • Managed runtime, lifecycle controls, security, and compliance.
