What is SAP Knowledge Graph?

SAP Knowledge Graph is a solution that connects your company’s data fabric to AI for accurate and contextual responses.

Trust your AI

Joule and other AI services provide trusted answers by avoiding hallucinations

 

Simplify your everyday work

Employees and Joule perform at their best with full business context

 

Always be on top of your business

AI agents get all the data they need with access to all data sources

 

Key benefits

Safely run at your best

SAP Knowledge Graph grounds AI in the right data so AI applications avoid hallucinations and provide accurate information for the best business decisions.

Elevate your workforce’s expertise

With Joule and SAP Knowledge Graph, users combine their knowledge, business data, and AI for the best possible outcomes.

Gain peace of mind and reduce IT costs

SAP proactively manages the business context used by SAP Knowledge Graph, reducing your data science effort to effectively ground the AI.

Grounds AI in all customer data on SAP systems
