What is tabular AI on SAP?

Tabular AI on SAP is purpose-built AI for structured data. It combines tabular foundation models such as SAP-RPT and Prior Labs' TabPFN with the orchestration capabilities in SAP AI Core to deliver faster, contextualized, and more accurate predictions at scale, without custom model training or fragmented pipelines.

Unify tabular foundation models and capabilities for predictive insights at scale:

  • Access pretrained, fully managed tabular foundation models from SAP and Prior Labs, built for predictions on structured data.

  • Extend the reach of tabular AI with SAP models and tools to data outside SAP systems.

  • Uncover powerful insights through advanced what-if and additional analysis, such as causation behind business outcomes. 

  • Orchestrate tabular workflows across models and predictive capabilities, even when data is changing, incomplete, or missing. 

  • Monitor and govern tabular AI performance across your organization.

Frequently asked questions

Tabular AI applies machine learning to structured data, or the rows and columns across enterprise data sources that power most business decisions. Organizations are building a strategy around it today because tabular foundation models (TFMs) make it practical to generate accurate predictions at scale, without requiring dedicated model training for every use case. Tabular AI on SAP goes further, bringing TFMs and large language models (LLMs) together with orchestration, centralized management, and governance through a single AI service.

Tabular foundation models are AI systems pretrained on large, structured datasets to learn common tabular data patterns. Unlike LLMs, which are trained on text, tabular foundation models operate natively on rows and columns, fully understanding the rich semantics and relationships in structured data. As fully managed, pretrained models, TFMs eliminate the need for custom training, accelerating time to value while reducing cost and complexity.

A prediction is the statistical output of a tabular AI model. A predictive insight adapts that prediction to prompt users to act with increased precision, based on a complete understanding of the semantics and relationships in your structured data. Tabular AI on SAP goes further by operationalizing predictions and insights with specialized capabilities, such as tabular orchestration, context selection, and registry, that increase the contextualization and reasoning of the AI models based on your business data. It helps you unify your AI practice, cut costs, and deliver the management, governance, and observability needed to scale.

SAP-RPT (relational pretrained transformer) is a fully managed, table-native foundation model that uses predictive capabilities such as regression and classification, and contextualization options like in-context learning, to deliver accurate, explainable predictions from structured business data. It works across business use cases, such as finance, supply chain, sales, and HR, without requiring any model training or fine-tuning. In comparison with other tabular model alternatives, SAP-RPT are trained with real world-business data from decades of experience across domains, and with the business process knowledge that only SAP can provide.

SAP AI Core is the fully managed, scalable AI runtime on the SAP Business AI Platform for deploying and operating AI at enterprise scale. For tabular AI, it brings large language and tabular foundation models, orchestration, context registry and selection, targeted retrieval, and batch inference together in one service. It balances cost, performance, and accuracy through model choice and automatically surfaces the most relevant prediction examples from large datasets, processing data efficiently and asynchronously.

Tabular foundation models are available through the SAP AI Core model library, including the latest versions of SAP-RPT and TabPFN from PriorLabs. Developers can build custom AI applications with them, while models like SAP-RPT already power SAP solutions and Joule Agents, embedding intelligent predictions directly into business workflows.
