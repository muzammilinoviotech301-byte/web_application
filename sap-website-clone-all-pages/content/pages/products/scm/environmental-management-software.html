One solution to operationalize your environmental compliance

SAP S/4HANA for EHS environment management empowers companies to assess, monitor, analyze, and optimize their air, water, waste environmental impact.

The solution tracks and minimizes emissions impact while ensuring regulation and permit compliance. It manages key business processes for:

  • Air emissions, including greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions

  • Water and wastewater

  • Waste

  • Compliance, including obligation registers and permits

Key features

Compliance obligation management

  • Maintain obligation registers and track task fulfillment

  • Stay informed of changes to legal and operational compliance standards

  • Ensure compliance performance with SAP S/4HANA Embedded Analytics

Automated data collection, calculation, and validation

  • Automate multisource data capture for more informed decision-making and accurate reporting

  • Monitor compliance with real-time, audit-ready tracking

  • Validate collected and calculated data using plausibility, warning, and exceedance threshold checks

 

AI-assisted permit management

  • Extract and capture permit data from PDF documents automatically

  • Generate tasks needed for permit requirements using AI

  • Create permit coverage audit trails and reduce data omissions

Built-in equation and emission factors

  • Standardize calculations based on predefined formulas and emission factors

  • Align with regulatory standards for sustainability, such as the GHG Protocol

  • Calculate and analyze environmental footprints for sustainability tracking and reporting
