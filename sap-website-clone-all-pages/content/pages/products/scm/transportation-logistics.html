What is SAP Transportation Management?

The SAP Transportation Management application integrates fleet and logistics management throughout your network, helping you to reduce complexity, increase efficiency, and improve agility for a more sustainable, risk-resilient supply chain.

Maximize your ROI on global transportation and domestic shipping through sustainable:

  • Transportation and demand planning.

  • Interactive freight tendering.

  • Freight settlement.
