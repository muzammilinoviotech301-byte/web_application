Maintenance scheduling

Busy maintenance teams use enhanced scheduling to flexibly respond to changes in the demand picture in a way that maintains operational efficiency.

Optimized workforce utilization Minimize downtime and increase productivity with efficient allocation of equipment, personnel, and materials.

Lightweight dispatching Schedule work directly to resources and personnel with a web-based planning tool for creating work assignments.

Proactive scheduling Reduce costs and downtime with proactive scheduling that helps prevent unplanned downtime and costly breakdowns.

Improved asset reliability and availability Plan and execute maintenance jobs, minimize the risk of unforeseen breakdowns, and meet demand for asset availability.  

Mid- to long-term planning

Increase your visibility into demand over longer horizons and make quick adjustments that align with operational realities.

Efficient capacity planning and work allocation Gain insights into upcoming demand, work types, and priorities to build plans that support demand.

Dynamic capacity adjustment for demand changes Make quick adjustments in response to shifts in demand and keep operations agile and efficient.

Work center integration Incorporate work center capacities to build plans that align with operations, minimize bottlenecks, and simplify planning.

Schedule management

With improved visibility and powerful collaboration tools, you can manage schedules and monitor progress through completion.

Planning and communications Plan and communicate effectively to allocate resources, collaborate on schedules, and improve productivity. 

Recurring schedules Automate recurring schedules to streamline operations, save time, maintain consistency, and minimize administrative burden. 

Schedule monitoring Track schedules in real time, proactively resolve issues, and optimize performance through data-driven analysis.

Scheduling for assets

Drive efficiency with comprehensive asset scheduling views for precise scheduling, safe operations, and reduced bottlenecks.

Comprehensive asset overview for scheduling View the maintenance schedule for assets in a clear hierarchical view that displays sub-assets under superior assets.

Proactive maintenance planning for assets Use Gantt charts to visualize upcoming maintenance and optimize resource allocation with accurate downtime assessments.

Clear and detailed definition of maintenance events Define maintenance events and shutdowns clearly to better understand critical operational activities and plan accordingly.
