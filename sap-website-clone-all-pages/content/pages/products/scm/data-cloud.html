Explore Supply Chain Intelligence use cases for your teams

Supply Chain Intelligence uses AI to transform supply chain operations by unifying data, simulating scenarios, and automating execution.

Deliver scenario intelligence across the value chain

Gain full supply chain visibility and use reliable AI to improve planning, forecasting, and risk management.

Unify supply chain and finance data

Harmonize logistics, supplier, and finance data products with third-party signals like weather, transportation disruptions, and vendor scores to fuel planning and procurement.

Resolve order conflicts from maintenance shifts

Identify production overlaps caused by shifted maintenance and auto-adjust order schedules to preserve fulfillment and revenue.

Simulate maintenance risk scenarios instantly

Use natural language to assess impact, simulate downtime scenarios with Joule, and find options that reduce revenue at risk.

Predict supplier disruptions early

Use supplier risk signals—like delays, regional disruptions, or ESG risk—to forecast delivery issues before they impact production timelines.

Improve demand-supply alignment

Detect demand shifts early using external signals and embedded KPIs, then rebalance supply plans across regions and distribution centers.
