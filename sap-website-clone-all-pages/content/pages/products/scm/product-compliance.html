Key features

Product marketability and component compliance

  • Automatically assess and verify products for different compliance requirements.

  • Ensure adherence to regulations, stakeholder requirements, and industry standards throughout the product lifecycle with integrated compliance checks.

  • Verify registrations and track regulated substances.

Compliance document management

  • Maintain substance and regulatory information in a central system.

  • Manage compliance documents per product and country.

  • Send safety data sheets to recipients automatically through integration with the logistics process.

Dangerous goods management

  • Streamline classification of hazardous materials using embedded dangerous goods content.

  • Automatically create dangerous goods documents.

  • Ensure safe transportation of hazardous materials with integrated compliance checks.

Frequently asked questions

SAP S/4HANA for product compliance is a comprehensive software solution that helps companies from all over the world and from different industries ensure product compliance and safety throughout the product lifecycle. Use it to efficiently manage regulatory and customer requirements; classify products; automatically assess compliance; track registrations and substance volumes; create compliance documents; package, label, transport and store hazardous materials properly; and provide compliance and safety information to all stakeholders.

More than 1,500 product-centric companies (including manufacturing, retail, and wholesale industries and logistic service providers) from all over the world benefit from the SAP solution. Users are product stewards, dangerous goods experts, and procurement, production, and sales employees.

SAP S/4HANA for product compliance is technically embedded in the different deployment options of SAP S/4HANA (on premises, private cloud, public cloud) and therefore requires an SAP S/4HANA installation. As it is not part of the SAP S/4HANA user licenses, it requires a separate license.
