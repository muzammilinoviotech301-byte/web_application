Frequently asked questions

SAP Asset Performance Management helps asset owners, managers, plant managers, and reliability engineers measure and improve the performance of their assets and optimize maintenance strategies. It provides more control and simplifies planning while increasing accuracy. With asset performance management software from SAP you can boost asset and human productivity, improve asset and operational efficiency, detect and predict equipment failures proactively, optimize your return on assets and spending, and simplify and clarify your maintenance and service strategies.

SAP Asset Performance Management requires SAP Enterprise Asset Management (SAP EAM) (SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition 2020 or 2021 FPS01 and above, or ERP 6.0 EHP 7 Sp14 or EHP 8).
