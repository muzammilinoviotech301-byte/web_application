Frequently asked questions

SAP Green Token creates a secure, auditable chain of custody for your materials by linking every transaction to its origin and sustainability attributes. This transactional-level traceability helps companies generate the data and documentation required for regulatory compliance and reporting.

SAP Green Token is designed to support multiple sustainability and traceability frameworks. Today, it focuses on ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification), and EUDR (EU Deforestation-Free Regulation), with the flexibility to adapt to future regulatory requirements.
