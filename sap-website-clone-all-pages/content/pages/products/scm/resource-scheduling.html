Transform maintenance planning and scheduling processes with SAP S/4HANA

Gain actionable insight into your maintenance workload and available capacities for current and upcoming maintenance activities. With this priced capability for SAP S/4HANA Asset Management that enables resource scheduling, you can check forecasted work center utilization, build a schedule, and determine required capacities before executing scheduled activities.

  • Analysis of current workload

  • Creation of a graphical maintenance schedule

  • Simulation of workload and work center use and collaboration on simulations

Key benefits

Gain insights into current and forecasted workloads

Locate open work orders quickly and build an optimal schedule through simulation analysis before releasing assignments for execution.

Optimize the use of maintenance resources

Control maintenance workloads by adjusting activities in your schedule, distributing work evenly across capacities, and collaborating across departments.

Reduce asset downtime and increase productivity

Group and schedule maintenance orders by asset while considering tasks that require planned downtime and identifying intermediary dependencies.

Key features

KPI dashboard for maintenance planners

  • Get an overview of due maintenance orders

  • Gain insights into the use of your work centers and drill down into further applications and details to take action

  • Keep track of your maintenance backlog

Maintenance schedule simulation

  • Build a schedule for the target period or day by simulating scope and workload and share the schedule for collaboration and optimization

  • Schedule maintenance activities to optimize the use of your work centers

  • Dispatch all scheduled activities at once and release them for execution

Graphical overview of maintenance activities

  • Get an easy-to-understand overview of your maintenance schedule

  • Track work status and execution progress

  • Change maintenance activities to level work center utilization
