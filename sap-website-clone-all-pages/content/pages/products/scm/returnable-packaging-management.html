SAP Returnable Packaging Management | Eco-Friendly Packaging

Built on SAP Business Technology Platform, SAP Returnable Packaging Management integrates with SAP systems, drives sustainability by fostering a closed-loop supply chain, reduces packaging costs through efficient planning and packaging rentals, and promotes collaboration with supply chain partners.

Benefits

  • Reduce returnable packaging spend

    Track the receipts and return of packaging materials in real time.

  • Optimize logistic costs

    Track shipments and receipts of returnable packaging to and from partners to minimize loss, waste, and damage in real time.

  • Decrease carbon footprint

    Make supply chains more sustainable by using returnable packaging instead of one-time packaging and have inventory transparency.

  • Avoid revenue loss due to fulfillment Issues

    Gain visibility over full and empty packaging materials, enabling you to proactively address container shortages.

Rental management

Monetize your returnable packaging by establishing rental fees. Improve your container rental process through invoice verification.

Collaboration through the portal

Managing packaging agreements

Avoid supply chain disruptions by improving packaging data quality. Collaborate with different stakeholders on one digital platform to avoid inconsistencies and double work.
