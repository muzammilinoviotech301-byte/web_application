Ensure compliant product sales
The sales representative creates a sales order containing compliance-relevant products.
The system automatically runs a compliance check and blocks the noncompliant sales order due to a missing marketability assessment, displaying warnings for missing safety data sheets and transport restrictions.
The product steward is automatically informed about the missing marketability assessment and documentation for the sales order.
The product steward assigns relevant compliance purposes to the product, triggers an automatic assessment, attaches the required safety data sheet, and approves the sale.
The sales representative confirms that the sales order is no longer blocked.
The sales representative reviews the dangerous goods transport restriction, sees that air transport is forbidden but that international maritime transport is allowed, and saves the sales order.
The sales representative creates a sales order containing compliance-relevant products.

Ensure compliant dangerous goods transportation
The system automatically creates a freight unit based on the sales order.
The transportation planner lists the transportation mode as “sea” and creates freight orders and bookings. Transport is allowed without warnings. A mixed-loading check is automatically performed if more products are included.
The transportation planner reviews the required dangerous good documents, which are automatically generated by the system.
The transportation planner creates the outbound delivery and hands it over to the shipping specialist.
The shipping specialist reviews the delivery note, with dangerous goods data automatically populated by the system.
The product steward can monitor the shipping status of the required safety data sheet to the customer.
The system automatically creates a freight unit based on the sales order.
