What is SAP Digital Manufacturing?

SAP Digital Manufacturing is a manufacturing operations management (MOM) platform that supports agile operations and empowers a sustainable workforce.

With SAP Digital Manufacturing you can connect the shop floor with the top floor to minimize risk and control costs.

  • Cloud deployment on SAP Business Technology Platform
  • Visibility across departments with AI-guided KPIs and analytics
  • Flexible execution coordinated with planning and logistics
  • Skills and certification tracking integrated with SAP SuccessFactors

  • Stage products and tools according to production needs

  • Track and validate both direct and indirect labor

  • Design task automations based on individual workers’ actions

Frequently asked questions

MES stands for manufacturing execution system, a software solution used in manufacturing industries to manage, monitor, and control production processes on the factory floor. It serves as a bridge between enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems and shop floor–control systems to help ensure that production processes are executed efficiently.

In manufacturing, MOM stands for manufacturing operations management. It includes the processes, systems, and software used to manage and optimize manufacturing operations throughout the production lifecycle. MOM covers a broader scope than a manufacturing execution system (MES), integrating aspects of the manufacturing process.

MII stands for manufacturing integration and intelligence. It's a software platform designed to offer real-time data integration, analysis, and reporting across systems in a manufacturing environment. MII connects shop-floor systems with business or operational systems, analyzing data to improve decision making and enhance operational efficiency.

SAP Manufacturing Execution and SAP Manufacturing Integration and Intelligence are being retired, and version 15.5 is the final release. We’ve established a program to help customers transition to SAP Digital Manufacturing; for details, see the SAP Customer Evolution Kit for SAP Digital Manufacturing.

The impact of the cloud on manufacturing is profound, not only in the way that companies execute production but also in the way that IT organizations support operations. Manufacturing companies are using the latest technology, such as AI and contextualized data, to guide operations and ensure reliable productivity. This can be achieved without IT organizations having to maintain multiple systems across locations.
