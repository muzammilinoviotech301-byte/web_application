What is SAP Logistics Management ?

SAP Logistics Management is a cloud-based logistics solution that combines warehousing and transportation management solutions to streamline your operations with network-centric collaboration.

Orchestrate logistics by linking warehouses with supply-chain partners through a unified platform that includes:

  • Warehouse execution.

  • Transportation dispatching.

  • Network collaboration.

  • An AI logistics assistant.

  • SAP Warehouse Logistics app.

Key features

Unified logistics platform

  • Orchestrate low to medium complexity warehousing and transportation processes with a single automated and efficient SaaS solution integrated with SAP Cloud ERP Private.

  • Ensure smooth operations with full interoperability between SAP Transportation Management and SAP Extended Warehouse Management in SAP Cloud ERP Private.

End-to-end process execution

  • Get seamless integration with carriers and supply chain partners through SAP Business Network.

  • Minimize idle time with dock appointment scheduling.

  • Achieve in-transit visibility with real-time execution status.

AI-assisted logistics

  • Reduce your operational workload and speed up processes with real-time insights and automation through Joule.
