Intelligence-driven serialization for a connected life-sciences supply chain

Strengthen end-to-end product integrity amid global regulations. SAP Traceability Hub enables precise serialization compliance, protecting patient safety and building a resilient supply chain.

Benefits

  • Easily comply with global pharmaceutical serialization

    Gain end-to-end visibility into serialized products across the global supply chain. A centralized, compliant data repository delivers complete track-and-trace history, improving traceability, reducing errors, and supporting regulatory compliance.

  • Improve collaboration with trading partners and regulators

    Leverage a secure serialization network to share trusted track-and-trace data across the supply chain. A centralized model simplifies onboarding, ensures data consistency, accelerates compliance reporting, and reduces cost and risk.

  • Secure global growth through track-and-trace compliance

    Strengthen global growth by achieving full compliance with pharmaceutical serialization and track-and-trace regulations. Meeting evolving requirements across markets accelerates product approvals, smooths entry into new countries, and reduces the risk of delays, shipment holds, or costly rework. Robust compliance also builds trust with regulators and partners, turning your serialization investments into a strategic advantage that unlocks new commercial opportunities worldwide.

Features

View media gallery

Simplify regulatory reporting with preconfigured support

Simplify regulatory reporting with ready-to-use serialization connections and message formats. Preconfigured support for FMD and DSCSA enables out-of-the-box compliance across markets without custom integrations.

Onboard partners with convenient self-service access

Reduce onboarding costs and accelerate connectivity by enabling guided self-service access to the serialization network. Partners join faster, improving supply chain agility while strengthening traceability and compliance.

Resolve issues faster with AI-powered error insights

Reduce supply chain disruptions and keep distribution processes running smoothly with embedded Business AI that accelerates the resolution of errors in track‑and‑trace data exchange by analyzing message failures, pinpointing root causes, and recommending fixes. This protects the compliant product flow, and sustains efficient, end‑to‑end distribution operations.
