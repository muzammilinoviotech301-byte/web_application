What is PLM system integration?

PLM system integration enables interoperation between SAP and external PLM systems to enable efficient product data flows and ease collaboration.

  • Out-of-the-box product data integration

  • End-to-end support of advanced business processes

  • One common, extensible domain model

Key benefits

Seamless digital thread through a simplified architecture and pre-configured processes to minimize costs of implementation and operations

Connect data in a single product master

Collaborate on engineering bill of materials (BOMs) with geometric data and configuration, tightly integrated and pervasive across system borders.

Maintain transparency in change management

Manage the impact of change on program plans, manufacturing BOMs, and service BOMs by providing visibility to all stakeholders. 

Integrate market-leading ERP systems with PLM systems

Manage complex product configurations for downstream processes where data is in use—such as manufacturing, supply chain, and logistics departments.

 

Key features

Product data integration

  • Leverage bi-directional out-of-box integrations and preconfigured, extendable processes

  • Achieve high performance with automatic process triggers

  • Gain a unified view of data in external systems through data federation

Business process integration

  • Enable new product development and introduction (NPDI) scenarios with manufacturing engineering

  • Integrate external PLM systems using Siemens Teamcenter, 3DExperience, Windchill, and Autodesk

  • Realize formal change and configure-to-order scenarios

Efficient and transparent data management

  • Realize seamless material management for better data quality and transparency with simplified supplier management

  • Integrate product structure management for various BOM types and advanced variant configuration for better collaboration

  • Establish efficient production planning, including PEO snapshots
