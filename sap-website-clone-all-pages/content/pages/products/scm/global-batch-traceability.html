SAP Global Batch Traceability

Get instant insight into your corporate batch genealogies and their distribution by unifying them across different systems.
Request a quote

Understand the relationships within your batch genealogies

Perform immediate reporting on your global batch usage and distribution, as well as material balances, by linking batch records from disparate business systems.

Gain a corporate-level repository that connects to multiple record systems and links batches. With the SAP Global Batch Traceability application, you can access a cohesive corporate view of product batch genealogies and their distribution.

  • Capture and linking of batch usage across distributed systems
  • In-the-moment report generation for precision recall, withdrawal, or hold
  • On-premises deployment

Key benefits

Improve the monitoring of batch-product quality

Help ensure the quality of your batch-managed products by complying with batch posting processes that support good manufacturing and distribution practices.

Act swiftly when resolving quality defects

Respond and report on product issues quickly with the immediate reporting and insights you need to enact holds, withdrawals, or recalls.

Key features

Capture and linking of batch usage across systems

  • Explore your product genealogies graphically
  • Monitor your global batch usage and distribution proactively
  • Support Good Manufacturing and Distribution Practices

In-the-moment report generation on batches

  • Perform reporting with a selection of multiple batches in a single run
  • Use templates to report external and internal distribution records and material balances
  • Enable precision recalls, withdrawals, and holds
