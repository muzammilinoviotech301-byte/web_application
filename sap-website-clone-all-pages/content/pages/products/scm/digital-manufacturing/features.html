SAP Digital Manufacturing features

Request a demo
Watch the video

Insights

Data analysis from top floor to shop floor and across operations provides real-time views tailored to your area(s) of responsibility.

Live monitoring

Enhance awareness with KPIs on any tile or page to guide action and impact operations.

Operational analytics

Get visibility across all locations and modes of production using embedded SAP Analytics Cloud.

Advanced analytics

Perform detailed overall equipment effectiveness analysis with prebuilt and adaptable calculations.

Extensibility

Securely reach into the manufacturing data model using the latest APIs and services.

Orchestration

Closed-loop planning within the enterprise helps you adjust production to fit your changing needs and digitally respond to unforeseen events in real time.

Dispatch and monitoring

Schedule and dispatch operations, monitor operational events and resource statuses, allocate labor, and automatically balance production in coordination with your supply chain.

Labor management and scheduling

Assign employees to cost centers and shift schedules for direct and indirect labor tracking.

Skills and certifications

Manage and track employee skills and certifications in a skills matrix that is optionally integrated with SAP SuccessFactors.

Execution

The bidirectional integration of business systems with operations ensures continuous alignment with planning, logistics, quality, maintenance, safety, and workforce.

Hybrid manufacturing

Support many types of production scenarios (repetitive, discrete, process, and service) all in one application.

Production operator dashboard

Guide workers and coordinate automation tasks with a simple interface for a no- or low-touch experience.

Coordination

Stage product, capture quality, report unplanned maintenance, track labor, and ensure compliance and efficiency.

Extensible

Use a no-code production operator dashboard designer or further customize your experience with a pro-code approach using Kyma.

Automation

Using your existing infrastructure, the application guides work automatically and in real time.

Design processes

Graphically define process workflows to coordinate people, process, and automation.

Communication

Synchronously and asynchronously communicate with diverse technical systems and manage their business context following the latest industrial standards.

Issue resolution

A collaboration space helps address and prevent issues by involving both internal and external stakeholders in structured, problem-solving processes.

Report issues

Upload visual content from any device or system to report issues for quality control.

Manage issues

Review issues, group them logically, and provide guidance for resolution.

Create problem-solving processes

Use AI to auto-generate processes based on historical approaches and fine-tune them to your collaboration needs.

Manage problem-solving processes

Analyze the root cause of problems, assign tasks with follow-up actions, and create learning materials for ongoing improvement.
