Streamline supply chain planning and control for meat, poultry, or fish

Centralize processes for planning and controlling your meat, poultry, or fish supply chain so you can produce quality products that meet the needs of your customers. The SAP Meat and Fish Management application by msg enables you to manage livestock operation and procurement, harvesting, disassembly, and further processing.

  • Connectivity between all business users

  • Ability to compare planned, actual, and target data

  • Reporting on segmentation gain and loss

  • On-premises deployment

Key benefits

Simplify processes with one point of access

Derive insight from real-time, centralized data available through a single point of access.

Accelerate decision-making with real-time insight

Simplify the storage, processing, and flow of information and insight across your organization.

Lower compliance risk with proactive tracking

Build traceability and standards into every aspect of your supply chain to ensure compliance.

Key features

Planning and monitoring precision

  • Provide a new level of connectivity across your business users

  • Optimize purchasing with an integrated planning strategy

  • Monitor current and future requirements based on sales, operational planning, or customer demand

Management of meat processes

  • Reduce revenue loss due to stock-outs

  • Increase data accuracy on planning, inventory, and customer demand

  • Get a clear view of detailed segmentation costs to make timely and efficient decisions

Simpler operations through improved procurement

  • Help ensure a reliable flow of livestock and meat

  • Handle complex livestock pricing schemes by reducing time and avoiding errors

  • Operate with animal classification data and control the origin
