SAP S/4HANA Supply Chain for extended service parts planning

Get the right parts in the right place at the right time across the entire supply network.
Contact us

Take your aftermarket business to the next level

Benefit from service parts planning capabilities and transparency throughout your supply chain – from the moment demand occurs to product delivery.

Plan across your supply network in real time. With the SAP S/4HANA Supply Chain solution for extended service parts planning, you can evaluate your parts inventory based on part volume, velocity, segment, and other practical business rules.

  • End-to-end parts planning for distribution centric businesses

  • Transparency and planning capabilities

  • Real-time, network-based planning for complex and volume-intense processes

  • Planning and execution in a single system

Key benefits

Balance inventory investment and customer service

Minimize your inventory holding costs while consistently providing the level of service your customers expect.

Centralize and automate planning

Save manual effort for more strategic work by automating and streamlining processes, such as service parts planning.

Gain transparency and improve planning accuracy

Increase transparency into stock quantities and requirements across the entire service parts supply chain.

Key features

Demand management

  • Capture historical demand

  • Manage and reorganize demand

  • Make use of external predictors for forecasting and leverage powerful forecasting methods including machine learning algorithms

Inventory planning

  • Make better stocking and non-stocking decisions

  • Calculate safety stock using embedded multi-echelon inventory optimization

  • Determine the economic order quantity

Supply planning and distribution

  • Roll up requirements through the network

  • Generate purchase requisitions, planned orders, and release schedules

  • Distribute available supply based on specific considerations

Simplified path to innovation

  • Support tactical and operational planning and execution in one system

  • Provide a central user interface for your planners

  • Opt for integration to enable real-time analytics
