SAP Intelligent Agriculture

By digitalizing your farming processes and services, your agribusiness company can sustainably increase farming efficiency.

Enabling data-driven farming operations with intelligent agriculture

With data science and machine learning capabilities, you can harness the power of your data to optimize plan-to-harvest processes.

Agribusinesses capture an increasing amount of data in the field and on the farm. The SAP Intelligent Agriculture solution can help you efficiently use this data to refine processes and make decisions that help your farming business thrive.

  • Cloud deployment

  • Enterprise-grade farm data management

  • Agile and open farming intelligence framework

  • Efficient farming processes

Key benefits

Achieve consistency in your farming data

Improve data quality and connect existing data sets to increase productivity and gain valuable insights. 

Increase productivity in farm management

Manage, schedule, and execute farming activities more efficiently throughout the growing cycle with everyone working from common farming plans and schedules.

Digitalize your own farming experience

Develop valuable, differentiating farming decision support models in corporate assets that drive farming efficiency and sustainability.

Key features

Farm data management

  • Manage farming and field data consistently across the enterprise
  • Develop a broad crop-independent data model
  • Enable integration with intelligent enterprise solutions from SAP

Farming processes

  • Plan and schedule farming tasks and resources
  • Keep records of field history and performance, helping you plan past, current, and future crops
  • Use a farming cockpit to obtain a current map-based overview of farm and field usage

Farming intelligence

  • Use structured and unstructured data from a variety of sources for data science and machine learning scenarios
  • Develop new decision support models or integrate existing ones
  • Increase efficiency and sustainability in farming processes by providing intelligent executable recommendations
