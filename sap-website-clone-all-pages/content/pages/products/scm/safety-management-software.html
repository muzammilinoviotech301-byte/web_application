What is SAP S/4HANA for EHS workplace safety?

Our comprehensive safety solution helps to simplify incident handling, identify hazards, and mitigate risks for a safer, more efficient and compliant workplace.

With advanced and automated safety software, you can promote a workplace culture that helps keep workers safe and productive.

The solution supports these key processes:

  • Incident management

  • Operational risk management

  • Chemical management

  • Change management

  • Occupational health

Key features

Incident management

  • Track incidents for consistent entry, investigation, and corrective actions within a centralized system.

  • Support multiple recording methods including conversational AI, mobile, and offline options.

  • Handle various incident types and observations with a specialized data structure and strong security.

Workplace safety insights

  • Access real-time insights on key regulatory KPIs such as OSHA to ensure safety standards alignment.

  • Detect hotspots and emerging patterns on safety performances to inform decision-making.

  • Initiate corrective and preventative tasks based on actionable data insights to address potential risks.

HR process integration

  • Ensure continuous workforce education with learning management system integration.

  • Assign targeted learning tasks following safety incident investigations to address and rectify skill gaps.

  • Proactively allocate training programs based on risk assessments.

Safety instruction preparation with AI

  • Use AI capabilities to analyze risk assessment and job hazard data to create detailed safety instructions.

  • Tailor safety guidelines to specific machine and equipment risks identified in assessments for improved accuracy.

  • Free up time to spend on high-value safety management tasks through automation.
