Commodity Physicals

Advanced commodity pricing engine

  • Integrate commodity pricing and calculation capabilities across purchase and sales orders, contracts, invoices, and settlement processes.

  • Define pricing formulas using a flexible, rules-based framework aligned to your business and market requirements. 

  • Use centralized commodity market data as a single source of truth for consistency, accuracy, and transparency across transactions. 

Expense management for commodity businesses

  • Centralize and govern your expense rule framework in a single, flexible model to ensure consistency, transparency, and control.

  • Automatically assign expenses to relevant documents across the process chain to ensure accurate and consistent financial tracking.

  • Adapt to changing business requirements with a fully flexible and configurable expense management approach.

  • Integrate expense data into reporting and analytics to gain clear visibility into cost drivers, compliance, and financial performance.

 

FX risk management for commodity businesses

  • Automatically determine commodity price exposure directly from physical documents, ensuring timely and accurate risk visibility. 

  • Execute a fully automated hedging flow to reduce manual intervention and improve operational efficiency. 

  • Support multiple hedging strategies, including accumulation, netting, and direct hedging, for diverse risk management approaches. 

  • Utilize native integration with treasury and risk management systems for seamless processing and control across operations. 

SAP Commodity Management, AI-assisted commodity work center

  • Generate contracts using natural language, accelerating contract creation while maintaining consistency and control. 

  • Reduce manual effort and operational complexity with a highly automated framework that adapts across contract types. 

  • Guide users with prefiltered, context-aware options to ensure accurate contract outcomes when inputs are incomplete or ambiguous. 

SAP Agricultural Contract Management

  • Consolidate complex agricultural terms and conditions into a single source of truth, to govern commodity contracts and mitigate risk. 

  • Support partial and differential pricing with fixed and unfixed prices, from contract to invoice.  

  • Track delivered load details and apply them during contract application and settlement, with adjustments created automatically.

Commodity derivatives

Manage derivatives

  • Support a broad range of exchange-traded instruments, including futures and listed options, as well as OTC instruments.

  • Operate on centralized commodity market data to ensure consistent valuation, pricing, and risk assessment across instruments.

  • Integrate into valuation processes and accounting postings to ensure accurate financial representation and compliance.

  • Use the Derivative Contract Specification framework with position management to maintain control over derivative exposures.

Commodity derivative order and trade execution

  • Create and place hedge orders directly from physical documents, simplifying the transition from operational exposure to risk management.

  • Receive and process broker confirmations via FIX protocol messages to ensure fast, reliable, and standardized communication. 

  • Automatically create corresponding hedge positions within your system, reducing manual effort and operational risk. 

  • Feed hedge transactions directly into risk reporting to provide real-time visibility into exposure and hedging effectiveness. 

Commodity hedge accounting

  • Enable IFRS 9–compliant cash flow hedge accounting to ensure regulatory adherence and financial transparency.

  • Support commodity swaps and commodity forwards to cover a wide range of hedging instruments. 

  • Automate hedge accounting postings to reduce manual effort and operational risk. 

  • Support fully automated on‑behalf‑of hedging scenarios to manage centralized risk strategies across entities. 

Broker reconciliation for commodity derivatives

  • Automate reconciliation processes to investigate and resolve deviations with less human intervention.

  • Efficiently identify missing and erroneous trades and positions to decrease commodity risk exposure. 

  • Support fast, efficient, and accurate commodity trade and position reconciliation with a standardized cloud solution.

Commodity risk analytics

Commodity risk analytics is key in assessing risk resulting from commodity transactions and enables timely hedging to protect margins

  • Gain a full view of commodity price risk exposure across both physical documents and derivative instruments.

  • Obtain a quantity-based overview of hedged and unhedged positions to clearly understand your current risk profile. 

  • Value physical documents and derivative contracts against current market data using mark-to-market risk reports for accurate assessments. 

  • Analyze trading performance with detailed profit and loss reporting to identify gains, losses, and key value drivers.
