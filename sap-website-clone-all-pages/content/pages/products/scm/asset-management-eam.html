Frequently asked questions

Enterprise resource planning (ERP) is a software system that helps you to manage and integrate the core parts of your business, supporting automation and processes in finance, human resources, manufacturing, supply chain, services, procurement, and more. Enterprise asset management (EAM) is an important business area within supply chain management and incorporates the management and intelligent maintenance of physical assets owned by a company throughout the entire asset lifecycle.

  • What is ERP?

  • What is EAM?

A computerized maintenance management system (CMMS) is a tool to automate maintenance and operations management. It allows asset-intensive industries to focus on driving asset uptime. An EAM system enables asset-intensive industries to manage the lifecycle of physical assets including capital planning, procurement, installation, performance, maintenance, compliance, risk management, and asset disposal.

EAM software helps organizations manage the whole lifecycle of physical assets including capital planning, procurement, installation, performance, maintenance, compliance, risk management, and asset disposal.

 

Learn more:

  • What is EAM?

Asset collaboration is the aspect of asset management that brings together various stakeholders in the physical asset lifecycle to collaborate, cooperate, and meet asset management objectives. Where asset management incorporates the management and maintenance of physical assets owned by a company throughout the lifecycle of an asset, asset collaboration involves the sharing of asset data and workflow processes across asset owners, operators, manufacturers, and service providers. Asset collaboration activities span asset definition, commissioning, and maintenance, and help organizations achieve optimal asset management. To learn more, read about asset collaboration on SAP Business Network.
