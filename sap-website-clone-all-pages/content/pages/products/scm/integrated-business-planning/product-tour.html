Overview
The future of supply chain is autonomous, and your people lead the way. Explore how SAP Integrated Business Planning (SAP IBP) combines AI with decision-centric planning to help you act faster, adapt smarter, and move toward an autonomous enterprise.
Strategic planning
Supply chain planning doesn’t exist in a vacuum. It begins with financial planning, which represents a company’s strategy in financial terms.
Consider financial targets, goals, and implications in supply chain planning processes.
Include your trading partners in the supply chain planning process with SAP Business Network natively integrated with SAP IBP.
Optimize your supply chain design in line with your business objectives with AIMMS SC Navigator. Supply chain design and redesign have significant cost implications and must be mathematically optimized.
Visually compare existing supply chain designs with calculated alternatives with AIMMS SC Navigator.
Let AI compare and summarize each alternative design based on user-defined KPIs and calculate ROIs using operating expenditure vs. capital expenditure trade-offs with AIMMS SC Navigator.
