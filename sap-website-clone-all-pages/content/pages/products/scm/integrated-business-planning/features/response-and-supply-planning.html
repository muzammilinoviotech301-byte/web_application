Empower planners with AI-driven supply planning

Create decision-centric constrained supply plans that satisfy demand while fulfilling inventory target objectives.

Multilevel supply planning 

Create an effective supply plan for your entire network by modeling across locations and multilevel bills of materials. Planners have the flexibility to decide which constraints to include in a feasible supply plan.

Unconstrained heuristic, prioritization, or optimization algorithm options 

Prioritization and optimization algorithms enable SAP Integrated Business Planning to perform a feasible and constrained supply plan in just one step while considering all modeled constraints.

Empower planners with post-planning decision intelligence

Make smarter and faster decisions with AI-assisted supply optimization results analysis.

Visibility into supply optimization runs

Integrated Business Planning utilizes gen AI to analyze planning run logs, address encountered issues, provide detailed explanations and resolution  suggestions,  and compare different planning runs to derive meaningful insights. 

Greater supply planner productivity 

By comparing different planning scenarios, planners can quickly understand the results of a supply optimizer run and reduce lost sales and inventory costs.

Empower planners with collaboration and simulations

Collaborate with suppliers and respond to short-term disruptions and changes.

Medium- to long-term rough-cut capacity planning 

Send component requirements to suppliers, utilizing their firm commitments as constraints for supply plans based on real data instead of static assumptions.

Scenario simulation  

Run and compare what-if scenarios and analyze differences via dashboards or detailed tables. Easily share your findings and updates with other planners in your organization to enable cross-team collaboration. 

Empower planners with executional alignment

Create consistency with synchronized planning and post-production activities.

Supply planning, production planning, and detailed scheduling

After determining the source production plant and planned order quantities, a handover process converts them into scheduling orders that SAP S/4HANA can sequence.

Synchronized planning 

Enable real-time and bi-directional data transfer between SAP S/4HANA and SAP IBP through standard integration cycles.

Deployment and load-building capabilities 

Create a feasible distribution plan downstream through the supply chain network.
