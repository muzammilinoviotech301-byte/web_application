FAQ

SAP supports companies across the mining value chain to streamline operations, enhance data visibility, improve supply chain efficiency, improve worker safety, and support compliance and innovation. Key business areas supported for mining include commercial, inbound supply chain, mine development and operations, sustainability, finance, and human capital management. SAP integrates AI, data, and applications into one unified system, creating a continuous cycle of value that drives business transformation.

SAP Business AI is embedded into SAP applications and uses business data and context to help mining companies streamline operations to maximize profits and run equipment efficiently. It helps ensure safety and compliance and minimize waste and impact. Intelligent agents can constantly monitor supply chains for end-to-end visibility, keeping mining operations competitive while maximizing equipment uptime and output. The solution also offers insights into the condition of every mining asset in real time, helping companies actively manage their portfolio, drive growth, and keep people safe.
