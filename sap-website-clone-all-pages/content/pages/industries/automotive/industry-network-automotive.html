Frequently asked questions

Catena-X is a joint project of automotive OEMs, industry, and IT suppliers. Together with BMW, Deutsche Telekom, Bosch, Siemens and ZF Friedrichshafen as well as the former German Federal Minister of Economic Affairs and Energy, SAP initiated an open scalable network based on Gaia-X (in Q4, 2020).  With the Catena-X Automotive Network, we jointly want to provide a common standard for the secure and interoperable exchange of data and information across companies along the automotive value chain. The cloud-based network is open to participants across the automotive value chain. Regardless of size, automakers and their global partners, dealers, and suppliers can join the network. Catena-X  owns the network while SAP is a member of Catena-X and provides solutions that are certified by Catena-X.

The Catena-X ecosystem consists of three areas – the Catena-X Automotive Network , the development environment, and the operating environment:

  • The Catena-X Automotive Network is responsible for standardization, certifications, and governance of the Catena-X ecosystem. Membership is continuously growing and members can participate in working groups to actively shape the Catena-X ecosystem. SAP is one of the founding members of the association.

  • The Catena-X consortium, which is funded by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy, will perform the core development work during the funding period until mid-2024. SAP is one of a total of 28 partners of the Catena-X consortium.

  • In the operating environment, the various open source and commercial services and business applications are run by different providers. SAP is one of ten shareholders of the recently founded joint venture Cofinity-X, the first officially certified operating company.

Catena-X aims to provide greater resiliency and transparency to the automotive supply chain, improving its quality and efficiency for all companies involved. This open and collaborative data ecosystem perfectly reflects SAP’s vision to enable every organization to become a network of intelligent, sustainable enterprises. It also gives the companies leveraging the Catena-X Automotive Network access to a broad portfolio of automotive IT solutions from SAP, from enabling product traceability across the supply chain to tracking and calculating Scope 3 emissions.

Trust, interoperability, and data sovereignty, these are the objectives and values for secure and sustainable peer-to-peer data exchange between organizations and companies. Based on the International Data Spaces (IDS) concept, SAP offers the Eclipse Dataspace Connector (EDC) component as part of SAP Integration Suite in its new Data Space Integration capability. Users and customers of SAP solutions can access SAP-based and third-party hosted data through the Catena-X ecosystem.

There is a minimal set of requirements you need to participate in the Catena-X ecosystem:

  • Register your company through an operating company (such as Cofinity-X) and get a business partner number (BPN)

  • Install an EDC or use SAP Integration Suite

  • Define the use case and business application you want to enable Catena-X network connectivity.

Catena-X connects providers and users irrespective of their company size. The majority of Catena-X Automotive Network participants are SMEs and include many companies with little data readiness or digital footprint.
