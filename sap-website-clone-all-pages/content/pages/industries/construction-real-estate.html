ERP streamlines operations across project financials, HR, compliance, procurement, maintenance, and sales. ERP automates key business processes and provides real-time insights, allowing businesses to make faster decisions, improve efficiency, and deliver excellence in project delivery.

SAP and Smartapp ONE™ connect the field to the back office by linking jobsite activity, contracts, timesheets, and supplier data directly to project financials. This seamless integration eliminates manual reentry and delays, ensuring critical updates such as costs, progress, and change orders flow automatically into the system. The result is realtime visibility, fewer errors, and a single source of truth for teams managing budgets, compliance, and project and portfolio performance.

SAP brings the power of AI to construction and real estate by weaving intelligence into every step of the project and property lifecycle. From predicting costs and timelines to optimizing jobsite resources and safety, AI helps teams deliver projects faster and with greater confidence. In real estate, AI analyzes leasing, energy, and portfolio data to uncover new revenue opportunities while reducing waste and emissions. By unifying data across finance, operations, and assets, SAP gives companies the insights they need to work smarter, minimize risk, and build long-term value.

SAP provides real estate companies with advanced tools and solutions to effectively manage the customer lifecycle. Through streamlined processes and automation, SAP enables efficient handling of the lead-to-lease and lead-to-sales processes from managing lease and sales contracts, onboarding tenants, and managing tenant service and maintenance requests. Its integrated platforms enable real-time data access to enhance decision-making. As a result, real estate companies can optimize operations, reduce costs, and improve tenant and customer satisfaction.
