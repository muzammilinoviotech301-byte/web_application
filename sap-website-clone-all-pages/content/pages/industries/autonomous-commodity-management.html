Frequently asked questions

Autonomous Commodity Management is an SAP approach that connects commodity trading, logistics, processing, and risk management through industry-trained AI agents and assistants. It coordinates the commodity management process from origination to settlement, helping businesses improve margins, increase capacity utilization, and reduce operational costs.

AI agents improve commodity trading and contract administration by automating handoffs, enriching trade information, and delivering timely insights. AI for commodity trading helps traders manage high-volume deals and identify arbitrage opportunities. AI-powered commodity trading also connects trading decisions with contracts, freight, and risk, helping teams coordinate execution across the commodity lifecycle.

Commodity management helps businesses respond to market volatility by tracking price exposure and consolidating physical and financial commodity positions. Commodity trading and risk management software supports derivatives management, hedging, and visibility into mark-to-market valuations and profit and loss.

Autonomous Commodity Management supports industries that are exposed to commodity price fluctuations and complex material flows, including agribusiness, automotive, mill products, mining, and oil, gas, and energy. It helps to connect purchasing, trading, transportation, inventory, and processing decisions across these industries.
