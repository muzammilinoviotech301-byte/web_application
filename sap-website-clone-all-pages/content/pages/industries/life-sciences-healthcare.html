SAP Intelligent Clinical Supply Management is critical for life science organizations to streamline clinical trial management, improve transparency, reduce cycle times, and enhance collaboration between stakeholders involved in clinical trials. The solution helps organizations optimize their supply chain processes, reduce manual tasks, and ensure compliance with stringent industry regulations. It can also integrate with Interactive Response Technology (IRT) and Clinical Trial Management Systems (CTMS), facilitating demand forecasting and managing specific blinding and randomization needs for clinical trials.

SAP Advanced Therapy Orchestration is a dedicated solution that supports personalized cell and gene therapies. This specialized platform is designed to handle the intricacies of managing individualized treatments and their supply chains.

 

SAP Advanced Therapy Orchestration streamlines orchestration from order to release with automated workflows and precise, real-time management. It helps reduce fulfillment lead times, improve operational efficiency, and enhance patient safety through complete traceability, chain of identity, and integrated collaboration.

SAP Batch Release Hub for Life Sciences automates and simplifies the batch release process. It addresses compliance needs, reduces manual effort, and integrates with various quality management systems to enhance operational efficiency.

Novartis Pharma AG utilized SAP Batch Release Hub for Life Sciences to streamline its batch release processes, consolidating data from 15 IT systems and improving productivity through automation.

SAP helps life sciences companies comply with global drug serialization and reporting requirements by supporting serial number management, secure supply chain data exchange, and regulatory reporting.

 

SAP Traceability Hub Connectivity enables secure exchange of serialized product data across the pharmaceutical supply chain and supports regulatory reporting with ready-to-use connections and message formats. Preconfigured support for regulations such as the U.S. Drug Supply Chain Security Act (DSCSA) and the EU Falsified Medicines Directive (FMD) helps simplify compliance across markets.

 

SAP Advanced Track and Trace for Pharmaceuticals on SAP S/4HANA provides advanced serial number management, including randomization, and supports end-to-end product traceability to help life sciences companies meet global drug serialization and reporting requirements while integrating with warehouse operations, third-party logistics providers, and other supply-chain partners.
