FAQ

Autonomous Adaptive Production is a closed-loop agentic AI solution that orchestrates end-to-end production workflows for customized orders. Powered by AI agents for manufacturing, it eliminates process silos and knowledge gaps across the entire value chain—from sales and engineering to delivery—activating structural value and advancing the Autonomous Enterprise.

Industrial manufacturing companies gain the most from Autonomous Adaptive Production—helping complex solution providers manage highly customized orders. AI agents for manufacturing also serve consumer, automotive, and high-tech industries that manage complex, customer-tailored production, tendering, and delivery challenges.

Autonomous Adaptive Production by SAP supports CTO and ETO through the CTO+ approach, combining standardized product configurations with customer-specific engineering. AI-supported capabilities help automate tender and inquiry analysis, quote creation, configuration, feasibility assessment, engineering, cost planning, and change-request management. By connecting these activities end to end, manufacturers can respond faster to complex requirements, reuse standardized and modular components, improve cost and feasibility decisions, and efficiently manage customized orders from quotation through production.

SAP's industrial AI agents embed natively across sales, engineering, supply chain, and manufacturing roles—bridging analytics and execution within the system of record. By integrating business rules by default and automating CTO and ETO process management, teams reduce project lead times, cut change management costs, and increase engineering productivity without disrupting existing operations.
