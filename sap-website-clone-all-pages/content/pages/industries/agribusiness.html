FAQ

Integrating applications, data, and AI into a continuous improvement cycle has a compounding effect and creates real business value. This allows agribusiness companies to realize efficient and sustainable farming, better support origination, improved trading and grower engagement, and agile operations, as well as build resilient supply chains and business networks.

Leading agribusiness companies are implementing AI-supported, data-driven farming processes to optimize farming efficiency and sustainability. They are digitalizing and automating quality control processes, consolidating and standardizing soil sensor data, automatically determining irrigation thresholds, and generating irrigation recommendations. Additionally, detailed field information and advanced satellite data and crop simulation enable them to refine agriculture and food industry planning.
