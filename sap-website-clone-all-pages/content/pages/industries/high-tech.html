FAQ

SAP offers an integrated, cloud-based ERP platform designed to address the industry’s specific challenges around rapid innovation, flexible business models, supply chain complexity, and sustainability requirements. The cloud ERP suite combines deep industry expertise with advanced technologies, supporting companies in transforming and optimizing the full range of business processes—from R&D and manufacturing to sales, service, and monetization.

SAP provides a comprehensive portfolio tailored for high-tech companies, spanning ERP, supply chain, manufacturing, product lifecycle, sales, subscription revenue, finance, analytics, sustainability, and talent management.

The combined use of SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, SAP Subscription Billing, and SAP Entitlement Management enables software providers to flexibly design, monetize, deliver, and optimize their digital products and services across all business models and markets.

Cloud ERP brings together process integration, scalability, innovation, supply chain optimization, and business model flexibility—making it foundational for the competitiveness and resilience of high-tech companies in today’s rapidly changing digital landscape.

SAP integrates AI both as embedded, no-code intelligence in standard solutions and as a flexible platform for co-innovation—making high-tech companies more efficient, agile, and competitive in today’s digital economy. In addition, SAP delivers ready-to-use AI agents designed for high-tech business priorities, including agents for:

  • Recurring revenue monitoring and simulation (for XaaS).

  • Shop floor supervisors and manufacturing optimization.

  • Outsourced manufacturing orchestration.

SAP supports sustainability in the high-tech industry through a comprehensive portfolio of solutions, tools, and best practices designed to embed sustainability insights and controls into core business processes. These products enable companies to manage emissions, optimize resource use, ensure regulatory compliance, and drive responsible innovation across the lifecycle—from product design to supply chain and circular business models.
