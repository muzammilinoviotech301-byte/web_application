Frequently asked questions

A digital health platform provides a modular and composable foundation that connects clinical, operational, and financial domains through a governed data layer. SAP supports this vision by delivering an open, data-centric platform that leverages standards such as FHIR, integrates with any EMR, and enables organizations to seamlessly combine SAP and partner solutions. AI-driven innovation, growing interoperability requirements, and increasing operational complexity are redefining the technology needs of healthcare organizations.

SAP delivers healthcare-specific business capabilities across every operational and administrative domain—financial management, workforce, supply chain, procurement, and governance—on a modular, composable foundation that lets organizations adopt new capabilities without disrupting core operations. Working alongside clinical systems rather than replacing them, SAP adds the operational intelligence and connectivity that care delivery depends on. SAP Business AI enables faster, more confident decisions and automates routine workflows through AI agents while keeping humans in control.

Grounded in decades of healthcare industry best practices, SAP Business AI Platform turns data into confident decisions across every dimension of how a healthcare organization runs. Joule, SAP's AI solution, brings clinical and operational context into every workflow across a healthcare organization, helping finance teams close faster, supply chain teams anticipate disruptions, workforce teams manage compliance, and care teams reduce administrative burden.

With more than 50 years of experience navigating industry regulations, SAP embeds governance and compliance by design across every building block it delivers: data policies apply consistently across every system; access is controlled and auditable; and records are retained across the full lifecycle. For organizations operating across borders, SAP Sovereign Cloud deployment options ensure data residency and regulatory requirements are met in every jurisdiction.

SAP's partner ecosystem extends core business capabilities with healthcare-specific functionality, including clinical workflows, hospital information systems, revenue cycle management, and region-specific operational capabilities. Many partner solutions are tailored to local healthcare environments and regulatory requirements, reflecting the regional depth healthcare organizations need.
