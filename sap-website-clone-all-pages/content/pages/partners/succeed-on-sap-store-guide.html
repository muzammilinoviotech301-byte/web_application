Frequently asked questions

  • What is SAP Store?

    SAP Store is the official global enterprise marketplace where customers discover, try, buy, and renew over 3,000 partner-built solutions. It serves as a centralized digital storefront for applications spanning 40 categories and 25 industries, allowing customers to easily extend their core SAP environments.

  • How do I become an SAP Partner to sell on the store?

    To sell solutions on the marketplace, you must join the SAP Partner Program . This program provides businesses with the global brand authority, development technology, and direct customer access required to build and deploy integrations within the SAP ecosystem.

  • How do I list and publish a solution on the SAP Store?

    Eligible partners can publish applications by logging into the partner portal to initiate the listing setup . The process involves configuring your solution's integration parameters, defining transactional checkout workflows, and choosing between standard automated pricing or customized multi-layered contract terms.
