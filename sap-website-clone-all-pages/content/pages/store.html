How to buy on SAP Store

  • What is SAP Store?

    SAP Store is an online marketplace where SAP customers can buy business solutions, and partners can list and sell theirs. Through the SAP app store, customers around the world can discover, try, purchase, and renew more than 3,000 enterprise solutions from SAP and our trusted partners. Available in more than 200 countries and territories globally, the SAP service marketplace within SAP Store provides customers with real-time access to innovative software and services that integrate with SAP platforms. SAP partner offerings allow customers to extend their existing SAP technology and solutions with a mix of free trials and paid apps and services. Partners can grow their businesses using SAP Store to market and deliver their apps, add-ins, and integration kits to SAP customers globally.

  • How do I access SAP’s marketplace?

    You can access the online SAP marketplace and explore SAP and partner solutions through SAP Store. Use the search bar or browsing tools on the SAP Store home page to find SAP and partner solutions.

  • How do I find solutions to try or buy on SAP’s store?

    First, make sure that the country selector at the top right of the SAP Store page displays your current country. Then, type what you’re looking for into the search bar or browse by categories. On the search results page, you can refine the results with filters like categories, industry, works with, solution type, publisher, certification, and trial availability.

  • Why should I buy through SAP Store?

    Customers worldwide buy from SAP Store because it helps them confidently find, try, and buy enterprise solutions that solve their unique business needs. The growing marketplace SAP catalog includes more than 3,000 solutions across categories and industries. SAP Store offers a range of customer benefits, including simple, secure, and flexible buying experiences, seamless integration with SAP products, transparent pricing and contract terms, and faster time to value—customers can go from purchasing a solution to accessing it in less than 15 minutes without licensing delays. SAP Store customers also enjoy best-in-class onboarding and support with access to step-by-step guides, documentation, and escalation paths through SAP’s support infrastructure—along with partner support.

  • What types of solutions are available?

    SAP Store offers enterprise solutions across categories, including AI, business technology, CRM and customer experience, data cloud, financial management, supply chain management, and sustainability, to name a few. SAP marketplace solutions also span all industries, from aerospace to education to utilities. You can start browsing with smart filters that guide your search by category, industry, SAP product compatibility, or deployment type.

  • Do I need to be an SAP customer to buy partner products on SAP Store?

    You don’t need to be an SAP customer to purchase solutions published by SAP partners in SAP Store. However, existing customers will benefit the most from these partner solutions since most of them work with or extend SAP solutions.

  • How does purchasing work?

    Once you’ve found a solution you want to buy, SAP Store offers flexible purchase paths to fit your needs. Many listings offer a Buy now option, enabling you to complete a purchase online with full visibility into pricing, terms, and deployment options. To purchase one of these solutions, select Buy now and log in with your business account. For solutions with a Request a quote button, enter your information to connect with SAP or the partner directly to continue your purchase.

    Many SAP Store solutions also offer free trials, so you can try before you buy. Additionally, with some partner solutions, you have the option to request a private offer through SAP Store. Private offers allow you to negotiate prices and terms directly with the partner and close the transaction within the store.

  • How does support work?

    SAP Store offers onboarding and support when you purchase a solution. SAP customers get access to step-by-step guides, documentation, and escalation paths through SAP’s support infrastructure—along with partner support. For all partner solutions, technical support is provided by the partner to ensure customers get accurate answers and the best possible experience.

  • Do I need to have an account to purchase from SAP online?

    For most SAP solutions and all partner solutions, you’ll be asked to create or log in to a valid SAP account to complete your purchase. A few select products allow you to purchase as a guest. For those solutions, you’ll see Check out as guest as an option in your cart. Once you’ve logged into your SAP account, you’ll have capabilities according to your designated buyer type. See the account information FAQ for more information on buyer types.

  • How can I get a custom price quote for an SAP solution?

    SAP Store is designed to be a self-service, digital marketplace, so we don’t issue formal price quotes for SAP Store orders. For SAP solutions, when you select Buy now to begin the purchase process, the SAP Store configurator calculates the price based on your configuration. You can select Bookmark if you want to save the configurator information for later.

    With some partner solutions, you have the option to Request a quote. This allows you to get a custom quote and negotiate prices and terms directly with the partner.

  • Why should partners list solutions on SAP Store?

    Partners should list solutions on SAP Store to reach new customers, accelerate sales, and drive business growth. Partners get access to SAP’s large, global customer base in more than 200 countries. These qualified customers are actively searching for ready-to-go enterprise solutions that work with the SAP products they use. SAP Store offers several benefits designed to help partners scale efficiently. Store listings integrate directly into SAP cloud applications, enabling real-time discovery by business users, right at the point of need. With global transaction handling, SAP also simplifies the complexities of invoicing, tax, and payment in markets where you may not be directly active. And if your solution is built on SAP Business Technology Platform, you can take advantage of provisioning and integration automation that speeds up delivery and access for your customers.

  • How can I buy more of what I have from SAP Store?

    To purchase more of the solutions you already have, visit SAP for Me and sign in to access SAP Store for post sales.

  • Can partners buy SAP solutions for their customers on SAP Store?

    Yes, we have an indirect store on SAP for Me. Learn more.
