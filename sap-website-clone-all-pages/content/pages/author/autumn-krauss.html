Autumn D. Krauss, PhD

Chief Scientist

Dr. Autumn D. Krauss is Chief Scientist at SAP SuccessFactors, where she leads the Future of Work Research Lab. An organizational psychologist and scientist-practitioner with more than 20 years of applied research and consulting experience, she translates evidence-based insight into business strategy, HR technology direction, and practical guidance for organizations navigating the future of work.

Her expertise spans employee experience, organizational culture, transformation and change, talent practices, and employee health, safety, and well-being, with a current focus on how AI is reshaping work, HR, and HR technology.
