Frequently asked questions

SAP Cloud ERP Private is a tailored-to-fit cloud ERP that adapts to your organization's unique transformation while enabling the migration of your on-premises ERP investment from SAP, such as SAP ERP Central Component (SAP ECC), SAP S/4HANA, and SAP ERP.

Visit our innovations page to learn about the innovations available in the latest release.

The solution has a two-year release cycle, with new innovations and enhancements rolled out every six months. Every release receives maintenance for seven years.​

SAP has two cloud ERP solutions to choose from:

  • SAP Cloud ERP: Our ready-to-run cloud ERP managed by SAP.

  • SAP Cloud ERP Private: Our tailored-to-fit cloud ERP that adapts to your unique business needs while enabling the migration of your on-premises ERP investment from SAP.
