Key findings from SIA: Why SAP Fieldglass is transforming workforce management

SAP Fieldglass stands apart in the VMS market, proving that these platforms are more than tools; they are the cornerstone of modern workforce transformation.

Amber RothUpdated on July 2, 2026

Vendor management systems (VMSs) have evolved from straightforward tools into indispensable solutions for navigating the intricacies of modern workforce management. At SAP, we are proud to be at the heart of this transformation, delivering innovative solutions to businesses worldwide.

A recent report by Staffing Industry Analysts (SIA), VMS Global Landscape and Differentiators, underscores the pivotal role VMSs play in the modern business landscape. Nearly 80% of large companies now rely on these platforms to streamline operations, manage contingent workers, and drive efficiency. This is a testament to the growing demand for sophisticated tools capable of simplifying workforce management. Here, I’ll delve into the key findings of the report, spotlighting SAP Fieldglass as a leader in this space.

The rise of VMS: Meeting the challenges of modern workforce management

Initially designed to manage contingent workers sourced through staffing agencies, vendor management systems have grown remarkably in scope. Today, they encompass diverse sourcing channels, including statement of work (SOW) service contracts and direct sourcing programs. This evolution highlights the increasing complexity of workforce management as businesses face challenges such as globalization, regulatory compliance, and the need for agile operations.

With tools like SAP Fieldglass, businesses can effectively navigate these complexities, optimizing workforce strategies while maintaining cost control and compliance. One key advantage of VMS platforms lies in their ability to integrate with broader enterprise systems. This ensures that data flows seamlessly across departments, from human resources to procurement and finance. Such integration reduces inefficiencies, improves decision-making, and fosters collaboration across teams, all while enabling businesses to scale operations in response to growing demands.

Global reach and industry excellence

SAP Fieldglass stands out with its unparalleled global reach—a feature emphasized in SIA’s report. Operating in over 180 countries and supporting more than 22 languages, our platform exemplifies what it means to be a truly global solution. Beyond multilingual support, SAP Fieldglass offers full invoicing capabilities in 118 countries, ensuring seamless financial operations and compliance with local regulations across borders.

Our industry coverage is equally noteworthy. SAP Fieldglass serves a diverse range of sectors, from manufacturing and healthcare to finance and public administration. Each industry brings unique challenges, and our platform addresses them with customized solutions. This versatility positions SAP Fieldglass as a vital asset for organizations with complex, cross-industry needs.

The platform also supports organizations in adapting to emerging workforce trends. With the rise of hybrid work environments and the gig economy, businesses increasingly depend on contingent workers and freelance talent. SAP Fieldglass enables companies to seamlessly integrate these new labor categories into their workforce strategies, ensuring they remain competitive in a rapidly evolving market.

Service developments and strategic investments

SIA’s findings highlight several key differentiators that set SAP Fieldglass apart in the VMS market. Among the most notable is our connected ecosystem, featuring productized integrations to SAP applications and an open API framework to connect to any technology. By creating a cohesive ecosystem, customers can optimize their workforce strategies, streamline processes, leverage a unified data model, and take advantage of cross-platform analytics ultimately driving enhanced productivity and a holistic view of all business processes.

Furthermore, SAP continues to invest in cutting-edge technologies aimed at redefining how businesses manage their workforce. Our platform leverages advanced analytics and artificial intelligence to provide actionable insights into labor trends, vendor performance, and cost optimization.

Security and continuous improvement

Security is another critical area where SAP Fieldglass excels. In today's interconnected world, protecting sensitive workforce data is paramount. Our platform incorporates robust security protocols to safeguard information while ensuring compliance with global privacy standards.

At SAP, we also recognize the importance of continuous improvement. By actively listening to client feedback and monitoring market trends, we strive to evolve and adapt our services to meet the changing needs of businesses.

Customer satisfaction and loyalty

Customer perceptions of SAP Fieldglass further validate its leadership in workforce management solutions. Rated highly for innovation, capability, and responsiveness, the platform has cultivated a remarkably loyal customer base. This underscores the value SAP Fieldglass delivers in addressing the complexities of modern workforce strategies while fostering strong, lasting partnerships with organizations worldwide.

The findings from SIA reaffirm what we at SAP have always believed—VMSs are more than tools; they are the cornerstone of modern workforce transformation. As businesses continue to evolve, we remain steadfast in our mission to lead this transformation, supporting our clients with the best-in-class solutions they deserve.
