Learn how to strategically select and develop the next generation of your workforce.

This research examines the growing challenges facing early-career talent in a rapidly evolving job market shaped by AI, declining entry-level opportunities, and widening skills gaps. Drawing on insights from early talent and HR, it outlines the risks of underinvesting in early talent and provides actionable strategies for HR leaders to redesign roles, improve readiness, and build a stronger, future-ready workforce.
