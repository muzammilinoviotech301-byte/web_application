Q3 2026 Financial Results
Christian Klein, CEO

“Q3 was an outstanding quarter, with current cloud backlog up 26% at constant currencies. Our Autonomous Enterprise strategy is not just resonating, it is being adopted. Supplementary Information
Financial Results1

Cloud revenue growth was positively impacted by several quarter-specific effects, mainly a multi-quarter revenue catch-up from customers returning from financial distress.

Total revenue in the third quarter included a contribution of €49 million from the acquisitions of Reltio, Dremio, and Prior Labs.
 

IFRS and non-IFRS operating profit included a negative contribution of €59 million and €39 million, respectively, from the aforementioned acquisitions. In addition, IFRS and non-IFRS operating profit growth benefited from prior year negative impacts of €0.1 billion each from a change in case law affecting SAP's other tax litigation and from a workforce transformation, both recorded in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Share Repurchase Program

In January 2026, SAP announced a new share repurchase program with an aggregate volume of up to €10 billion and a term until December 31, 2027. As of September 30, 2026, SAP had repurchased 25,205,983 shares at an average price of €164.79 resulting in a purchased volume of approximately €4.2 billion under the program.

Business Outlook

Financial Outlook

For 2026, SAP is updating its cloud revenue and cloud and software revenue outlook and now expects:

  • To generate cloud revenue towards the upper end of the outlook range of €25.8 – 26.2 billion at constant currencies (2025: €21.02 billion), up 23% to 25% at constant currencies.

  • To generate cloud and software revenue towards the upper end of the outlook range of €36.3 – 36.8 billion at constant currencies (2025: €32.54 billion), up 12% to 13% at constant currencies.

SAP continues to expect:

  • €11.8 – 12.2 billion operating profit (non-IFRS) at constant currencies (2025: €10.42 billion), up 13% to 17% at constant currencies.

  • Approximately €10 billion free cash flow at actual currencies (2025: €8.24 billion).

  • An effective tax rate (non-IFRS) of approximately 29% (2025: 30.5%).2

  • Constant currencies current cloud backlog growth to slightly decelerate (2025: 25%).

SAP further expects:

  • Constant currencies total revenue growth in 2026 to remain at similar levels as in 2025 (10.6%) and to accelerate in 2027.

  • Total operating expenses (non-IFRS) to grow at 80% to 90% of total revenue growth in 2027.

  • Constant currencies software support revenue decline rate to accelerate in the coming years as a consequence of an acceleration of customers transforming to the cloud.

SAP's current cloud backlog outlook for full-year 2026 is based on the assumption of a stable macroeconomic environment in the fourth quarter.

 

[2] The effective tax rate (non-IFRS) is a non-IFRS financial measure and is presented for supplemental informational purposes only. We do not provide an outlook for the effective tax rate (IFRS) due to the uncertainty and potential variability of gains and losses associated with equity securities, which are reconciling items between the two effective tax rates (non-IFRS and IFRS). These items cannot be provided without unreasonable efforts but could have a significant impact on our future effective tax rate (IFRS).

Non-Financial Outlook

For 2026, SAP continues to expect:

  • Cloud Customer Satisfaction (Cloud CSAT) to be in a range of 75% to 76% (2025: 75%).

  • The Employee Engagement Index to be in a range of 74% to 78% (2025: 76%).

  • The Business Health Culture Index (BHCI) to be in a range of 80% to 82% (2025: 81%).

  • To steadily decrease carbon emissions across the relevant value chain (2025: 3.6 Mt).

Business Highlights

Business Highlights

In the third quarter, customers around the globe continued to choose the “RISE with SAP” journey. These customers included: AstraZeneca, Aydem Enerji, Banco Bilbao, Fairstone Bank of Canada, Grupo SBF, Infineon Technologies, Nagel Group Logistics, and NTN Corporation.

 

A5X, Bitzer, BLACKROLL, Greenbrier Maxion (GBMX), Krannich Solar Group, Petroplus, Rittal, and SUZHOU ELE.MFG chose “SAP GROW”.

 

Austrian Post (Österreichische Post), Commerz Real, Decathlon, Fraport, Henkel, MAN INDUSTRIES LIMITED, and Toyota Motor Philippines Corporation chose SAP’s AI and data solutions.

 

Key customer wins across SAP’s solution portfolio included: Chery Automobile, DOUGLAS Group, Helvetia Holding, Kao Corporation, NTT DATA, Tauranga City Council, and Wipro.

 

Bertelsmann, Eldorado Brasil, enersuisse, HUGO BOSS, Infineum, Infosys, Mondelez, Panasonic, Roche, and Thalia Bücher went live on SAP solutions in the third quarter.

 

In the third quarter, SAP’s cloud revenue performance was particularly strong in APJ and EMEA and solid in the Americas region. Brazil, France, Germany, Italy, India, South Korea and Spain had outstanding performance, while Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the U.S. were particularly strong.

 

On July 6, SAP announced that it has completed the acquisition of Dremio, an open, high-performance data lakehouse platform.

 

On July 17, SAP announced that it has completed the acquisition of Prior Labs, the pioneer of Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs).

 

On October 6, SAP showcased the Autonomous Enterprise in action at SAP Connect, demonstrating how Joule Work and Joule Assistants generate value for customers across every line of business.

In addition, SAP announced that they entered into an agreement for SAP to acquire TechWolf, a provider of a leading AI work intelligence platform.
