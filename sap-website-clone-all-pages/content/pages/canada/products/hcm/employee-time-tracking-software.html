What is SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking?

SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking is a time and attendance solution that helps you simplify complex employee time tracking, boost productivity, and reduce compliance risk, with actionable insights and native payroll integrations for accurate, timely pay.

Let your entire workforce record work time quickly and easily, so they can get paid accurately and on time.

  • Intuitive employee time recording with AI across devices
  • Mobile clock-in and clock-out with geofencing
  • Flexible configurations, instant validation, and attestation for compliance with policies
  • Time sheet approval center for mass approvals and exception handling
  • One platform for all your scheduling, time, and payroll needs

Key features

Built-in compliance and validation

A rules engine enforces country, industry, company, and union policies, including overtime thresholds, rest periods, rounding, and flex time. Every entry is validated instantly at the point of entry. Time sheet attestation creates an auditable record of employee confirmation.

Time Assistant

AI agents autofill time entries based on employee patterns and assigned schedules, nudge employees on missing or incomplete submissions, and answer time and absence policy questions instantly through natural language, all without HR intervention. Available across web, mobile, and Microsoft Teams.

Manager approval center and analytics

A unified approval center lets managers approve time sheets in bulk, with alerts that surface exceptions and pending items. Dashboards cover absence rates, labor costs, and headcount, with filters for location, business unit, and time period.

Automated payroll integrations

Time data flows automatically into SAP and 3rd payroll solutions, with overtime, premiums, and shift differentials calculated and ready for pay processing, no manual exports or reconciliation needed.

Frequently asked questions

SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central is the prerequisite for SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking. It must be implemented before Time Tracking can be activated and used.

The minimum order quantity for SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking is 1 user.

SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking is deployed as a Public Cloud SaaS solution. It is not part of the S/4HANA suite.
