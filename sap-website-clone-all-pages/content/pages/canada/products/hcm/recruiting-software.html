What is SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors?

SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors is an AI recruiting software platform that combines a connected applicant tracking system (ATS) and recruiting CRM, integrated with SAP SuccessFactors HCM to connect hiring to your full employee lifecycle.

Source, engage, and hire top talent with an AI-first applicant tracking system built for any hiring need.

  • AI-driven candidate matching, screening, and interview scheduling.

  • Native SMS, WhatsApp, and email candidate communication in one place.

  • Recruiting CRM with talent pooling, nurture campaigns, and engagement analytics.

  • Built for corporate, high-volume, and seasonal hiring at any scale
