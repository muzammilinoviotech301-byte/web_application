Frequently asked questions

SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals is a solution designed to help organizations effectively manage employee performance and goal setting. It enables businesses to align individual objectives with corporate strategies, optimize employee engagement, and drive organizational agility. With features such as goal cascading, continuous feedback and coaching, and robust performance assessments, it empowers organizations to build high-performing teams by ensuring alignment between strategic objectives and employees’ contributions to overall success and recognizing and rewarding top talent.

A performance management system is a structured framework that tracks the performance of employees in a manner that is consistent and measurable, ensuring that employees’ contributions are aligned with the organization’s strategic objectives. By promoting real-time and ongoing collaboration between managers and employees to set clear expectations, define measurable goals, evaluate performance, and provide constructive feedback, performance management systems help increase overall productivity and engagement, minimize turnover, and drive better business outcomes.

Dynamic teams are temporary groups formed to work on specific projects and disband once completed. This flexible approach allows employees to move between projects based on their skills and organizational needs. However, challenges such as team alignment, collaboration, and leadership visibility often arise. SAP SuccessFactors is addressing these challenges by providing a foundational dynamic team’s feature that allows users to create a team, add team members, and define roles and responsibilities.  Combined with SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals, organizations can use the feature to track the performance of these teams while driving personalized development and growth opportunities.

The talent intelligence hub is a centralized framework available within SAP SuccessFactors solutions that helps organizations understand the skills their employees have today, the gaps that exist, and the ways they can close those gaps. With machine learning and AI-powered talent intelligence, the talent intelligence hub provides highly personalized learning and development recommendations that help ensure employees develop their skill sets while pursuing a career aligned to their ambitions. This accelerates upskilling and reskilling at scale while delivering unique talent experiences for every employee across the SAP SuccessFactors HCM suite.

The Performance Preparation Agent, one of the AI agent capabilities within the Joule solution, helps managers prepare for effective, data‑driven performance and development conversations in SAP SuccessFactors Performance & Goals. It automatically gathers employee data such as goals, achievements, and feedback, and turns it into personalized insights and talking points for 1:1s and reviews. The agent reduces manual preparation and recommends next steps, enabling more focused, fair, and growth‑oriented conversations.
