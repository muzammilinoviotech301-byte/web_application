What is SAP SuccessFactors Compensation?

Our intuitive compensation management software helps you move beyond siloed decisions and manual processes to build strategic compensation plans aligned with employee, business, and budget needs.

With the SAP SuccessFactors Compensation solution, you can improve individual and business performance through personalized, timely rewards and recognition programs.

  • Make informed decisions about employee pay.

  • Automate and simplify employee compensation planning.

  • Centralize employee compensation management.

  • Provide clear employee compensation statements.
