ABOUT THIS GUIDE

The SAP TechEd Innovation News Guide for 2026 shows how SAP’s vision of the Autonomous Enterprise is becoming a reality by equipping people to build, connect and govern enterprise AI, from intent to trusted execution.

This guide builds upon previous Innovation News Guides from the SAP Sapphire and SAP Connect events in 2026, and spotlights ongoing innovation across Joule Work and SAP Business AI Platform. The Joule Work user experience allows people to describe a goal, with AI then orchestrating the right agents, applications and data to achieve it. SAP Business AI Platform offers deep process context, semantically rich data and purpose-built models with enterprise governance. Together, these will help organizations scale AI from pilots to enterprise-wide deployments that drive business impact.

To help drive this impact, new learning programs will provide developers, partners and practitioners with the skills they need to help customers get lasting value from their SAP Business AI investments.

Feedback and insights gained during early adoption programs have helped shape and refine the offerings. Continuing this momentum, general availability for Autonomous Enterprise assistants and agents, Joule Work and Joule Studio is now underway in a phased approach. General availability for other innovations starts in October, with further capabilities to be rolled out as SAP continues to expand the SAP Business AI portfolio.

JOULE

JOULE

​​New Joule Work Features Help Accelerate Autonomous AI​

Joule Work gains new capabilities designed to help customers more efficiently and securely execute complex business processes across enterprise systems. Announced at the SAP Sapphire conference in May 2026, Joule Work provides a user interface (UI) where people describe a goal and AI orchestrates the right agents, applications and data — spanning both SAP and non-SAP systems — to achieve it. General availability of Joule Work is now underway in a phased approach.

New capabilities include:

  • New agentic thinking capability for complex workflows: Provides additional support for Joule Work to reason, plan and execute complex tasks across organizations. Enterprises can now deploy agentic AI more securely, with built-in governance features including audit trails, role-based permissions and human-in-the-loop checkpoints. This agentic thinking capability is a critical piece of SAP’s vision for the Autonomous Enterprise, which runs on AI assistants and agents that manage and execute connected processes and tasks. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.
  • Expanded SAP Knowledge Graph support for customers’ unique environments: New capabilities will help organizations incorporate richer, company-specific information. This context will be available to the Joule solution, helping AI assistants and agents to reason more accurately and drive meaningful business impact. General availability is planned for Q1 2027. Read more in the SAP Knowledge Graph section below.
  • Enhanced security for agent-to-agent interoperability: SAP is adding new capabilities to its agent gateway to help third-party agents connect more securely with Joule Agents. Third-party agents can invoke Joule Agents via a secure gateway that enforces access control, allowing agents from different providers to participate in the same business workflow, coordinated through a single, governed entry point. This helps expand existing agent-to-agent (A2A) features, provides deeper connectivity between SAP and non-SAP agents, and advances SAP’s commitment to interoperability. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.
  • New governance solution for centralized management of AI interactions: A new SAP Enterprise AI Gateway solution will offer a centralized way to manage AI traffic across the enterprise to help consolidate authentication, policy enforcement, routing, monitoring, usage tracking and auditing. Offering one control plane for AI interactions, it will help organizations move from isolated AI pilots to governed, production-ready AI agents and applications. General availability is planned for Q1 2027. Read more in the SAP Enterprise AI Gateway section below.

SAP BUSINESS AI PLATFORM

JOULE STUDIO

New Joule Studio Features Help Build and Extend Enterprise Agentic Solutions

The Joule Studio solution, announced at SAP Sapphire in May 2026, gains new capabilities that help customers and partners build, extend, orchestrate and operate enterprise agentic solutions. General availability of Joule Studio is now underway in a phased approach.

To further simplify agentic development, Joule Studio is natively integrated with Joule Work, SAP’s primary engagement layer. This integration gives business users and developers a single place to create custom agents, extend existing Joule Agents and orchestrate workflows through SAP’s partnership with n8n.

Additionally, Joule Studio enhancements provide developers with the business context they need to more easily turn business intent into enterprise solutions. Developers can enter their intent, and Joule Studio helps identify processes, data and APIs; map system relationships; account for authorizations; and translate business requirements into a solution. It does this through its integration with SAP LeanIX, SAP Signavio and SAP Knowledge Graph solutions, as well as SAP Domain Models.

Enhancements include:

  • Tighter integration between Joule Studio and SAP Business Data Cloud: Development teams will be able to create and access data products in the SAP Business Data Cloud solution directly within Joule Studio. Customers can opt to give agents runtime access to trusted business data within the controls already established and authorized by IT teams. This will help automate what was previously a manual process. General availability is planned for Q1 2027.
  • Intent-based development capabilities: In one step, the Joule Studio CLI tool can configure Model Context Protocol (MCP) connections, development skills and authorizations. When developers open the project in their preferred IDE, business and product requirements are already available as structured project files. They can focus on agent logic and differentiated business value rather than infrastructure setup. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.
  • Easier agent extensions: Existing Joule Agents can be extended by adding instructions that provide further context, with tools like MCPs, APIs and workflow triggers, using n8n capabilities. All modifications go through a governed pipeline before production. General availability is now underway in a phased approach. General availability for extensions using additional tools and workflow triggers is planned for Q1 2027.
  • Deployment on Joule Studio runtime: Agents built using Joule Studio can now be deployed on Joule Studio runtime, allowing them to be discoverable via the SAP AI Agent Hub solution and accessible to users within Joule Work conversations. Customers can benefit from the security, observability and authorizations provided by Joule Studio runtime. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.

Joule Studio Helps IT and Business Users Collaborate on Development

New Joule Studio features are designed to help business experts and professional developers build custom agents together in a shared, aligned development environment using the tools and environment best suited to their roles.

  • Role-based access: Gives IT teams greater control over how contributors participate in development, while maintaining oversight of changes. Line-of-business users can shape requirements and business logic in the web experience, while developers can inspect and refine generated components and continue developing in their preferred IDE or the Joule Studio CLI tool. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.
  • Design and run n8n workflows: Will support smoother workflow orchestration, helping developers design, automate and connect complex, multi-step business processes across SAP and non-SAP systems. General availability is planned for Q1 2027.

Additional Joule Studio Capabilities Support Agent Lifecycle Management

New Joule Studio capabilities will help customers govern and scale agentic solutions more confidently, with tools to help users test behavior, control access, track changes and maintain oversight throughout the development and deployment lifecycle. The new features offer enhanced visibility into agent behavior, control over production updates, and governance over agent access. This can help developers better manage production changes, which can be more easily tracked, approved and reversed — creating an audit trail for compliance reviews and incident response.

  • Pre-deployment testing capabilities: Provide teams with a built-in sandbox to validate agent behavior before it reaches production. Teams can use this isolated environment to run test queries, inspect traces and use auto-generated unit tests before deploying. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.
  • Offline evaluations: Will help assess accuracy, A2A call correctness and resistance to prompt-injection against auto-generated scenarios. Changes to prompts, models or data sources can be verified in an isolated environment before moving to a shared environment. General availability is planned for late Q4 2026.
  • Additional governance with Joule Studio runtime and NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform: Provides runtime-level isolation with configurable policies and guardrails that keep agent execution contained and auditable. General availability is now underway in a phased approach.

SAP INTEGRATION SUITE

New SAP Enterprise AI Gateway Provides Control Across AI Agents, Apps and Models

Organizations face growing challenges around security, governance, compliance and cost control as AI agents increasingly connect to external models and services. Managing these risks through project-specific controls can slow enterprise-scale AI adoption.

The new SAP Enterprise AI Gateway solution, part of SAP Business AI Platform, is designed to address this challenge by extending enterprise-grade governance to AI interactions. Offering one control plane for AI interactions, it will help organizations move from isolated AI pilots to governed, production-ready AI agents and applications.

The new solution builds on SAP Integration Suite, which customers already use to connect business applications, data and processes. SAP Enterprise AI Gateway will provide centralized management for AI traffic (for example, MCPs, APIs, A2As and LLMs) across the enterprise, consolidating authentication, policy enforcement, routing, monitoring, usage tracking and auditing in a single, managed layer.

For developers, SAP Enterprise AI Gateway will help accelerate and simplify agent development through a standardized MCP gateway and developer hub experience. Developers will be able to discover and subscribe to MCP servers from any provider, access tools through a consistent interface, and reuse existing APIs and integration flows as agent-ready capabilities.

For platform and governance teams, SAP Enterprise AI Gateway will provide consistent security, access controls and auditability across AI initiatives. Identity-based authentication, policy-driven authorization and consumption monitoring will provide the visibility and control needed to help manage risk, ensure compliance and optimize AI spending at scale.

General availability is planned for Q1 2027.

SAP Integration Suite Provides Real-Time Context to Agents and Applications

New real-time capabilities in SAP Integration Suite help keep critical information in sync and provide more timely business context to applications and agents, supporting the ability of teams to respond to business changes faster.

Enhancements include:

  • Expanded distributed tracing capabilities for SAP Integration Suite, advanced event mesh: Offer support for SAP S/4HANA, providing full observability and performance monitoring across event-driven architectures (EDAs).
  • Unified, real-time monitoring with SAP Cloud ALM: Helps customers monitor all integration scenarios directly within the SAP Cloud ALM solution, including those generated by SAP Integration Suite, advanced event mesh. This feature provides centralized visibility into integration patterns in operation between SAP and non-SAP landscapes.
  • SAP Integration Suite, edge integration cell, additional tenant: Allows customers on the RISE with SAP journey to run public cloud SAP Integration Suite capabilities directly in private cloud environments through a fully managed runtime service. This helps customers use agentic AI across hybrid environments and connect their business processes without having to manage and maintain complex Kubernetes clusters. The additional tenant is pre-provisioned and can be deployed quickly to support integration scenarios in private landscapes.

All capabilities noted above are generally available now.

Expanded AI Capabilities and Third-Party Support in SAP Integration Suite Help Accelerate Developer Productivity

New SAP Integration Suite capabilities help developers build integrations and manage increasingly complex landscapes more efficiently across a broader set of enterprise applications. The latest innovations help teams prepare their landscapes for agentic AI by streamlining the development and management of integration workflows.

New features include:

  • AI-assisted message processing error resolution: Provides an embedded AI bug analyzer and solution recommender that helps simplify integration management and debugging cycles. This is generally available now.
  • AI detection of irregular request payload sizes: Automatically flags anomalies, providing proactive security and operational oversight for critical integrations and API traffic across enterprise landscapes. This is generally available now.
  • User-generated message mapping artifacts: Will automatically construct mapping expressions and help save time on manual schema setup. This will be available through the cloud integration capability in SAP Integration Suite by uploading sample source, target payload and expected output files. General availability is planned for Q1 2027.
  • Enhanced integration with GitHub: Helps developers manage and deploy integration flow artifacts directly within Git repositories and perform core Git operations natively within the platform. This new connectivity helps developers more easily compare flows, reconcile code conflicts and streamline version management in their Git repositories. This is generally available now.
  • RosettaNet support in SAP Integration Suite: Equips enterprises with native capabilities to standardize and automate high-tech, supply chain and B2B communications. This supports RosettaNet Implementation Framework (RNIF) adapter and Partner Interface Process (PIP). This is generally available now.
  • Third-party application adapters: Increase the ability of both users and agents to integrate and automate processes across the enterprise landscape. Adapters include: Gmail, Google Sheets, GoToWebinar, Microsoft Excel and Salesforce Service Cloud. These are generally available now.

DATA AND ANALYTICS

Expanded Lakehouse Capabilities Support a More Open Approach to Data Management

New lakehouse capabilities will provide native support for Apache Iceberg and a distributed SQL query engine. Supported by Dremio, now part of SAP, these capabilities will help customers more easily access and use SAP and third-party data for AI and analytics. Integration with Joule Work will enable customers to engage with the new lakehouse features using natural language prompts.

SAP helps unify fragmented business context through a business data fabric architecture, bringing together multiple data sources while keeping business process knowledge, policies and logic intact. The new lakehouse capabilities will extend the business data fabric architecture with a more open approach to storing, processing and governing data across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

The new lakehouse capabilities demonstrate continued momentum for integrating recently acquired technologies and advance SAP’s open data ecosystem strategy with additional support for open-source standards. With the new component, customers can reduce complexity while preserving architectural flexibility. Other potential benefits include accelerated queries and access to insights, lower storage and integration costs, fewer data copies, faster access to trusted data, and a consistent governance model across multi-cloud landscapes.

Dremio capabilities include a native Apache Iceberg catalog based on Apache Polaris (integrated with the SAP Business Data Cloud catalog) and a distributed SQL engine that can query a variety of lakehouse catalogs and relational databases. SAP already supports Delta Lake and now gains support for Apache Iceberg.

The SAP Reltio solution continues to gain momentum since its announcement at the SAP Connect program in October 2026, with added support for building unified data products. Together with the SAP Master Data Governance application in SAP BDC, it will deepen SAP’s native master data management capabilities within the business data fabric.

General availability of lakehouse capabilities and SAP Reltio data product capabilities is planned for Q1 2027.

New SAP BDC Ontology Manager Delivers Curated Knowledge for AI

The new SAP Business Data Cloud Ontology Manager solution in SAP Business Data Cloud will provide an AI-assisted experience for curating domain-specific ontologies — focused, reusable business context for AI applications and agents.

This solution will complement SAP Knowledge Graph, which provides customers with rich business context related to their SAP systems. Developers using SAP Business Data Cloud Ontology Manager will be able to select relevant context for their custom agents and publish domain-specific views tailored to specific business challenges, such as understanding customers, investigating equipment failures or helping AI agents recommend next best actions. These assets can also be reused for other AI projects, helping reduce time and token use.

The solution will be able to access both SAP data products and customer-managed data products, allowing customers to extend business context with their own relationships, rules and processes. SAP Business Data Cloud Ontology Manager is designed to deliver deep business context that enables AI to act autonomously and deliver positive business outcomes.

SAP Business Data Cloud Ontology Manager is planned to be in beta in November 2026.

Joule Simplifies Database Administration in SAP HANA Cloud

New Joule agentic capabilities within SAP HANA Cloud will help simplify the work of database administrators, allowing them to use a conversational interface to automate tasks such as monitoring system health, troubleshooting issues, optimizing database and SQL performance, and managing lifecycle operations.

The new capabilities will address growing operational challenges for enterprises deploying AI at scale. Database administration remains a highly specialized discipline that can be time-consuming and manual. At the same time, AI and agent-based workloads have increased query volumes, making it harder to predict database resource consumption.

By automating administrative tasks in SAP HANA Cloud, database teams can reallocate expertise toward more strategic work and scale support for growing AI-driven workloads. Moving forward, customers will benefit from a single experience that covers administrative work across their SAP HANA Cloud landscape. AI-assisted diagnosis and remediation will also help reduce the time specialists spend investigating and resolving incidents.

General availability for these capabilities is planned for Q4 2026.

New SAP Knowledge Graph Capabilities Enrich Joule with Customer-Specific Context

New capabilities for the SAP Knowledge Graph solution will help organizations incorporate richer, company-specific information, enabling Joule to deliver more relevant responses grounded in data from across customer landscapes.

SAP Knowledge Graph has traditionally included data from specific SAP applications. Moving forward, the solution will incorporate data from additional SAP and non-SAP sources that are unique to each customer.

The new SAP Knowledge Graph features will help data architects:

  • Incorporate customer-managed data products from the SAP Business Data Cloud (SAP BDC) solution, extending value for current SAP BDC customers.
  • Use data that resides within extensions of SAP Autonomous Suite offerings, broadening the reach of SAP Knowledge Graph beyond SAP reference data models to include customer-specific information.

These innovations will provide SAP Knowledge Graph with customer-specific business context, helping Joule and AI assistants and agents to reason more accurately and drive meaningful business impact.

General availability for all features is planned for Q1 2027.

SAP AI CORE

SAP-RPT AI Model Family Expands Use Cases for Predictions and Decisions

Expanded tabular AI capabilities in the SAP-RPT AI model family will support the use of structured data for faster, more accurate predictions and decisions. Part of SAP Business AI Platform, these innovations will provide additional context that supports the ability of AI to reason and take appropriate action.

Although LLMs are powerful tools for handling unstructured data, they often struggle with the structured data that is critical to everyday business processes and decisions. Tabular AI enables AI to process, understand and make predictions using structured data in business applications, databases and other sources. Unlike traditional machine learning, tabular AI delivers more precise, explainable predictions without custom training or feature engineering.

SAP was an early mover in tabular AI with its 2025 launch of the SAP-RPT-1 AI model, and continues to bring new models and capabilities to market for AI developers, data teams and business users.

Innovations include:

  • Time series forecasting: A new SAP-RPT-TS AI model helps customers predict values across time-indexed business metrics for key forecasting use cases, such as demand forecasting in the SAP Integrated Business Planning (SAP IBP) solution, without requiring custom machine learning. Developers can use the model through SAP HANA Cloud or the SAP AI Core foundation. SAP-RPT-TS is generally available now.
  • Decision intelligence: A new SAP-RPT-Decisions AI model will help customers automate and scale decision-making in applications and agentic workflows. Typical use cases span core SAP business processes such as approving a customer order in SAP S/4HANA, routing supply chain exceptions in SAP IBP, or selecting the right approval path for a purchase requisition in SAP Ariba solutions. Developers will be able to access the model through SAP AI Core. General availability for SAP-RPT-Decisions is planned for Q4 2026.
  • Multi-table processing and anomaly detection: A new SAP-RPT-2 AI model will expand SAP-RPT-1.6 capabilities beyond regression and classification tasks, with support for anomaly and outlier detection, structured data embeddings for AI-assisted analysis, and native multi-table processing. These additions will help customers automatically surface unusual patterns in business data — such as unusual transactions in the SAP S/4HANA Finance solution — and give business teams and AI agents faster, deeper insights into enterprise data with less manual preparation work. Developers can access the model through SAP AI Core. General availability for SAP-RPT-2 is planned for Q1 2027.
In partnership with HPE, SAP announced the deployment of SAP-RPT on the HPE Spaceborne Computer-2 aboard the International Space Station (ISS), one of the most significant edge-compute validations of any enterprise AI model to date.

SAP Document AI Innovations Advance Autonomous Document Processing

Expanded intelligent document processing capabilities in the SAP Document AI solution will include the new SAP-DOC-1 AI model, deeper integration with Joule Work and the Joule Studio solution, and increased configurability and flexibility for administrators.

Businesses routinely handle large volumes of incoming documents that do not conform to existing templates in their systems. This can create automation gaps that require manual intervention to repair. The new SAP-DOC-1 AI model will help lower barriers to intelligent document processing across teams. It is built to more accurately extract information from documents without requiring up-front configuration, making it immediately usable across many document types and formats.

Business users and developers will benefit from deeper integration of SAP Document AI with Joule Work and Joule Studio. Joule Work will help business users process, query and extract information from documents needed to trigger the next steps in a business workflow. Developers can use Joule Studio to build document-aware agents and workflows, using agentic document-processing tools for extraction and grounding.

New SAP Document AI capabilities will give administrators greater flexibility and control, helping reduce manual effort while enabling optimized document processing for specific business needs.

These features will include:

  • A document splitting node: Divides multi-page files into logical segments, supporting workflows that process complex and composite documents.
  • Custom prompting: Helps administrators define exactly what information to extract and select the AI model best suited to a task.

Faster and more accurate document processing will help agents process documents as part of workflows across supported applications and systems.

General availability for all enhancements is planned for Q1 2027.

BUSINESS TRANSFORMATION MANAGEMENT

AI Governance Expands with New Joule Assistant and SAP AI Agent Hub Capabilities

A new AI Governance Assistant for compliance with the EU AI Act and new governance capabilities will help address critical barriers to scaling enterprise AI: keeping AI systems classified, compliant and operating within defined organizational guardrails.

Governance innovations include:

  • AI Governance Assistant: Will support compliance with the EU AI Act and other regulatory frameworks directly within the enterprise architecture inventory that organizations already maintain. Users can upload existing system documentation, and the Joule Assistant will extract relevant content, identify gaps and propose a risk classification (prohibited, high risk, limited risk or minimal risk) mapped to the relevant regulatory frameworks, along with citations to the relevant requirements. For organizations that have typically managed this work with spreadsheets and point-in-time surveys, compliance assessments will now be able to run continuously against a live inventory. The assistant will flag incomplete documentation, pre-fill fields from completed assessments, identify systems that appear high-risk but lack classification, and trigger reassessment automatically when a fact sheet changes materially. All outputs will be framed as drafts for legal and compliance review. The assistant will be part of the SAP AI Agent Hub solution, which provides visibility and governance of all SAP and non-SAP AI agents across an organization’s IT landscape. General availability is planned for Q4 2026.
  • Enterprise knowledge repository feature: Will help connect and synthesize architecture decisions and standards, landscape data and documentation. This governed knowledge will be readily available as ingestible context for agents and can be reused. General availability is planned for Q1 2027.

Reliable AI agents need systems that are classified and documented, knowledge that is governed and version-controlled, and data interfaces built to understand intent. Together, these capabilities address this foundational requirement of the Autonomous Enterprise.

SERVICES, LEARNING AND CROSS-PORTFOLIO

New SAP Business AI Platform Solution Architect Certification and Learning Journey

A new SAP Business AI Platform solution architect certification has been designed for developers, partners and practitioners. Aligned with the SAP Business AI Platform strategy, the certification validates architects’ ability to design solutions across the three platform pillars: build; contextualize and reason; and govern. Candidates must demonstrate architectural judgment across these three pillars, and show their expertise in holistic platform design versus knowledge of a single domain.

Scenarios and practical use cases in the redesigned exam assess how architects orchestrate multi-agent workflows. Candidates may use Joule and reference SAP documentation and SAP Learning materials during the exam.

For partners, this certification offers skills in a high-growth area of the SAP portfolio. It positions developers and architects to lead engagements focused on custom agent development, helping organizations move beyond out-of-the-box AI to tailored, enterprise-grade solutions.

Additionally, the Becoming an SAP Business AI Platform Solution Architect learning journey incorporates new content covering the platform solutions.

The new certification and learning journey are generally available now.

Free Joule Studio Certification Supports Agentic Development Skills

A new Joule Studio developer certification, now available on the SAP Learning site, teaches participants how to build and extend custom AI agents, applications and workflows using Joule Studio.
