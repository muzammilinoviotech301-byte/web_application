# SAP > SAP SE is a global enterprise software company headquartered in Walldorf, Germany. SAP provides cloud applications for finance, spend management, supply chain, human capital management, customer experience, and ERP, alongside an AI platform and a portfolio of AI agents and assistants - serving customers across most industries. SAP's flagship applications include SAP S/4HANA Cloud, SAP SuccessFactors, SAP Ariba, SAP Customer Experience, and SAP Business Network. The SAP AI Platform provides data, models, and governance for enterprise AI, and Joule is SAP's enterprise AI copilot delivered through a growing catalog of AI agents and assistants. This llms.txt covers the global (English) sap.com site.
